# Zero Range

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero_Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero_Range) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You are lethal when using ranged weapons against adjacent targets.

**Prerequisite:** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** When firing a ranged weapon at a target within or adjacent to your [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"), you gain a +1 bonus on the attack roll and deal +1 die of damage on a hit.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") or [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feats. This feat does not apply to [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons") or [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"), or in [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat").
