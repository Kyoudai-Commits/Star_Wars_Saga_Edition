# Gamorrean

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/c/c6/Gamorrean.webp/300px-Gamorrean.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Gamorrean.webp)

Gamorreans are green-skinned, porcine creatures native to [Gamorr](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorr "Gamorr"). Their tendency toward violence makes them valued as criminal enforcers; though viewed as mindless brutes, they don't care so long as they are paid to fight.

Gamorrean civilization revolves around the never-ending wars between their clans. Preferring large melee weapon in combat, many see ranged weapons as cowardly. Males live to fight; females farm, hunt, and manufacture weapons. Inter-clan hatred rarely fades, and anyone hiring Gamorreans should be aware of their clans to avoid infighting.

## Gamorrean Characteristics

**Personality:** Gamorreans are brutish, violent, and proud. They respect physical prowess and have no problem facing death against a foe they feel they are on at least equal footing with.

**Physical Description:** Gamorreans average about 1.8 meters tall and 140 kilograms, with thick green skin covering powerful muscles. Close-set eyes, a thick snout, tusks, and small horns give them a distinct look.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Gamorreans age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-6 Years | 7-12 Years | 13-29 Years | 30-39 Years | 40-44 Years | 45+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The preindustrial world of [Gamorr](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorr "Gamorr").

**Languages:** Gamorreans speak Gamorrean. They have no written version of the language. They can learn to understand, but not speak other [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages").

**Example Names:** Gartogg, Jubnuk, Ortugg, Ugmush, Venorra, Warlug.

**Adventurers:** Some Gamorreans leave their homeworld as slaves, but later escape or buy out their contracts. Others sell their contracts, finding employment as bodyguards or enforcers. A Gamorrean is constantly at war; it's only a matter of figuring out who the enemy is. Gamorrean heroes tend to select the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class.

## Gamorrean Species Traits

Gamorreans share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** Gamorreans have a +2 bonus to [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), but suffer a -2 penalty to both their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"). Gamorreans possess greater physical power, but have limited agility and intelligence.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Gamorreans have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Gamorreans have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Bonus Feat:** Gamorreans gain [Improved Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold "Improved Damage Threshold") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Great Fortitude:** Gamorreans gain a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), accounting for their great physical resistance.
- **Natively Phonetic:** Gamorreans can learn to understand, read, and write (but not speak) other [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages").
- **Primitive:** Gamorreans do not gain [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"), or [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") at first level, even if their [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") normally grants them.
- **Weapon Familiarity ([Arg'garok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arg%27garok "Arg'garok")):** Gamorreans treat the [Arg'garok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arg%27garok "Arg'garok") as an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon"), instead of an [Exotic Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Melee) "Exotic Weapons (Melee)").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Gamorreans can speak Gamorrean. They have no written version of the language. In addition, all Gamorreans understand, read, and write (but not speak) Basic.
