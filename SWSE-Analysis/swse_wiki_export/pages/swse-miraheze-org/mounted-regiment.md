# Mounted Regiment

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Regiment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Regiment) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies act as mounted cavalry, specializing in using [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts") in war.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, once per round as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to your mount being attacked, you can make a [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") check; if the result exceeds the mount's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), use your check result against the attack instead of using the mount's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
