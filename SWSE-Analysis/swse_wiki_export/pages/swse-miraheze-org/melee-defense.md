# Melee Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are trained at using your combat ability for defense as well as offense.

**Prerequisite:** [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13

**Effect:** When you use a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), you can take a penalty of up to -5 on your attack roll and add the same number (up to +5) as a dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). This number may not exceed your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). The changes to attack rolls and [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") last until the start of your next turn.

**Normal:** A character without the Melee Defense feat can [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") while using the attack action to take a -5 penalty on his attack roll and gain a +2 dodge bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
