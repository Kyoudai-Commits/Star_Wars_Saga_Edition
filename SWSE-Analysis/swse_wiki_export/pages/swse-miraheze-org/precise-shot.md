# Precise Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at timing your [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") so that you don't hit your ally by mistake.

**Prerequisite:** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** You can shoot or throw a ranged weapon at an opponent engaged in melee combat with one or more of your allies without taking the standard -5 penalty for [Attacking into a Melee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacking_into_a_Melee "Attacking into a Melee").

**Special:** If another [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") fires into a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), it takes a -5 penalty on its attack roll, unless the [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") has the Precise Shot feat.
