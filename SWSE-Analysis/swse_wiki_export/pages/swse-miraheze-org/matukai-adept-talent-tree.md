# Matukai Adept Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Matukai_Adept_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Matukai_Adept_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

All Matukai strive to achieve harmony between the physical and the spiritual aspects of their lives. As a result, they find ways to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in improve their bodies. Only Force-users that are part of [The Matukai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Matukai "The Matukai") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Body Control

You can add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier instead of your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). Additionally, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to become immune to [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"), [Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"), and [Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disease "Disease") until the end of the encounter.

### Physical Surge

Whenever you roll an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check at the start of combat, you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") immediately, regardless of whether or not you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised").

### Soft to Solid

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") when you are damaged by an attack, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the rigidity of your skin, gaining [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10 until the end of your next turn.

### Wan-Shen Defense

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can use your [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") to parry your opponents' attacks, gaining a +1 deflection bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") until the start of your next turn. You must have your [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") in hand to use this Talent, and you do not gain the deflection bonus if you are [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") or otherwise unaware of the incoming attack. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, the deflection bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +3).

### Wan-Shen Kata

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

You treat the [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") as a Medium weapon instead of a Large weapon. You can use the [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") and [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") feats with a [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen"), substituting your attack bonus with the [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") for your [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check. You must have your [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") in hand to use this Talent. In addition, when you use the [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") as a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"), you reduce all attack penalties for attacking with both ends of the weapon by 1.

### Wan-Shen Mastery

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, **Wan-Shen Kata**, [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you make two attacks with your [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen"), each one against a different target within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). You must have your [Wan-Shen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wan-Shen "Wan-Shen") in hand to use this Talent.
