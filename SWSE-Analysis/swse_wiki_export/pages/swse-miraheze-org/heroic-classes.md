# Heroic Classes

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Five basic Classes, known as Heroic Classes, are available in the *Star Wars Roleplaying Game*. Characters with levels in Heroic Classes are called Heroic Characters, or Heroes. Unlike [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") supporting characters, Heroic Characters have many special abilities that make them extraordinary.

At 1st level, you must choose a Heroic Class for your character. The five Heroic Classes are:

- **[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi")** combine physical training with mastery of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Jedi concentrate on battle prowess, defense, and [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") training. Additionally, they are ambassadors of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), protecting the Republic from all dangers. Few are strong enough in The Force, and have the devotion to walk the Jedi's path, but those few are awarded with a powerful ally. They walk in a larger world than those who neither feel nor heed [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").
- **[Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles")** use their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and natural [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") to make their way in the galaxy. From true royalty to elected officials, military commanders to crime lords, traders, merchants, ambassadors, Holovid stars, and influential corporate magnates, character types who appear in the Noble class are varied and numerous. Some bring honor to the name. Others are sly, treacherous, and dishonorable to the core. With a winning smile, golden tongue, a powerful message, or a knack for making compromises, the Noble commands respect, makes friends, and inevitably influences people.
- **[Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels")** are rogues- good, bad, and neutral- who either live outside the law or fight against it in order to get the upper hand. They can come from any world or region of the galaxy. Most use their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") to accomplish tasks, and many rely on [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") as a fallback when all else fails. The Scoundrel gets by with bravado, cunning, duplicity, and trickery. They live by their wits, lying, cheating, stealing, and even fighting when the need arises.
- **[Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts")** are natural explorers and adventurers, full of curiosity and trained to handle the out-of-the-way locations where they often operate. Scouts tend to be independent, signing on when the credits are good and their skills are best utilized and tested. Scouts understand the lay of the land and the orbit of the stars. They know how to recognize danger and locate the basic necessities for survival. The Scout seeks knowledge, tries to solve mysteries, and to find a path through the wild regions, often becoming a decent pilot along the way, and usually learns how to protect themselves from whatever hides over the next hill or beyond the most distant nebula.
- **[Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers")** combine discipline with martial skills to become the best pure warriors in the galaxy. Soldiers can be stalwart defenders of those in need, cruel marauders, or brave adventurers. They can be hired guns, noble champions, or cold-hearted killers. They fight for glory, for honor, to right wrongs, to gain power, to acquire wealth, or simply for the thrill of battle.

## Multiclassing

When a single-class character gains a level, he or she may choose to increase the level of his or her current class or pick up a new Heroic Class at 1st level. The GM may restrict the choices available according to how he or she handles classes, skills, experience, and training.

For instance, the character may need to find a teacher to instruct them in the way of the new class. Additionally the GM may require the player to declare what class his or her character is "working on" before he or she makes the jump to the next level, so the character has time to practice new skills.

In this way, gaining the new Class is the result of previous effort rather than a sudden development.

A [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), for example, might become a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/[Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"). Adding the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Class would give them proficiency in more weapons, a better [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and so on, but it would also mean that they don't gain new [Noble Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Talents "Noble Talents") and thus is not as powerful a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") as they otherwise would have been.

Rules for creating and advancing Multiclass characters can be found below.

### Class and Level Features

As a general rules, the abilities of a Multiclass character are the sum of the abilities of each of the character's Classes.

- **Class Level:** "Character Level" is a character's total number of levels. It derives from overall XP earned and is used to determine when [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") boosts are gained as per [Level Benefits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Level_Benefits "Level Benefits"). "Class level" is the character's level in a particular Class, as per the individual Class tables located on their respective pages. For a single-class hero, a Character Level and Class Level are the same.
- **Base Attack Bonus:** Add the [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") of each class to get the character's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). For instance, a 6th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/2nd-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") has a [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") of +6 (+4 for [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), +2 for [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier")).
- **Defenses:** A character who takes their first level of a new Heroic Class gains a Class bonus to one or more of their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"); however, this Class bonus does not stack with other Class bonuses.
- **Skills:** When you select a new class, you do not gain any new [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"). However, your list of [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills") expands to include those of the new Heroic Class. If you take the [Skill Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training "Skill Training") feat, you may choose your new [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") from the [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills") of any Class in which you have levels. For example, Arani is a Multiclass [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/[Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") who takes the [Skill Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training "Skill Training") feat. Her new [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") may be chosen from either the Noble's or Soldier's list of [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills").
- **Starting Feats:** When you select a new Heroic Class, you do not gain all of its starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). Select one [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") from the list of starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). For example a 1st-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") decides to take a level of [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") and gains one [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") of their choice from the Soldier's list of starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"); they select [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)"). A Multiclass character includes any remaining Bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") of their new Heroic Class to that class's list of Bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats").
- **Talents:** If a character gains a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") as a consequence of gaining a level, they must select a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") associated with the Heroic Class in which they gained the level. For example, Arani is a 2nd-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") who decides to take a level in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") Class, which grants her a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). She must select her new [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from [Soldier Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talents "Soldier Talents") (or from [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees"), if she has the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat), since it was a [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") level that granted her the [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent").
- **Bonus Feats:** A Multiclass character that gains a Class Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must select it from the Bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") available to that particular Heroic Class. For example, Arani is a 2nd-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/1st-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") who decides to take a second level in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class. Doing so grants her a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), which she must select from the [Soldier Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Bonus_Feats "Soldier Bonus Feats") list.

### How Multiclassing Works

Arani, a 4th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), decides she wants to expand her repertoire by learning some soldiering. When Arani reaches 10,000 XP, she becomes a 5th-level character. Instead of becoming a 5th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), however, she becomes a 4th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/1st-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"). (How exactly she picked up this new area of focus isn't critical to the campaign, though the player and GM are encouraged to create an in-game reason and opportunity for the hero to do so.)

Now, instead of gaining the benefits of a new level of [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), she gains the benefits of becoming a 1st-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"). She gains a Soldier's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") (1d10 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier), a 1st-level Soldier's +1 [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"), a Soldier's +2 Class bonus to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and a [Soldier Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talent "Soldier Talent"). Because she gained a level, all of her [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), and [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")) increase by 1.

The benefits described above are added to the scores Arani already had as a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"). She doesn't gain any of the benefits a 5th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") gains. On achieving 15,000 XP, Arani becomes a 6th-level hero. She decides she'd like to continue along the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") path, so she increases her [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") level instead of her [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") level.

Again she gains the Soldier's benefits for attaining a new level rather than a Noble's. At this point, Arani is a 4th-level [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble")/2nd-level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"). Her combat skill is a little better than a 4th-level Noble's would be because she has learned something about fighting during her time as a [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier").

Her [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") is +5 (+3 from her [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") class and +2 from her [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class). Her [Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), and [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will") Defenses each increase by 1.

At each new level, Arani decides whether to increase her [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") level or her [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") level. Of course, if she wants to have even more diverse abilities, she could acquire a third Class, such as [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"). At some point, she may also qualify for a Prestige Class. In general, a character can Multiclass as many times as there are Heroic Classes available.

## Prestige Classes

Prestige Classes offer abilities and powers otherwise inaccessible to heroes and GM characters, focusing them in specific directions. A character with a Prestige Class is generally more specialized, yet perhaps slightly more powerful, than a character without one. A character can't begin as a member of a Prestige Class. Abilities granted by Prestige Classes are acquired only by meeting the requirements specific to each example, which almost always demand- in effect- that a character be a least mid-level. Additionally, there may be non-rules-related requirements that must be met in-game, such as group membership fees, special training exercises, or quests.

Prestige Classes are purely optional, and always under the purview of the Gamemaster. Even though a few examples can be found below, they are idiosyncratic to each campaign, and thus many Gamemasters may choose to not allow them or only use them for GM characters. Some Prestige Classes (such as [Crime Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord "Crime Lord"), [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice"), and [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord")) are more appropriate for GM characters, and the Gamemaster may declare these classes off-limits for heroes. For this reason, a player should consult with their Gamemaster before angling her character toward a particular Prestige Class.

All Prestige Classes have certain requirements that a character must fulfill prior to taking the first level in that class. Among the most common requirements are a minimum Character Level, a minimum [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"), being [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in certain [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), or having certain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). These requirements make it easier for certain types of characters to become members of a given Prestige Class. For example, a [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") could potentially take their first level of [Bounty Hunter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunter "Bounty Hunter") as an 8th-level character.
