# Logic Upgrade: Tactician

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Tactician](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Tactician) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to use your systems and equipment to arm yourself.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to grant a single ally a +5 bonus to their next attack roll against an opponent.

**Normal:** Successfully aiding an attack roll grants a +2 bonus to the ally's next attack roll against the opponent.
