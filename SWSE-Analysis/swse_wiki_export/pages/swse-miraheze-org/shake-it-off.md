# Shake It Off

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shake_It_Off](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shake_It_Off) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You have learned to shake off debilitating conditions.

**Prerequisites:** [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** You can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") instead of three to move +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Normal:** It takes three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to move +1 step up along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
