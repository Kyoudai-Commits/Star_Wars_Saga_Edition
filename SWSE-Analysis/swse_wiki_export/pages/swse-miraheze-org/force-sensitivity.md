# Force Sensitivity

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are Force-sensitive, allowing you to call on [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and learn to draw on its powers.

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

**Effect:** You can make [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks, and [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") is considered a [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill") for you. In addition, whenever you gain a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), you have the option of selecting a [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") instead. You must meet the prerequisites of the [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") to select it.

**Normal:** You can't make [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks or select [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents") unless you have the Force Sensitivity feat.
