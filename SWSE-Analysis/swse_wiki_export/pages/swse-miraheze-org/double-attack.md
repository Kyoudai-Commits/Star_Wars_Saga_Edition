# Double Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can make an additional attack during a round of combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6

**Effect:** Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you are proficient. When you use the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you may make one additional attack when wielding such a weapon. However, you take a -5 penalty on all attack rolls until your next turn because you're trading precision for speed.

**Normal:** Making a single attack is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

**Special:** You may select this Feat multiple times. Each time you select this Feat, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"). If you take this feat with [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"), it also applies with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").

## Dual Weapon Mastery vs Double Attack

**Q:** I'm curious as to the perceived balance factor of Dual Weapon Mastery versus Double Attack. [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I") is available sooner, and you can offset its penalties by taking more [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). Double Attack works for only one type of weapon, and you can reduce its penalties only with Prestige Class [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). Since both grant an extra attack, why would you want Double Attack?

**A:** The balancing factor is that Dual Weapon Mastery works only with [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") weapons (that is, weapons that do less damage), but Double Attack/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") works with [Two-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Two-Handed "Two-Handed") weapons (but only one type of them). For example:

- A Dual Weapon Mastery specialist might use two [Heavy Blaster Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Pistols "Heavy Blaster Pistols"), which deal 3d8 points of damage per weapon, or a potential of 6d8 points from both.
- A Double Attack specialist might use a [Heavy Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Rifle "Heavy Blaster Rifle"), which deals 3d10 points of damage but has a much better [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). In addition, the weapon is capable of [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), so it can deal up to 5d10 points of damage with [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire").

This also means that Double Attack can be used with much larger weapons, such as those on a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), while a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") would have no particular reason to use Dual Weapon Mastery because such weapons can't be operated with one hand.

Finally, don't forget that someone can use both Dual Weapon Mastery and Double Attack/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack"). So even if Dual Weapon Mastery appears more attractive at first, you might want to take Double Attack/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") later.

## Multiple Attacks with Multiple Targets

**Q:** Can you use Double Attack or [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") to attack the same target multiple times in the same round, or do you have to attack different targets?

**A:** The extra attacks from Double Attack, [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack"), and fighting with multiple weapons can be directed at the same target or at different targets, in any combination and any order that you choose.

## Double and Rapid Attacks

**Q:** It is possible to use Double Attack with [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike")?

**A:** Yes. [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), and [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") all can be used when making multiple attacks, regardless of their source (Double Attack, [Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack"), or fighting with multiple weapons).
