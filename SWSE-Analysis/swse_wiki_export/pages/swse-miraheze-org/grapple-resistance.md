# Grapple Resistance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple_Resistance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple_Resistance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You make it difficult for enemies to [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") you, or attack objects in your possession.

**Effect:** You get a +5 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when an enemy makes a [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack against you, and you get a +5 bonus to all opposed [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks. All objects that you are holding or carrying gain a +5 bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when attacked (see [Attack an Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_an_Object "Attack an Object")).
