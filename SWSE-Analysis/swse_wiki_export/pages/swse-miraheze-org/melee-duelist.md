# Melee Duelist

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Throughout the history of the galaxy, dueling has seen many degrees of acceptability, militarily, legally, and socially. Some cultures or subcultures regard dueling as an honorable and civilized method of settling disputes, but others see dueling as barbaric. During [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), melee combat has resurged, forcing many to study dueling as a means to survive on the battlefield. In addition, dueling is still regarded as a sport on many planets. In fact, dueling is allowed on more civilized worlds than gladiatorial combat, given the higher cultural status dueling enjoys.

The Melee Duelist specializes in fighting a single opponent. The Melee Duelist selects a primary weapon of choice, studying and practicing maneuvers, feints, and tricks to perfection. Competitive duelists train with several types of weapons because they may not know ahead of time which types of weapons will be used in a duel. Battlefield duelists expand their abilities to take on small groups, because they cannot depend on the luxury of fighting a single opponent in the middle of a skirmish. Jedi can also become Melee Duelists to improve their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat, and expand their knowledge beyond the standard Jedi [Lightsaber Forms](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree"). Sith of all ranks and types can specialize in Melee Duelist, as many of them do not have direct access to learning [Lightsaber Forms](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree").

**Examples of Melee Duelists in Star Wars:** Ice, Marl, Qesya Vth'naar, Tabor.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Melee Duelist, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- Minimum Level: 7th
- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** +7
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Melee Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense "Melee Defense"), [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Melee Weapon)

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Melee Duelists gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Melee Duelists gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Melee Duelists gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Melee Duelist gains an additional [Melee Duelist Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist_Talent "Melee Duelist Talent"). A Melee Duelist may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Melee Duelist Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist_Talent_Trees "Melee Duelist Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Master of Movement

You know how to take advantage of whatever terrain you are fighting in. A number of times per encounter equal to half your Melee Duelist level, you can either ignore the movement penalty for moving through [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") or over low objects on a single [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), or reroll a single [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") or [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check, keeping the better of the two results.
