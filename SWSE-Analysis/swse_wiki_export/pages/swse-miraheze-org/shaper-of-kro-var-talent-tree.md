# Shaper of Kro Var Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaper_of_Kro_Var_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaper_of_Kro_Var_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[The Shapers of Kro Var](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Shapers_of_Kro_Var "The Shapers of Kro Var") are deeply in touch with the four classic elements of air, earth, fire, and water. They use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manipulate these elements at a fundamental level, creating impressive effects, such as ripping stone, walls of solid wind, and gouts of flame. The Talents listed here are a small fraction of the "spells" that these primitive Force-users are capable of using. Only Force-users that are part of [The Shapers of Kro Var](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Shapers_of_Kro_Var "The Shapers of Kro Var") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Combustive Force

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Combustion in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[Combustion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combustion "Combustion")."*** |

*Prerequisite:* [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to add 1d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage to any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that causes damage to a single target. A target that takes [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage also catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire").

### Earth Buckle

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to create a 3x3 square area of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") centered on you. You ignore any penalties for moving on [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") that you create using this Talent.

### Fluidity

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check modifier in place of your [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check modifier when making [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") checks. If you are entitled to an [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). Additionally, when you attempt a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") or attempt to break free of a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to be treated as if you were one size category larger.

### Thunderclap

*Prerequisites:* [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush"), [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

When you use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that deals damage, you can use the [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") feat against that target as though you had made a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack").

### Wind Vortex

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to surround yourself with whirling winds. You gain [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from all other targets and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons"). This effect lasts until the end of the encounter.
