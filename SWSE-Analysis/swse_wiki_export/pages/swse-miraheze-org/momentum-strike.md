# Momentum Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Momentum_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Momentum_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

You know how to put the full weight of your momentum into attacks while riding a mount or a [Speeder Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bike "Speeder Bike").

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") or [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

**Effect:** When riding a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") as a mount or on a [Speeder Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bike "Speeder Bike") (as a passenger or a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")), you add +1 die of damage to any [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") if your [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") has already moved at least its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") this turn.
