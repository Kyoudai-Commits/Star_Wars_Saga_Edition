# Sith Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") believe in order through tyranny. In ancient days, Sith Warriors battled Jedi Knights for galactic supremacy until, finally, the few remaining Sith were driven into hiding. Since then, [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") have conspired to annihilate [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and everything they stand for.

## Core Talents

### Dark Healing

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to heal wounds by drawing life energy from another creature within 6 squares of you. Using this ability is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must succeed on a ranged attack roll. If the attack equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you deal 1d6 points of damage per Class Level to the target, and you heal an equal amount of damage. If the attack fails, there is no effect.

### Dark Scourge

You have dedicated your life to wiping out [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), and your hatred of them knows no bounds. Against Jedi characters (that is, characters belonging to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi")), you gain a +1 [Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side") bonus on attack rolls.

### Dark Side Adept

[Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that are strongly tied to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") flow through you more easily. You can reroll any [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made when activating [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, but you must keep the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Dark Side Master

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Adept**

[Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that are strongly tied to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") flow through you more easily. You can reroll any [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made when activating [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, and can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to keep the better of the two results.

### Force Deception

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") modifier instead of your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check modifier when making [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") checks, as you use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to cloak your vile treachery. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill. If you are entitled to a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Improved Dark Healing

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Healing**

Your **Dark Healing** Talent improves. The range of this ability increases to 12 squares, and even if that attack fails, the target takes half damage, while you heal an equal amount of damage.

### Wicked Strike

*Prerequisites:* [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)"), **[Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)")**

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to move the target -2 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

## Additional Talents

### Affliction

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") carries the taint of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") more so than even other Dark Side users. When you damage a single opponent with one of your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), that target also takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage at the beginning of its next turn, before taking any Actions.

### Dark Healing Field

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Improved Dark Healing**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to heal wounds by drawing life energy from up to three targeted creatures within 12 squares of you. Once per encounter, you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If the check result equals or exceeds a target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage per every [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") and [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord") Class Level you possess. You heal half the total damage dealt (cumulative from all targets). If the attack fails, the target takes half damage, and you heal that amount.

### Drain Force

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Affliction**

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") when you damage a Force-sensitive opponent, the dark taint of your power allows you to sap some of the opponent's strength, and convert it to personal power, regaining one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). Additionally, the target loses one [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Sith Alchemy

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Master**

Your knowledge of Sith sorcery allows you to imbue talismans and other objects with the power of the [Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side").

- *Create Sith Talisman:* You can spend one [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a portable object with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), creating a Sith Talisman that provides offensive strength to a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack. Creating the talisman takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").
  - While you wear or carry a Sith Talisman on your person, add 1d6 to damage rolls with your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). You increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by one when you first put on or carry a Sith Talisman.
  - You can only have one Sith Talisman active at any given time, and if it is destroyed, you cannot create another one for 24 hours.
- *Create Sith Weapon:* You can alchemically treat a properly prepared weapon to become a Sith Weapon. You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue the weapon with the properties of the [Sith Alchemical Weapon Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemical_Weapon_Template "Sith Alchemical Weapon Template") (this process takes one hour to complete).

### Stolen Form

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisites:* Any [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique"), [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

You have learned how to use a Jedi fighting technique to defeat Jedi in combat. Choose one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree"); you gain the benefits of that [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") and are considered to have it [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. You must meet all the prerequisites as normal for the chosen Talent, in addition to the prerequisites of this Talent. You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, choose a different Talent from the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree").
