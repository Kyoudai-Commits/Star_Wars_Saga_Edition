# Attack Combo (Fire and Strike)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Fire_and_Strike)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Fire_and_Strike)) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can make deadly ranged and melee attacks in rapid succession.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +9, [Attack Combo (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Melee) "Attack Combo (Melee)"), [Attack Combo (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Ranged) "Attack Combo (Ranged)")

**Effect:** If you hit a single target with two consecutive [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"), [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks"), and/or [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") during the same turn, any additional [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"), [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks"), or [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") you make until the end of your next turn (including [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") and attacks made as [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions")) deal +1 die of damage on a hit. This extra damage stacks with extra damage granted by other [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") or [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").
