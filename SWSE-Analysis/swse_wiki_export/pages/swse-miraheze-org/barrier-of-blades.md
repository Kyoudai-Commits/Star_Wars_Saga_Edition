# Barrier of Blades

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Barrier_of_Blades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Barrier_of_Blades) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You whip your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") around you, creating a barrier through which blaster fire rarely penetrates.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Until the start of your next turn, you can use the results of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against incoming non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") ranged attacks.

**Lightsaber Form ([Shien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien "Shien")):** If you have the **[Shien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien "Shien")** Talent, you can redirect (as per the [Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot") Talent) one blaster bolt that misses you while this Form Power is in effect.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to have this Form Power also apply to attacks from ranged weapons set to [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). If such an attack hits you while this Form Power is in effect, you take half damage; if it misses you, you take no damage.
