# Skill Focus

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

One of your [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") is particularly well honed.

**Effect:** Choose one [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") in which you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained"). You gain a +5 competence bonus on all [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill").

**Special:** This feat may be selected multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill").
