# Prescience

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prescience](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prescience) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") grants you a flash of insight in dealing with your enemies.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** One target within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +1 insight bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against attacks or effects originating from the target, as well as a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls made against the target. |
| 20 | Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +2 insight bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against attacks or effects originating from the target, as well as a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls made against the target. |
| 25 | Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +3 insight bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against attacks or effects originating from the target, as well as a +3 insight bonus on attack rolls made against the target. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to affect one additional target in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").
