# Opportunistic Trickery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Trickery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Trickery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When your opponent lets down its guard, you know how to set it off balance for several moments.

**Prerequisites:** **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")**, [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes")

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you, once per turn you can sacrifice that attack to reduce the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 2 for the following round.
