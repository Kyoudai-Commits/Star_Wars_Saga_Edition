# Pitiless Warrior

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pitiless_Warrior](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pitiless_Warrior) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Whenever you fell an opponent, the victory stokes your bloodlust and prepares you for more carnage.

**Prerequisite:** [Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan "Trandoshan")

**Effect:** Whenever you reduce a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + one-half your Character Level. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.
