# Hyperblazer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperblazer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperblazer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You are familiar with blazing new routes and navigating the tangled web of hyperspace in [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions").

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

**Effect:** When traversing the Hyperspace Tangle, you reduce by half the calculation time and penalties on [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks to [Astrogate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogate "Astrogate") (see [Hyperspace Navigation Challenges](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperspace_Navigation_Challenges "Hyperspace Navigation Challenges")). You also reduce hyperspace mapping time by half when mapping new hyperspace routes.
