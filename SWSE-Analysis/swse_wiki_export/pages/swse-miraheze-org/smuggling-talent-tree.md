# Smuggling Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Smuggling_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Smuggling_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Smuggling is part skill, part talent, and part luck. The most notorious smugglers in the galaxy get along by making use of their quick wits and savvy.

## Core Talents

### Art of Concealment

Some smugglers are adept at hiding contraband and weapons, even on their person. When making a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to [Conceal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conceal "Conceal") an item, you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") even under pressure. Additionally, you can [Conceal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conceal "Conceal") an item as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Fast Talker

*Prerequisite:* **Art of Concealment**

Smugglers must be quick to explain discrepancies in their cover stories. Once per day, you can [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") when attempting to [Deceive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceive "Deceive").

### Hidden Weapons

*Prerequisite:* **Art of Concealment**

If you draw a [Concealed Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealed_Weapon "Concealed Weapon") and, before the end of the same round, make an attack against a target that failed to notice the item, the target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you. You can draw a concealed item or a stowed item as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Additionally, if you have the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat, you can reduce this to a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Illicit Dealings

Smugglers have a knack for locating and negotiating illicit deals. When using [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") to [Haggle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haggle "Haggle") for [Restricted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted "Restricted"), [Military](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military "Military"), or [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") goods you may roll twice, keeping the better of the two results.

### Surprise Strike

Sometimes a smuggler has to fight his way out of a bad situation. If you fail any [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to convey [Deceptive Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Information "Deceptive Information"), you can initiate combat and make a single [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") in the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round") (or with a melee or ranged weapon, if you have the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat); all other combatants are considered [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") even if they are aware of you.
