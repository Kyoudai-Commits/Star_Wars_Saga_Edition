# Bando Gora Captain Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bando_Gora_Captain_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bando_Gora_Captain_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

The cultists of [The Bando Gora](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bando_Gora "The Bando Gora") have a reputation for being merciless, and you are no exception. Only Force-users that are part of [The Bando Gora](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bando_Gora "The Bando Gora") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Bando Gora Surge

Whenever you move up the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any means, you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + your Heroic Level. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") are consumed before regular [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), and unused [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") go away at the end of the encounter. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from multiple sources do not stack.

### Force Fighter

Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to an attack roll, you heal a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") result if the attack hits.

### Resist Enervation

Whenever an effect would move you down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to negate that movement down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Victorious Force Mastery

*Prerequisite:* [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

Whenever an enemy you have damaged in this encounter is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you may automatically return one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").
