# Force Stun

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Stun) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You call upon [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to overload an enemy's senses, potentially stunning them.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 6 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). For every 5 points by which you exceed the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target moves an additional -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5.

**Special:** When you use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to move the target an additional -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
