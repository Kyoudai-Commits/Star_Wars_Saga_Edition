# Dual Weapon Mastery II

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are a master at fighting with two weapons and double weapons.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 15, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I")

**Effect:** When you attack with two weapons, or with both ends of a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon") as part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you take a -2 penalty (instead of a -10 penalty) on all attack rolls until the start of your next turn. You only gain this reduced penalty if you are wielding a weapon with which you are proficient. [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") cannot be used in one hand, so this feat has no effect on them.

**Normal:** If you use a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action to make more than one attack on your turn, you take a -10 penalty on all attack rolls for the round.
