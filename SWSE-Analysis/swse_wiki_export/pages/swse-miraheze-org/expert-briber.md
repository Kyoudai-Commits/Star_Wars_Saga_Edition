# Expert Briber

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Briber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Briber) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are adept at greasing the palms of contacts to minimize the amount of the bribe normally required.

**Prerequisite:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

**Effect:** When using the [Haggle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haggle "Haggle") application of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill, you reduce the DC to reduce the price of the item haggled over by 10.
