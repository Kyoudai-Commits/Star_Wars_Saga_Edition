# Opportunist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Profiteers, gunrunners, and privateers can use the chaos of war to make a tidy profit, and you are no exception. Your talents allow you to make the best of a bad situation, keep your pockets filled with credits, and keep you long enough to spend them.

## Core Talents

### Advantageous Opening

When an enemy or ally in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") rolls a Natural 1 on an attack roll, you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a single target within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

### Retribution

When a target moves one of your allies in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any means, you gain a +2 insight bonus to your attack rolls against that target until the end of your next turn.

### Slip By

When you damage a target, until the beginning of your next turn, you can move through that target's space. Moving through the target's space might still provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") as normal, and you must end your movement in a legal space.

### Thrive on Chaos

*Prerequisite:* **Advantageous Opening**

When an enemy or ally within 20 squares of you is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + one-half your Character Level. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.

### Vindication

*Prerequisite:* **Retribution**

When an enemy you have damaged is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), your next attack made before the end of the encounter deals +1 die of damage.
