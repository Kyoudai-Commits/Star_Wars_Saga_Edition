# Mobile Scout Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobile_Scout_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobile_Scout_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Always mobile, [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts") have developed ways to ride and drive over all types of terrain.

## Core Talents

### Battle Mount

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride"), **Expert Rider**, **Terrain Guidance**

You know how to fight from a living mount. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Covered Attack:* Once per encounter, when you use the [Use Mount as Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Mount_as_Cover "Use Mount as Cover") (but not [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover")) aspect of the [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") skill, you can make an attack as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), but you must have a free hand to do so.
- *Reduce Profile:* You gain additional [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from your mount. When you succeed in the [Use Mount as Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Mount_as_Cover "Use Mount as Cover") application of the [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") skill, you gain [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover"). If you fail by less than 10, you still gain the benefit of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). You gain no benefit if you fail by 10 or more.
- *Swift Attack Mount:* Once per encounter, the mount you are riding can make an attack as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Expert Rider

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

You can reroll any [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

### Mechanized Rider

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") and [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

You use your riding experience to improve your skills on a [Speeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder "Speeder") or a [Swoop Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swoop_Bike "Swoop Bike"). When riding a [Speeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder "Speeder") or a [Swoop Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swoop_Bike "Swoop Bike") (or another, similar [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle")), you can use the following applications of the [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") skill: [Fast Mount or Dismount](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fast_Mount_or_Dismount "Fast Mount or Dismount"), [Soft Fall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soft_Fall "Soft Fall"), [Stay in Saddle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stay_in_Saddle "Stay in Saddle"), and [Use Mount as Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Mount_as_Cover "Use Mount as Cover").

### Terrain Guidance

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

You effectively guide your mount through rough terrain. When in control of your mount, you can make a DC 20 [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to negate the effect of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") on your mount's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").
