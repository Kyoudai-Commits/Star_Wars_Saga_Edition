# Bothan SpyNet Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan_SpyNet_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan_SpyNet_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are trained to work within the famed Bothan SpyNet. [The Bothan SpyNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bothan_SpyNet "The Bothan SpyNet") is a secret collection of organic and electronic resources and personnel spread throughout the galaxy, accessed by computer or in person.

## Core Talents

### SpyNet Agent

*Prerequisite:* [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan"), or two Talents from the [Infiltration Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltration_Talent_Tree "Infiltration Talent Tree")

You can use your [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check modifier instead of your [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check modifier when making [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") checks. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") skill for the purpose of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") reroll, you can reroll your [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Bothan Resources

*Prerequisite:* **SpyNet Agent**

Your status within the SpyNet gives you access to additional resources, and you know the best sources for [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare") or [Restricted Items](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted_Items "Restricted Items"). With a successful DC 20 [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check, you can purchase standard [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), weapons, and transport services at 50% of the going rate, or [Rare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rare "Rare") and Restricted [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), weapons, and transport services at 75% of the going rate.

### Knowledge is Life

*Prerequisite:* **SpyNet Agent**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and make a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check against a DC equal to 15 + the target's CL. If the check is successful, for the remainder of the encounter you gain a +2 morale bonus to the [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") of your choice against that target.

### Knowledge is Power

*Prerequisite:* **SpyNet Agent**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and make a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check against a DC equal to 15 + the target's CL. If the check is successful, for the remainder of the encounter you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") against that target on a natural roll of 19 or 20.

If you have another ability that increases your weapon's critical range against that target (such as the Elite Trooper's **[Extended Critical Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extended_Critical_Range "Extended Critical Range")** Talent, or the Jedi Knight's **[Vaapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vaapad "Vaapad")** Talent), you increase this range by 1 (for example, from 19-20 to 18-20). However, anything other than a Natural 20 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll anything other than a Natural 20 and still miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").

### Knowledge is Strength

*Prerequisite:* **SpyNet Agent**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and make a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check against a DC equal to 15 + the target's CL. If the check is successful, for the remainder of the encounter you gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls against that target.

### Six Questions

*Prerequisite:* **SpyNet Agent**

You have mastered the basic [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") philosophy of **Six Questions** to glean more information from contacts through fewer questions. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and make a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check against a DC equal to 15 + the target's CL. If the check is successful, you learn a target's Character Level, [Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes "Classes"), [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"), and the target's available [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") and [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points").
