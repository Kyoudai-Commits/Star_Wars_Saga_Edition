# Kinetic Combat

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinetic_Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinetic_Combat) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manipulate your chosen weapon, allowing it to operate independent of your grasp.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") melee weapon you currently have drawn.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If you succeed on a DC 20 check, you can telekinetically move a [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") melee weapon you hold up to 12 squares away, and make an attack with it. The weapon is considered to be held by you, even if it is hovering nearby and not in your hands. The attack uses your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") modified by your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier as the attack bonus for the attack roll. A successful attack deals normal melee damage, adding one-half your Heroic Level to damage as normal, but substituting your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier in place of your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier as the bonus toward damage.

[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that would normally enhance a weapon's use do not do so while you are using *Kinetic Combat* to wield the weapon (for example, you cannot use [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") or [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack") while wielding the weapon). The weapon [Threatens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatens "Threatens") all squares adjacent to it, and it can be used to make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") if a target provokes one from the weapon. Any [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") made by the weapon count as though they were made by you.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with a weapon wielded using *Kinetic Combat*.

You can maintain *Kinetic Combat* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Kinetic Combat* Force Power is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") each round you can direct the weapon to move up to 6 squares, and make a single attack against a target adjacent to the weapon, though the weapon must remain within 12 squares of you or the Force Power's effect ends. If you take damage while maintaining *Kinetic Combat*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Kinetic Combat*.
