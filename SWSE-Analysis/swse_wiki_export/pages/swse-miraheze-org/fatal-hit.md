# Fatal Hit

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fatal_Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fatal_Hit) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Enemies you take down don't get up again.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When your attack drops a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you may choose to automatically kill that enemy even if the damage does not exceed the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"). In addition, you can perform the [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

**Normal:** A creature that drops below 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") becomes [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious") if the damage dealt does not exceed its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"). Performing a [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") is a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").
