# Pistoleer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistoleer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistoleer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are skilled in the use of [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") and gain certain benefits depending on which weapon you use.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)")

**Effect:** When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") with which you are proficient, you gain one of the following benefits, depending on the kind of [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") you use.

- **[Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol"):** You treat the [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol") as an [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon.
- **[Heavy Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Pistol "Heavy Blaster Pistol"):** You do not treat the [Heavy Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Pistol "Heavy Blaster Pistol") as an [Inaccurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inaccurate "Inaccurate") weapon.
- **[Hold-Out Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hold-Out_Blaster_Pistol "Hold-Out Blaster Pistol"):** If you fire a [Hold-Out Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hold-Out_Blaster_Pistol "Hold-Out Blaster Pistol") at a target that has not yet acted in combat, you gain a +2 bonus to your attack rolls with that weapon against that target. This bonus applies to all such attacks until the target attacks.
