# 4th-Degree Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[4th-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droids "4th-Degree Droids") are Battle Droids, Destroyer Droids, Security Droids, War Droids, Probe Droids, and Assassin Droids. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Combat Repairs

Once per day, as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can use the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill to [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") yourself, as opposed to the normal 1 hour.

### Droid Smash

You can use your mechanical strength when wielding a melee weapon. You add 2 x your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus to melee damage rolls when wielding a weapon in one hand.

### Targeting Package

You can take two consecutive [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to activate special targeting software. When you do so, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls on your next attack if your target is at Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") (or within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") for [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks")) and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You lose the benefit of this Talent if you lose [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target or if you take any other action before making your attack. The effect of this Talent stacks with that of the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") feat.

## Additional Talents

### Just a Scratch

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* Equipped with [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor") or [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor"), Proficient with Armor equipped

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can reduce the damage from a single attack that targets your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by an amount equal to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").

### Target Acquisition

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), designate an enemy target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") as an acquired target. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls for all attacks against that target until the end of the encounter, as long as the target remains within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

### Targeted Lock

| --- |
| ***This section was titled Target Lock in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[Target Lock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Target_Lock "Target Lock")."*** |

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* **Target Acquisition**

You lock onto the target designated by the **Target Acquisition** Talent. If the target leaves your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), you automatically reacquire the **Target Lock** as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") if the target comes back within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You also gain a +5 bonus to your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill when opposing the target's [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks.

### Weapons Power Surge

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* Equipped with a Weapon [Tool Mount](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mount "Tool Mount")

Once per encounter, as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you can increase the damage dealt by one of your weapons by 1 or 2 damage dice in exchange for moving -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") for each die increased. The weapon must be permanently mounted to your chassis (as a [Tool Mount](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mount "Tool Mount")), and it must use your internal power supply. Handheld weapons, such as [Blaster Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifles "Blaster Rifles"), do not qualify for this Talent.
