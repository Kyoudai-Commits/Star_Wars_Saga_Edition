# Dreadful Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dreadful_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dreadful_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You deal horrendous damage while raging.

**Prerequisites:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** While Raging, your Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls increases to +5.

**Normal:** A character with the [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") Species Trait gains a +2 bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls while Raging.
