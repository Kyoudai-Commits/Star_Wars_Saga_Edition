# Imperceptible Liar

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperceptible_Liar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperceptible_Liar) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have worked hard to eliminate your behavioral tells, making it almost impossible to discern when you are lying.

**Prerequisite:** [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).
