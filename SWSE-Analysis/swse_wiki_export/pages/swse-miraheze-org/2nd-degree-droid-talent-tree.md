# 2nd-Degree Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[2nd-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree_Droids "2nd-Degree Droids") are typically Astromech Droids and some Technical Droids. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Adept Assistant

When you successfully [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") character on a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"), or [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, you add +5 to the check result (rather than the normal +2).

### Mechanics Mastery

You can always [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check, even if distractions or hazardous circumstances would normally prevent you from doing so.

### Vehicle Mechanic

Once per day you can spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in successive rounds to make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to restore 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") and move it +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). You also restore 1 [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") to the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") for every point by which you exceed the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") DC.

## Additional Talents

### Burst Transfer

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can double the amount of data transferred with the [Binary](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary "Binary") language in a single round when communicating with other Droids with **Burst Transfer**, and you can [Access Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Access_Information "Access Information") in half the time when making [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks to find general or specific information.

### On-Board System Link

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

While aboard a Starship or Vehicle and plugged into the ship's systems by [Scomp Link](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scomp_Link "Scomp Link"), [Droid Socket](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Socket "Droid Socket"), or basic data port, you can [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power") or [Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields") as two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") instead of three.

### Quick Astrogation

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

Your speedy electronic [Astrogation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogation "Astrogation")-calculation routines allow you to cut calculation time in half. Additionally, when attempting a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check to [Astrogate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogate "Astrogate"), you can make the calculation as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

### Scomp Link Slicer

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* Any two Talents from the [Slicer Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicer_Talent_Tree "Slicer Talent Tree") (**Burst Transfer** can be one of these Talents)

Your inherent speed and advanced skill reduce the danger of slicing computer systems. You must be physically linked to the system you are slicing. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Eradicate*: You can use [Disable or Erase Program](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disable_or_Erase_Program "Disable or Erase Program") on a computer that is Friendly or Helpful to you. With this Talent, Disabling or Erasing a Program takes 5 minutes and requires a DC 15 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check.
- *Lockout*: If you succeed on an opposed [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check when you [Issue Routine Command](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Issue_Routine_Command "Issue Routine Command") to counteract another programmer's actions, you automatically lock the other programmer out of the system. He or she must succeed in an opposed [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check against you to regain access to the system. You resist the attempt as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").
- *Untraceable*: As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you automatically keep a Hostile computer from tracing your location if you fail a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check by 10 or less.
