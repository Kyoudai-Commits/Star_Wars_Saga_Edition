# Follow Through

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follow_Through](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follow_Through) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have learned to take down an enemy with an attack and then use your momentum to dash across the battlefield.

**Effect:** If you deal enough damage to your opponent with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") to reduce it to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you can immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). You can use this ability once per turn.

**Special:** If you have the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat, you can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") before making the extra [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") granted by the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat.
