# Assured Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assured_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assured_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You trade power for accuracy.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, rolling twice and keeping the higher result. However, you take a -5 penalty on your damage roll. |
| 20 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, rolling twice and keeping the higher result. However, you take a -2 penalty on your damage roll. |
| 25 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, rolling twice and keeping the higher result. However, you take a -1 penalty on your damage roll. |
| 30 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, rolling twice and keeping the higher result. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Juyo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juyo "Juyo")):** If you have the **[Juyo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juyo "Juyo")** Talent and miss with both attack rolls, this Form Power remains in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") instead of being spent as normal.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to roll your attack roll three times, keeping the highest result.
