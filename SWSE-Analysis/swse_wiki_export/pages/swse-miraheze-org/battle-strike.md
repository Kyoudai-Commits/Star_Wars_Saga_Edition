# Battle Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to enhance your battle prowess.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on your next attack roll made before the end of your next turn and deal an additional 1d6 points of damage if the attack hits. |
| 20 | Gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on your next attack roll made before the end of your next turn and deal an additional 2d6 points of damage if the attack hits. |
| 25 | Gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on your next attack roll made before the end of your next turn and deal an additional 3d6 points of damage if the attack hits. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal an additional 2d6 points of damage on your next attack.
