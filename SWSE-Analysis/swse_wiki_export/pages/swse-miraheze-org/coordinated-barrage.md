# Coordinated Barrage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Barrage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Barrage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

When you combine fire with an ally, your barrage can have devastating results on the attacker.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, [Coordinated Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack "Coordinated Attack")

**Effect:** When you [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") ally's attack, for every 3 points that the ally's attack roll beats the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the attack deals +1 die of damage. The maximum number of bonus damage dice the attack receives is equal to the number of allies with this feat that aided on the attack roll or +5 dice, whichever is lower.
