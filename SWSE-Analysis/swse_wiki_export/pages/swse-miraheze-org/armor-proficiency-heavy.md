# Armor Proficiency (Heavy)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Heavy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Heavy)) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are proficient with [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor"), and can wear it without impediment.

**Prerequisite:** [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)")

**Effect:** When you wear [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor"), you take no [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") on attack rolls or [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"). Additionally, you benefit from all of the armor's special [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonuses (if any).

**Normal:** A character who wears [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor") with which they are not proficient takes a -10 [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") on attack rolls as well as [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made using the following [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"): [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"), [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), and [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim"). Additionally, the character gains none of the armor's special [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonuses.
