# Cerean

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/0/0b/Cerean.webp/300px-Cerean.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Cerean.webp)

Cereans are a sophisticated and cultured humanoid Species native to [Cerea](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerea "Cerea"), a world on the fringes of known space. Their elongated heads distinguish them from most other humanoid [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). Cereans established contact with the rest of the galaxy shortly before [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic") was transformed into [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), swiftly gaining fame as expert astrogators, cryptographers, and economists. Few patterns or trends escape the notice of a Cerean.

Cerean society is matriarchal, and the Cerean culture's traditional values emphasize living in harmony with nature and minimizing any impact on the environment from technology. The peaceful philosophies of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") appeal to Cereans, and many join the Order.

## Cerean Characteristics

**Personality:** Cereans tend to be calm, rational, and extremely logical.

**Physical Description:** Cerean males average at 2.0 meters tall and 78 kilograms, while a typical Cerean female stands at 1.8 meters tall and 58 kilograms. They have elongated craniums housing binary brains, and have a maturity rate and life span similar to those of [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans").

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Cereans age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 Years | 11-15 Years | 16-35 Years | 36-53 Years | 54-64 Years | 65+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The blissful planet [Cerea](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerea "Cerea").

**Languages:** Cereans speak, read, and write both Cerean and Basic.

**Example Names:** Ki-Adi, So Leet, Sylvn, Ti-Dal, Maj-Odo.

**Adventurers:** Cereans who become adventurers do so in spite of their peaceful traditions. Still, when a cause or situation leads down this path, a Cerean tries to keep these traditions intact, avoiding aggression when possible. The binary brain allows a Cerean to constantly weigh both sides in any disagreement and give two points of view equal consideration. This ability extends even to issues surrounding [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and a Cerean [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") often contemplates [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") and [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") simultaneously.

## Cerean Species Traits

Cereans share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** Cereans have a +2 bonus to both their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). Cereans are bright and intuitive but less coordinated than most other species.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Cereans have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Cerean have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** A Cerean with [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Initiative)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Initiative) "Skill Focus (Initiative)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Intuitive Initiative:** Despite their general lack of coordination, Cereans' reaction speed is superior to that of most other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). A Cerean may choose to reroll any [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Cereans can speak, read, and write both Basic and Cerean.
