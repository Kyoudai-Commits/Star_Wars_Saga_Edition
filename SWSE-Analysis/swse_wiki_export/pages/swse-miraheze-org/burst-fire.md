# Burst Fire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

When using a ranged weapon in [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") mode, you can fire a short burst at a single foe.

**Prerequisite:** Proficient with [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons")

**Effect:** When using a ranged weapon with [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") capability, and that you are proficient with, in [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") mode, you may fire a short burst as a single attack against a single target. You take a -5 penalty on your attack roll, but deal +2 dice of damage. [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") capable of [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") can be used with this feat normally. Additional dice of damage are added before any multiplier from the [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") is applied.

The effects of this Feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Deadeye](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadeye "Deadeye") or [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feats.

**Special:** Firing a burst expends five shots, and can only be done if the weapon has at least five shots remaining. If you do not have a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") score of 13 or higher, increase the penalty on attacks to -10 when using this feat with non-[Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").
