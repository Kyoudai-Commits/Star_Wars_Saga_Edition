# Shard

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/4/40/Shard.webp/300px-Shard.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Shard.webp)

Shards are crystalline beings native to the planet [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)"). Although immobile and silicone-based, Shards are sentient and can communicate with others through computers and other electronic receivers. Some Shards, like [The Iron Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights "The Iron Knights"), have themselves placed inside Droid Bodies that they can manipulate telepathically. Many, if not all, Shards are Force-sensitive.

Shards in Droid Bodies, disconnected from their group-mind, became insatiably curious, and almost intoxicated by the new sensory experiences. Individual Shards began understanding the ideas of limited time, thinking and acting much faster than their counterparts in the group-minds. Some of the Shards remaining on [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)") regarded this as a corruption of their traditional culture, while others welcomed the knowledge gained from Shards who eventually returned to their colonies.

## Shard Characteristics

**Personality:** Shards are patient, spending all of their time in slow, contemplative thought. Political decisions are made by mutual consensus, which sometimes takes centuries to reach.

**Physical Description:** In their natural state, Shards take the form of irregularly faceted cylindrical crystals, thirty to forty centimeters in length. Faint pulses of light- thought signals propagating through their "brains"- can be seen inside their bodies. Shards are immobile in their natural state, but can be installed in specially modified [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). Most Shards that venture out into the galaxy install themselves into a Droid Shell (see below).

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** As inorganic beings, Shards are not affected by the process of [Aging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aging "Aging").

**Homeworld:** The awe-inspiring crystalline world of [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)"), located in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)").

**Language:** Shards are able to telepathically communicate with other Shards without a need for language. Shards that enter Droid Bodies become learned in [Binary](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary "Binary"), and most Shards that interact with non-Shards tend to learn Basic.

**Example Names:** Firkrann, Ilum, Luxum.

**Adventurers:** The vast majority of Shards that leave [Orax](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orax?action=edit&redlink=1 "Orax (page does not exist)") join [The Iron Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights "The Iron Knights") as [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), although Shards of other [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") aren't unheard of.

## Shard Species Traits

Shards share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Shards receive a +2 bonus to their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). Shards have powerful minds, but must rely on somewhat clumsy Droid Bodies. A Shard retains its [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score, even when inside a Droid Shell.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Shards have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Bonus Trained Skill:** Shards gain [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") as a bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), regardless of their [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class").
- **Droid Shell:** Shards must inhabit Droid Shells to travel and function in the galaxy at large. In this form, they gain the following traits:
  - Shards must undergo [Maintenance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Maintenance "Maintenance"), as per a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid").
  - Shards are immune to [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"), [Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Radiation "Radiation"), noncorrosive [Atmosphere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atmosphere "Atmosphere") Hazards, and [Vacuum](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vacuum "Vacuum"). They can regain [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") only through the [Repair Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Droid "Repair Droid") application of the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill. They do not benefit from [Natural Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Healing "Natural Healing"), unless they have the **[Force Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Repair "Force Repair")** Talent.
  - Shards are considered cybernetic creatures for the purposes of [Ion Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Damage "Ion Damage").
  - Shards can make use of [Droid Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Systems "Droid Systems"), subject to some limitations. Shards can install the following, with a Cost Modifier of x1: [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages"), [Droid Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Armor "Droid Armor"), [Communications Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Communications_Systems "Communications Systems"), [Compartment Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Compartment_Space "Compartment Space"), [Sensor Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sensor_Systems "Sensor Systems"), and [Translator Units](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Translator_Units "Translator Units").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Shards can speak, read, and write both Basic and [Binary](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary "Binary"). Shards can communicate with other creatures only when connected to a computer or a [Vocabulator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vocabulator "Vocabulator").
