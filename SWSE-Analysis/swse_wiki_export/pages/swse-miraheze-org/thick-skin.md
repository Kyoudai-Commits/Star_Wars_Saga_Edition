# Thick Skin

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thick_Skin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thick_Skin) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your thick hide protects you from damage and fends off toxins and other hazards.

**Prerequisite:** [Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan "Trandoshan")

**Effect:** You gain a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
