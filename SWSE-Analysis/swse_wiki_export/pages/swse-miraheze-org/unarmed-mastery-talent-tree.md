# Unarmed Mastery Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Mastery_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Mastery_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You have advanced training in one or more martial art form and have turned your body into a lethal weapon. Any character can practice these forms, but through dedications and perseverance you have exceeded the abilities of other beings.

## Core Talents

### Flurry of Blows

When you make multiple [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") as a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you reduce the penalty to your attack roll by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times. Each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty to your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Hardened Strike

If you deal damage with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") to a creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that has [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), you reduce the value of that [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by 1 until the end of the encounter. Cumulative attacks against the same target do not stack.

### Punishing Strike

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can make an immediate [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") (in addition to other effects of a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit")) against a single target within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). You can use this Talent only once per turn and only while wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").
