# Wroshyr Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wroshyr_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wroshyr_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have learnt that in the dangerous jungles of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk"), your mighty [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") must be tempered by wariness so that predators don't get the drop on you.

**Prerequisite:** [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee")

**Effect:** When you first enter your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), you gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 10 + one-half your level. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.
