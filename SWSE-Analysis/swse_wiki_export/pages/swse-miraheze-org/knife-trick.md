# Knife Trick

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knife_Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knife_Trick) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can use a concealed weapon to deadly effect.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), [Lightning Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightning_Draw "Lightning Draw"), [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw")

**Effect:** If you have a [Concealed Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealed_Weapon "Concealed Weapon"), you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") squares as though armed with a melee weapon. When you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you can draw a weapon you have successfully concealed on your person and make a single attack against the target.

If you choose not to draw your [Concealed Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealed_Weapon "Concealed Weapon") and attack with it, you cannot make the [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") unless you would otherwise normally be able to do so.

**Normal:** With the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat, drawing or holstering a weapon is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
