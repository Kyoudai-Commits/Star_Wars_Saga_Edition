# Quarren

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/4/4e/Quarren.webp/250px-Quarren.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Quarren.webp)

The Quarren hail from the distant Outer Rim planet of [Dac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dac "Dac"), sharing the world with the sentient humanoid [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari"). The [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari") live on the surface of the world, while the more isolationist Quarren dwell in oxygen-filled cities in the deep recesses of the oceans. Offworld, Quarren generally stay clear of galactic politics. Instead, they often become involved with shadowy occupations such as pirates, smugglers, and spy networks. Many Quarren blame both the Empire and the Rebels (even more than the [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari")) for their homeworld's devastation during the Galactic Civil War.

## Quarren Characteristics

**Personality:** Practical and conservative, Quarren tend to hate change and distrust anyone who displays overt optimism and idealism.

**Physical Description:** The average Quarren stands 1.8 meters tall and weigh 72 kilograms. They have leathery skin and heads that resemble four-tentacled squids, hence the name "Squid Head" sometimes used by others.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Quarren age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-11 Years | 12-16 Years | 17- 40 Years | 41-57 Years | 58-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The watery planet [Dac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dac "Dac").

**Languages:** Quarren speak, read, and write Quarrenese and Basic. They tend to also learn Mon Calamarian as well.

**Example Names:** Kelmut, Seggor, Tessek, Tsillin, Vekker, Vuhlg.

**Adventurers:** Quarren leave their homeworld to escape their dependency on the [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari"). They tend to seek out the fringes of society, operating as [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") or [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") in criminal organizations.

## Quarren Species Traits

Quarren share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** Quarren receive a +2 bonus to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"), but take -2 penalties to both [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Quarren are extremely resilient and physically tough, though they tend toward deficiencies in wisdom and social graces.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Quarren have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Quarren have a base speed of 6 squares. They also have a swim speed of 4 squares.
- **Breathe Underwater:** As amphibious creatures, Quarren can't drown in water.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Adept traders and negotiators, Quarren with [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Persuasion)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Persuasion) "Skill Focus (Persuasion)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Expert Swimmer:** A Quarren may choose to reroll any [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. In addition, a Quarren may choose to [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks, even when distracted or threatened.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Quarren ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Quarren can speak, read, and write both Basic and Quarrenese.
