# Crucitorn

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crucitorn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crucitorn) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You ignore the debilitating effects of physical pain, and focus despite great physical trauma.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (to an attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"))

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against any damage you take from the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). |
| 20 | You gain a +10 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against any damage you take from the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). |
| 25 | You gain a +15 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against any damage you take from the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). |
| 30 | You gain a +20 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against any damage you take from the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by an additional +5.
