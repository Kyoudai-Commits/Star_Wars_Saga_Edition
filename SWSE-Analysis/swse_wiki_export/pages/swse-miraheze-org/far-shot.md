# Far Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Far_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Far_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are better at shooting distant foes.

**Prerequisite:** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** When you use a ranged weapon against targets at Short, Medium, or Long [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), the [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") category is considered one less. In other words, you take no penalty on ranged attack rolls made at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), a -2 penalty on ranged attack rolls made against targets at Medium [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), and a -5 penalty on ranged attack rolls made against targets at Long [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

**Normal:** When making a ranged attack roll, a character takes a -2 penalty against targets at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), a -5 penalty against targets at Medium [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), and a -10 penalty against targets at Long [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

This feat works normally with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"). However, if you are using [Missiles or Torpedoes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Missiles_or_Torpedoes "Missiles or Torpedoes") and you miss with your attack roll, the projectile does not gain the benefit of this feat for the second attack roll.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Let's say I have a sniper with the Far Shot Feat who [Aims](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aims "Aims") before making a single attack with an [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon that has a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"). Do any of these effects stack with one another?

**A:** Far Shot, [Aiming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming "Aiming") with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"), and using an [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon all stack, providing progressively better attacks against distant targets. I can see how the precise interaction might be a bit confusing, so here are the [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalties (Point-Blank, Short, Medium, and Long) for each combination:

**Non-[Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon (such as a [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle")):**

- With no other modifiers: Point-Blank +0, Short -2, Medium -5, Long -10
- With Far Shot OR [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"): Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium -2, Long -5
- With Far Shot AND [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"): Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long -2

**[Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon (such as a [Sporting Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Rifle "Sporting Blaster Rifle")):**

- With no other modifiers: Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium -5, Long -10
- With Far Shot OR [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"): Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long -5
- With Far Shot AND [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope"): Point-Blank +0, Short +0, Medium +0, Long +0

Thus, if a trained sniper with the Far Shot Feat uses a good sniping weapon (anything with the [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") quality plus a mounted [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope")) and takes the time to [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim"), that sniper can attack a target at Long [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") without taking a penalty on the attack roll.
