# Vehicle Systems Expertise

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Systems_Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Systems_Expertise) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You may use your technical expertise to manipulate a Vehicle's systems more quickly than usual.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist")

**Effect:** You may use the [Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields") or [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power") Actions by spending two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions"). Also, once per encounter, you may attempt to use either of these Actions as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") by making a DC 30 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check.

**Normal:** The [Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields") and [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power") Actions normally requires you to spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions").
