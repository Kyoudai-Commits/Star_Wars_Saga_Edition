# Blazing Chain Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blazing_Chain_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blazing_Chain_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Blazing Chain members develop abilities that aid them in raids and [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") combat. They do not rely on [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), so they apply [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to blasters and other weapons in ways that other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") have not attempted. Only Force-users that are part of [The Blazing Chain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Blazing_Chain "The Blazing Chain") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Force Directed Shot

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to alter the flight of your blaster bolt, projectile, or similar missile from a thrown or ranged weapon. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), select one square within 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You treat that square as the origin square for your next [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with an Energy weapon made before the end of your next turn, and determine [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") and [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") as though you were firing from that square. You must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target of the attack from the square you occupy.

### Negate and Redirect

*Prerequisites:* *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")*

When you successfully use the *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")* Force Power against a ranged Energy weapon (such as a blaster), you can redirect some of the shot's energy at an enemy target, in addition to the Force Power's usual benefit. As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") immediately following your successful use of the *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")* Force Power, choose one enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), and within 6 squares of you, and make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").

If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes Energy damage equal to one-half of the damage you negated.

### Rising Anger

*Prerequisites:* [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

You tap directly into your anger to increase the effectiveness of your attacks. Once per round as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to an ally taking damage or being moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by an enemy, you gain a +1 morale bonus to your next attack roll. If you use this Talent again before you make an attack, the bonus increases by 1, up to a maximum of +5.

### Rising Panic

*Prerequisites:* [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

You start a **Rising Panic** in an enemy who observes your attacks and actions, inhibiting the enemy's ability to respond. Once per round as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to an enemy damaging one of your allies or moving one of your allies down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").

If the check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you move the target -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). An enemy moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by this Talent does not fall unconscious, but instead must spend its actions fleeing combat for one minute or until it moves up the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.
