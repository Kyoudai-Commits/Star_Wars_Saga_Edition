# Espionage Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Espionage_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Espionage_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You use your knowledge of stealth and survival to make yourself a master of espionage and a valuable resource for those who wish to keep their hands clean in dirty situations.

## Core Talents

### Fade Out

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

You know how to make yourself scarce when dealing with suspicious or hostile beings. You can use your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill, not [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), to [Create a Diversion to Hide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Create_a_Diversion_to_Hide "Create a Diversion to Hide"). If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill, you gain a +5 bonus to your skill check for the purpose of [Creating a Diversion to Hide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Creating_a_Diversion_to_Hide "Creating a Diversion to Hide").

### Keep Together

Whenever you are hit or missed by a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), provided that you end your movement adjacent to an ally. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Prudent Escape

Whenever you reduce a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or otherwise render a creature unconscious, you can choose two allies within 6 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You and the allies you chose can immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to their [Speeds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeds "Speeds") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Reactive Stealth

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

When you are missed by a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") and have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") or [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from the attacker, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") and make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to become hidden from your attacker, provided you still have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") or [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") at the end of your movement.

### Sizing Up

Once per encounter, you can make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of a single target that is within 6 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If you succeed, you gain a +2 insight bonus to all [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and attack rolls against the target until the end of the encounter.
