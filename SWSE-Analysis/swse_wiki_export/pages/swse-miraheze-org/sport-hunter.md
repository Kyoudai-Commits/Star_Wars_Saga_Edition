# Sport Hunter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sport_Hunter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sport_Hunter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are skilled in the use of slugthrowers and sporting weapons and gain certain benefits depending on which weapon you use.

**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)") or [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)")

**Effect:** When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a slugthrower or sporting weapon with which you are proficient, you gain one of the following benefits, depending on the kind of weapon you use.

- **[Slugthrower Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slugthrower_Pistol "Slugthrower Pistol"):** When you use a [Slugthrower Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slugthrower_Pistol "Slugthrower Pistol") at Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), you deal +1 die of damage with the weapon.
- **[Slugthrower Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slugthrower_Rifle "Slugthrower Rifle"):** When you use a [Slugthrower Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slugthrower_Rifle "Slugthrower Rifle"), the damage dice increase from d8 to d12.
- **[Sporting Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Pistol "Sporting Blaster Pistol"):** When you use a [Sporting Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Pistol "Sporting Blaster Pistol"), reroll any result of 1 on the damage dice until you get a result other than 1.
- **[Sporting Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Rifle "Sporting Blaster Rifle"):** When using a [Sporting Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sporting_Blaster_Rifle "Sporting Blaster Rifle"), you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls when you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before firing.
