# Inspiration Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") are renowned for their ability to inspire their followers and urge them to greatness. You can often get results out of their friends, allies, and followers that other leaders cannot. All of the Talents in this Tree are Mind-Affecting effects. Moreover, you can't use any of these Talents on yourself.

## Core Talents

### Bolster Ally

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can bolster an ally within line of sight, moving them +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), and giving them a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to their Character Level, if they're at one-half their maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or less. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away.

You can't **Bolster** the same ally more than once in a single encounter.

### Ignite Fervor

*Prerequisites:* **Bolster Ally**, **Inspire Confidence**

Whenever you hit an opponent with a melee or ranged attack, you can (as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")) choose to give one ally within your line of sight a bonus to damage on their next attack equal to their Character Level. Once their fervor has been ignited, the affected ally doesn't need to remain within line of sight of you; if their next attack misses, they lose the bonus to damage granted by this Talent.

### Inspire Confidence

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can inspire confidence in all allies in your line of sight, granting them a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, and a +1 morale bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for the rest of the encounter, or until you're unconscious or dead. Once **Inspired**, your allies don't need to remain within line of sight of you.

### Inspire Haste

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can encourage one of your allies within line of sight to make haste with a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). On that ally's next turn, that ally can make a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") that normally requires a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") instead.

### Inspire Zeal

*Prerequisite:* **Ignite Fervor**

Whenever an ally within line of sight of you makes an attack that moves an opponent down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") (such as dealing damage that equals of exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold")), that ally moves the target an additional -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

## Additional Talents

### Beloved

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Bolster Ally**, **Inspire Confidence**

Your allies hold you in such esteem that when you are threatened or injured, you can impel them to action. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Guardian:* Choose one ally as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). As long as you remain within 6 squares of the ally, you gain a +2 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.
- *Reprisal:* Make a single melee or ranged attack against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If your attack roll succeeds and if that target attacks you before the end of your next turn, one ally within 6 squares can make an attack against that target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").
- *To Me!:* Spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Whenever you take any damage before the beginning of your next turn, each ally within line of sight can move 2 squares as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Willpower

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Inspire Confidence**

You can share your strength of will with your allies. Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can grant all allies within line of sight a +2 morale bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). This bonus lasts for the remainder of the encounter, and once it is granted your allies need not remain within line of sight of you to retain this bonus.
