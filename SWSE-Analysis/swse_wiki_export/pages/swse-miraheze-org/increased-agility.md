# Increased Agility

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increased_Agility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increased_Agility) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Due to your own natural abilities or specific training, you move faster or farther than normal when climbing, jumping, and swimming.

**Prerequisite:** [Conditioning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditioning "Conditioning")

**Effect:** You increase your Climb Speed, Swim Speed and Jump distance by +2 squares and do not lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when climbing.
