# Republic Commando Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Republic_Commando_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Republic_Commando_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have demonstrated that you are a highly skilled and capable soldier for the Republic and work well in a commando squad.

### Ambush Commando

When you successfully hit an opponent that has not yet acted in combat, you deal +2 dice of damage with the attack.

### Higher Yield

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

Once per encounter, you can choose to deal +1 die of damage with a single [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade"), [Mine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mine (page does not exist)"), or other [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive").

### Rapid Reload

You can retrieve a stored [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell") or [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") and reload your weapon as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Shoulder to Shoulder

Whenever you begin your turn adjacent to an ally, you gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your Heroic Level. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from various sources do not stack.

### Strength in Numbers

If you are within 10 squares of an ally, you can add +2 to your [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction").

### Weapon Shift

*Prerequisite:* **[Gun Club](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gun_Club "Gun Club")**

If you use a ranged weapon as a melee weapon (as with the **[Gun Club](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gun_Club "Gun Club")** Talent), you gain a +2 bonus to melee attack rolls with that weapon.
