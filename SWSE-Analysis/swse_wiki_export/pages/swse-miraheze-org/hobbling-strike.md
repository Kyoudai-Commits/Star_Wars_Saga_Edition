# Hobbling Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hobbling_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hobbling_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You know where to hit your enemy to slow it down.

**Prerequisite:** **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")** or [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") or [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike").

**Effect:** Whenever you would deal extra damage by using **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")**, [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), or [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), you can reduce the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by 1 square until the end of the encounter, instead of dealing the extra damage.
