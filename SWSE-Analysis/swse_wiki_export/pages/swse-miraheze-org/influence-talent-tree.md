# Influence Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influence_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influence_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

One of your greatest strengths is your ability to exert influence over your opponents.

## Core Talents

### Presence

You can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") a creature as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") (instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")).

### Demand Surrender

*Prerequisite:* **Presence**

Once per encounter, you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to demand surrender from an opponent who has been reduced to one-half or less of its hit points. If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), it surrenders to you and your allies, drops any weapons it is holding, and takes no hostile actions. If the target is higher level than you, it gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you or any of your allies attack it, it no longer submits to your will and can act normally. You can only use this Talent against a particular target once per encounter. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

Unlike other Mind-Affecting effects, you need only to hail a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to **Demand Surrender**. You can demand surrender of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that has been reduced to one-half or fewer of its hit points, even if the crew is uninjured. You make your check against the Vehicle's [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander"); all [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") crews are assumed to have a [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of 10. If the CL of the target [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is greater than your Heroic Level or the CL of your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), the target gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). You can't use this Talent to **Demand Surrender** from a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that is more than one size larger than yours under any circumstances- [Star Destroyers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_I-Class_Star_Destroyer "Imperial I-Class Star Destroyer") don't surrender to [X-Wings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/T-65B_X-Wing_Starfighter "T-65B X-Wing Starfighter").

### Improved Weaken Resolve

*Prerequisites:* **Presence**, **Weaken Resolve**

As **Weaken Resolve**, except that the target doesn't stop fleeing from you if it is wounded.

### Weaken Resolve

*Prerequisite:* **Presence**

Once per round, when you deal damage equal to or greater than the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"); if the result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you fill the target with terror, causing it to flee from you at top speed for 1 minute. The target can't take [Standard Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Actions "Standard Actions"), [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions"), or [Full-Round Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Actions "Full-Round Actions") while fleeing, but the target stops fleeing and can act normally if it is wounded. As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") or [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), the target can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") (if it has not already spent one earlier in the round) to negate the effect. The effect is automatically negated if the target's level is equal to or higher than your Character Level. This is a Mind-Affecting fear effect.

Unlike with other [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects, you need only to hail a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to weaken the crew's resolve. When you deal damage that equals or exceeds a Vehicle's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you may use this Talent; in addition, you may [Ready](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ready "Ready") an Action to use this Talent after any [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") on your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") deals damage that equals or exceeds a Vehicle's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"). You make your check against the Vehicle's [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander"); all [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") crews are assumed to have a [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of 10. The effect is automatically negated if the target Vehicle's Challenge Level is greater than your Heroic Level or your Vehicle's Challenge Level.

## Additional Talents

### Fluster

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Presence**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

You get under an opponent's skin. Once per encounter, make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") one creature within line of sight as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). On a success, instead of the normal effect of an [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") application of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill, the affected creature can take only a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. If the target is a higher level than you, it gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against the [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") check. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Intimidating Defense

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Presence**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") one creature that is making a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against you, and is within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If you succeed, you impose a -5 penalty to that attack roll. If the target is a higher level than you, it gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against the [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") check. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
