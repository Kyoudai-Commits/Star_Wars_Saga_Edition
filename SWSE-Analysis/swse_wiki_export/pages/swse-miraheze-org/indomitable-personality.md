# Indomitable Personality

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Indomitable_Personality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Indomitable_Personality) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Your powerful personality helps you cope with attacks that assault your will.

**Prerequisite:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"); this benefit lasts until the end of your next turn.
