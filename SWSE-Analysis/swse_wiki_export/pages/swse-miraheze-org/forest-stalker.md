# Forest Stalker

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forest_Stalker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forest_Stalker) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are one of the most skilled hunters among your Species and have learned to move through the forest with nary a whisper.

**Prerequisite:** [Ewok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewok "Ewok")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.
