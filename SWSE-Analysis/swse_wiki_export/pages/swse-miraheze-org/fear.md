# Fear

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fear](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fear) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You summon [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to instill fear in your enemies.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side"), [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 12 squares and within line of sight

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**Compare the result of the check to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target is affected as determined by the results of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. This is a Fear effect. The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target can take only one [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") on its next turn. |
| 20 | The target can take only one [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn. |
| 25 | The target can take only one [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. |
| 30 | The target can take no [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") on its next turn. |

**Special:** When you successfully use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") on an opponent, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to impose a -2 penalty on all of the target's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the beginning of your next turn.
