# Scoundrel

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Scoundrels are rogues- good, bad, and neutral- who either live outside the law or fight against it in order to get the upper hand. They can come from any world or region of the galaxy. Most use their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") to accomplish tasks, and many rely on [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") as a fallback when all else fails. The Scoundrel gets by with bravado, cunning, duplicity, and trickery. They live by their wits, lying, cheating, stealing, and even fighting when the need arises.

## Adventurers

Many Scoundrels live a life of adventure for the excitement it provides. Others go adventuring to advance their illicit careers. Some are good-hearted rogues in it for the thrill, or to right a wrong done to them or those they love. Others are despicable knaves who serve only one master- the greed that swells inside them. More often, an adventurous Scoundrel falls somewhere in the middle, changing allegiance and attitudes as the political climate changes, until something larger than themselves sets them on a particular course through the galaxy. Adventurous Scoundrels call themselves smugglers, pirates, outlaws, gamblers, slicers, con artists, thieves, rogues, and spies.

### Characteristics

Scoundrels have a knack for getting into and out of trouble. They have an instinct for self-preservation that keeps them alive, but it's usually tempered with a need to experience the thrills that their profession has to offer, and many adventurous Scoundrels are also (probably) saddled with a sense of honor that sometimes make them go against their natural inclinations.

### Backgrounds

Scoundrels don't often start out seeking to defy authority and break the law. Some are thrust into the profession as a means of rebellion. Others wind up on the wrong side of the law due to bad luck, poor decisions, or circumstances beyond their control. The skills they pick up along the way make them great members of any mission team.

### Examples of Scoundrels in Star Wars

Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, Dash Rendar, Dexter Jettster, Ghent, Watto

### Class Features

- **Abilities:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") are the Scoundrel's most important [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"), because they must have quick reflexes and a sharp wit to survive. [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") is important for talking one's way out of trouble, and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") is useful for spotting trouble before it finds the Scoundrel.
- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, a Scoundrel gains a +2 Class bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +1 Class bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** Scoundrels begin play at 1st level with a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 18 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At each level after 1st, Scoundrels gain 1d6 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Skills:** 1st-level Scoundrel are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 4 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"),
  - [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"),
  - [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") (all; taken individually),
  - [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"),
  - [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"),
  - [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"),
  - [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")
- **Starting Feats:** At 1st level, a Scoundrel gains the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):
  - [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")
- **Force Points:** Scoundrels gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") left over from the previous levels are lost.
- **Credits:** A 1st-level Scoundrel starts play with 3d4 x 250 credits.

### Talent Trees

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Scoundrel gains an additional [Scoundrel Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Talent "Scoundrel Talent"). A Scoundrel may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Scoundrel Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Talent_Trees "Scoundrel Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a Scoundrel gains a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the [Scoundrel Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Bonus_Feats "Scoundrel Bonus Feats") list. As with normal [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), you must meet the prerequisites to select the [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). The Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be from the [Scoundrel Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel_Bonus_Feats "Scoundrel Bonus Feats") list, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
