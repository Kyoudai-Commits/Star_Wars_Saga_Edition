# Angled Throw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Angled_Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Angled_Throw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Your amazing throws bounce [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades") off walls into open hatches or through congested battle zones.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When throwing a [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") or grenadelike weapon, you can attempt to bounce it off a wall or other surface close to your target. If you attack roll exceeds a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 15, you ignore [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") and [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover") (but not [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover")) with your attack.
