# Corruption

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

DestinyForce Power

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Your [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") is to corrupt an individual, organization, or location. You may seek to turn a person to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") or indoctrinate a group of people in the ways of evil. Your [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") may also be to become corrupted yourself, either by another character, an organization, or a series of life-changing events that unfold over time. The Corruption should be a long-term goal requiring a great deal of time and effort. In [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), opportunities for Corruption are more often on a personal scale than an organizational one. The new Sith might attempt to Corrupt a surviving Jedi into joining [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). An individual isolated by losses in the war between [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") and [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") might be Corrupted into joining a pirate or criminal organization.

Examples of this [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") include the Emperor's corruption of Anakin Skywalker, a dark-force user transforming a Jedi shrine into a temple of evil, or an Imperial officer convincing an Alliance cell to betray [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance"). Additionally, a character's [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") could be to allow themselves to be corrupted by [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"); their fall into darkness becomes the path to achieving this [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny").

- **Destiny Bonus:** For 24 hours, you and any allies within 10 squares of you gain a +1 [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks").
- **Destiny Penalty:** You take a -2 penalty on all [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks") for 24 hours.
- **Destiny Fulfilled:** Increase two [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") of your choice by +1 each. In addition, if your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") is less than your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score, your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") increases until it's equal to your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score signifying that you've turned to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to send a bolt of pure [Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side") vileness into an enemy.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any. Compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target is affected.

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and half that amount again at the start of its turn. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") by 5 or more, the target takes half that damage again in the following round. |
| 20 | The target takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and half that amount again at the start of its turn. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") by 5 or more, the target takes half that damage again in the following round. |
| 25 | The target takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and half that amount again at the start of its turn. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") by 5 or more, the target takes half that damage again in the following round. |
| 30 | The target takes 5d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and half that amount again at the start of its turn. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") by 5 or more, the target takes half that damage again in the following round. |

The target adds a size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"): Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium and smaller, +0.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you successfully damage a target by using this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to have all creatures adjacent to the target take half this damage if the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check also exceeds their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). These secondary targets do not take additional damage in the following round.
