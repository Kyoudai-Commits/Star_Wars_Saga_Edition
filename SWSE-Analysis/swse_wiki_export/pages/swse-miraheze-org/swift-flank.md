# Swift Flank

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Flank) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You leap over or dash around your opponent, striking before they have time to react.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus one adjacent enemy.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You can move up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"); this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent target. If the space you occupy and the space you started in are spaces that [Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank "Flank") the opponent, the opponent is considered to be [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked") for your attack. |

You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent target. If the space you occupy and the space you started in are spaces that [Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank "Flank") the opponent, the opponent is considered to be [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked") for your attack. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Vaapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vaapad "Vaapad")):** If you have the **[Vaapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vaapad "Vaapad")** Talent, your target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you until the end of your turn.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase your movement by 2 squares.
