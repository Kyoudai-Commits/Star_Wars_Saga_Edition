# Saber Swarm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saber_Swarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saber_Swarm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You slash at your opponent rapidly, aiming many short strikes at the target's body.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -5 penalty. |
| 25 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -2 penalty. |
| 30 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks against a single target. Each of these attacks is made at a -1 penalty. |
| 35 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks against a single target. You suffer no additional penalties for these attacks. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Ataru](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ataru "Ataru")):** If you have the **[Ataru](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ataru "Ataru")** Talent, your opponent takes a -5 penalty on all [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to block your attacks with the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** Talent or the *[Makashi Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi_Riposte "Makashi Riposte")* Form Power.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares before or after you use this Form Power. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
