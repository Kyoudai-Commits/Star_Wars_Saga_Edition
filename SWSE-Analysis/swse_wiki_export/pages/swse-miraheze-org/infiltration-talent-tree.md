# Infiltration Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltration_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltration_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are trained to secretly infiltrate enemy strongholds and organizations by force or by guile.

## Core Talents

### Always Ready

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

You are accustomed to operating in response to enemy actions. When your [Readied Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Readied_Action "Readied Action") is triggered, it does not change your [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count").

### Concealed Weapon Expert

You are deadly with an [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") strike, [Hold-Out Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hold-Out_Blaster_Pistol "Hold-Out Blaster Pistol"), [Knife](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knife "Knife"), [Vibrodagger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrodagger "Vibrodagger"), or other small concealable weapons (as determined by the Gamemaster). Once per round you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to reroll an attack using one of these weapons, but you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Creeping Approach

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single opponent within 12 squares that is unaware of you as the target of this Talent. Until the beginning of your next turn, that target may not make [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks to notice you, even if you enter the target's [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If you or any of your allies attack the target, the effect of this Talent ends.

### Set for Stun

You are particularly adept with [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") weapons. If you are using a ranged weapon that deals [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage (including a lethal weapon set to [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun")), you can spend two consecutive [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to activate this Talent. If the [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage on your next attack exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you move the target -3 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), instead of the normal -2 steps.

### Silent Takedown

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

You are skilled at quietly knocking out or eliminating guards and others when they are caught unaware. If you damage an opponent that is unaware of you, that opponent cannot speak or make other noises until the end of your next turn. This is a [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") effect.
