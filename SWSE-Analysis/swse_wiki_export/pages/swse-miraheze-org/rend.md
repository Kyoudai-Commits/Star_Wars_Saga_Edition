# Rend

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rend](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rend) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can move a single target, whether it is a creature or object, in two different directions simultaneously.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and within 6 squares of you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result with the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the check equals or exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target takes 3d6 points of damage. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is a Natural 20, you score the equivalent of a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"), and the target takes double damage. If you deal enough damage to reduce the target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you rip the target into two separate parts (living creatures are automatically killed).

**Special:** If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check succeeds, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal an additional 2d6 points of damage to the target.
