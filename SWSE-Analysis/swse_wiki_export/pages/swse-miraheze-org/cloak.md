# Cloak

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cloak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cloak) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You bend light around your body, rendering yourself invisible to anyone looking in your direction.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You are considered to have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against all targets until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 20 | You are considered to have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against all targets until the beginning of your next turn. Additionally, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 25 | You are considered to have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against all targets until the beginning of your next turn. Additionally, you gain a +3 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 30 | You are considered to have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against all targets until the beginning of your next turn. Additionally, you gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks until the beginning of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can maintain *Cloak* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Cloak* Force Power is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Cloak*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Cloak*.

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the bonus to your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks by +5.
