# Light Side Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

[The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is not the easiest course to follow, but its promise of peace and harmony can help even the most weak-willed of Force-users on the right path. You must have a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 0 to select Talents from this tree; if your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") ever increases to at least 1, you lose access to all Talents in this Talent Tree until your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") drops back to 0.

## Core Talents

### At Peace

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the end of the encounter or until you attack, whichever comes first.

### Attuned

*Prerequisite:* **Focused Attack**

When you roll a Natural 20 on an attack roll against a target with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, you can activate a single [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor immediately as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Focused Attack

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reroll an attack against a creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, keeping the better of the two results.

### Surge of Light

Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can return any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, you can use this Talent one additional time per encounter.
