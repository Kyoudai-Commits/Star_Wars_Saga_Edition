# Turret Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Turret_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Turret_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You can construct small, disposable, automated Turrets.

## Core Talents

### Blaster Turret I

Once per encounter, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can create a Turret (Size Tiny, [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") +4, [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") +4, [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") 10, 10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") 8) that can be mounted to any flat surface. The Turret fires as a standard [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol") once per round, using your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") plus your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus and dealing 3d6 points of Energy damage.

The Turret fires at any target you designate (a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), once per round on your turn), though you must remain adjacent to the Turret to control it. The Turret is expended at the end of the encounter.

### Blaster Turret II

*Prerequisite:* **Blaster Turret I**

Your Turret's capabilities increase in the following ways: [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") +8, [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") +8, [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") 12, 15 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") 10, and the Turret deals 3d8 points of Energy damage. The Turret can be directed by a remote control at a range of 12 squares.

### Blaster Turret III

*Prerequisites:* **Blaster Turret I**, **Blaster Turret II**

Your Turret gains the ability to fire twice per round, with a -5 penalty on each attack roll, and gains [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 5.

### Ion Turret

*Prerequisite:* **Blaster Turret I**

You can construct a Turret that is highly effective against [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). The Turret deals [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage instead of normal damage.

### Stun Turret

*Prerequisite:* **Blaster Turret I**

You can construct a nonlethal Turret. The Turret deals [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage instead of normal damage.

### Turret Self-Destruct

*Prerequisite:* **Blaster Turret I**

Your Turret self-destructs automatically when it reaches 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). It explodes in a 2-Square radius, dealing its normal damage. If you are adjacent to the Turret, you can disable this feature as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").
