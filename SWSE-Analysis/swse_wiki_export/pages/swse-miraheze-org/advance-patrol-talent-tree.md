# Advance Patrol Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advance_Patrol_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advance_Patrol_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Your skills at observation and stealth are frequently called upon as you gather information about enemy forces.

## Core Talents

### Forward Patrol

*Prerequisite:* **Watchful Step**

At the start of a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round") in which you are not caught by [Surprise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise "Surprise"), you can designate one ally within 6 squares of you as able to retain his or her [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round").

### Mobile Scout

*Prerequisites:* **Forward Patrol**, **Watchful Step**

You know that the key to winning a fight is keeping your enemies from pinning you down. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Evasive Assault:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). If the attack successfully deals damage, you gain a +5 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn.
- *Expeditious Attack:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), then [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
- *Yielding Assault:* When you are damaged by an enemy's [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can move up to one-half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"). On your next turn, you gain a favorable circumstance (+2 bonus) to your first attack roll against the same enemy that damaged you.

### Trailblazer

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival")

During your turn, you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to allow all allies within 6 square of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to count the first square of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") as normal terrain each time they [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move").

### Watchful Step

You can use your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check modifier instead of your [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") modifier when making [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks. If you are entitled to an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check reroll, you can reroll your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). You are considered to be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative").
