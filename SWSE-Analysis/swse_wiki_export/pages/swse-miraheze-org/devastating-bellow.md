# Devastating Bellow

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastating_Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastating_Bellow) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your bellow produces greater sonic energy than do the bellows of other members of your Species.

**Prerequisite:** [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian")

**Effect:** Your [Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bellow "Bellow") attack deals 4d6 points of damage on a hit and half damage on a miss.

**Normal:** Without this feat, your [Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bellow "Bellow") attack deals 3d6 points of damage on a hit.
