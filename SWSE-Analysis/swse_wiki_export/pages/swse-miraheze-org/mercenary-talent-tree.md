# Mercenary Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mercenary_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mercenary_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Mercenaries are blasters-for-hire who sell their services to the highest bidder. Though money might motivate them, few types of soldiers are more capable of operating independently. [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this Talent Tree can be used to qualify for the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") Prestige Class, in addition to those listed in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*.

## Core Talents

### Commanding Presence

Once per encounter, you can activate this Talent as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Until the end of the encounter, all your opponents within 6 squares of you take a -2 penalty to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect. Additionally, [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") is now considered a [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill") for you.

### Dirty Fighting

Once per encounter, if you successfully damage an opponent with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you reduce the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by 2 points for the remainder of the encounter.

### Feared Warrior

*Prerequisite:* **Commanding Presence**

Your abilities on the battlefield are well known and feared. When you reduce an opponent to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") with an attack, you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") against all targets within 6 squares. If your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that target takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls for the remainder of the encounter. This Talent affects any given target only once per encounter. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.

### Focused Warrior

Your training makes you confident and disciplined in combat. When you successfully deal damage to an opponent in combat, you gain a +5 morale bonus to [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the start of your next turn. You lose this bonus to [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") if you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") or [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") for any reason.

## Additional Talents

### Combined Mercenary Fire

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Combined Fire in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[Combined Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Fire "Combined Fire")."*** |

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* [Coordinated Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack "Coordinated Attack")

You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls on attacks against targets that have been damaged by an ally since the end of your last turn.

### Mercenary's Determination

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Mercenary's Grit**

As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), on your turn, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to double your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") for 1 round. You must wait 5 rounds between each use of this ability.

### Mercenary's Grit

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

When you are affected by any debilitating condition, you can convert the condition's modifier from a penalty to a bonus for 1 round as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). At the end of your next turn, move -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Mercenary's Teamwork

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Combined Mercenary Fire**, [Coordinated Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack "Coordinated Attack")

You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls for each ally that has damaged your target since the end of your last turn (to a maximum of +10).
