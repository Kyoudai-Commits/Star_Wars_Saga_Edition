# Sabotage Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sabotage_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sabotage_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are skilled at improvising weapons, handling [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives"), and disrupting the enemy.

## Core Talents

### Device Jammer

You can construct a short-range jammer that affects a specific type of electronic device such as a personal [Energy Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Shields "Energy Shields"), [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink"), [Portable Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Portable_Computer "Portable Computer"), or [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad"). As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you select a particular piece of personal, portable electronic [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") (any item listed as equipment excluding [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), that has an electronic component) and make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to build the **Device Jammer**.

If the check is successful, all devices of the chosen type cease to function while within 12 squares of your position for the remainder of the encounter. You may only have one **Jammer** (Device or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) active at a time.

### Droid Jammer

You can construct a short-range jammer that affects [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you make a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to build the **Droid Jammer**. When a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") comes within 6 squares of you, compare the result of your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to the Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your check result equals or exceeds the Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the Droid can only take [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") as long as it remains within the radius of the **Droid Jammer**.

[Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") that are immune to the effect of a [Restraining Bolt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restraining_Bolt "Restraining Bolt") are immune to the effects of this Talent. The **Droid Jammer** functions for the remainder of the encounter. You may only have one **Jammer** (Device or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) active at a time.

### Extreme Explosion

*Prerequisites:* **Skilled Demolitionist**, **Shaped Explosion**

You know how to set large charges and use dozens of charges for extremely large explosives. You increase the [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius of any [Mines](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mines "Mines") or [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives") by 1 square.

### Mine Mastery

You can place a [Mine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mine (page does not exist)") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

### Shaped Explosion

*Prerequisite:* **Skilled Demolitionist**

You know how to set charges to direct a blast in a specific direction or manner. You can shape an explosion caused by [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives") or [Mines](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mines "Mines") that you set into a line or a cone instead of a radius. The length of the line is equal to 2 x the radius of the explosive [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst"); the length of the cone is equal to 3 x the radius of the [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst"), and either the line or the cone originates from the square where the [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives") are placed.

### Skilled Demolitionist

You can set a [Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Detonator?action=edit&redlink=1 "Detonator (page does not exist)") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and your [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives") never go off as the [Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Detonator?action=edit&redlink=1 "Detonator (page does not exist)") is being placed, even if you fail the check by 10 or more. You must still roll to determine if the charge otherwise goes off as planned (see [Handle Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Handle_Explosives "Handle Explosives")).
