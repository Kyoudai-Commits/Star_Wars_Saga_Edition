# Medic

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medic) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

The galaxy is a dangerous place. Vicious creatures, massive battles, barroom brawls, lethal environments, and other kinds of threats imperil people everywhere. Doctors, nurses, and other medical personnel provide vital care to the critically injured, but are less available to those operating in the field. The Medic fills this void, providing on-the-spot advanced medical treatment and support.

Medics are highly trained healers, able to function in the worst of conditions and circumstances. They possess great knowledge of the healing arts and sciences. They know quick and effective treatment methods and use them to perform near-miracles to save injured and dying creatures.

Medics live to heal others. The most altruistic among them heal any and all within their abilities. Disillusioned or vengeful Medics might choose to heal only their allies and companions. Many Medics serve as [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") medical officers, battlefield surgeons, or corporate medical personnel, or in portable medical facilities. A Medic on the shadier side of society might operate a back-alley clinic, serve a criminal organization, or work as the personal physician of a crime lord.

**Examples of Medics in Star Wars:** Dhagon Gent, Elassar Targon, Jos Vondar, Ton Phanan.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Medic, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)"), [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Surgical Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgical_Expertise "Surgical Expertise")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Medics gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Medics gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points** Medics gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, every time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Medic gains an additional [Medic Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medic_Talent "Medic Talent"). A Medic may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Medic Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medic_Talent_Trees "Medic Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Medical Secrets

As a Medic's medical skills grow, they gain insight into specific medical procedures and treatment of specific [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th and so on), the Medic gains a [Medical Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Secret "Medical Secret"), giving them a bonus when using a specific application of the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill. A Medic can select a given treatment only once.
