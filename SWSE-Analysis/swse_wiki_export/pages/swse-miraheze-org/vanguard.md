# Vanguard

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vanguard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vanguard) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Fighting an effective war and taking out the enemy is necessary if your army is to live to fight another day. But you must find the enemy first, and those sensor results can be a little deceptive at times. Send out the Vanguard to track them down.

Vanguards, simply put, are advance scouts who specialize in locating the enemy and relaying its position to their allies. With the stealth and sharp senses required to achieve their goals, they specialize in locating and observing the enemy's movements, all the while obscuring their presence from them. If the situation arises, they might try to take a few of the enemy down before retreating back to friendly territory, but their primary purpose is to serve as their group's eyes and ears.

Vanguards are typically attached to military units near the front lines. They operate best in small groups as individuals. In either case, Vanguards excel at sneaking close to hostile forces, stealing away with the knowledge of their location, and watching them fall to the might of their allies.

**Examples of Vanguards in Star Wars:** 91st Reconnaissance Corps Clone Troopers, Aleenan Scouts, Antarian Rangers, Neyo (CC-8826), Rebel Plex Troopers.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Vanguard, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth").
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** Any two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from either the
  - [Camouflage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Camouflage_Talent_Tree "Camouflage Talent Tree") or
  - [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Vanguards gain a +4 class bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and a +2 class bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Vanguards gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Vanguards gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, every time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Vanguard gains an additional [Vanguard Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vanguard_Talent "Vanguard Talent"). A Vanguard may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Vanguard Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vanguard_Talent_Trees "Vanguard Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Surprise Attack

Whenever a Vanguard attacks a target that is unaware of them or otherwise denied their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), they gain a bonus on their first attack roll in a round against that target equal to one-half their Class Level.
