# Combustion

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combustion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combustion) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to agitate particles in the air to create a pyrokinetic spray of sparks.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All targets within a 2x2 square area (the entire area must be within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight")).

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") of each target in the affected area. If the check result equals or exceeds a target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 4d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage and catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire"). This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"). The result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check determines the attack bonus of the [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") each round it burns. This replaces the [Fire's](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") normal attack bonus.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to move one target hit by this Force Power -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
