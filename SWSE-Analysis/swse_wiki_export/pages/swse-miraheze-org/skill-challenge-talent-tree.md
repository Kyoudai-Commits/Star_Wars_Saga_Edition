# Skill Challenge Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You excel at complex tasks and are always called upon when your allies need something done quickly and done right.

## Core Talents

### Guaranteed Boon

Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") in a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") and accrue a Failure for that [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), you regain that [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Leading Skill

Whenever you earn a Success in a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), you gain a +2 insight bonus to your next [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") made with a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") in the same [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge").

### Learn from Mistakes

Whenever you accrue a Failure in a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), you grant the next ally to take an action in the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") a +2 insight bonus to a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), provided that the ally takes a different action (and uses a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill")) than you did.

### Try Your Luck

Whenever you accrue a Failure in a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), choose one ally. The next time that ally uses the same [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") that you used to accrue a Failure before the end of the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), that ally rolls two dice on the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), keeping the better of the two results.
