# Superior Skills Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Superior_Skills_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Superior_Skills_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You're not just good at what you do, you're the best. Failure is but a stumbling block, and great success always follows.

## Core Talents

### Assured Skill

When you select this Talent, choose one [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). Whenever you roll a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), you can choose to lose any competence bonuses to that [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") and instead roll twice, keeping either result.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to gain the benefits of this Talent.

### Critical Skill Success

Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), choose one other [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). Once before the end of your next turn, you can choose to gain a +5 competence bonus to a check with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Exceptional Skill

When you select this Talent, choose one [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"). Whenever you roll a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), a result of 2-7 on the die is always treated as though you had rolled an 8.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to gain the benefits of this Talent.

### Reliable Boon

Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), you always reroll a result of 1 on any of your [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") dice, and continue to reroll until you get a result of 2 or higher.

### Skill Boon

When you select this Talent, choose one [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"). Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), increase the die type of your [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") by one step (i.e. from d6 to d8, d8 to d10, d10 to d12), to a maximum of d12.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to gain the benefits of this Talent.

### Skill Confidence

*Prerequisite:* **Critical Skill Success**

When you select this Talent, choose one [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"). Whenever you roll a Natural 19 or a Natural 20 on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), you gain the benefits of the **Critical Skill Success** Talent and also gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to gain the benefits of this Talent.

### Skillful Recovery

When you select this Talent, choose one [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"). Whenever you fail a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). That [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") can only be spent to add to a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you chose for this Talent. If the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not spent by the end of the encounter, it is lost. For the purposes of this Talent, failing a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") means failing to get the minimum possible result from the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check").

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to gain the benefits of this [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent").
