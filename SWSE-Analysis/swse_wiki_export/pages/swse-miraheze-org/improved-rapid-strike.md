# Improved Rapid Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Rapid_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Rapid_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Your [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") is more effective with [Light Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapons "Light Melee Weapons").

**Prerequisite:** [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike")

**Effect:** When using a [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon") or [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") with the [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") feat, you can take a -5 penalty to your attack roll to gain +2 dice of damage on a successful strike.

**Special:** This does not stack with the [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") Feat or extra damage provided by any source that does not stack with the [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") feat (such as [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing")). If you do not have a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") of 13 or higher, the penalty to your attack roll is -10 instead of -5.
