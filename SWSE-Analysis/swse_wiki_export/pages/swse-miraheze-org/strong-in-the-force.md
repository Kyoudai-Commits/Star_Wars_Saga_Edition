# Strong in the Force

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strong_in_the_Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strong_in_the_Force) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You have a particularly strong connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**Effect:** When you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to adjust the result of an attack roll, [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check"), you roll d8s instead of d6s.
