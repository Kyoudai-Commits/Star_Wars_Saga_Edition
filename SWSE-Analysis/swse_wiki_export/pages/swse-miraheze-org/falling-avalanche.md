# Falling Avalanche

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Falling_Avalanche](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Falling_Avalanche) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You raise your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") above your head then bring it crashing down on your opponent with incredible force.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, provided that it is no more than one size category larger than you. If your attack does damage, you can push the target 1 square away from you, and then move into the space your enemy just vacated. Neither your movement nor the target's movement provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You cannot move a target that is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |
| 20 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, provided that it is no more than one size category larger than you. If your attack does damage, you can push the target 2 squares away from you, and then move through spaces your target passed through to end adjacent to your target. Neither your movement nor the target's movement provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You cannot move a target that is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |
| 25 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, provided that it is no more than one size category larger than you. If your attack does damage, you can push the target 3 squares away from you, and then move through spaces your target passed through to end adjacent to your target. Neither your movement nor the target's movement provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You cannot move a target that is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |
| 30 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, provided that it is no more than one size category larger than you. If your attack does damage, you can push the target 4 squares away from you, and then move through spaces your target passed through to end adjacent to your target. Neither your movement nor the target's movement provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You cannot move a target that is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")):** If you have the **[Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")** Talent, after successfully activating this Form Power, you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") which can only be used to activate the **[Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")** Talent. If this temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to knock your opponent [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") after its forced movement.
