# Lightning Burst

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightning_Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightning_Burst) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You call upon [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to cause lightning to arc out from your body, striking adjacent enemies.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All enemies adjacent to you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to each target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the attack hits, the target takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage and moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). If the attack misses, the target takes half damage and does not move along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to increase the damage dealt by +2d6.

You can maintain the *Lightning Burst* Force Power as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), provided you haven't moved since activating the Force Power. When you maintain a *Lightning Burst*, make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of each target in range (as described above).
