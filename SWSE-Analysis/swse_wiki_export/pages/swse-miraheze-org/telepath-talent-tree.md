# Telepath Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepath_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepath_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have mastered the art of mental manipulation, using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to touch, read, and affect the minds of others with ease.

## Core Talents

### Mind Probe

When you touch a living creature with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 3 or higher, you can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to probe its mind for secrets. You must be adjacent to the target, and using the **Mind Probe** is a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). If the target is unwilling, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, equaling or exceeding the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). This ability otherwise functions exactly as the [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") skill's [Learn News and Rumors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Learn_News_and_Rumors "Learn News and Rumors"), [Learn Secret Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Learn_Secret_Information "Learn Secret Information"), and [Locate Individual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locate_Individual "Locate Individual") applications.

Your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check must still exceed the base [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") skill DCs in order to retrieve the information you seek, but you need not pay anything in bribes, and you retrieve the information as a part of the [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). Failing the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") by 5 or more does not cause someone to notice that you are seeking the information.

### Perfect Telepathy

You can communicate in full sentences and complete thoughts when you use the [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") aspect of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, instead of just in basic phrases. However, the target of your [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") can still only communicate in basic emotions or single thoughts.

### Psychic Citadel

You gain a [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") equal to your Class Level.

### Psychic Defenses

*Prerequisite:* **Psychic Citadel**

Whenever another creature targets you with a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] descriptor, it automatically takes [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage equal to 1d6 x your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum x1).

### Telepathic Intruder

Whenever you use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] descriptor successfully against a target, until the end of your next turn you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made to activate [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents") against that same target.
