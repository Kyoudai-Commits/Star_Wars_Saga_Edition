# Order of Shasa Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Order_of_Shasa_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Order_of_Shasa_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Order of Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Order_of_Shasa "The Order of Shasa") is still developing training techniques and abilities, taking most of their influence from Manaan's oceans. Only Force-users that are part of [The Order of Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Order_of_Shasa "The Order of Shasa") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Deception Awareness

Realizing how devastating the consequences would have been if [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") had successfully deceived her and her companions during the Jedi Civil War, Shasa has developed a technique for using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to detect deceptions. You gain +5 to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against uses of the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill.

Additionally, you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead of a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check to [Sense Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Deception "Sense Deception") and [Sense Influence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Influence "Sense Influence"). You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") for the purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Greater Weapon Focus (Fira)

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Fira)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Fira) "Weapon Focus (Fira)")

You have mastered the chosen weapon of [The Order of Shasa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Order_of_Shasa "The Order of Shasa"). You gain a +1 bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls with a [Fira](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fira "Fira"). This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the [Weapon Focus (Fira)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Fira) "Weapon Focus (Fira)") feat.

### Progenitor's Call

You have learned to sense the call of your ancestors and wield it through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Once per encounter, you can telepathically disrupt an opponent by making a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you succeed, the target is confused by the call, moving -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and losing its [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") on its next turn.

A creature can only be affected by this power once per encounter, and the [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") can be removed with a DC 15 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check or by resting for 8 hours. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Waveform

Taking cues from Manaan's oceans, you know how to emulate wave action using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), when you use a [Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic") Force Power (that is, a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*] descriptor), you manipulate the particles of the environment around you to add to the force of the impact, allowing you to add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier to any damage dealt (minimum +1).

### Fira Mastery

*Fira Mastery Talent created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

You treat a [Fira](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fira "Fira") as a [Simple Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Melee) "Simple Weapon (Melee)") instead of as an [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)"). Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with a [Fira](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fira "Fira"). This Talent counts as the [Weapon Focus (Fira)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Fira) "Weapon Focus (Fira)") Feat for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. If you also have the [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)") Feat, the attack bonus provided by this Talent does not stack with the attack bonus provided by [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)").
