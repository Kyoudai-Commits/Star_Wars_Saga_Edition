# Hawk-Bat Swoop

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hawk-Bat_Swoop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hawk-Bat_Swoop) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You leap into action against your foe, striking with your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") before other enemies have a chance to react.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares; this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you end your movement adjacent to a target, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 20 | You [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 4 squares; this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you end your movement adjacent to a target, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 25 | You [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 6 squares; this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you end your movement adjacent to a target, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 30 | You [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 8 squares; this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). If you end your movement adjacent to a target, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Ataru](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ataru "Ataru")):** If you have the **[Ataru](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ataru "Ataru")** Talent, you can also [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares after you resolve your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal +2 dice of damage with your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack.
