# Signature Device

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Signature_Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Signature_Device) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You specialize in designing and modifying specific types of technology.

**Prerequisite:** [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist")

**Effect:** You designate a single weapon, suit of armor, vehicle, or other item as your Signature Item. When making [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks to modify that piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"), you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on the check. In addition, you can allow the device to gain two Traits from the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") feat.

To install the second Trait, you must succeed on a DC 30 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check. Once installed, the device can use only one Trait at a time, and switching from one Trait to the other requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to adjust the item's settings.

You can have only one Signature Device at a time, but you can designate another device as your Signature Item (the former item loses all benefits gained from this feat).
