# Mandalorian Warrior Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Warrior_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Warrior_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Mandalorians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mandalorians "The Mandalorians") are talented and ferocious warriors. Their skillful training, warrior culture, and advanced combat abilities make them formidable foes in any era. The following Talents are particularly suited to [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era").

## Core Talents

### Armored Mandalorian

*Prerequisites:* [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, **Mandalorian Glory**, Proficient with Armor

Mandalorians wear armor constantly and learn to adjust to take an impact on the strongest section of their armor. You add your armor's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") bonus as an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your Elite Trooper [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") (with a maximum bonus equal to your base [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") DR). Additionally, if [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") do not ignore the DR of the armor you are wearing (such as [Cortosis Weave/Phrik Alloy General Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Weave/Phrik_Alloy_General_Template "Cortosis Weave/Phrik Alloy General Template")), [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") do not ignore your [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") Damage Reduction.

### Mandalorian Advance

Veteran Mandalorians know how to move on the battlefield. Once per encounter, on your turn, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") before any other Action.

### Mandalorian Ferocity

*Prerequisites:* [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

Mandalorians can be ferocious fighters. Select one [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") you are proficient with. Once per encounter, when making more than one attack in a round, you can add one damage die to each successful hit with the selected [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"). You can take this Talent more than once, selecting a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") each time.

### Mandalorian Glory

Above everything else, Mandalorians fight for glory in battle. Once per encounter, when you reduce an opponent's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to 0, you gain a +5 attack bonus with your next attack roll during the same encounter.

### Mandalorian Martial Arts Mastery

*Mandalorian Martial Arts Mastery Talent created by Miraheze user JuFroSamurai.*

Whereas other martial disciplines train in light clothing that doesn't restrict movement, Mandalorian warriors live and train in their Beskar'gam until it feels like a second skin. Once per round, as a swift action, until the end of your next turn you may treat yourself as wearing light or no armor strictly for the purposes of activating or gaining the benefits of [Martial Arts Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Feats "Martial Arts Feats") or [Martial Arts Forms Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Forms_Talents "Martial Arts Forms Talents") that would otherwise require the user to be wearing light or no armor.
