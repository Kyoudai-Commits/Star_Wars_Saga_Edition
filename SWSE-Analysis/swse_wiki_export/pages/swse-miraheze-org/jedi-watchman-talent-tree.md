# Jedi Watchman Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Watchman_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Watchman_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Jedi Watchmen are elite Jedi tasked with protecting and watching over a specific world or region of space. They seek out and destroy threats to the Republic and [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). As such, they have enhanced their abilities to blend in and covertly investigate threats at their source. All Jedi Watchmen are appointed and approved by the Jedi Council before training; many begin as [Jedi Consulars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Consular_Talent_Tree "Jedi Consular Talent Tree"). A Jedi Watchman's assignments might change, as directed by the council.

## Core Talents

### Force Warning

Allies within 12 squares can choose to reroll their [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks at the start of combat, but must keep the result of the reroll, even if it is worse. Furthermore, if any allies within 12 squares are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") at the start of an encounter, but you are not, you can designate a number of those allies equal to your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1); those allies are no longer considered [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), and can act normally during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round").

### Improved Quick Draw (Lightsabers)

*Prerequisites:* [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw"), [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

If you are carrying a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") (either in your hand or at your belt), you can draw the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), ignite it, and make a single attack during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), even if you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"). If you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take any single Action of your choice, as normal. Additionally, once per turn you may draw and ignite a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your turn.

### Sheltering Stance

*Prerequisites:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**, **Vigilance**

Whenever you are adjacent to an ally, you may use the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents on attacks that target that ally without the need to spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Vigilance

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") you may designate one adjacent ally as the target of this Talent. That target gains a +1 deflection bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") as long as you remain adjacent to them. You may change the target of this Talent as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Watchman's Advance

*Prerequisite:* **Force Warning**

When acting in the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), you and your allies can take an extra [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Any character can gain only one extra [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), regardless of the number of characters with this Talent in your group.
