# Hatred

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hatred](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hatred) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You give yourself over to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), letting your hate radiate out from your body in palpable waves.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All creatures within 6 squares of you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). |
| 20 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). |
| 25 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). |
| 30 | Until the end of your next turn, any creature that starts its turn within 6 squares of you takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty to all its [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by an additional +1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.

You can maintain *Hatred* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Hatred* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Hatred*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Hatred*.
