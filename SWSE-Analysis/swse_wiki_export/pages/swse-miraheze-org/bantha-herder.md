# Bantha Herder

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Herder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Herder) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You know how to frame your targets with your [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"), moving the targets where you want them to go.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you damage a Large or smaller creature with a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a weapon you are proficient with, compare the result of your attack roll to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you can move the target 1 square in any direction as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

If your [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") deals damage to multiple eligible creatures, you can use this benefit against all of them. You can't move a target that's being [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), and you can't move a target into a solid object or into another creature's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space").
