# Leader of Droids

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leader_of_Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leader_of_Droids) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You are experienced in leading [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and you know how to direct them tactically.

**Effect:** Whenever you provide a beneficial, Mind-Affecting effect to your allies, you may select from among these allies a number of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") equal to your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 1). The [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") you designate ignore their Mind-Affecting immunity with regards to this effect, provided they are willing to ignore this immunity.
