# Skills

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your character's Skills represent a variety of abilities, and you get better at them as you go up in level. A Skill Check takes into account your training (Trained skill bonus), natural talent ([Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") modifier), and luck (the dice roll). It may also take into account your Species' knack for certain Skills or the armor you're wearing ([Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty")), among other things.

Skill Checks are typically made against a Difficulty Class (DC). The DC is a number set by the GM (using the Skill rules as a guideline) that you must score as a result on your skill check to succeed. For example, climbing the outer wall of a ruined warehouse may have a DC of 15. To climb the wall, you must get a result of 15 or better on a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check.

To make a Skill Check, roll:

**1d20 + One-Half Your Character Level (rounded down) + Key [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") Modifier + 5 (if you are Trained) + Miscellaneous Modifiers**

### Skill Descriptions

Below is the format for skill descriptions. Headings that do not apply to a particular skill are omitted in that skill's description.

- **Key Ability:** The [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") whose modifier applies to the Skill Check.
- **Trained Only:** If "Trained Only" appears on the line beneath the skill name, you must be Trained in that Skill to use it. If "Trained Only" is omitted, the skill can be used Untrained except for some uses. If any special notes apply to Trained or Untrained use, they are covered in the "Special" section.
- **Armor Check Penalty:** If "Armor Check Penalty" appears on the line beneath the skill name, a character takes a penalty on skill checks made with the skill if they're wearing Armor with which they are not proficient. The size of the [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") depends on the type of armor: [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor"), -2; [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor"), -5; or [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor"), -10.
  - For example, Rorworr the [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") is proficient with [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") only. If he attempts to [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") in [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor"), he takes a -5 [Armor Check Penalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Check_Penalty "Armor Check Penalty") on his [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") check.
- **Retry:** Any circumstances that apply to successive attempts to use the skill successfully. If this paragraph is omitted, the skill check can be tried again without any inherent penalty other than consuming additional time.
- **Special:** Any special notes that apply, such as rules regarding Untrained use and whether you can Take 10 or Take 20 when using the skill.
- **Time:** How much time it takes to make a check with this skill, if that information hasn't already been covered elsewhere. In general, using a Skill that requires concentration (and thus distracts you from being fully aware of what's going on around you) provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from an opponent if you are within that opponent's [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area").

### Trained Skills

When you make a character, you are allowed to select a number of Skills based on your character [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum of 1 Trained Skill). Trained Skills are selected from the character's list of [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills") at 1st level, and a character may acquire new Trained Skills by taking the [Skill Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training "Skill Training") feat.

The major difference between a Trained Skill and an Untrained Skill is that you gain a +5 bonus on Skill Checks if you're Trained in the Skill. However, some Skills (such as [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")) can't be used Untrained.

## Taking 10

A Skill Check represents an attempt to accomplish some goal, usually in the face of some sort of time pressure or distraction. Sometimes, though, you can use a skill under more favorable circumstances and eliminate the luck factor.

When you're not in a rush and not being threatened or distracted, you may choose to Take 10. Instead of rolling 1d20 for the Skill Check, calculate your result as if you had rolled a 10 (an average roll on a d20). For many relatively routine tasks, Taking 10 results in success. Distractions, threats, and danger make it impossible for a character to Take 10. You also can't Take 10 when using a Skill Untrained, though the GM may allow exceptions for truly routine activities.

For example, Rorworr the [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") has a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") modifier of +10. The steep, rocky slope he's climbing has a DC of 15. With a little care, he can Take 10 and succeed automatically. But partway up the slope, a bounty hunter begins taking blaster shots at him from up above. Rorworr needs to make a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check to reach the bounty hunter, and this time he can Take 10 only because of the Wookiees' ability to Take 10 on [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") checks while under pressure.

### Taking 20

When you have plenty of time (generally 2 minutes for a Skill that can normally be checked in 1 round), and when the Skill being attempted carries no penalty for failure, you can Take 20. Taking 20 represents making multiple rolls, assuming that eventually you will roll a 20. Instead of rolling 1d20 for the Skill Check, calculate the result as if you had rolled a 20. Taking 20 means you keep trying until you get it right. Taking 20 takes twenty times as long as making a single check.

For example, Rorworr comes to a cliff face. He Takes 10 to make the [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), for a result of 20 (10 plus his +10 modifier). However, the DC is 23, and the GM tells him that he fails to make progress up the cliff. (His check result is at least high enough that he doesn't fall.) Rorworr can't Take 20 because there is a penalty associated with failure (falling, in this case).

Later, Rorworr finds a small bunker in the cliff-side and searches it. The GM notes in the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill description that each 1-square area takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to search (and she secretly assigns a DC of 15 to the attempt). She estimates that the floors, walls, and ceiling of the bunker make up about twenty squares (about 45 square meters), so she tells Rorworr's player that it takes 2 minutes to search the whole bunker. Rorworr rolls 1d20 and adds his +5 modifier. The result of 11 fails.

Now Rorworr declares that he is going to search the whole bunker high and low, for as long as it takes. The GM takes the original time of 2 minutes and multiples it by 20, for 40 minutes. That's how long it takes Rorworr to search the whole bunker in exacting detail. Now Rorworr's player treats his roll as if it were a 20, for a result of 25. That's more than enough to beat the DC of 15, and Rorworr finds a [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad") discarded in a waste disposal unit.

When more than one character tries the same Skill at the same time and for the same purpose, their efforts may overlap.

### Individual Events

Often, several characters attempt action, and each succeeds or fails on his or her own.

For example, Rorworr and each of his companions need to [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") a slope to get to the top. Regardless of Rorworr's result, the other characters need successful checks too. Every character makes a skill check.

### Cooperation

Sometimes the individual heroes react to the same circumstance, and they can work together to help each other out. In this case, one hero is considered the leader of the effort and makes a skill check while each helper makes a skill check against DC 10. (You can't Take 10 on this check.) For each helper who succeeds, the leader gets a +2 circumstance bonus (as per the rule for Favorable Circumstances). In many cases, a character's help won't be beneficial, or only a limited number of characters can help at the same time. The GM limits cooperation as they see fit for the circumstances.

For instance, if Kelko has been badly wounded, Vor'en Kurn can try a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to keep him from dying. One other character can help Vor'en. If the other character makes a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check against DC 10, then Vor'en gets a +2 bonus on the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check he made to help Kelko. The GM rules that two characters can't help Vor'en at the same time because a third person would get in the way.

Cooperation may not require all characters to make the same skill check. If Kelko and Deel Surool try to find information about illegal business on a Hutt's datapad, only one can make a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check. While Kelko is actually checking the datapad, Deel Surool realizes his [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)") may be able to give hints on what to look for. Surool can make a DC 10 [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)") check to give Kelko a +2 bonus to his [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check.

Some situations may make a Skill easier or harder to use, resulting in a bonus or penalty to the modifier for the Skill Check, or a change to the skill check's DC. It's one thing for Kelko, a [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"), to hunt down enough food to eat while he's camping for the day on [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), using a [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check. Foraging for food while crossing 100 kilometers of Tatooine's Jundland Wastes is an entirely different matter.

The GM can alter the odds of success in four ways to take into account exceptional circumstances:

- Give the Skill user a +2 circumstance bonus to represent circumstances that improve performance, such as having the perfect tool for the job, getting help from another (see Combined Attempts), or possessing unusually accurate information.
- Give the Skill user a -2 circumstance penalty to represent circumstances that hamper performance, such as being forced to use improvised tools or possessing misleading information.
- Reduce the DC by 2 (or assign penalties to an Opposed Check) to represent circumstances that make the task easier, such as having a friendly audience or performing work that doesn't have to be perfect.
- Increase the DC by 2 (or add bonuses to an Opposed Check) to represent circumstances that make the task harder, such as having a hostile audience or performing work that must be flawless.

Circumstances that affect your ability to perform the Skill change your modifier. Circumstances that modify how well you have to perform the Skill to succeed change the DC. A bonus on your modifier and a reduction in the check's DC have the same result- they create a better chance that you will succeed. But they represent different circumstances, and sometimes that difference is important.

### Example

For example, Deel Surool the [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek") [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") wants to befriend a group of [Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan "Trandoshan") thugs drinking in a cantina. Before beginning his performance, Deel listens to the [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") so that he can judge their mood. Doing so improves his chances of taking the right approach when introducing himself, giving him a +2 bonus to the skill modifier for his [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check. The [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") are in a good mood because they recently received a sizeable payoff, so the GM reduces the bonus they receive for an Indifferent [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") to +0. (Deel's attempt at diplomacy isn't better just because the [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") are in a good mood, so he does not gain a bonus to add to his modifier.)

However, the leader of the gang, a [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") bounty hunter, has been unable to locate the [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") he's tracking, and he's suspicious of Deel. (Didn't the datafile suggest the [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") was often seen in the company of a [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek")?) He gains a +2 bonus to his [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") to resist being persuaded (in addition to the normal +2 for being Indifferent).

Deel rolls a 6 and adds +8 for his modifier (including +2 for his impromptu research). His result is 14. The [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") have a [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of 13, so Deel's result is high enough to shift their [Attitudes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitudes "Attitudes") to Friendly, but not their leader ([Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") 16). The [Trandoshans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshans "Trandoshans") applaud Deel Surool and offer to buy him drinks, but their leader eyes him suspiciously.

Some Skill Checks are Opposed Checks. They are made against a randomized number, usually another character's Skill Check result. For example, to sneak up on a guard, you need to beat the guard's [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check result with your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check result. You make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, and the GM makes a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check for the guard. Whoever scores the highest result wins the check.

For ties on Opposed Checks, the character with the higher modifier wins. For instance, if a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check opposed by a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check result in a tie, the sneaker's [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check modifier would be compared to the noticer's [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check modifier. If those scores are the same, roll again.

Some Species Traits, [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and other special abilities allow you to reroll a Skill Check. You must declare that you are using this option immediately after making the check but before any effects are resolved. Furthermore, you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse. For all purposes, the result of the reroll is treated as the real result of your Skill Check.

### Keeping the Better Result

Some Species Traits, [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and other special abilities are more flexible, allowing you to reroll but keep the better of the two results. In most cases, this is more restricted and only available a limited number of times per day (such as the **[Knack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knack "Knack")** Talent) or requires you to spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") (such as the **[Force Power Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Adept "Force Power Adept")** Talent). As always, you must declare that you are using this option immediately after making the skill check but before any effects are resolved.

### Multiple Rerolls

Sometimes you have more than one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), trait, or other special ability that allows you to reroll the same Skill Check. In this case, you may choose to take each reroll one at a time in whatever order you wish, resolving each one before deciding whether to use another.

For example, a [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean") [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") makes an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check. Dissatisfied with the result, he decides to use the Cerean's Intuitive Initiative ability that allows him to reroll his [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, keeping the new result. Unfortunately, the second roll is even worse, so he decides to use the **[Knack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knack "Knack")** Talent to reroll one more time, this time keeping the better of the second and third rolls. Alternatively, he could have opted to use **[Knack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knack "Knack")** first, keeping the better of the first and second rolls, and then (if necessary) using Intuitive Initiative to roll a third time, keeping the third result instead of the better of the first two.

## Order of Rerolls

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

The **[Stalwart Subordinates](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stalwart_Subordinates "Stalwart Subordinates")** Talent introduces a new concept of forcing an enemy to reroll and take the lowest result. This can cause some confusion when the target of this effect has the ability to reroll skill checks. When any reroll effects from different sources clash, resolve the skill check as follows: the character makes its skill check first, including any rerolls it gains from its own abilities. Once the final result of the skill check is determined, apply any reroll effects from enemies.

1. For example, a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") makes a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check against an Officer's ally. The [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") has the ability to reroll the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check, taking the better result.
2. The [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") has the **[Stalwart Subordinates](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stalwart_Subordinates "Stalwart Subordinates")** Talent. The [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") makes their skill check first, rolling twice (a 14 and a 17) to take the better result (17).
3. The **[Stalwart Subordinates](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stalwart_Subordinates "Stalwart Subordinates")** effect then applies, requiring the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") to roll one more time (an 11), taking the lowest result. The Scoundrel's final [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check result is 11.

In general, you can try a Skill Check again if you fail, and you can keep trying indefinitely. Many Skills, however, have natural consequences for failing that must be accounted for. Some Skills can't be tried again once a check has failed for a particular task. For most Skills, when a character has succeeded at a given task, additional successes are meaningless.

For example, if Deel Surool misses a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to open a mechanical lock, he can try again and keep trying. If, however, an alarm sounds when the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check is missed by 5 or more, then failing has its own penalty. Similarly, if Rorworr misses a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check, he can keep trying, but if he misses by 5 or more, he falls (after which he can get up and try again if the fall wasn't too far or too painful).

If a Skill carries no penalty for failure, you can Take 20 and assume that you keep trying until you eventually succeed.
