# Demoralizing Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demoralizing_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demoralizing_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You know how to dishearten your opponents when they least expect it.

**Prerequisite:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

**Effect:** When you successfully deal damage to an opponent with an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you can immediately make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") the opponent as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").
