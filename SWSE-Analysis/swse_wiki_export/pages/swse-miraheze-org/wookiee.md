# Wookiee

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:31Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/4/44/Wookiee.webp/250px-Wookiee.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Wookiee.webp)

Wookiees are widely recognized as one of the strongest and fiercest intelligent [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") in the galaxy. Wookiees have many customs and traditions that revolve around honor and loyalty, including the special bond called the honor family and the sacred pledge called the Life Debt. A Wookiee never uses his or her climbing claws in combat; doing this is considered dishonorable and a sign of madness.

## Wookiee Characteristics

**Personality:** Wookiees tend to be honorable, rash, loyal, and short-tempered.

**Physical Description:** Wookiees are large furry bipeds, with a typical male standing at 2.2 meters tall and weighing 125 kilograms, while a typical female stands at 2.0 meters tall and weighs 100 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Wookiees age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-12 Years | 13-17 Years | 18-300 Years | 301-350 Years | 351-399 Years | 400+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The forest world of [Kashyyyk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kashyyyk "Kashyyyk").

**Languages:** Wookiees speak Shyriiwook, consisting of complex grunts and growls. They understand Basic but lack the ability to speak it.

**Example Names:** Chewbacca, Gorwooken, Groznik, Lowbacca, Ralrra, Rorworr, Salporin, Tarfful.

**Adventurers:** Wookiee adventurers usually start out as wanderers or explorers as [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), but some find this path by forming an honor family with (or owning a Life Debt to) other adventurers.

## Wookiee Species Traits

Wookiees share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Wookiees receive a +4 bonus to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), and +2 bonus to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"), but suffer -2 penalties to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Wookiees are powerful and hardy, but they aren't agile, tend to be impulsive, and have little patience for diplomacy.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Wookiees have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Wookiees have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Expert Climber:** Wookiees are great climbers and may choose to [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") checks, even when distracted or threatened.
- **Extraordinary Recuperation:** A Wookiee regains [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") at double the normal rate (see [Natural Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Healing "Natural Healing")).
- **Intimidating Presence:** Known to pull off the ears of [Gundarks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gundarks "Gundarks"), Wookiees may choose to reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check made to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") others, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Natively Phonetic:** Wookiees can learn to understand, read, and write (but not speak) other [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages").
- **Rage:** Once per day, a Wookiee can fly into a Rage as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). While Raging, the Wookiee temporarily gains a +2 Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls, but cannot use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that require patience and concentration, such as [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"). A fit of Rage lasts for a number of rounds equal to 5 + the Wookiee's [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
  - At the end of its Rage, the Wookiee moves -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The penalties imposed by this [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") persist until the Wookiee takes at least 10 minutes to recuperate, during which time the Wookiee can't engage in any strenuous activity.
- **Weapon Familiarity ([Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster") and [Ryyk Blade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ryyk_Blade "Ryyk Blade")):** Wookiees treat the [Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster") as a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") instead of an [Exotic Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapon (Ranged)"). They additionally treat the [Ryyk Blade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ryyk_Blade "Ryyk Blade") as an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") instead of an [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Wookiees can speak Shyriiwook and understand Basic.
