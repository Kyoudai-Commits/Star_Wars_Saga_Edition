# Force Slam

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You pound one or more creatures with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All targets within a 6-Square [Cone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cone "Cone") and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Make one roll and compare the result to each target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the result equals or exceeds a target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), it takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage and is knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"). If the result is less than a target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), it takes half damage and is not knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"). Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

**Special:** When you use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal an additional 2d6 points of damage to targets in the area.
