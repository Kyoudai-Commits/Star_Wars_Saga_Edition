# Tumble Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can use your melee weapon to hinder acrobatic enemies.

**Effect:** When an opponent attempts to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") through a square you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") with a melee weapon with which you are proficient, add your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") to the DC of the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check made to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble"). If the target fails the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble"), you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against that opponent as normal.

**Special:** You cannot use this feat when you are [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").
