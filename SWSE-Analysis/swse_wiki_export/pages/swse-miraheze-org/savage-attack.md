# Savage Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Savage_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Savage_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are well trained in attacking multiple times in a round.

**Prerequisite:** [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** Choose one [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") you have selected for the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat. When you use a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action and successfully hit the target with your first attack, you deal +1 die of damage on each successful attack remaining in the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") made against that target.
