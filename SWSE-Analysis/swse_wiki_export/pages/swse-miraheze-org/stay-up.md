# Stay Up

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stay_Up](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stay_Up) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can soldier on in spite of damage dealt to you.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when you would normally take damage from an attack, you can instead choose to take half damage and move -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
