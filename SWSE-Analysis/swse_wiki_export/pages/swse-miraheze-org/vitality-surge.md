# Vitality Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vitality_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vitality_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can keep yourself in top fighting form at any point in the fight.

**Prerequisite:** [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind")

**Effect:** You can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") even if you are not at or below half your maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").

**Normal:** Without this feat, you can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") only if you are at or below half your maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").
