# Stava Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stava_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stava_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the Stava unarmed fighting style of the [Noghri](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noghri "Noghri") and can subdue an enemy with ease.

**Prerequisites:** [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack")

**Effect:** When making a [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") attack, you are considered to be one size category larger for the purpose of determining the size of the opponent that you can [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab"). If you are capable of making a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack, you are considered to be one size category larger for the purpose of determining your size modifier to [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks.

When making an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against a target while [Charging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging "Charging") an opponent, if your attack roll is successful, you can make an immediate [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") attack against that target as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain the benefit of this feat.

**Special:** If you have the **[Stava Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stava_Expertise "Stava Expertise")** Talent, you can add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier and your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier to [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks. You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain this special benefit.
