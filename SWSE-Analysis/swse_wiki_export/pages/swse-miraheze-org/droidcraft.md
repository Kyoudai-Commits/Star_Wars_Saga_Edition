# Droidcraft

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droidcraft](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droidcraft) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can repair [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") quickly.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** You can use the [Repair Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Droid "Repair Droid") aspect of the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill in 10 minutes.

**Normal:** Using [Repair Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Droid "Repair Droid") typically takes 1 hour.
