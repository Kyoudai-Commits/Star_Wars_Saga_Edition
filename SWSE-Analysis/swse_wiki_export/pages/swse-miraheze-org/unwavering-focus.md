# Unwavering Focus

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unwavering_Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unwavering_Focus) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Others might try to sway you, but once you focus on an objective, you cannot be stopped.

**Prerequisite:** [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak")

**Effect:** Whenever you are targeted by a Mind-Affecting effect that requires a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") against your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can impose a -2 penalty to that [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check").
