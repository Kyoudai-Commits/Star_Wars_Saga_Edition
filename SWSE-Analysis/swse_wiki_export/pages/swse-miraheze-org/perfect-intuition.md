# Perfect Intuition

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfect_Intuition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfect_Intuition) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your insights serve you well, giving you some of the fastest reaction times among members of your Species.

**Prerequisite:** [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.
