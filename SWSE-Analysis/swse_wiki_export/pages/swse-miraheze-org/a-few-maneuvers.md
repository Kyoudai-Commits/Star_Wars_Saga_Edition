# A Few Maneuvers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/A_Few_Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/A_Few_Maneuvers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

You can weave, juke, and roll to avoid enemy fire in the thick of combat.

**Prerequisites:** [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge"), [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")

**Effect:** When piloting a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller, you grant a +2 dodge bonus to your Vehicle's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). Furthermore, if you are the target of a [Missile or Torpedo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Missile_or_Torpedo "Missile or Torpedo") and the attack roll misses by 5 or more, the projectile self-destructs harmlessly.
