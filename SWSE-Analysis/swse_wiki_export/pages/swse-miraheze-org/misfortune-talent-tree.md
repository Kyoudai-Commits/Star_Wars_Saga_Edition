# Misfortune Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your mother always said you were trouble. Now, your enemies know it, too.

## Core Talents

### Dastardly Strike

Whenever you make a successful attack against an opponent that is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). This Talent can be used only against characters, not objects or [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles").

### Disruptive

By spending two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions"), you can use your knack for causing trouble and instigating chaos to disrupt your enemies. Until the start of your next turn, you suppress all morale and insight bonuses applied to enemies in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

### Skirmisher

If you move at least 2 squares before you attack and end your move in a different square from where you started, you gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls until the start of your next turn. You gain the benefit of this Talent with [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems") only if you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot").

### Sneak Attack

Any time your opponent is [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") or otherwise denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you deal an additional 1d6 points of damage with a successful melee or ranged attack. You must be within 6 squares of the target to make a **Sneak Attack** with a ranged weapon. You may select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, your **Sneak Attack** damage increases by +1d6 (Maximum +10d6).

### Walk the Line

*Prerequisite:* **Disruptive**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can do or say something that catches your enemies off guard. All opponents within 6 squares of you, and within your line of sight take a -2 penalty to their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the start of your next turn. The penalty is negated if [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is broken. This Talent has a range of 6 squares at [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale").

## Additional Talents

### Backstabber

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisite:* **Sneak Attack**

You can take advantage of your adversary's distractions, no matter how momentary or fleeting. Once per turn, when you [Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank "Flank") a target, you can treat that target as if they were [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") for one of your attacks.

### Befuddle

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

If you succeed on a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check against a target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), until the start of your next turn you can move though the [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area") of that target as part of your [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"). Each [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") square that you move through counts as 2 squares of movement.

### Cunning Strategist

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Disruptive**, **Walk the Line**

You can create opportunities to chip away at our opponents' defenses. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *Create Opening:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If you damage the target, the target takes a -5 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.
- *Crippling Attack:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). Until the start of your next turn, the target takes a -2 penalty to their base speed.
- *Vicious Attack:* Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") against two opponents that are within 2 squares of each other. Make separate attack rolls at a -5 penalty against each target, but roll damage once only.

### Hesitate

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can fill your opponent with doubt by making a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") against a single target that can hear and understand you within 12 squares of you. If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes a -2 penalty to its base speed, and if the target takes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), it must also spend its [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

This penalty lasts until the end of the target's next turn.

### Improved Skirmisher

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Skirmisher**

When you move at least 2 squares before your attack and end your move in a different square from where you started, you gain a +1 bonus to all your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the start of your next turn.

### Improved Sneak Attack

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisites:* **Sneak Attack**, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

You can use the **Sneak Attack** Talent against a target within 12 squares, instead of within 6 squares.

### Seducer

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You excel at seduction through deception. If you fail a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to change a target's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"), you can immediately reroll the check using your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill in lieu of your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill. You must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Seize Object

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Once per encounter as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can attempt to seize a held, carried, or worn object from an adjacent target by making a [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attack, with a +10 bonus on your attack roll. If the attack succeeds, you are now holding the object, and you cannot use this Talent in place of the [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") Action. You cannot conceal the use of this Talent from the target.

### Sow Confusion

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Hesitate**

Once per encounter, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check and compare the result to the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of all enemies in your line of sight. If the check result equals or exceeds an enemy's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that enemy must spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") in addition to a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to make an attack until the start of your next turn.

### Stymie

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Once per round, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a target within 12 squares of you and in your line of sight as the target of this Talent. Until the beginning of your turn, you can cause that target to take a -5 penalty on all checks made with a single [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). You must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target to make use of this Talent, and declare which [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") is to be penalized at the time this Talent is activated. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Sudden Strike

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Skirmisher**, **Sneak Attack**

Whenever you would gain the benefit of the **Skirmisher** Talent and you successfully hit your opponent, you deal **Sneak Attack** damage in addition to the normal damage dealt by the attack.

### Weakening Strike

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Dastardly Strike**

Whenever you deal damage to an opponent denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you can choose not to move the target down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and instead impose a -5 penalty on all your opponent's attack and melee damage rolls until the end of your next turn.
