# Bone Crusher

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Crusher](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Crusher) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are skilled at dealing lasting damage to [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") opponents.

**Prerequisites:** [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush"), [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin")

**Effect:** When you deal damage to a [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") opponent, the opponent also moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
