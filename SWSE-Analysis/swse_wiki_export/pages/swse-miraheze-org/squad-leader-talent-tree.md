# Squad Leader Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Squad_Leader_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Squad_Leader_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You are a capable military leader who can issue orders to a squad of soldiers with poise and efficiency.

## Core Talents

### Commanding Officer

You gain a single [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower"). Choose either the [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower"), [Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower"), or [Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower"), generating the follower's statistics using the [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") rules. This [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") gains one Armor Proficiency feat of your choice and [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)"), in addition to those provided by the [Follower Templates](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower_Templates "Follower Templates"). The [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") must meet the prerequisites for the Armor Proficiency feat you select.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do, you gain one additional [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") (maximum of three [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers")).

### Coordinated Tactics

*Prerequisite:* **Commanding Officer**

Each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") gains the [Coordinated Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack "Coordinated Attack") feat, provided he or she meets the prerequisite. If your [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") later meets the prerequisite for the feat, they gain the feat at that time.

### Fire at Will

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, **Commanding Officer**

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you and one of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") can make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against one target (each) in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You each take a -5 penalty to your attack rolls.

### Squad Actions

*Prerequisite:* **Commanding Officer**

You and your squad have learned to work together as a team, and have an established set of tactics that you have practiced to perfection. You can use any of the following actions on your turn.

- *Autofire Barrage:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack against legal target spaces. For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") who is armed with a ranged weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") and has a [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the area targeted by your [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), you can designate one additional square as targeted by your [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") (that square must be adjacent to your original target area).
- *Open Fire:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a single target. For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") who is armed with a ranged weapon and has a [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, add +2 to your damage roll on a successful hit.
- *Painted Target:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a single target. You gain a competence bonus on your attack roll equal to the number of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") who are armed with a ranged weapon and have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target. Thus, if you have three armed [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") with [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, you gain a +3 competence bonus on your attack roll.
