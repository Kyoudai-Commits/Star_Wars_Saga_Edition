# Critical Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

At the cost of speed, you can make a devastating attack.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +9, [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Melee Weapon)

**Effect:** While wielding a weapon for which you possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat, you can take two consecutive [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to increase the critical threat range of your next [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") by 1.

Thus, if a weapon scores a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") on a Natural 20, it can score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") on a natural roll of 19 or 20 instead. However, any roll other than a Natural 20 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll a Natural 19 and miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"). You lose the benefits of Critical Strike if you lose [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target or if you take any other Action before making your attack.
