# Surveillance Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surveillance_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surveillance_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have mastered the art of surveillance, transforming the information you gather with scouting into a distinct advantage on the battlefield.

## Core Talents

### Advanced Intel

*Prerequisite:* **Spotter**

If you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") at the beginning of combat, you can use the **Spotter** Talent as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your first turn, including during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round").

### Hidden Eyes

If you have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from a target, you gain a +5 circumstance bonus on all [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks made against that target.

### Hunt the Hunter

When you use a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to actively look for hidden enemies, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against any one enemy you notice with your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check.

### Seek and Destroy

*Prerequisite:* **Hidden Eyes**

If you make a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack against a target that is unaware of you, that target cannot make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check to notice you until after the attack is resolved, even if you move away from [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") or [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment").

### Spotter

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check with a DC equal to 10 + the CL of a single target enemy in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If you succeed on the check, you and all your allies that can hear and understand you gain a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls against that target until the end of your next turn.
