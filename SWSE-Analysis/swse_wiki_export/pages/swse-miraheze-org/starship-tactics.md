# Starship Tactics

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are trained to make use of [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") and are skilled at space combat.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"), [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")

**Effect:** You add to your [Starship Maneuver Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver_Suite "Starship Maneuver Suite") a number of [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1). You can add the same [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") more than once.

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, you add to your [Starship Maneuver Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver_Suite "Starship Maneuver Suite") a number of new [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1). A character with the [Gunnery Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunnery_Specialist "Gunnery Specialist") feat can take this feat without meeting the other requirements but if they do not possess the other requirements they can only select [*Gunner*] [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") when they select this feat.
