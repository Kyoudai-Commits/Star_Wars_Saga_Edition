# Logic Upgrade: Skill Swap

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Skill_Swap](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Skill_Swap) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You are able to quickly swap one programmed [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") for another. A [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with a [Basic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basic_Processor "Basic Processor") can use this trick to enable it to attempt [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") in which it is [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained").

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Basic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basic_Processor "Basic Processor")

**Effect:** Select a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you are not [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in (other than [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")). As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can swap this [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") for one you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in. You do not gain any benefit from the original [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") while it is swapped out. You are not considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the swapped-in [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), and therefore you cannot use any [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained")-only options or add a bonus to your [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). You still add one-half your level and your associated [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") bonus, as usual.

**Special:** If you become [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") selected for this feat, you can select another [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to replace it. You can take this feat more than once, each time selecting a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to swap.
