# Bothan Will

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan_Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan_Will) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You epitomize the legendary willpower and resolve of the [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") people.

**Prerequisite:** [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan")

**Effect:** Whenever an attack or [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") targets your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") and fails to equal or exceed it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the start of your next turn.
