# Resurgence

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resurgence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resurgence) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Catching your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") allows you to seize the advantage in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** When you catch your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you immediately gain a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to be used immediately.
