# Fold Space

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to bend space, transporting an object almost instantaneously from one place to another.

**Time:** [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")

**Targets:** One held or unattended object within 6 squares and [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), or one [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy. (If the object you are transporting contains other objects or creatures (such as a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship")), everything contained within the object is transported as well.)

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the maximum size of the object you can transport, and the distance you can transport it:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You can move a Medium or smaller object up to 6 squares. |
| 25 | You can move a Large or smaller object up to 12 squares. |
| 30 | You can move a Huge or smaller object up to 18 squares. |
| 35 | You can move a Gargantuan or smaller object up to 24 squares. |
| 40 | You can move a Colossal or smaller object up to 30 squares. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the maximum size of the object you can move by two categories, up to Colossal (Cruiser) in size, or double the distance the object is transported.
