# Alter Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alter_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alter_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") grants you considerable power over your environment as well as others around you.

## Core Talents

### Disciplined Strike

Whenever you use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that has an area of effect (such as *[Force Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam "Force Slam")*), you may exclude a certain number of targets from the effects of that power. The number of targets you may exclude in this manner is equal to your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum of 1).

### Telekinetic Power

Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate a Power with the [*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*] descriptor, you may choose to use that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") again immediately as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). You may direct the second use of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against any eligible target.

### Telekinetic Savant

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you may return one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*] descriptor to your suite without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You may take this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, you may use this Talent one additional time per encounter.

## Additional Talents

### Aversion

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to radiate an invisible aura that makes other beings want to avoid you. Until the end of the encounter, all squares within 2 squares of you are considered [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") for your enemies. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

### Force Flow

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

For reasons unknown to you, [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force") flows through you in an irregular fashion. Whenever you roll a natural 1 on an attack roll or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If you do not spend this [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Illusion

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to create an **Illusion** that seems perfectly real to anyone who views it. You designate the form and complexity of the **Illusion**, as well as its size and location, and make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. (The Illusion's size also affects your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, applying a penalty for exceptionally large **Illusions**. The penalties are -1 for Huge **Illusions**, -2 for Gargantuan **Illusions**, -5 for Colossal **Illusions**, and -10 for Colossal (Frigate) or larger **Illusions**. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.)

When a creature views the **Illusion**, compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"); if your check result exceeds it's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), it believes the **Illusion** to be real. Any physical interaction with the **Illusion** (such as touching it, passing through it, or shooting it with a blaster) immediately reveals the true nature of the **Illusion**, and the creature is no longer deceived. The **Illusion** lasts for a number of minutes equal to your Heroic Level.

### Illusion Bond

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* **Illusion**

Whenever you create an **Illusion** of a humanoid using the Illusion Talent, you are able to see and hear as though you were standing in the space occupied by your **Illusion**.

### Influence Savant

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can return one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] descriptor to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, you can use it one additional time per encounter.

### Link

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can lend your strength in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to another character, creating a bond between you through which [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") flows. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can designate one willing ally within 12 squares of you and in your line of sight. This ally must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill.

As long as you remain within 12 squares of each other, you can aid each other on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). However, the character using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Reaction takes a -5 penalty on all [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the end of their next turn. This **Link** lasts until the end of the encounter, or until you or your ally ends the **Link** (a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")). You can have only one active **Link** at a time.

### Masquerade

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* **Illusion**

You can use the **Illusion** Talent to create a disguise for yourself. You use the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made to create the **Masquerade** for the purposes of creating a [Deceptive Appearance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearance "Deceptive Appearance"), as per the application of the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill. All other rules and restrictions for using the **Illusion** Talent still apply.

### Move Massive Object

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Telekinetic Power**, *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")*

When you successfully use the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power to move an object of Large size or bigger, you can make an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with the object instead of throwing it at or dropping it on a single target, as normal for the Force Power. You must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate the Force Power to do this, and the area you target is based on the size of the object: Large, 2x2; Huge, 3x3; Gargantuan, 4x4; Colossal and larger, 6x6.

When you use *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* to make an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with the object you are moving, you compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of each creature, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), and [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") in the target area. If your check equals or exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), it takes damage from the object (as determined by the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power). Otherwise, the target takes half damage. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

### Suppress Force

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisites:* **Influence Savant**, *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")*

You can convince others that they have been cut off from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), even if that is not the case, preventing them from making [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks. Whenever a target with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 3 or higher within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") attempts to make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check for any reason, you can spend one use of the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* Force Power as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

You make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, and if your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result, that target's skill check is negated, and the action it was attempting fails.

### Telekinetic Prodigy

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Telekinetic Savant**

When you take the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat and select *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* as one of your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), you can also select one extra [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to add to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") for free. This extra [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") must contain the [*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*] descriptor.

You can only gain one extra [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") each time you take the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat, regardless of how many times you choose the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power. If you increase your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score at a later time and select the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* power, you only gain an additional power if you did not take the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* power when you first chose that feat. Otherwise, you gain an additional [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as normal.

### Telepathic Influence

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Telepathic Link**

You naturally and unconsciously influence those who are regularly around you. Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on an attack roll or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, instead of regaining all your spent [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), you may instead choose to grant one ally within 12 squares of you a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If your ally does not use this temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Telepathic Link

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You form an enhanced **Telepathic Link** with a willing ally, that possesses the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). The **Telepathic Link** is maintained until you choose to remove it (no Action required). As long as you remain within one kilometer of your target, you and the target can communicate telepathically as though you were speaking. Once per encounter, you may use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") from your target's [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (if the target consents), or you may allow the target to use one of your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). You may only have one **Telepathic Link** active at a time.
