# Strong Bellow

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strong_Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strong_Bellow) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can use your lungs to [Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bellow "Bellow") without growing fatigued.

**Prerequisite:** [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian")

**Effect:** Once per encounter when you use your [Bellow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bellow "Bellow") ability, you move one less step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
