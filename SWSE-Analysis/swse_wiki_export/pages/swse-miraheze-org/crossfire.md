# Crossfire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crossfire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crossfire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are skilled at aiming your shots so that they have a chance of hitting more than one target.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** If you miss when making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target that has "soft" [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") (that is, [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") provided by another character, creature, or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")), you can immediately make an attack roll (with the same weapon and at the same attack bonus) against the target that is providing the "soft" [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). You can only use this feat once per round.
