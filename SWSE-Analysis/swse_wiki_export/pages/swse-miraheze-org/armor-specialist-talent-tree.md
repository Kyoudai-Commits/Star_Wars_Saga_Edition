# Armor Specialist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can maximize the benefits of wearing [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") while reducing or eliminating some of its drawbacks.

## Core Talents

### Armor Mastery

*Prerequisite:* **Armored Defense**

The maximum [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus of your armor improves by +1. You must be proficient with the armor you are wearing to gain this benefit.

### Armored Defense

When calculating your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you may add either your Heroic Level or your Armor bonus, whichever is higher. You must be proficient with the armor you are wearing to gain this benefit.

### Improved Armored Defense

*Prerequisite:* **Armored Defense**

When calculating your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you may add your Heroic Level plus one-half your Armor bonus (rounded down), or your Armor bonus, whichever is higher. You must be proficient with the armor you are wearing to gain this benefit.

### Juggernaut

*Prerequisite:* **Armored Defense**

Your armor does not reduce your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") or the distance you can move while [Running](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running "Running"). You must be proficient with the armor you are wearing to gain this benefit.

### Second Skin

*Prerequisite:* **Armored Defense**

When wearing armor with which you are proficient, your Armor bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") each increases by +1.

## Additional Talents

### Shield Expert

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)")

You are an expert in using [Energy Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Shields "Energy Shields") for maximum effectiveness. Once per encounter, you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to regain 10 points of SR (up to the shield's maximum) on an active [Energy Shield](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Shield "Energy Shield").
