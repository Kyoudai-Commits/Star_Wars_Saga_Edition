# Intimidator

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidator) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

When you [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") others, either physically or verbally, you shake their confidence so severely that it affects their performance.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

**Effect:** When you successfully use the [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") application of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill against a creature, instead of the normal results, the creature takes a -5 penalty to all [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") (including [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")) and a -2 penalty to all attacks when you are within its [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), until the end of your next turn.

If aboard another [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), your enemy must be able to see or detect your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

**Special:** You cannot use this feat with the [Maniacal Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Maniacal_Charge "Maniacal Charge") feat.
