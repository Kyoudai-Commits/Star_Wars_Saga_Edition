# Hideous Visage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hideous_Visage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hideous_Visage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can twist your features into a startling mask.

**Prerequisite:** [Shapeshift](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapeshift "Shapeshift")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check against one opponent that can see you. If the check equals or exceeds the opponent's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you move the target 1 square away from you and it takes a -1 penalty on all attacks until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.
