# Triple Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You can make an additional attack during a round of combat.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +11, [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"). When you use the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you may make one additional attack when wielding such a weapon. However, you take a -5 penalty on all attack rolls until your next turn because you're trading precision for speed. The extra attack and penalty stack with those of [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack"). If you take this feat with [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"), it works normally with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").

**Normal:** Making a single attack is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

**Special:** You may select this feat multiple times. Each time you select this feat, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

## Multiple Attacks with Multiple Targets

**Q:** Can you use [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") or Triple Attack to attack the same target multiple times in the same round, or do you have to attack different targets?

**A:** The extra attacks from [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack"), Triple Attack, and fighting with multiple weapons can be directed at the same target or at different targets, in any combination and any order that you choose.

## Double and Rapid Attacks

**Q:** It is possible to use [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") with [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike")?

**A:** Yes. [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), and [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") all can be used when making multiple attacks, regardless of their source ([Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack"), Triple Attack, or fighting with multiple weapons).
