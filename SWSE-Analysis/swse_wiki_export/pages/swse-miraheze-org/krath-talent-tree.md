# Krath Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krath_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Krath_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") specialize in experimenting with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and Sith sorcery. Only Force-users that are part of [The Krath](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Krath "The Krath") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Dark Side Manipulation

Your Sith sorcery experimentation has provided you with a method of manipulating [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). Once per encounter, when using a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") in an act that would increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"), you may treat the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as if you had rolled the maximum on the die.

### Krath Illusions

*Prerequisite:* **[Illusion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illusion "Illusion")**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can reduce the penalty for large **[Illusions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illusions "Illusions")** by one half (rounded down, minimum -1).

### Krath Intuition

You have a natural ability to use Dark Side artifacts, such as [Sith Talismans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Talismans "Sith Talismans") and alchemical items. Once per encounter, you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to treat the damage from a [Sith Alchemical Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemical_Weapon "Sith Alchemical Weapon") as though you had rolled the maximum damage on the dice.

### Krath Surge

Once per encounter, using rudimentary Sith sorcery, you channel [Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side") energy in a manner that boosts one use of a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can add 1 die of damage (if the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") deals damage) or extend the range of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") by 6 squares (if it has a range beyond yourself or a single target). Using this Talent automatically adds the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor to the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") used.
