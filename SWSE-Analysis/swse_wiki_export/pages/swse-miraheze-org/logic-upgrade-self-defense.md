# Logic Upgrade: Self-Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Self-Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Logic_Upgrade:_Self-Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can use your acquired knowledge and experiences to increase your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses").

**Prerequisite:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can grant yourself a +2 morale bonus to the [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") of your choice until the end of your next turn.
