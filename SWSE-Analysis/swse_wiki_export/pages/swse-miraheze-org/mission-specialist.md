# Mission Specialist

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mission_Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mission_Specialist) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are so good with certain [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that others around you benefit from your guidance and example.

**Effect:** Choose one [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") in which you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained"). Allies who are not [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") gain a +2 competence bonus to the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") while they are within 12 squares of you.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you select this feat, it applies to a different [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). Only allies who have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat gain this bonus to [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks.
