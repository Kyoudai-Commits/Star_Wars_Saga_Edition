# Shield Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can surge the power of a Vehicle's shields through your direct data link at precisely the right moment to reduce the damage to the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") from incoming enemy fire.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or [Cyborg Hybrid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cyborg_Hybrid "Cyborg Hybrid"), [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes damage above its [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"), after the [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is reduced you can reduce the amount of damage sustained by the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") by an amount up to the remaining [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"). However, the [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is immediately reduced by one point for every point of damage reduced.

**Special:** The [Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields") Action cannot be used on the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") until a full round after the use of Shield Surge. You must have a [Scomp Link](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scomp_Link "Scomp Link") or similar direct data link to the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to use this feat.
