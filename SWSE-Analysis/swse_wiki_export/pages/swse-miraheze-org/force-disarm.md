# Force Disarm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disarm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent by using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to pull the weapon from their grasp.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares, and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Use this check in place of your attack roll when attempting to [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") the target. If your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt succeeds, you may choose to let the item drop to the ground in the target's fighting space or have the item fly into your hand (in which case you must have a free hand to catch it).

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to damage or destroy the target weapon instead. If your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt succeeds, the weapon takes damage equal to your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result. You must declare that you are using this option before making your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt. If you have the [Improved Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm "Improved Disarm") feat, you gain the +5 bonus on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate *Force Disarm* as though you were making an attack roll
