# Zeison Sha Warrior Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zeison_Sha_Warrior_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zeison_Sha_Warrior_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Zeison Sha talented enough to become Warriors focus their powers on manipulating the objects around them. They are also masters of their signature weapon: the [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade"). Only Force-users that are part of [The Zeison Sha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Zeison_Sha "The Zeison Sha") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Discblade Arc

*Prerequisite:* Proficient with [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade")

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with your [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade"), striking three targets, all of which must be within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") for your [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade"). This attack uses the [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") rules; you make one attack roll and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of every target you designate.

### Distant Discblade Throw

*Prerequisite:* Proficient with [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade")

When you use a [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade"), you treat it as a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") (instead of a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon")) for the purpose of determining [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

### Recall Discblade

*Prerequisite:* Proficient with [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade")

When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade") (or use the **Discblade Arc** Talent above), after the attack is resolved you can make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to call the weapon back to your hand as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Telekinetic Vigilance

*Prerequisite:* *[Intercept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intercept "Intercept")*

You can return the *[Intercept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intercept "Intercept")* Force Power to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Weapon Specialization (Discblade)

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Discblade)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Discblade) "Weapon Focus (Discblade)")

You gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls with your [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade").

### Discblade Mastery

*Discblade Mastery Talent created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

You treat a [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade") as a [Simple Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon "Simple Weapon") that can be [Thrown](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown "Thrown"), instead of as an [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"). Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with a [Discblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Discblade "Discblade"). This Talent counts as the [Weapon Focus (Discblade)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Discblade) "Weapon Focus (Discblade)") Feat for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. If you also have the [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)") Feat, the attack bonus provided by this Talent does not stack with the attack bonus provided by [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)").
