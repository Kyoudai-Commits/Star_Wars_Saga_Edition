# Shockboxer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the [Shockboxing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing "Shockboxing") fighting style and can use your fists to devastate your enemies. Although you trained for competitive [Shockboxing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing "Shockboxing"), much of your expertise can also be applied outside the ring.

## Core Talents

### Defensive Jab

*Prerequisite:* **Retaliation Jab**

When you are [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") and take the [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") Action, you can make a single [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") against an adjacent target.

### Nimble Dodge

If an enemy misses you with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares, but you must end your movement adjacent to your attacker.

### Retaliation Jab

If an enemy misses you with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can automatically deal damage equal to your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier (minimum 1 point of damage) to your attacker, if the attacker is within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

### Stinging Jab

When you hit a target with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can choose to deal half damage with your attack. If you do so, your enemy also deals half damage on all [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") he or she makes until the end of your next turn.

### Stunning Shockboxer

*Prerequisite:* **Stinging Jab**

When you deal [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage to a target with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), after the [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") is halved, roll one extra die of damage and add that to the damage subtracted from the target's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").
