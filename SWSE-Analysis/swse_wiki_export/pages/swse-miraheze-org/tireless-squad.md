# Tireless Squad

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tireless_Squad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tireless_Squad) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies train for combat scenarios in which your ability to hang in for the long haul is the difference between victory and defeat.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check, you provide a bonus of +4 instead of +2.
