# Jedi Consular Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Consular_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Consular_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") that follow the path of the consular are skilled ambassadors. You prefer to use the strength of your words and the wisdom that [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") provides to solve problems.

## Core Talents

### Adept Negotiator

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can weaken the resolve of one opponent with your words. The target must have an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 3 or higher, and it must be able to see, hear, and understand you. Make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check; if the result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), it moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The target gets a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") if it is higher level than you.

If the target reaches the end of its [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), it does not fall unconscious, instead, it cannot attack you or your allies for the remainder of the encounter unless you or one of your allies attacks it or one of its allies first. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

You affect the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") of the specific character (usually the Vehicle's [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander")), not a Starship.

### Force Persuasion

*Prerequisite:* **Adept Negotiator**

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") modifier instead of your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check modifier when making a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill. If you are entitled to a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Master Negotiator

*Prerequisite:* **Adept Negotiator**

If you successfully use the **Adept Negotiator** Talent, your target moves an additional -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect. You affect the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") of the specific character (usually the Vehicle's [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander")), not a Starship.

### Skilled Advisor

You can spend a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") advising an ally, thereby granting them a +5 bonus on their next [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). If you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), the bonus increases to +10. The target must be able to (and willing) to hear and understand your voice. You cannot advise yourself. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

## Additional Talents

### Adversary Lore

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can peer into [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and search for weaknesses in the defenses of your enemies. Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of a target creature within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

If the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that target takes a -2 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against you and all allies who can hear and understand you until the end of your next turn.

### Aggressive Negotiator

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Adept Negotiator**

Whenever you damage an opponent with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks you make before the end of your next turn, even if you would not normally be able to.

### Cleanse Mind

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can remove one ongoing [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect (such as the effects of **[Demand Surrender](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Demand_Surrender "Demand Surrender")** or **[Weaken Resolve](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weaken_Resolve "Weaken Resolve")** Talents, or the effect of being moved to the end of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by the **Adept Negotiator** Talent, or the ongoing effects of the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* Force Power) from a single allied target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

### Collective Visions

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

When you use *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, or a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that has *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* as a prerequisite, other Force-users with *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* in their [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") can [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), if they are within 6 squares of you.

### Consular's Vitality

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Once per round as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you grant one ally within 12 squares of you (and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight")) [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier. These [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") last until the beginning of your next turn (at which point any remaining [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") are lost), and any damage dealt to that ally comes out of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first.

You take a -5 penalty on all [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the beginning of your next turn.

### Consular's Wisdom

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Adept Negotiator**

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can choose one ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") that can hear and understand you. Until the end of the encounter, that ally adds your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects.

### Entreat Aid

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to let one adjacent ally use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action (as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")) to assist you with a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"). You must make the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") before the end of your turn, or the benefit of the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action is lost.

An ally that has already used the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist you since the end of your last turn may not be targeted by this Talent.

### Force of Will

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You gain a +2 insight bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). Also, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to give all allies within 6 squares of you a +2 insight bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") that lasts for the remainder of the encounter. This bonus does not extend to allies outside the range of the effect, even if they move within 6 squares of you later on.

Allies who benefit from this Talent must remain within 6 squares of you to retain the insight bonus, and they lose it if you are knocked unconscious or killed. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

### Guiding Strikes

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

When you deal damage to a target by making a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack on your turn, you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") before the end of your turn to activate this Talent. If you do so, allies adjacent to the target at the time you make the attack gain a +2 circumstance bonus to melee attack rolls against the target until the start of your next turn.

### Improved Consular's Vitality

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Consular's Vitality**

Whenever you damage a target with a successful [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, you may use the **Consular's Vitality** Talent as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") instead of a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") until the start of your next turn.

### Know Weakness

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* **Adversary Lore**

Whenever you use **Adversary Lore** on a target successfully, that target also takes an additional 1d6 points of damage from any successful attack made against it by you or an ally who can hear and understand you until the end of your next turn.

### Recall Power

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to return a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"), you regain two [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") instead of one.

### Renew Vision

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

Once per encounter, you can regain all expended uses of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Visionary Attack

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, **WatchCircle Initiate**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check after you or an ally within 12 squares misses with a melee or ranged attack, removing one use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (as though you had activated the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power")). If your check result equals or exceeds the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of the target of that missed attack, the attacker can reroll the missed attack roll.

This counts as using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power against the target, but this Talent replaces the normal rules and effects of *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*. Any attack can only be affected by this Talent once (thus, multiple characters cannot use this Talent on the same attack to allow multiple rerolls).

You take a cumulative -5 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the beginning of your next turn when you use this Talent.

### Visionary Defense

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*, **WatchCircle Initiate**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check after you or an ally within 12 squares is the target of a melee or ranged attack (but before the results of the attack roll are known), removing one use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (as though you had activated the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power")).

If your check result equals or exceeds the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of the attacker, you grant the target of that attack a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against that attack.

This counts as using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power against the attacker, but this Talent replaces the normal rules and effects of *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*. Any attack can only be affected by this Talent once (thus, multiple characters cannot use this Talent on the same attack to allow multiple rerolls).

You take a cumulative -5 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the beginning of your next turn when you use this Talent.

### WatchCircle Initiate

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and remove one use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (as though you had just activated the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power")). If successful, you subtract 1 from your [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") total (this cannot be subtracted from temporary [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points"), and does not count as spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point")), and add 1 to the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") total of an ally within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

This counts as using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power against the target, but this Talent replaces the normal rules and effects of *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*.
