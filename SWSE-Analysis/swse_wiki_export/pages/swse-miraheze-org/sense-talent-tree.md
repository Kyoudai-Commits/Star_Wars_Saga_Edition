# Sense Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Your attunement to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") grants you uncanny powers of perception.

## Core Talents

### Force Perception

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead of a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check to [Avoid Surprise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Avoid_Surprise "Avoid Surprise"), [Notice Targets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Notice_Targets "Notice Targets"), [Sense Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Deception "Sense Deception"), or [Sense Influence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Influence "Sense Influence"). You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill. If you are entitled to a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Force Pilot

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check modifier instead of your [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check modifier when making [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill. If you are entitled to a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Foresight

*Prerequisite:* **Force Perception**

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reroll an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, keeping the better of the two rolls. Additionally, if you roll a Natural 20 on the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check reroll, you immediately regain the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") spent to activate this talent.

### Gauge Force Potential

*Prerequisite:* **Force Perception**

By focusing on a specific creature in your line of sight, you can gauge how strong in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") they are. This takes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and requires a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If your check result meets or beats the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you know whether or not they have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat, you know how many [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") they know (but not which ones specifically), and you know how many [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") they currently have.

### Visions

*Prerequisites:* **Force Perception**, *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

Whenever you use the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to see into the target's past or future instead of the present. You declare how far into the target's past or future you wish to look, up to a maximum of 1 year per your Character Level. Any information gained about a target's future is subject to change, depending on whether steps are taken to alter that future.

*Many young Jedi with the ability to gain glimpses of the past, present, or future often become concerned with the best way to utilize this information. Having such a vision might reveal information hitherto unknown by the rest of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and might contain personal, embarrassing, or even dangerous knowledge. A Padawan should be mindful of another being's feelings and privacy, but discovering information that disturbs [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") should be acted upon. When in doubt, turn to your Master to determine the best course of action.*

## Additional Talents

### Feel the Force

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to ignore all [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") for 1 minute.

### Force Reflexes

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Force Pilot**, [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics")

When activating a [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reroll your [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, keeping the better of the two results.

### Heightened Awareness

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus to your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check. You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, you add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus an additional time.

### Instinctive Navigation

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* **Force Pilot**

You can substitute your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill for any [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check made to [Astrogate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogate "Astrogate") or [Use Sensors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Sensors "Use Sensors") while you are a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot").

### Motion of the Future

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisites:* **Force Perception**, *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can peer into the future in search of signs of danger, removing one use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (as though you had just activated the Force Power). Any time before the end of your next turn when you are the target of an attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can force your attacker to reroll the attack roll (or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check) against you, keeping the second result. This counts as using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power against the attacker, but this Talent replaces the normal rules and effect of that Force Power.

### Psychometry

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Force Perception**, *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

When you use the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power, you can choose to target an object you hold instead of a character or creature. You can look into the targeted item's past, up to a maximum of 5 years per your Character Level. Any information gained about the object's past is based on the thoughts and emotions of the person holding or carrying the object at the time you perceive, which can skew the results of the vision.

### Shift Sense

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision") for 1 minute or until the end of the encounter, whichever is longer.
