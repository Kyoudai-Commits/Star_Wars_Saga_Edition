# Warrior Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warrior_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warrior_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Warriors draw upon their natural aggressiveness, powerful bodies, and fighting abilities to defeat their enemies. Whether they fight with primitive or advanced weaponry, they are fearsome combatants.

### Champion Warrior

*Prerequisites:* **Warrior's Awareness**, **Warrior's Determination**

You fight for protection, honor, or glory for yourself and others. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Champion's Pride:* Your pride and dedication drives you on in the most difficult moments. When you use your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you move +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and remove one Fear effect or Mind-Affecting effect in addition to the normal benefit of [Second Winds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Winds "Second Winds").
- *Disarming Hit:* When you hit and damage a creature and that damage equals or exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you can make a [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attack against that target as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). If using a ranged weapon, you must also have the **[Ranged Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Disarm "Ranged Disarm")** Talent.
- *Masterful Strike:* You land an impressive blow against your enemy. When making a successful [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") or [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") (except when using a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")), increase your damage by 2 points for every 5 points by which your attack roll exceeds your target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

### Quick Study

*Prerequisite:* **Warrior's Awareness**

You excel at turning an enemy's tricks against it. Once per encounter, if an enemy attacks you using a non-[Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent"), you can use the same Talent against it on your next turn. You must use an appropriate weapon or item, if required by the Talent, although you do not have to have the prerequisites. You can take this action even if your enemy's attack misses you.

### Simple Opportunity

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

You can make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") when using a [Simple Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Ranged) "Simple Weapon (Ranged)") against a single target. [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades"), as well as other [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") Simple Weapons, can not be used with this Talent.

### Warrior's Awareness

You learn your enemy's preferred tactics quickly. When an enemy character makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against you for at least the second time in an encounter, make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). If the check is successful, you gain a +1 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against that character until the end of the encounter.

You can use this against only one character at a time, until that character is incapacitated or killed, or until you voluntarily switch targets by dropping the use of this Talent for one full round (you can take other [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") normally).

### Warrior's Determination

Your natural determination carries you through tough battles. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can ignore one non [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force")-related effect, [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), or ability that exceeds your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), you can ignore one Mind-Affecting effect, even if it is the result of a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique"), or [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret").
