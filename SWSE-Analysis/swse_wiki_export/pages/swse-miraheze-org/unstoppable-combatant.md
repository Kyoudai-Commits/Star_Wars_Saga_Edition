# Unstoppable Combatant

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unstoppable_Combatant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unstoppable_Combatant) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can keep coming back for more punishment, no matter what your enemies throw at you.

**Prerequisite:** [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind")

**Effect:** You can catch more than one [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") per encounter.

**Normal:** Even if you can catch multiple [Second Winds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Winds "Second Winds") per day, without this feat you are limited to one [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") per encounter.
