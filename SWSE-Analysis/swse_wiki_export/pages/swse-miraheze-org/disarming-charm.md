# Disarming Charm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming_Charm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming_Charm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Like many among your Species, you know how to blend in with galactic society, using your natural gregarious disposition to keep others off guard.

**Prerequisite:** [Sullustan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullustan "Sullustan")

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully use the [Change Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Change_Attitude "Change Attitude") application of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill to change a target's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"), you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") or [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks against that target for the next 24 hours.
