# Spray Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spray_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spray_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can fire a short burst at an enemy while protecting nearby allies from errant shots.

**Effect:** When you have a weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), you can reduce the area targeted by your [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack to 1 square.
