# Beastwarden Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beastwarden_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beastwarden_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have learned to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to touch the minds of the [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts"), soothing them and convincing them to do your bidding.

## Core Talents

### Beastwarden Charm Beast

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check when attempting to change the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of an undomesticated creature with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 2 or less. Additionally, you do not take the normal -5 penalty on the check if the creature can't speak or understand your language. This Talent is identical to the **[Dathomiri Charm Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomiri_Charm_Beast "Dathomiri Charm Beast")** Talent, and both are considered to be the same Talent for the purposes of satisfying prerequisites.

### Bonded Mount

*Prerequisite:* **Beastwarden Charm Beast**

Whenever you encounter a domesticated [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") with a Friendly or Helpful [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") toward you, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to bond the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") to you as a mount. A **Bonded Mount** shares an emphatic link with you, allowing you to sense its emotions as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

When you [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") a **Bonded Mount**, your mount uses your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") instead of its own. Additionally, if your mount has any special senses (such as [Scent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scent "Scent"), [Darkvision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision"), or [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision")) that you do not possess, you gain the benefits of its special senses as long as you are riding that mount.

### Entreat Beast

*Prerequisite:* **Beastwarden Charm Beast**

You can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to convince a small [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") to carry objects, deliver messages, or perform other minor tasks for you. If you are near a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") that is at least Indifferent to you (whether this be a pet you bring with you or a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") encountered in the wild), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the Beast's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If your [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") equals or exceeds the Beast's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") performs one task for you from the following list:

- *Deliver an Object:* The [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") attempts, to the best of its ability, to deliver one object from your person to another target within 30 squares of you.
- *Retrieve an Object:* The [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") attempts, to the best of its ability, to retrieve one unattended object within 30 squares of it and its [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and bring it to you.
- *Manipulate a Small Object:* The [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") attempts, to the best of its ability, to press a button, pull a lever, or otherwise perform some minor activation of an unattended item within 30 squares.

### Soothing Presence

*Prerequisite:* **Beastwarden Charm Beast**

Whenever you encounter a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") with an Unfriendly [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") toward you, you automatically shift its [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") to Indifferent (no [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") required).

### Wild Sense

*Prerequisite:* **Beastwarden Charm Beast**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to touch the mind of a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") with an Indifferent or better [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") toward you, provided it is within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). When you do so, the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") makes an immediate active [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, and you are considered to perceive everything the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") does, including noticing targets, as though you had made the check.

Additionally, until the end of your turn, you are considered to have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to anything the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") has [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to.
