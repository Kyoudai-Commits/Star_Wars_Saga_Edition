# Mighty Swing

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are capable of delivering jarring [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks").

**Prerequisite:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13

**Effect:** You can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to deal +1 die of damage on your next [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") in the same round.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") feat.
