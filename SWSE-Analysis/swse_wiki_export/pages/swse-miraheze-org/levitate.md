# Levitate

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Levitate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Levitate) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can float up or down without anything or anyone to assist you.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain a Fly Speed of 2 squares but can only move vertically. You may move up to your Fly Speed as part of the action used to activate *Levitate*, and if you end this movement adjacent to a horizontal surface, you can move 1 square onto that surface as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). At the start of your next turn, if you are not standing or holding onto a solid surface, you fall to the ground. |
| 20 | You gain a Fly Speed of 4 squares but can only move vertically. You may move up to your Fly Speed as part of the action used to activate *Levitate*, and if you end this movement adjacent to a horizontal surface, you can move 1 square onto that surface as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). At the start of your next turn, if you are not standing or holding onto a solid surface, you fall to the ground. |
| 25 | You gain a Fly Speed of 6 squares but can only move vertically. You may move up to your Fly Speed as part of the action used to activate *Levitate*, and if you end this movement adjacent to a horizontal surface, you can move 1 square onto that surface as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). At the start of your next turn, if you are not standing or holding onto a solid surface, you fall to the ground. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to activate *Levitate* when falling. You can reduce your fall distance by a number of squares equal to the Fly Speed provided by this Force Power. If you reduce the fall distance to 0 squares, you land safely on your feet.

You can maintain *Levitate* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Levitate* Force Power is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and whenever you maintain this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") you can move vertically up to your Fly Speed. If you take damage while maintaining *Levitate*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Levitate*.
