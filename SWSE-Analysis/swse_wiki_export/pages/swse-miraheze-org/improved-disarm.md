# Improved Disarm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are skilled at [Disarming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming "Disarming") opponents in melee combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13, [Melee Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense "Melee Defense")

**Effect:** You gain a +5 bonus on any melee attack roll made to [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent. In addition, if you fail to [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent, they don't get to make a free attack against you.

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat, when you use the *[Force Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disarm "Force Disarm")* Force Power, you gain the +5 bonus from Improved Disarm on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as though you were making an attack roll.
