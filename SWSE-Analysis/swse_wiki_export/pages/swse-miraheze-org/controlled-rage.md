# Controlled Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Controlled_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Controlled_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to channel your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage").

**Prerequisite:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage")

**Effect:** You enter a [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). Your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") ends 1 round after you declare it is finished.

**Normal:** You must use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to begin a [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") and may not end it at will.

**Special:** You may not use this feat to extend the number of rounds of [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") available to your character.
