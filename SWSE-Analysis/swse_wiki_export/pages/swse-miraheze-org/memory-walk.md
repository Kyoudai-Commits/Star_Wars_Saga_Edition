# Memory Walk

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Walk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Walk) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You torment an enemy by causing them to relive their most horrible memories.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*, *[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 3 or higher, within 6 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and loses its [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. |
| 25 | Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and loses its [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn. |
| 30 | Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and loses its [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") on its next turn. |
| 35 | Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and loses both its [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and its [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by an additional +2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.

You can maintain *Memory Walk* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Memory Walk* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Memory Walk*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Memory Walk*.
