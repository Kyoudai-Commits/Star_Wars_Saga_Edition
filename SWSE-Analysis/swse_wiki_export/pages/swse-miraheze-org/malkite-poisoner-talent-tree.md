# Malkite Poisoner Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malkite_Poisoner_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malkite_Poisoner_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

You have mastered the deadly arts of [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") from the masters on [Malkii](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malkii?action=edit&redlink=1 "Malkii (page does not exist)"). You can employ a wide array of lethal toxins with which you can quickly and efficiently destroy your targets.

## Core Talents

### Malkite Techniques

Once per encounter, you can apply a toxin to any non-Energy Slashing or Piercing weapon as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If an attack roll with that weapon also exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), that target is [Poisoned](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poisoned "Poisoned"). Each round on the creature's turn, the [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") makes an attack roll (1d20 + your Heroic Level) against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the attack succeeds, the target takes damage equal to 1d6 + one-half your Heroic Level and moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

A target moved to the end of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by the [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") is unconscious but continues to take damage as long as the [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") continues to attack. The [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") attacks each round until it misses, or until the victim is cured with a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check (DC 10 + your Heroic Level).

### Modify Poison

*Prerequisite:* **Malkite Techniques**

You can modify the deliver method of a [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") ([Contact](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Contact "Contact"), [Ingested](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ingested "Ingested"), [Inhaled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inhaled "Inhaled")) to another delivery method by succeeding in a [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") check (DC equal to the Poison's [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") DC). The Poison's capabilities and specific effects are unchanged.

### Numbing Poison

*Prerequisite:* **Malkite Techniques**

Any target you [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") is automatically denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), for a long as it remains [Poisoned](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poisoned "Poisoned").

### Undetectable Poison

*Prerequisite:* **Malkite Techniques**

The [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") DC needed to cure a [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison") you have use against a target increases by 5.

### Vicious Poison

*Prerequisite:* **Malkite Techniques**

Any [Poisons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poisons "Poisons") you have used against a target gain a +2 bonus to their attack rolls made against that target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
