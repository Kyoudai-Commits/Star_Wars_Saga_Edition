# Wroonian

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wroonian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wroonian) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/1/1b/Wroonian.webp/300px-Wroonian.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Wroonian.webp)

Wroonian society is based upon the desire for personal wealth and material possessions. Despite this, they are a carefree and mellow Species that enjoy taking risks and living for the moment. Wroonians distrust all types of authority, thanks in part to their planetary government's near-constant instability.

## Wroonian Characteristics

**Personality:** To a Wroonian, life is worth living only if you are having fun while getting rich.

**Physical Description:** Though identical to [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") in most respects, Wroonians are set about by their blue skin and dark blue hair. A typical Wroonian stands 1.6 meters tall.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Wroonians age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-11 Years | 12-15 Years | 16-40 Years | 41-59 Years | 60-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The Inner Rim planet [Wroona](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wroona?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wroona (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** Basic and Wroonian.

**Example Names:** Azoora, Dasha Defano, Drixar, Fillio Androu, Fume Pachoola, Iona Wince, Kabalard Vinne, Linx Mallicker, Llitishi, Nell Indigo, Pelf Pachoola, T'Charek Haathi, Tis Dolan.

**Adventurers:** The Wroonians' dislike of authority leads them to becoming [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") and [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), with only a scant few becoming [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") or [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers").

## Wroonian Species Traits

Wroonians share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Wroonians receive a +2 bonus to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"). Wroonians are graceful and swift, but often their physical prowess lags behind other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Wroonians have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Wroonians have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Free Spirit:** Wroonians are stubborn and hard to be deterred, giving them a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Soothing Voice:** A Wroonian may choose to reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Wroonians can speak, read, and write both Basic and Wroonian.
