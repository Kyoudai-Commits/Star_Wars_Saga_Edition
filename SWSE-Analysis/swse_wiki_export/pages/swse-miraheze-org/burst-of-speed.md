# Burst of Speed

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_of_Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_of_Speed) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are capable of incredible bursts of speed.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to twice your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). At the end of your movement, you move -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
