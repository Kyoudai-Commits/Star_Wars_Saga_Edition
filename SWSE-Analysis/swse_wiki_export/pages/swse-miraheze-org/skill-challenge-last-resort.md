# Skill Challenge: Last Resort

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Last_Resort](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Last_Resort) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When failure threatens, you throw everything you have at the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") to prevent disaster.

**Effect:** Once per [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), when you or an ally accrues a third Failure (which would normally end the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge")), you or the ally can reroll the attempt, keeping the better result.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [Skill Challenges](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenges "Skill Challenges").
