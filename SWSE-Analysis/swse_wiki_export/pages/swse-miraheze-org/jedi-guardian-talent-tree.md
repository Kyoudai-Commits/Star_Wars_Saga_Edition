# Jedi Guardian Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Guardian_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Guardian_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") that follow the path of the guardian are more combat-oriented than other [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), honing their skills to become deadly combatants.

## Core Talents

### Acrobatic Recovery

If any effect causes you to fall [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"), you can make a DC 20 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to remain on your feet.

### Battle Meditation

The Jedi technique known as **Battle Meditation** allows you and your allies to work together seamlessly and with a level of precision that can only come from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to give you and all allies within 6 squares of you a +1 insight bonus on attack rolls that lasts until the end of the encounter.

This bonus does not extend to allies outside the range of the effect, even if they move within 6 squares of you later on. Allies who benefit from the **Battle Meditation** must remain within 6 squares of you to retain the insight bonus, and they lose it if you are knocked unconscious or killed. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

This affects all allied [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") within 6 squares at [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale").

### Elusive Target

When fighting an opponent or multiple opponents in melee, other opponents attempting to target you with ranged attacks take a -5 penalty. This penalty is in addition to the normal -5 penalty for firing into melee, making the penalty to target you -10.

### Force Intuition

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check modifier instead of your [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") modifier when making [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") skill. If you are entitled to an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

You may use this Talent to determine the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") of a Starship if you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot").

### Resilience

You can spend a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to move +2 steps up the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

## Additional Talents

### Close Maneuvering

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Once per turn, you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to designate a target. Until the start of your next turn, your movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") from that target, provided that you end your movement adjacent to that target.

### Cover Escape

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisite:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**

When you successfully spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to negate a attack against an adjacent ally with the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents, that ally can move up to 2 squares as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Defensive Acuity

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

When you take the [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") Action, you deal +1 die of damage with [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attacks and gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to negate an attack with the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents. These benefits last until the end of your next turn.

### Exposing Strike

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

When you use a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to deal damage to a target, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to make that target [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") until the end of your next turn.

### Forceful Warrior

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

When you score a Critical Hit with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you gain 1 temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Grenade Defense

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can use the [Move Light Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Light_Object "Move Light Object") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill to cast aside [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades") that are thrown at you. As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") when you are attacked by a [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") of any kind, you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check with a DC equal to the attack roll of the incoming [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") attack.

If your check equals or exceeds the DC, you hurl the [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") to a location where it explodes harmlessly, negating the attack. Whether or not you are successful, you take a -5 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the start of your next turn.

### Guardian Strike

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Whenever you use a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to deal damage to a target, that target takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls against any target other than you until the beginning of your next turn.

### Hold the Line

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

When you make a successful [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target leaving your [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), you stop the target's movement, ending its Action.

### Immovable

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can activate this Talent as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Until the start of your next turn, anyone attempting to move you involuntarily (such as with a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") or the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power) takes a -5 penalty to attack rolls or [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made to use that effect that would move you. An enemy can only take the penalty from this Talent once per attempt, regardless of how many targets have used this Talent.

### Improved Battle Meditation

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Battle Meditation**

You may activate your **Battle Meditation** Talent as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), instead of as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). The range of the **Battle Meditation** extends out to 12 squares. Opponents within the radius of your **Battle Meditation** suffer a -1 penalty to all attack rolls.

### Mobile Combatant

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

When you end your movement adjacent to an opponent, you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to activate this Talent. If the designated opponent [Moves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moves "Moves") or [Withdraws](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraws "Withdraws") before the beginning of your next turn, you can choose to move with that opponent, up to a total distance equal to your current [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

Unless your opponent uses the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action or makes an [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to avoid [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"), its movement provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you for the first square moved as normal (but no subsequent squares in the same movement). If your target moves farther than your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), you must still end this movement closer to the target than you began.
