# Mystic Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mystic_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mystic_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have studied [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in a radically different way from other Force-users, relying on rituals and mystic lore to harness its power.

## Core Talents

### Channel Vitality

You can fuel your mastery of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") with your own vitality. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can move -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") to gain a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). This temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") lasts until the end of your turn, at which point it is lost if it has not been used.

### Closed Mind

Whenever a creature uses a Mind-Affecting effect on you that targets your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), it must roll the attack roll or [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") twice, taking the lower result.

### Esoteric Technique

When you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to activate a [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") or [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret"), you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 10 + your Class Level until the end of the encounter.

### Mystic Mastery

Whenever you gain a level, you also gain a number of additional [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to the number of [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents") you possess (maximum +6).

### Regimen Mastery

*Prerequisite:* [Force Regimen Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimen_Mastery "Force Regimen Mastery")

You gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made to perform a [Force Regimen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimen "Force Regimen").
