# Recovering Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recovering_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recovering_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

When you get back into the fight, you do not let your wounds hamper you.

**Effect:** When you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you move +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
