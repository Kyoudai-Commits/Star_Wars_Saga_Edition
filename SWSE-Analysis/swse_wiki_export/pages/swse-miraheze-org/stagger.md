# Stagger

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stagger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stagger) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to lash out at a nearby enemy, causing it to stumble.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** One target adjacent to you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage and is pushed 1 square away from you. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to affect all enemies adjacent to you, in which case compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to each target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
