# Rocket Jumper Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocket_Jumper_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocket_Jumper_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are an expert in the use of [Jet Packs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Packs "Jet Packs"). You can use this form of transport to perform specialized aerial assaults and maneuvers.

## Core Talents

### Jet Pack Training

You can activate a [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your turn. You need not make [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks to land safely with a [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack").

### Burning Assault

*Prerequisite:* **Jet Pack Training**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can expend one of your Jet Pack's charges to make an attack with the [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack"), treating it as a [Flamethrower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flamethrower "Flamethrower"). You cannot use this Talent when you are flying. You are considered proficient in the [Flamethrower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flamethrower "Flamethrower") for purposes of making this attack.

### Improved Trajectory

*Prerequisite:* **Jet Pack Training**

You always use the proper trajectories to maximize the efficiency of your rocket-pack burn rates. You increase your fly speed by 2 squares when using a [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack").

### Jet Pack Withdraw

*Prerequisite:* **Jet Pack Training**

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") when an opponent moves adjacent to you, you can expend one charge of your [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack") to fly and [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), or [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw").
