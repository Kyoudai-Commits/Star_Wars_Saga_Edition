# Melee Specialist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Specialist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Specialist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Your skill with hand-to-hand combat is unmatched, and you can wield weapons with lethal accuracy and power.

## Core Talents

### Accurate Blow

Choose one [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)") or one of the following [Weapon Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Groups "Weapon Groups") in which you are proficient: [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons"), [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), or [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"). When you make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a weapon from the chosen group and the attack roll exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 5 or more, you deal +1 die of damage with the attack.

### Close-Quarters Fighter

Whenever you occupy the same square as your target or are adjacent to your target, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls against that target.

### Ignore Armor

Once per encounter, when you make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), you can ignore any Armor or [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonuses granted by your target's [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").

### Improved Stunning Strike

*Prerequisite:* **[Stunning Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning_Strike "Stunning Strike")**

When you damage an opponent with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") that moves the target down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), the target cannot take any Action requiring a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") or a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") on its next turn.

### Whirling Death

*Prerequisites:* **[Melee Smash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Smash "Melee Smash")**, **[Unrelenting Assault](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unrelenting_Assault "Unrelenting Assault")**

You twirl your weapon around you in a blur, creating a circle of death around you. Any enemy target that begins its turn adjacent to you takes damage equal to your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus. You must be wielding a melee weapon to be using this Talent.
