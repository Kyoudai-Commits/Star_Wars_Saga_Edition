# Soldier

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Soldiers combine discipline with martial skills to become the best pure warriors in the galaxy. Soldiers can be stalwart defenders of those in need, cruel marauders, or brave adventurers. They can be hired guns, noble champions, or cold-hearted killers. They fight for glory, for honor, to right wrongs, to gain power, to acquire wealth, or simply for the thrill of battle.

## Adventurers

Many Soldiers see adventures, raids on enemy strongholds, and dangerous missions as their jobs. Some want to defend those who can't defend themselves; others seek to use their muscle to carve their own place of importance in the galaxy. Whatever their initial motivation, most Soldiers wind up living for the thrill of combat and the excitement of adventure. Adventuring Soldiers call themselves guards, bodyguards, champions, enforcers, mercenaries, warriors, soldiers of fortune, or simple adventurers.

### Characteristics

Soldiers have the best all-around fighting abilities, and an individual Soldier develops styles and techniques that set them apart from their peers. A given Soldier might be especially capable with certain weapons, another trained to execute specific combat maneuvers. As Soldiers gain experience, they get more opportunities to develop their fighting skills.

### Backgrounds

Most Soldiers come to the profession after receiving at least some amount of formal training from a military organization, local militia, or private army. Some attend formal academies; others are self-taught and well-tested. A Soldier may have taken up their weapons to escape a mundane life. Another may be following a proud family tradition. Soldiers in a particular unit share certain camaraderie, but most have nothing in common except battle prowess and the desire to apply it to a given situation.

### Examples of Soldiers in Star Wars

Admiral Ackbar, Corran Horn, Captain Panaka, Captain Typho, General Crix Madine, Kyle Katarn, Wedge Antilles, Zam Wesell

### Class Features

- **Abilities:** Since most combat in the *Star Wars* universe uses blasters and other ranged weapons, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") is the Soldier's most important [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score"), followed closely by [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") and [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"). Don't underestimate the importance of [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), however, since many of a Soldier's useful [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") are based on these abilities.
- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, a Soldier gains a +1 Class bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +2 Class bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** Soldiers begin play at 1st level with a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 30 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At each level after 1st, Soldiers gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Skills:** 1st-level Soldiers are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 3 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"),
  - [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"),
  - [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)"),
  - [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"),
  - [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim"),
  - [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury"),
  - [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")
- **Starting Feats:** At 1st level, a Soldier gains the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):
  - [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)"),
  - [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")
- **Force Points:** Soldiers gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") left over from the previous levels are lost.
- **Credits:** A 1st-level Soldiers starts play with 3d4 x 250 credits.

### Talent Trees

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Soldier gains an additional [Soldier Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talent "Soldier Talent"). A Soldier may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Soldier Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Talent_Trees "Soldier Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a Soldier gains a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the [Soldier Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Bonus_Feats "Soldier Bonus Feats") list. As with normal [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), you must meet the prerequisites to select the [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). The Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be from the [Soldier Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier_Bonus_Feats "Soldier Bonus Feats") list, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
