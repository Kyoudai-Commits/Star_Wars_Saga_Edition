# Brawler Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brawler_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brawler_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You like to get "up close and personal" with your enemies and engage them in melee combat.

## Core Talents

### Expert Grappler

You gain a +2 competence bonus on [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attacks.

### Gun Club

You can use a ranged weapon as a melee weapon without taking a penalty on your attack roll. The weapon is otherwise treated as a [Club](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Club "Club") in all respects. If you are using a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") with a mounted [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet") or [Vibrobayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrobayonet "Vibrobayonet"), you may wield that weapon as a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"). The [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet") or [Vibrobayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrobayonet "Vibrobayonet") end is treated normally, and the other end is treated as a [Club](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Club "Club").

### Melee Smash

You deal +1 point of damage with [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks").

### Stunning Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Melee Smash**

When you damage an opponent with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), your opponent moves an additional -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") if your damage roll result equals or exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").

### Unbalance Opponent

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Grappler**

You are skilled at keeping your opponents off balance in melee combat. At the beginning of your turn, you can choose to designate an opponent no more than one size category larger or smaller than you. That opponent doesn't get to add their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus on attack rolls when targeting you (if the opponent has a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") penalty, they still suffer from that penalty). The opponent's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier applies to damage, as usual. You can select a new opponent on your next turn.

## Additional Talents

### Bayonet Master

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Gun Club**

When you take a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you can treat a ranged weapon with a [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet") (or [Vibrobayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrobayonet "Vibrobayonet")) as a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon"). You can attack with the [Bayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bayonet "Bayonet") (or [Vibrobayonet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrobayonet "Vibrobayonet")) and club a target with your ranged Weapon (as with the **Gun Club** Talent), ignoring the normal penalties for attacking with both ends of a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon").

### Cantina Brawler

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

While [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked"), you gain a +2 bonus on [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack rolls and damage rolls.

### Counterpunch

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When you [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively"), any adjacent creature that attacks you provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you.

### Crowd Control

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisite:* **Entangler**

You can [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") two adjacent creatures at a time.

### Devastating Melee Smash

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Melee Smash**

Once per encounter, you can attempt a **Devastating Melee Smash**. You must declare this special [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") before making the attack roll. If the attack rolls succeeds, add half your level to the damage, instead of the normal +1 bonus for the **Melee Smash** Talent. The damage from this Talent does not stack with any damage bonus provided by the [Powerful Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge "Powerful Charge") feat.

### Disarm and Engage

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When you successfully [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent, you can make an immediate free attack with the disarmed weapon at a -5 penalty. If you are not proficient with the weapon, you take the penalty for nonproficiency as well.

### Entangler

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When [Grabbing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbing "Grabbing") a target, you take a -2 penalty to your attack roll (instead of the normal -5 penalty). Until the target breaks the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab"), it takes a -5 penalty to attack rolls, including those made with Natural and [Light Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapons "Light Weapons") (instead of the normal -2 penalty).

### Experienced Brawler

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Stunning Strike**

You know how to fight when the odds are against you. You can use each of the following actions as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") once per encounter:

- *Avoid Attack:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an opponent within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain a +5 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against a single attack as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").
- *Fortified Mind:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an opponent within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain a +5 bonus to either your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") or [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against a single attack as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").
- *Focused Stance:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an opponent within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). At any time until the beginning of your next turn, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can move up to 2 squares. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Grabber

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You do not take a -5 penalty when using the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") Action.

### Hammerblow

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

If you are [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") and holding no items, you double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus to [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack rolls.

### Make Do

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When fighting with an [Improvised Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapon "Improvised Weapon"), you take no penalty on your attack rolls.

### Man Down

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Whenever an ally within 6 squares is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you can immediately move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") toward that ally as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Pick a Fight

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Cantina Brawler**

During the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), you and all allies within 6 squares of you gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls. Additionally, until the end of the encounter, you retain this bonus to attack rolls against any target you or your allies damage during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round").

### Reverse Strength

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You know how to use an opponent's strength against them. Whenever you successfully [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") an opponent, you deal damage equal to the opponent's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier (minimum 1 point).

### Strong Grab

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

When you successfully [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") an opponent, they must use a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to break the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab").

### Sucker Punch

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When your [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") damages an opponent that is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), that opponent cannot take [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") until the end of its next turn.

### Unrelenting Assault

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Melee Smash**

You launch yourself at your foe, attacking with weapons, limbs, or anything else available. Whenever you miss with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or the attack is negated, you still deal your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus in damage to the target (minimum 1), or 2 x your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus if you attack with a weapon you are wielding two-handed.
