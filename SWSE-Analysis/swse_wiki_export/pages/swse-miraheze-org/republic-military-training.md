# Republic Military Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Republic_Military_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Republic_Military_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are trained in Republic military fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in ranged combat. This training is generally given to members of the Republic military forces.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10 against an incoming attack if you have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from the attacker. You can gain the [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") as long as you have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), even if your opponent uses [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") to ignore your [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
