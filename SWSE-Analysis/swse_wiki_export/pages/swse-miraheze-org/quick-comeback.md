# Quick Comeback

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Comeback](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Comeback) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

When you take damage that sends you reeling, you can recover quickly and get back in the fight.

**Prerequisite:** [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean")

**Effect:** Whenever you are moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by an attack that deals damage that equals or exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), until the end of your next turn you can move +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). You can use this feat only once per attack that moves you down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
