# Farseeing

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You gain a vague, momentary impression of events happening around a particular being in some distant place.

**Time:** [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")

**Targets:** One creature you know or have met with before.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If your result is less than the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you gain no information (including whether the target is alive or dead) and cannot use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against the same target for 24 hours. If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you can sense whether the target is alive or dead and gain a vague sense of its immediate surroundings, what it's currently doing, and any strong emotions it is presently feeling. A dead target has a [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of 30 for purposes of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").

**Special:** If you successfully use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain a clear mental image of the target's surroundings, as well as other creatures and objects within 6 squares of it.
