# Category : Force Powers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Force_Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Force_Powers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

Your character's Force Powers collectively form a suite. When your character uses a Force Power, it's like playing a card and putting it in the discard pile. The Force Power takes effect, and it's no longer available to the character... at least for a while. When your character uses a Force Power, make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The check result determines the Force Power's effect.

Some Force Powers have all-or-nothing effects. Other Force Powers have multi-tiered effects, and your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result determines the maximum effect you can achieve, although you can always choose a lesser effect. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is too low to activate the Force Power's baseline effect, nothing happens and the action is wasted. Using a Force power removes it from your character's active "suite" of Force powers, regardless of whether the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check succeeds or fails.

You have different ways to regain spent Force Powers so that you can use them again:

- When combat is over and you have a chance to rest for 1 minute, you regain all of your Force Powers.
- If you roll a natural 20 on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate a Force Power, you regain all spent Force Powers at the end of your turn.
- You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") and immediately regain one spent Force Power.
- Some unique abilities (such as the **[Force Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Focus "Force Focus")** Talent) allow you to regain spent Force Powers in other ways.

### Force Power Descriptors

Some Force Powers are more strongly tied to one side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") than the other. These Force Powers carry either the [*Light Side*] descriptor or the [*Dark Side*] descriptor. Powers that target creatures' minds carry the [*Mind-Affecting*] descriptor, and the [*Telekinetic*] descriptor are those Force Powers that utilize telekinesis.

Using a Force Power with the [*Dark Side*] descriptor increases your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1. You cannot use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check when activating a Force Power with the [*Dark Side*] descriptor. Dark Side Powers stem from powerful negative emotions, and include *[Dark Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage "Dark Rage")* and *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")*.

If you have a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, you cannot use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check when activating a Force Power with the [*Light Side*] descriptor. Light Side Powers are generally beneficial and include *[Sever Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sever_Force "Sever Force")* and *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*.

A Force Power with the [*Mind-Affecting*] descriptor has no effect on creatures that are mindless (that is, creatures with no [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score) or creatures that are immune to Mind-Affecting effects.

A Force Power with the [*Telekinetic*] descriptor uses telekinesis to accomplish its ends. Many telekinetic powers interact with the world physically in similar ways, and are covered as a group by some [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"). If a target of a [*Telekinetic*] Force Power has [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), it gains a [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to its relevant [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") or on its opposed check to resist the Force Power.

If the target of a [*Telekinetic*] Force Power has [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), you take a penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate the Force Power unless you have used the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill this turn.
