# Improved Damage Threshold

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Damage_Threshold) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are harder to take down in a fight.

**Effect:** You increase your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by 5 points.

**Normal:** A creature without this Feat has a [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") equal to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") plus its size modifier (+5 for Large, +10 for Huge, +20 for Gargantuan, +50 for Colossal).

**Special:** You may take this feat more than once. Its effects stack. Each time you take this feat, increase your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by 5 points.
