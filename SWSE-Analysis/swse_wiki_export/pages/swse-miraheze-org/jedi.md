# Jedi

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Jedi combine physical training with mastery of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Jedi concentrate on battle prowess, defense, and [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") training. Additionally, they are ambassadors of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), protecting the Republic from all dangers. Few are strong enough in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and have the devotion to walk the Jedi's path, but those few are awarded with a powerful ally. They walk in a larger world than those who neither feel nor heed [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

## Jedi Multiclassing

**Q:** When [Multiclassing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclassing "Multiclassing"), do you need to have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat to take a level in the Jedi class?

**A:** No. You can take [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") as your starting [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") (or, if you wish, you can take a different starting [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat")).

**Q:** Does that mean that you could have a Jedi who is not Force-sensitive?

**A:** In game mechanical terms, yes, a character can have levels in the Jedi class without having the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat. Obviously, they can't have any [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), such as **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** and **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, that require a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to function. Furthermore, they can't have any [Jedi Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Talents "Jedi Talents") that must be activated by spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). Such abilities are meant to reflect their actual connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), so a character without the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat can't use them.

To an *in-universe observer*, though, such a character would never be described as a "Jedi." They would be regarded as someone who has studied some of the Jedi arts, but few would confuse that with being a member of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). As an analogy, consider that someone on Earth who studies kung fu would not automatically be thought of as a Shaolin monk.

Remember that the Jedi class isn't only about [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")- it's broader, encompassing all the Jedi arts. Thus, a non-Force-sensitive duelist might study under a Jedi and take levels in the Jedi class, becoming more skillful with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") despite having no ability to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") (and thus no access to some of the class's best [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), such as **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** and **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**). In fact, a character like [General Grievous](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/General_Grievous "General Grievous") fits this profile very well, and it would be perfectly valid to create his character with levels in Jedi to reflect his years of study under [Count Dooku](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Count_Dooku "Count Dooku").

Alternatively, a character with a close connection to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), such as an Antarian Ranger, might take levels in the Jedi class, despite being non-Force-sensitive. Such a character might find some Jedi Guardian or Jedi Consular techniques rubbing off on them after working together for so long.

Finally- to borrow from the Shaolin analogy- a martial artist might take levels in Jedi to pick up some [Jedi Guardian Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Guardian_Talents "Jedi Guardian Talents"), such as **[Acrobatic Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Recovery "Acrobatic Recovery")** or **[Elusive Target](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elusive_Target "Elusive Target")**, as part of their studies. In none of these cases, though, would such a character be considered an actual "Jedi" to an in-universe observer.

Nevertheless, remember that the rules on [Multiclassing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclassing "Multiclassing") specifically require that the Gamemaster approve of any [Multiclassing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclassing "Multiclassing") choice. Before you take levels in the Jedi class, the GM might require that you find a Jedi to teach you or that you have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") Feat (or both, or neither, or any other restriction that seems appropriate). There's absolutely nothing wrong with adding such a restriction if it fits the tone of the GM's campaign.

## Droid Jedi

**Q:** The rules for [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") say that you can't select Jedi as your class at 1st level. Given the ruling above, could a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") Multiclass and take levels in Jedi after 1st level?

**A:** Yes. In fact, in *Shadows of the Empire*, [Darth Vader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Vader "Darth Vader") had some customized [ASP Labor Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/ASP_Labor_Droids "ASP Labor Droids") that were modified to serve as [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")-sparring partners. Such [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") could be represented in the game by having at least some levels in the Jedi class to pick up [Lightsaber Combat Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talents "Lightsaber Combat Talents").

## Adventurers

All Jedi journey into the galaxy at large to further their own knowledge and to help those in need. They take their responsibility seriously, considering even the mundane missions to be personal tests. Most Jedi follow [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side"), but some become Darksiders and use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for evil or selfish intent. Those imbued with [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") refrain from using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for every task, preferring to find other solutions and save [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for when it is truly needed. Those who succumb to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") use every advantage at their disposal, wielding [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to solve problems as a soldier would use a blaster to destroy a [Stingfly](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stingfly?action=edit&redlink=1 "Stingfly (page does not exist)").

### Characteristics

The Jedi's strength flows from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), granting them special powers. This character often masters the ability to feel [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force") in the world within them. [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") are the most important abilities for a Jedi, although any of the other [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") are nearly as important.

### Backgrounds

A Jedi typically starts out as a Padawan Learner attached to a [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master"). (Prior to 1st level, the character trained as a student with the Jedi Council. They began their heroic career at 1st level when they are selected as a Padawan Learner.) The Jedi has a genuine desire to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to help the people of the galaxy. A Jedi has a deep connection to their mentor and [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), but often operates with only tangential contact with either their Master or Order.

Still, not all who study the Jedi arts are actually members of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). During the Old Sith Wars (thousands of years before the Battle of [Yavin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin "Yavin")), many Sith acolytes and initiates are often members of this class before becoming a [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") under a particular [Sith Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lord "Sith Lord"). This class is also common among other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") with strong ties to the Jedi arts, such as [The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai").

### Examples of Jedi in Star Wars

Luke Skywalker (after training under Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda), Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, Mace Windu, Bastila Shan, Quinlan Vos

### Class Features

- **Abilities:** A Jedi should be gifted in all [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities"), but [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") are the most important. [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") are also useful.
- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, a Jedi gains a +1 Class bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** Jedi begin play at 1st level with a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 30 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At each level after 1st, Jedi gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Skills:** 1st-level Jedi are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 2 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"),
  - [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"),
  - [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") (all; taken individually),
  - [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"),
  - [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") (derived from [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"))
- **Starting Feats:** At 1st level, a Jedi gains the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):
  - [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")
- **Force Points:** Jedi gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") left over from the previous levels are lost.
- **Lightsaber:** A Jedi begins play with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") provided by their Master. Later, they can build their own (see [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction")).
- **Credits:** A 1st-level Jedi starts play with 3d4 x 100 credits.

### Talent Trees

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Jedi gains an additional [Jedi Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Talent "Jedi Talent"). A Jedi may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Jedi Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Talent_Trees "Jedi Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a Jedi gains a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the [Jedi Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Bonus_Feats "Jedi Bonus Feats") list. As with normal [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), you must meet the prerequisites to select the [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). The Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be from the [Jedi Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Bonus_Feats "Jedi Bonus Feats") list, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
