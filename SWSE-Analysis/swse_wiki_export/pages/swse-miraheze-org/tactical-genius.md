# Tactical Genius

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tactical_Genius](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tactical_Genius) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are a master at using multiple [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") over the course of long space combats.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"), [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics"), [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")

**Effect:** You regain all spent [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers") at the end of any round in which you roll a Natural 20 on an attack roll.

**Normal:** Only a Natural 20 rolled on a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check to activate a [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") restores all [Starship Maneuvers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuvers "Starship Maneuvers").
