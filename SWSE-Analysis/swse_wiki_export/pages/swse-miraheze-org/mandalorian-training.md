# Mandalorian Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mandalorian_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are trained in Mandalorian fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in many types of combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Charging Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire "Charging Fire")

**Effect:** When using the [Charging Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire "Charging Fire") feat, you gain a +2 bonus to a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") made at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"). As with a normal [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you still take a -2 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), but you also gain a +2 morale bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the beginning of your next turn.

**Normal:** You gain no bonus to a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") make at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") with the [Charging Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire "Charging Fire") feat.
