# Primitive Warrior

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Warrior](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Warrior) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to make the most of primitive weapons and can wield them with deadly results.

**Prerequisite:** [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean")

**Effect:** You deal +1 die of damage with [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)").
