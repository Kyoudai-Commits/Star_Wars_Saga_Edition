# Wary Sentries

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wary_Sentries](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wary_Sentries) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies specialize in keeping tabs on your surroundings, making you excellent guards and advance scouts.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks, even when threatened or rushed.
