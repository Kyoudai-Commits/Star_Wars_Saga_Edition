# Lineage Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lineage_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lineage_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You lead a privileged life and reap the benefit of an upbringing beyond most citizens of the galaxy.

## Core Talents

### Connections

You are able to obtain [Licensed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Licensed "Licensed"), [Restricted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted "Restricted"), [Military](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military "Military"), or [Illegal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Illegal "Illegal") equipment without having to pay a licensing fee or endure a background check, provided the total cost of the desired [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") is equal to or less than your Character Level x 1,000 credits. In addition, when obtaining [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") or [Services](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Services "Services") through the [Black Market](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Market "Black Market"), you reduce the black market cost multiplier by 1.

### Educated

Thanks to your well-rounded education, you may make any [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") check [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained").

### Spontaneous Skill

*Prerequisite:* **Educated**

Sometimes you surprise others with your skill. Once per day, you may make an [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") skill check as though you were [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). Exception: you cannot use this Talent to make an Untrained [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as though you were [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the skill, unless you have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat.

You can select this Talent multiple times; each time you do, you can use it one additional time per day.

### Wealth

Each time you gain a level (including the level at which you select this Talent), you receive an amount of credits equal to 5,000 x your Class Level. You can spend these credits as you see fit. The credits appear in a civilized, accessible location of your choice or in your private bank account.

### Influential Friends

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Connections**

You have [Influential Contacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influential_Contacts "Influential Contacts") within a certain organization, planet, or region who can provide concrete information to you on certain subjects. Once per day, you can have one of these contacts make a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") on your behalf. The contact always [Takes 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Takes_20 "Takes 20") on the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") (even if the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") would normally not allow [Taking 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taking_20 "Taking 20")) and has a Skill modifier equal to 5 + one-half your Heroic Level. Contacting your [Influential Contacts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influential_Contacts "Influential Contacts") and receiving the benefit of the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") takes a number of minutes equal to 10 x the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") result.

### Powerful Friends

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Influential Friends**

You have a powerful contact who has an extended sphere of influence. The contact could be an Imperial Senator, a high-level military officer, a regional governor, an infamous crime lord, or another person of similar significance. Once per encounter, you can invoke the name or office of your powerful contact and [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on one [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, with no increase in the time needed to make the check.
