# Deceptive Drop

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Drop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Drop) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When you get the drop on opponents, you dazzle them with your speed and ferocity.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

**Effect:** During the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if you damage a [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") target, that target is also knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") if your attack roll also exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). The target adds its size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"):

- Medium and smaller, +0;
- Large, +5;
- Huge, +10;
- Gargantuan, +20;
- Colossal, +50.
