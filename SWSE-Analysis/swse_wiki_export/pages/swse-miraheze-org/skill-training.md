# Skill Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a new [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill").

**Effect:** Choose one [Untrained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained_Skill "Untrained Skill") from your list of [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"). You become [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill").

**Special:** This feat may be selected multiple times. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill").
