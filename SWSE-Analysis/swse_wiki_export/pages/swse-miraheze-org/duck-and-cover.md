# Duck and Cover

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duck_and_Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duck_and_Cover) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When exposed to an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), you can dive for cover.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

**Effect:** Whenever you are the target of an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") that misses, once per turn you can move 2 squares as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
