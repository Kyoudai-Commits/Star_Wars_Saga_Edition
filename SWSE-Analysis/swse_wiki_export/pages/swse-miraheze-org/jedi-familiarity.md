# Jedi Familiarity

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Familiarity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Familiarity) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have worked alongside Jedi or other Force-users and have developed an excellent rapport.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when you are targeted or affected by a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") originating from an ally, you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), which must be spent before the end of the encounter or it goes away. If the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") damages you or moves you down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you do not gain the benefit of this Feat.
