# Spacer's Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacer's_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacer's_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Whenever you pull off an amazing maneuver in a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), it gives you the confidence to accomplish similarly amazing actions in the near future.

**Prerequisite:** [Duros](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duros "Duros")

**Effect:** Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, you gain 1 temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If that Force Point is not spent before the end of the encounter, it is lost.
