# Shien Deflection

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien_Deflection](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien_Deflection) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You deflect an incoming attack and leap toward your attacker with fierce abandon.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (when an enemy makes a non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") ranged attack against you)

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack does half damage on a hit. You can then [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") toward your attacker, and if you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your attacker, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 20 | Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack does half damage on a hit. You can then [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +2 squares, toward your attacker, and if you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your attacker, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 25 | Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack does half damage on a hit. You can then [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +4 squares, toward your attacker, and if you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your attacker, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 30 | Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack does half damage on a hit. You can then [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +6 squares, toward your attacker, and if you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your attacker, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Shien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien "Shien")):** If you have the **[Shien](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shien "Shien")** Talent, until the start of your next turn, you take no cumulative penalties on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks for multiple **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** attempts.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to take no damage from the incoming attack if your check equals or exceeds the attack roll of the incoming attack.
