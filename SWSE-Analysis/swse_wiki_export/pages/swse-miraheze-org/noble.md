# Noble

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Nobles use their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and natural [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") to make their way in the galaxy. From true royalty to elected officials, military commanders to crime lords, traders, merchants, ambassadors, Holovid stars, and influential corporate magnates, character types who appear in the Noble class are varied and numerous. Some bring honor to the name. Others are sly, treacherous, and dishonorable to the core. With a winning smile, golden tongue, a powerful message, or a knack for making compromises, the Noble commands respect, makes friends, and inevitably influences people.

## Adventurers

Most Nobles wind up in dangerous situations because of something they believe in or because their job calls for it. Others hope to use their negotiating talents to navigate a course through the troubles around them, or seek to find profit in the troubles of others. Whatever their initial motivations, Nobles usually wind up taking to a cause and a goal that sustains them through the roughest missions. An adventuring Noble might be a senator's aide, a free trader, a diplomat, a true prince or princess, or an outlaw's lieutenant. Nobles often feel responsible for others, though some consider themselves to be better than those around them.

### Characteristics

The Noble fosters feelings of good will and honesty, or at least the illusion of such, to succeed. Where other [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") shoot first, the Noble starts out asking questions and hopes to finish by negotiating a deal. The Noble believes he or she can be more effective with words and deeds than with violence, though some draw a blaster when push comes to shove. The Noble is more comfortable in civilized regions of space, where law and order have some meaning. Of all the [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), Nobles have the best diplomatic and bargaining skills. They're good talkers, negotiators, and bluffers. They have a knack for inspiring others, and they make good leaders.

### Backgrounds

Nobles come to their profession in a variety of ways. Altruistic Nobles believe it is their duty and responsibility to serve and lead. More selfish Nobles seek the fame, wealth, and power often associated with the position they aspire to. Power-hungry Nobles take advantages of the system and wind up helping others only to help themselves. The halls of power are calling. How the Noble answers can make all the difference.

### Examples of Nobles in Star Wars

Leia Organa, Padmé Amidala, Bail Organa, Talon Karrde, Jabba the Hutt, Chancellor Valorum, Supreme Chancellor Palpatine

### Class Features

- **Abilities:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") is undoubtedly the Noble's most important [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score"), as the Noble's skill at interacting with others and projecting a sense of confidence are crucial for their success. [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") are also significant for their related [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills").
- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, a Noble gains a +1 Class bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +2 Class bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** Nobles begin play at 1st level with a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 18 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At each level after 1st, Nobles gain 1d6 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Skills:** 1st-level Noble are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 6 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"),
  - [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") (all; taken individually),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"),
  - [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"),
  - [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride"),
  - [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury"),
  - [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")
- **Starting Feats:** At 1st level, a Noble gains the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):
  - [Linguist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Linguist "Linguist") (must have the prerequisite [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 13),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")
- **Force Points:** Nobles gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") left over from the previous levels are lost.
- **Credits:** A 1st-level Noble starts play with 3d4 x 400 credits.

### Talent Trees

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Noble gains an additional [Noble Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Talent "Noble Talent"). A Noble may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Noble Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Talent_Trees "Noble Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a Noble gains a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the [Noble Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Bonus_Feats "Noble Bonus Feats") list. As with normal [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), you must meet the prerequisites to select the [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). The Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be from the [Noble Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble_Bonus_Feats "Noble Bonus Feats") list, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
