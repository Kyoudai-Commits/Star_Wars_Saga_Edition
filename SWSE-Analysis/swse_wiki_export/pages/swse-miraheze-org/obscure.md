# Obscure

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obscure](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Obscure) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to cloud an enemy's mind, making it harder for the enemy to see its target.

**[*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") that just made an attack roll.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes a -5 penalty on the attack roll. If the target misses with the attack, you can choose to make the target reroll the attack against another creature adjacent to it; this attack is also at a -5 penalty.

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to apply the penalty to all of the target's attack rolls made until the start of its next turn.
