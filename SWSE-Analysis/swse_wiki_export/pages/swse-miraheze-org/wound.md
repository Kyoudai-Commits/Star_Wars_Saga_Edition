# Wound

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wound](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wound) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You cause spasms in the lungs of your targets, painfully injuring them.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 6 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Make one roll and compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target receives 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage. The target adds a size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") for purposes of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium or smaller, +0.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by an additional +2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.

If the target moves 1 or more steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") as a result of being damaged by this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), the [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") becomes [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") until the target is treated with a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to perform [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery").
