# Flawless Pilot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flawless_Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flawless_Pilot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know how to handle a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") better than practically anyone else in the galaxy, and you can make any [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") do exactly what you want it to do.

**Prerequisite:** [Duros](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duros "Duros")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.
