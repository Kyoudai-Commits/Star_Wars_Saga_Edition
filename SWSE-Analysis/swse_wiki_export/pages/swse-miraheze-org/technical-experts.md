# Technical Experts

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Technical_Experts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Technical_Experts) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies are just as comfortable in the vehicle pool as on the battlefield and are capable of maintaining heavy equipment.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check, you provide a bonus of +4 instead of +2.
