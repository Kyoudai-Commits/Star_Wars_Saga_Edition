# Instinctive Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Your accuracy improves when you act on instinct. Though you might call it luck, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is with you.

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

**Effect:** When you use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when making an attack with a weapon with which you are proficient, you can reroll the attack and take the better result. Apply the results of the die from the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to the better result.
