# K'thri Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K'thri_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K'thri_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the K'thri style of unarmed fighting, striking with flashy moves and incredible speed.

**Prerequisites:** [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** Once per round, you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to make a single [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"), dealing your base [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage with no bonus to the damage roll from your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") or Heroic Level.

Once per encounter, when you make an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") that misses the target, you deal half damage to the target. You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain the benefits of this [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").

**Special:** If you have the **[K'thri Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27thri_Expertise "K'thri Expertise")** Talent, once per encounter when making a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack"), you can reroll one of your [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") rolls; however, you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse. You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain this special benefit.
