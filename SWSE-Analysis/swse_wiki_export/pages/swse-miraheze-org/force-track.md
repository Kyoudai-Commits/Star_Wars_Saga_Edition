# Force Track

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Track) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You peer into [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for guidance, picking up the trail of your quarry.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You determine the path taken by a specific creature you designate, provided you are within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the creature's trail. You can [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") the creature without needing to make [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks for up to 1 hour, provided the creature you are [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking") passed through the area within the last minute. |
| 20 | You determine the path taken by a specific creature you designate, provided you are within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the creature's trail. You can [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") the creature without needing to make [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks for up to 1 hour, provided the creature you are [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking") passed through the area within the last 10 minutes. |
| 25 | You determine the path taken by a specific creature you designate, provided you are within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the creature's trail. You can [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") the creature without needing to make [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks for up to 1 hour, provided the creature you are [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking") passed through the area within the last hour. |
| 30 | You determine the path taken by a specific creature you designate, provided you are within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the creature's trail. You can [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") the creature without needing to make [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks for up to 1 hour, provided the creature you are [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking") passed through the area within the last day. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") up to two targets simultaneously.
