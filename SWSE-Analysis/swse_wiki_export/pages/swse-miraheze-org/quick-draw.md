# Quick Draw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can draw and holster weapons with startling quickness.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** You can [Draw or Holster a Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_or_Holster_a_Weapon "Draw or Holster a Weapon") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I") Feat, You can draw or holster two weapons with a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"); you must have both hands free to draw both weapons, and each weapon must be able to be held in one hand. Additionally, if you have the [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)") Feat, you can draw and ignite [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** If a Jedi had the Quick Draw Feat and wanted to draw their [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), would they still have to use another [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to activate the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")?

**A:** No. The "[Draw a Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_a_Weapon "Draw a Weapon")" Action normally includes everything necessary to render that weapon immediately usable, including deactivating the safety on a [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol") and activating a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") or [Vibroblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibroblade "Vibroblade"). Weapons are designed to be drawn and armed very quickly, so the GM generally shouldn't require a separate [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") unless there's some unusual difficulty in play (such as when the wielder isn't proficient with the weapon). Similarly, you don't have to spend a separate [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") to deactivate or disarm a weapon when you holster it.

However, it is important to note that this applies only to the actual "[Draw or Holster a Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_or_Holster_a_Weapon "Draw or Holster a Weapon")" Action (that is, moving the weapon either to or from a holster, hanger, sling, and so on). It does not apply when picking up or dropping a weapon, nor does it apply when using the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") Skill to use [Sleight of Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sleight_of_Hand "Sleight of Hand") to get the weapon in your hand without attracting attention. In these cases, you're not getting the weapon in your hand as efficiently as you would when drawing or holstering it.
