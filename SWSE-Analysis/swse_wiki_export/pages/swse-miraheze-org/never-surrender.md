# Never Surrender

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Never_Surrender](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Never_Surrender) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You can fight your way back from the blackness of death and unconsciousness, staying alive in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** The first time you would be reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") in an encounter, you can make an [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), with a DC equal to the number of points of damage being dealt to you. If your check succeeds, you are instead reduced to 1 [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point").
