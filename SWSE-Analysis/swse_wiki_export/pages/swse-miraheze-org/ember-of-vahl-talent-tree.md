# Ember of Vahl Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ember_of_Vahl_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ember_of_Vahl_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Members of Vahl's cult look to their goddess for inspiration. Over the years, they have developed talents that slant toward the destructive. Only Force-users that are part of [The Ember of Vahl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ember_of_Vahl "The Ember of Vahl") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Initiate of Vahl

Having been formally initiated into Vahl's priesthood, you are resistant to the effects of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") and extreme heat. You take half damage from attacks that deal [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage, or no damage on a miss (if the [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage is part of an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack")).

### Reading the Flame

*Prerequisite:* **Initiate of Vahl**

You can enter a trance by staring into a flame of any size, gaining insight into the workings of the galaxy by meditating on the flame's movement. Whenever you use the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power or the [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, you can reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, keeping the better of the two results.

### Sword of Vahl

*Prerequisite:* **Initiate of Vahl**

Your devotion to Vahl allows you to eschew advanced weapons in favor of simple implements of war. You gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on attack rolls made with [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons").

### Vahl's Brand

*Prerequisite:* **[Empower Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empower_Weapon "Empower Weapon")**

Any additional damage you deal with an **[Empowered Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Empowered_Weapon "Empowered Weapon")** is considered to be [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage.

### Vahl's Flame

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to call forth the sparks of your goddess, wreathing your weapon in flames. Until the beginning of your next turn, any successful attack you make with a melee weapon deals an additional 1d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage.
