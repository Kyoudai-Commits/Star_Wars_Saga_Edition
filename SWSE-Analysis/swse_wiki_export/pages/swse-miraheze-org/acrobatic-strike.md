# Acrobatic Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your dexterous maneuvers and skilled [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") allow you to slip past a foe's defenses and deliver an accurate strike against them.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics")

**Effect:** If you succeed in [Tumbling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumbling "Tumbling") to avoid an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you gain a +2 competence bonus on the next attack roll you make against that foe as long as the attack occurs before the end of your current turn.
