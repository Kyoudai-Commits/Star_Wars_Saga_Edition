# Aquatic Specialists

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquatic_Specialists](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquatic_Specialists) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies have trained extensively in [Underwater Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Underwater_Combat?action=edit&redlink=1 "Underwater Combat (page does not exist)") and specialize in missions in aquatic environments.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim"), you swim at half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") (or you swim at your full [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")).
