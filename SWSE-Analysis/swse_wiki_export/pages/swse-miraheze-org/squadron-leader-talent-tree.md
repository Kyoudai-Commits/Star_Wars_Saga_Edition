# Squadron Leader Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Squadron_Leader_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Squadron_Leader_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are an expert at leading a squadron of smaller [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") into combat as a unit.

All [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") in the Squadron Leader Talent Tree apply to other [Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders"), [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"), and [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports") in a squad you command. A squadron includes any [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder"), [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter"), or [Space Transport](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transport "Space Transport") you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of, plus a maximum number of [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") equal to your [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") Class Level plus your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum one other [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle")). [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") must be able to communicate with you to gain the benefits of your Squadron Leader Talents, and you must designate which [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") are in your squadron at the beginning of your first turn as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

Each squadron can benefit from the [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") of only a single squadron leader. A squadron leader is normally designated when an encounter begins, but [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") without a squadron leader can join a squadron as long as both the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") and the squadron leader are willing, and the squadron leader has not reached their maximum squadron size.

## Core Talents

### Begin Attack Run

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you designate a single target. When using the [Attack Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Run "Attack Run") Action against that target, [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in your squadron gain a +5 bonus on their attack rolls (instead of the normal +2). You may have only one target designated at a time.

### Regroup

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can move all [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in your squadron +1 step on their [Condition Tracks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Tracks "Condition Tracks").

### Squadron Maneuvers

*Prerequisites:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, Any Talent from either the [Expert Pilot Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Pilot_Talent_Tree "Expert Pilot Talent Tree") or the [Gunner Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner_Talent_Tree "Gunner Talent Tree")

Choose one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that you already possess. The [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") you select must be from either the [Expert Pilot Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Pilot_Talent_Tree "Expert Pilot Talent Tree") or the [Gunner Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner_Talent_Tree "Gunner Talent Tree"). Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can impart the benefits of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") to all members of your squadron. Once gained, its benefits last until the end of the encounter.

### Squadron Tactics

*Prerequisites:* [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") 13, [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, **Squadron Maneuvers**, [Starship Tactics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Tactics "Starship Tactics")

Once per encounter, when you use a non-[*Attack Pattern*] [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver"), you grant all ships in your squadron the ability to use the same [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") once on their next turn. The [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of each ship that chooses to use the [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") must make any [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks or attack rolls the [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") requires- your success or failure with the [Starship Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Maneuver "Starship Maneuver") has no bearing on the success of other units in your squadron.
