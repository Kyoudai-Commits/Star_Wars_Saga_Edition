# Keetael Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Keetael_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Keetael_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Keetael learn to efficiently and secretly manipulate [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in combat. Because of their long lives, they gain certain advantages when attempting to view the past through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Only Force-users that are part of [The Keetael](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Keetael "The Keetael") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Conceal Force Use

You have learned to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") without telltale gestures, reducing the disturbances created in the process. Whenever you make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") you can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to convey [Deceptive Appearances](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearances "Deceptive Appearances") in order to conceal the effects of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") use. Normal modifiers for the Deception's complexity still apply.

### Force Direction

You can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to guide your [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") to their target. Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") roll, you can always add +3 (or +4, if you roll d8s for [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points")) instead of rolling the die.

### Force Momentum

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to add to the impact of your melee weapon, increasing your damage. Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") roll, if the attack hits you can also add the Force Point's result to the damage roll.

### Past Visions

*Prerequisites:* **[Visions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Visions "Visions")**

The long-lived [Draethos](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draethos "Draethos") are particularly adept at searching and understanding the past. When using *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* to look into the past, reduce your DC numbers by half. Also, you are able to see everything within 6 squares of your target clearly without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").
