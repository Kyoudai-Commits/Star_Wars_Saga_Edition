# Resist Force

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resist_Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resist_Force) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to protect yourself from an opponent's [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers").

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain +1 to the [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") score of your choice against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 20 | You gain +2 to the [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") score of your choice against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 25 | You gain +3 to the [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") score of your choice against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") used against you, until the beginning of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to apply the bonus provided by this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to any two [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") scores of your choice.

You can maintain *Resist Force* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Resist Force* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If you take damage while maintaining *Resist Force*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Resist Force*. You may have multiple instances of *Resist Force* active at once, but maintaining each one requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") each round, and each one must be activated by a separate use of *Resist Force*.
