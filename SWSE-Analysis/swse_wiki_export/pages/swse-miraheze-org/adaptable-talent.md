# Adaptable Talent

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adaptable_Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Adaptable_Talent) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You're a multitalented being, capable of pulling all kinds of tricks out of your sleeve.

**Effect:** Choose one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that you meet the prerequisites for, from a [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") you possess. Once per day, after at least 6 hours of rest, you can choose to swap out one of your current [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") for the [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") you chose for this feat. The [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") you swap out cannot be the prerequisite for any other [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") you have. You can swap back to your original [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") after at least 6 hours of rest.
