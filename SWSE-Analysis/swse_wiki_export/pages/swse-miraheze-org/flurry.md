# Flurry

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flurry](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flurry) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You attack using a series of quick strikes, sacrificing defense in the process.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When wielding only [Light Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapons "Light Weapons") or [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you can make a flurry of attacks. You take a -5 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), but you gain a +2 bonus on your [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls. This bonus on attacks and penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") apply until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** You can use this feat in place of the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") feat to qualify for the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") Prestige Class.
