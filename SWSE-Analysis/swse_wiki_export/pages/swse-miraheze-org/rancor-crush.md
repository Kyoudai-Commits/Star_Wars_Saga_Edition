# Rancor Crush

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rancor_Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rancor_Crush) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Your crushing hold on an enemy can knock it unconscious.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 15, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush"), [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin")

**Effect:** When you successfully [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") an enemy with the [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") feat and use the [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush") feat at the same time, the enemy moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") in addition to taking damage.

**Normal:** The [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush") feat deals damage to a [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") enemy but does not move the enemy down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
