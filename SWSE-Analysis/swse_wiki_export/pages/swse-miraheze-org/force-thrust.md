# Force Thrust

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to push a target away from you.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One object or character within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The target makes a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check. If you beat the target's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check, you push it back 1 square plus an additional square for every 5 points by which you exceed the target's check result. If you push the target into a larger object, the target takes 1d6 points of damage. The target adds its [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") and its size modifier to its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check: Colossal, +20; Gargantuan, +15; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium, +0; Small, -5; Tiny, -10; Diminutive, -15; Fine, -20. In addition, it gets a +5 stability bonus if it has more than two legs or is otherwise exceptionally stable.

**Q:** What happens if you use *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* against a door? How about *Force Thrust*?

**A:** If you use *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* against a door, you deal normal damage to it as the door strains against its frame. (Don't forget to apply the effects of the door's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction").) If the damage is sufficient to reduce the door to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), it becomes [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") and the door is pulled free, making the doorway passable.

If you use *Force Thrust* against a door, compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the door's [Break DC](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Break_DC "Break DC"). If successful, you have forced the door open. If not, the door remains intact and functional.

For those who are interested, an object's [Break DC](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Break_DC "Break DC") is calculated as if it were a character resisting a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") or *Force Thrust*. Take its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus, add 10 (the average result on a roll of 1d20, rounded down), and- in most cases- add 5 more for being exceptionally stable. (Some particularly fragile objects may deviate on this last detail, but that is fairly rare.)

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to apply a -5 penalty to the target's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check to resist your *Force Thrust*. Additionally, if you successfully push the target into a larger object, you deal an additional 2d6 points of damage from the extreme force of the thrust.
