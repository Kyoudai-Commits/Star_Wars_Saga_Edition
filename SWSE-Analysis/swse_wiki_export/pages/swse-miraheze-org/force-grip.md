# Force Grip

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to choke or crush your enemy.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target’s [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of damage and can only take a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on their next turn. Otherwise, the target takes half damage and may act normally, and you may not maintain the power. Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5. |
| 20 | If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target’s [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 4d6 points of damage and can only take a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on their next turn. Otherwise, the target takes half damage and may act normally, and you may not maintain the power. Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5. |
| 25 | If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target’s [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target takes 6d6 points of damage and can only take a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on their next turn. Otherwise, the target takes half damage and may act normally, and you may not maintain the power. Targets larger than Medium add a size modifier to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal +50, Gargantuan +20, Huge +10, Large +5. |

**Special:** You may maintain your concentration on the targeted creature to continue damaging it from round to round. Maintaining the *Force Grip* Power is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you suffer damage while maintaining a *Force Grip*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue concentrating.

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal an additional 2d6 points of damage with your *Force Grip*.
