# Energy Resistance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Resistance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Resistance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to protect you from damage caused by energy, sonic, fire, cold, and electrical sources.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 5 against Energy damage until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 25 | You gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10 against Energy damage until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 30 | You gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 15 against Energy damage until the beginning of your next turn. |
| 35 | You gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 20 against Energy damage until the beginning of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), rather than a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

You can maintain *Energy Resistance* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Energy Resistance* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If you take damage while maintaining *Energy Resistance*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Energy Resistance*.
