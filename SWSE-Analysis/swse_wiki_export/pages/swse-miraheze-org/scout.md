# Scout

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Scouts are natural explorers and adventurers, full of curiosity and trained to handle the out-of-the-way locations where they often operate. Scouts tend to be independent, signing on when the credits are good and their skills are best utilized and tested. Scouts understand the lay of the land and the orbit of the stars. They know how to recognize danger and locate the basic necessities for survival. The Scout seeks knowledge, tries to solve mysteries, and to find a path through the wild regions, often becoming a decent pilot along the way, and usually learns how to protect themselves from whatever hides over the next hill or beyond the most distant nebula.

## Adventurers

Many Scouts become adventurers to see what's in the next star system. They pick up skills that make them excellent members of any team. They are usually the best trackers, trailblazers, and survivalists the galaxy has to offer. Some Scouts are scientists and researchers who learn the skills of the profession so they don't have to rely on others. Some sell their services to the highest bidder. More often, the adventurous Scout has a good heart, a sense of honor, and a burning desire to embrace the wild regions of the galaxy and learn to conquer them- either figuratively or literally. Adventurous Scouts call themselves rangers, outriders, hunters, explorers, guides, adventurous scholars, and vanguards.

### Characteristics

Scouts temper insatiable curiosity with excellent survival instincts. They make use of [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), as well as [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), to spot and avoid dangers. The Scout has an undying faith in themselves and their abilities that sometimes allows the Scout to call upon reserves and an inner strength to get out of tough situations. Scouts like to be the first to reach any location, although they also want to reach it in one piece. They can be gruff or silent, jovial or talkative. They are confident and brave, and they often appreciate the wonders that the galaxy has to offer.

### Backgrounds

Scouts come to their profession in search of something, usually knowledge or secrets or the answers to the mysteries of the ages. Scholars, while possibly associated with an institute of learning or a particular government, often forsake the halls of academe for the pure research of working in the field. Pathfinders and explorers may work for a government or a military institution, or they may take on freelance contracts from anyone willing to pay for their services. Many Scouts develop into [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters"), especially those who combine military skills with their tracking and searching abilities. Every group of adventurers benefits from having a Scout in the party.

### Class Features

- **Abilities:** Most of the Scout's key [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") rely on [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"). [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") is also useful for the Scout [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in athletic abilities.
- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, a Scout gains a +1 Class bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and a +2 Class bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
- **Hit Points:** Scouts begin play at 1st level with a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 24 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At each level after 1st, Scouts gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Skills:** 1st-level Scout are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") equal to 5 + their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. They have the following [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"):
  - [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"),
  - [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"),
  - [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"),
  - [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"),
  - [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") (all; taken individually),
  - [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"),
  - [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"),
  - [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"),
  - [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride"),
  - [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"),
  - [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival"),
  - [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim")
- **Starting Feats:** At 1st level, a Scout gains the following starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):
  - [Shake It Off](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shake_It_Off "Shake It Off") (must have the prerequisite [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") of 13 and be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)"),
  - [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")
- **Force Points:** Scouts gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 5 + one-half their Character Level (rounded down) at 1st level, and every time they gain a new level in this [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") left over from the previous levels are lost.
- **Credits:** A 1st-level Scout starts play with 3d4 x 250 credits.

### Talent Trees

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Scout gains an additional [Scout Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Talent "Scout Talent"). A Scout may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Scout Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Talent_Trees "Scout Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), a Scout gains a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be selected from the [Scout Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Bonus_Feats "Scout Bonus Feats") list. As with normal [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), you must meet the prerequisites to select the [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). The Bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") must be from the [Scout Bonus Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout_Bonus_Feats "Scout Bonus Feats") list, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
