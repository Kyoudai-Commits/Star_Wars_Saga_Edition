# Lightning Draw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightning_Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightning_Draw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can draw and fire in one smooth action.

**Prerequisite:** [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can draw a holstered weapon and attack as a single [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

**Normal:** With the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat, drawing or holstering a weapon is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
