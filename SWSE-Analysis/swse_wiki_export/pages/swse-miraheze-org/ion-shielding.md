# Ion Shielding

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Shielding](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Shielding) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

Your systems are insulated to improve your resistance to [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage.

**Prerequisites:** ([Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") and [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13) or ([Cyborg Hybrid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cyborg_Hybrid "Cyborg Hybrid") and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13)

**Effect:** If the [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage (before being halved) equals or exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you move only -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Normal:** If the [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage (before being halved) equals or exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you normally move -2 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
