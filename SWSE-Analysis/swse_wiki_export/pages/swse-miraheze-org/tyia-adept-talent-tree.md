# Tyia Adept Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tyia_Adept_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tyia_Adept_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The masters of [The Tyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tyia "The Tyia") seek out Harmony while eschewing Discord. A Tyia Adept connects to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in search of unity, and attempts to align their body and spirit with the galaxy. Only Force-users that are part of [The Tyia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Tyia "The Tyia") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Cycle of Harmony

*Prerequisite:* **Tyia Adept**

When an ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") takes damage or moves down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any means, you can designate a different ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). The ally you designate gains [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum +1). Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost.

### Force Stabilize

*Prerequisite:* **Tyia Adept**

You can designate an ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"); that ally immediately takes its [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") if it has not yet done so in this encounter.

### Repel Discord

Whenever you are targeted by a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to give the creature that used that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against you a penalty on its [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate the power equal to its [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score").

### Stifle Conflict

You can choose to have any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") you activate deal [Stun Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun_Damage "Stun Damage") instead of normal damage.

### Tyia Adept

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to designate one non-[Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Until the end of your next turn, whenever you or the targeted ally takes damage from any source, you take half of the damage and your ally takes half of the damage. This halving of damage takes place before the damage is compared to your respective [Damage Thresholds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Thresholds "Damage Thresholds").
