# Pistoleer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistoleer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistoleer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are a master of wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") at the same time, and can use both [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") simultaneously in unusual ways.

## Core Talents

### Dash and Blast

*Prerequisites:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack")

Once per encounter as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), when you are wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), you may [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to twice your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") and make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with each [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol"). The normal penalties for attacking with two weapons apply to these attacks.

### Flanking Fire

*Prerequisite:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I")

Whenever you are [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked") by two (or more) opponents and are wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), you can make a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). This is provided that you target only opponents that [Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank "Flank") you and attack at least two targets.

### Guaranteed Shot

*Prerequisite:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I")

If you are wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") and make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with one of those [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), even if you miss you deal damage equal to half your Heroic Level to the target. This consumes a single shot from the weapon not making the attack, and the weapon you attack with uses as many shots required by the attack.

### Hailfire

*Prerequisite:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I")

When you are wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can make an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack with one of the [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") as though the weapon were set to [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), even if the [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") would not normally be capable of [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). The normal penalties for [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") still apply to this attack roll, and you may split the number of shots consumed between the two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols").

### Twin Shot

*Prerequisites:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot")

When you are wielding two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls when using the [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feat.
