# Force Regimen Mastery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimen_Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimen_Mastery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You learn one or more [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens"), which you can use to enhance your regular training.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"), [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

**Effect:** You learn a number of [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1).

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, you learn a number of additional [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1). If your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier permanently increases, you immediately gain a number of [Force Regimens](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Regimens "Force Regimens") equal to the number of Force Regimen Mastery feats you have taken.
