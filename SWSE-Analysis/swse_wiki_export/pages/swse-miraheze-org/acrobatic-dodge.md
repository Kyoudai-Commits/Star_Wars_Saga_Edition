# Acrobatic Dodge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Dodge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

When you react to an attack, your acrobatics training improves your mobility.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge"), [Mobility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobility "Mobility"), [Skill Focus (Acrobatics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Acrobatics) "Skill Focus (Acrobatics)")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") if a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") misses you, you can immediately move into an adjacent square without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

**Special:** You must be aware of the attacker to benefit from this feat. You can use this feat a second time during the encounter by spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").
