# Pushing Slash

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pushing_Slash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pushing_Slash) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*, *[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

You strike at your target with your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), then hurl them away from you with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. If the attack hits, in addition to dealing damage, compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check with the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you push the target 2 squares away from you. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). You cannot move the target if it is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |

If the attack hits, in addition to dealing damage, compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check with the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you push the target 3 squares away from you. You cannot move the target if it is [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Niman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Niman "Niman")):** If you have the **[Niman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Niman "Niman")** Talent, you also compare the results of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to all other adjacent enemies; if your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") result equals or exceeds an enemy's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), it is pushed away 1 square. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to knock the target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") at the end of its forced movement. (This applies only to the target of your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack).
