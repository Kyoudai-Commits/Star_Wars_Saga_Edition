# Force Scream

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Scream](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Scream) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You create an intense sonic scream, amplified by [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All creatures that are within 12 squares and can hear you

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**Compare the result of the check to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the check equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target sustains damage as determined by the results of your check. The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target(s) takes 1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage this round, and the target(s)'s [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is reduced by -5 points until the end of your next turn. |
| 20 | The target(s) takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage this round, and the target(s)'s [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is reduced by -5 points until the end of your next turn. |
| 25 | The target(s) takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage this round, and the target(s)'s [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is reduced by -5 points until the end of your next turn. |
| 30 | The target(s) takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage this round, and the target(s)'s [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is reduced by -5 points until the end of your next turn. |

The target adds a size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") for purposes of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium or smaller, +0.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reduce the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by -10, rather than -5.
