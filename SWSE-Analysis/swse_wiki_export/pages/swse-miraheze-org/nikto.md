# Nikto

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/5/5e/Nikto.webp/300px-Nikto.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Nikto.webp)

Widely recognized as a slave species of the [Hutts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutts "Hutts"), the Nikto have served their masters since before the founding of [The Old Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic "The Old Republic"), proving to be cunning warriors in the Hutts' battle with the legendary conqueror [Xim the Despot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Xim_the_Despot?action=edit&redlink=1 "Xim the Despot (page does not exist)"). Wherever the [Hutts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutts "Hutts") are, the Nikto are never far behind. Because of their homeworld's radioactive atmosphere, the Nikto have five distinct subspecies, each adapted to a specific type of terrain (see the [Nikto Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto_Survival "Nikto Survival") feat).

## Nikto Characteristics

**Personality:** Although lacking in personal charisma, Nikto posses a single-minded tenacity that makes them excellent followers. Eons of enslavement by the [Hutts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutts "Hutts") have left them more comfortable with taking orders from their masters than with thinking on their feet.

**Physical Description:** Tough, leathery skin and a lack of extensive facial muscles limit the range of Nikto facial expression. Their deep black eyes further enhance their reptilian appearance. A Nikto's subspecies can be determined by his or her skin tone and the presence of small spikes or horns. A typical Nikto stands at 1.8 meters tall and weighs 60 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Nikto age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 Years | 11-15 Years | 16-35 Years | 36-54 Years | 55-64 Years | 65+ Years |

**Homeworld:** [Kintan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kintan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kintan (page does not exist)"), a radioactive planet located within Hutt Space in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** All Nikto learn to read, speak, and write their own Nikto language along with either Basic or Huttese.

**Example Names:** Bok, Fi-Ek Sirch, Giran, Klaatu, Ma'kis'shaalas, Nysad, Wooof.

**Adventurers:** Nikto have the tenacity to pursue any career, but their status as a slave Species to the [Hutts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hutts "Hutts") forces them into roles as guards and [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers").

## Nikto Species Traits

Nikto share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Nikto receive a +2 bonus to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), but suffer -2 penalties to both their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Nikto are tough and hardened, but their subservient status stunts their willpower and creativity.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Nikto have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Nikto have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Great Fortitude:** Nikto gain a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), having evolved to survive the harsh environment of their homeworld.
- **Natural Armor:** A Nikto's tough hide provides a +2 Natural Armor bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). A Natural Armor bonus stacks with an Armor bonus.
- **Survival Instinct:** A Nikto can choose to reroll any [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Nikto can speak, read, and write Nikto, as well as either Basic or Huttese (chosen at [Character Creation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Creation "Character Creation")).
