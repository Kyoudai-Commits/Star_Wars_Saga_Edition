# Hold Together

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hold_Together](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hold_Together) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Sometimes [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") seem to hold together by your force of will.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to delay the effect of damage dealt to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are riding in or piloting until the end of the round. The [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") must be of Colossal size or smaller.
