# Pinpoint Accuracy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinpoint_Accuracy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinpoint_Accuracy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

This improved version of the [Aiming Accuracy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming_Accuracy "Aiming Accuracy") feat moves your enemy -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Prerequisite:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Aiming Accuracy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming_Accuracy "Aiming Accuracy"), [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** When using the [Aiming Accuracy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming_Accuracy "Aiming Accuracy") feat, a target you damage cannot take the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action until the end of its next turn.
