# Dodge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at dodging blows.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** During your turn, you designate an opponent and receive a +1 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against attacks from that opponent. You can select a new opponent on any action. A situation that makes you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (if any) also makes you lose dodge bonuses. Also, dodge bonuses stack with each other, unlike most other types of [Bonuses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonuses "Bonuses").

The [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") may use Dodge to grant the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") a +1 dodge bonus against a single opponent (whether a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or a single character).

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Charging Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire "Charging Fire") feat, you reduce your penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against all opponents to -1 when making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"). Additionally, you can add your dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against anyone who makes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against you while using the [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack") feat.
