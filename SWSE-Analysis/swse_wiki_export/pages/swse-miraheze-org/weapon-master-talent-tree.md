# Weapon Master Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Master_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Master_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at wielding a variety of weapons and can wield choice weapons with deadly precision and force.

## Core Talents

### Controlled Burst

Your penalty when making an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack or using the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") feat is reduced to -2. In addition, if you [Brace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brace "Brace") an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire")-only weapon, you have no penalty on your attack roll.

### Exotic Weapon Mastery

You are considered proficient with any [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"), even if you don't possess the appropriate [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency "Exotic Weapon Proficiency") feat.

### Greater Devastating Attack

*Prerequisites:* **Greater Weapon Focus** (Chosen Weapon), **[Devastating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastating_Attack "Devastating Attack")** (Chosen Weapon)

Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you're proficient. Whenever you make a successful attack against a target using the chosen [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or weapon from the chosen group, you treat your target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") as if it were 10 points lower when determining the results of your attack. This replaces the effects of the **[Devastating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastating_Attack "Devastating Attack")** Talent.

### Greater Penetrating Attack

*Prerequisites:* **Greater Weapon Focus** (Chosen Weapon), **[Penetrating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Penetrating_Attack "Penetrating Attack")** (Chosen Weapon)

Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you're proficient. Whenever you make a successful attack against a target using the chosen [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or weapon from the chosen group, you treat your target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") as if it were 10 points lower when determining the results of your attack. This replaces the effects of the **[Penetrating Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Penetrating_Attack "Penetrating Attack")** Talent.

### Greater Weapon Focus

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

Choose one [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you're proficient. You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with the chosen [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or weapon from the chosen group. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat. You may select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

### Greater Weapon Specialization

*Prerequisites:* **Greater Weapon Focus** (Chosen Weapon), **[Weapon Specialization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization "Weapon Specialization")** (Chosen Weapon)

Choose one [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or one of the following weapon groups: [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons"), [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"), [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles"), or [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"). You gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls with the chosen [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or a weapon from the chosen group. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the **[Weapon Specialization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization "Weapon Specialization")** Talent. You may select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

### Multiattack Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Heavy Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapon "Heavy Weapon") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (see [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack")), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Rifles)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (see [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack")), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

## Additional Talents

### Extended Threat

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

When using a ranged weapon eligible to make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"), you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") all squares within a 2-square radius.

### Ferocious Assault

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +12, **Controlled Burst**

Once per encounter, when making an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack, you can treat the attack as a 6-Square [Cone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cone "Cone"). Making this attack consumes 20 shots from the weapon's [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack").

### Multiattack Proficiency (Simple Weapons)

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Simple Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon "Simple Weapon") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (see [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack")), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Two-For-One Throw

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can [Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw "Throw") two weapons simultaneously. Both weapons target the same enemy or target square and are thrown with one hand. Make separate attack rolls for each weapon, each at a -10 penalty. The weapons must be similar, such as two [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades") or two [Knives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knives "Knives"), and they must be no larger than one size category smaller than you. The attack cannot exceed Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") (typically 8 squares).
