# Gran

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gran](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gran) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/f/f1/Gran.webp/300px-Gran.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Gran.webp)

Gran originally came from the world and system of [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)") in [The Expansion Region](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Expansion_Region?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Expansion Region (page does not exist)"). They are longtime members of galactic society, with numerous colonies. Although most of these colonies are peaceful, their colony on [Malastare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malastare "Malastare") quickly became embroiled in a bitter conflict with the native [Dugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dugs "Dugs"), forcing the Gran to act aggressively to protect themselves.

Gran place society over self, and they receive training that complements both their personal talents and society's needs. The desire to maintain balance within society caused the Gran to establish alien-only areas of [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)"). This was intended to restrict alien influence and avoid trouble between [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), such as what happened on [Malastare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malastare "Malastare"). During [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), the Empire has pushed the Gran leadership to grant access to all areas. When the Gran refused, [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)") was subjected to orbital bombardment. The [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)") Gran relented, but the incident inspired many Gran to resist the new Empire.

## Gran Characteristics

**Personality:** Gran are highly social and unable to stand solitude, or isolation from other Gran, for any length of time. Most are talkative, friendly, and hospitable. They are slow to anger. Gran friendships are for life. Gran from [Malastare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malastare "Malastare") and other colonies follow looser social codes than those from the homeworld .

**Physical Description:** The beige-skinned Gran have three distinctive eye stalks with pitch-black eyes, ahead of small horns and large, triangular ears. Males and females have similar builds. A typical Gran stands 1.6 meters tall and weighs 80 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Gran age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 Years | 11-15 Years | 16-40 Years | 41-62 Years | 63-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)"), with colonies on many other worlds, including [Hok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hok?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hok (page does not exist)") and [Malastare](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malastare "Malastare").

**Languages:** Gran speak, read, and write their own language of Gran, as well as Basic.

**Example Names:** Ainlee Teem, Aks Moe, Ask Aak, Baskol Yeesrim, Cera Vixe, Cruegar, Kea R- Lan, Mawhonic, Nadin Paal, Ree-Yees, Vee Naaq.

**Adventurers:** Heroic Gran are often [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles"), [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels"), [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), and bounty hunters. Prior to [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), Gran [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") were common, and it is possible that some survived the initial Jedi Purge. After the bombardment of [Kinyen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kinyen?action=edit&redlink=1 "Kinyen (page does not exist)"), individualistic Gran find themselves open to other methods of serving society, looking for new ways to resist the Empire.

## Gran Species Traits

Gran share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Gran receive a +2 bonus to their [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"). Gran are highly social creatures, but tend to value artistic value higher than scientific achievement.
- **Medium Size:** As medium creatures, Gran have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Gran have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** A Gran with the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") feat gains the [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot") feat as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Darkvision:** Gran ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. However, they cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
- **Innate Accuracy:** Once per encounter, a Gran can [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") by taking a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions").
- **Target Awareness:** A Gran takes no distance penalties on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks made to [Notice Targets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Notice_Targets "Notice Targets") for the first 50 squares between the Gran and its targets.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Gran can speak, read, and write both Basic and Gran.
