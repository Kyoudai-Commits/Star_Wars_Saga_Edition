# Jedi Sentinel Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Sentinel_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Sentinel_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") that follow the path of the Sentinel are the true enemies of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), hunting down evil and stopping the spread of darkness wherever they go. You are a master of subtlety and difficult to tempt with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), even when constantly confronted by its power.

## Core Talents

### Clear Mind

You may reroll any opposed [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made to oppose [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") checks. You must take the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Dark Side Sense

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") following the path of the Sentinel become exceptionally talented at rooting out evil. You may reroll any [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made to [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") against the presence and relative location of characters with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher. You must take the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Dark Side Scourge

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Sense**

Against creatures with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, you deal extra damage on melee attacks equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus (minimum +1).

### Force Haze

*Prerequisite:* **Clear Mind**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to create a "Haze" that hides you and your allies from the perception of others. You can hide a number of creatures in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") equal to your Class Level. Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and compare the result to the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of any opponent that moves into [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") of any creature hidden by your **Force Haze**. If your check result equals or exceeds the opponent's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), all hidden creatures are treated as if they had [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against the opponent.

The **Force Haze** lasts for up to 1 minute but is dismissed instantly if anyone hidden by the **Force Haze** makes an attack.

You may use this Talent to hide a single [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") while you are on board. The [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can't have a size penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), or [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks greater than your Heroic Level. Thus, a Gargantuan Starship (size penalty of -5) can be hidden only by a character of 5th level or higher.

### Resist the Dark Side

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Sense**

You gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") scores against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") originating from any dark Force-user (that is, any Force-use whose [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equals their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score).

## Additional Talents

### Dampen Presence

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

When you interact with another sentient creature, you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to reduce the impression you leave on it. When you have finished interacting with the creature, you make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, and if the check result exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), it does not remember interacting with you once you are gone.

A target that is higher in level than you gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") to resist this ability. This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect.

### Dark Retaliation

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Sentinel Strike**

Once per encounter, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to activate a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being targeted by a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor.

### Dark Side Bane

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Sense**

When you use a damage-dealing [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against a creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, you deal extra damage on a successful hit equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus (minimum +1).

### Gradual Resistance

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

If you take damage from the use of a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), until the end of the encounter you gain a +2 Force bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").

### Master of the Great Hunt

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on attack rolls and deal +1 die of damage on [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attacks made against a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+.

### Persistent Haze

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Clear Mind**, **Force Haze**

Whenever anyone concealed by your use of the **Force Haze** Talent attacks, you maintain [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") without having to make another [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. Only those who do not attack remain concealed; the attacker no longer has [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment"), even when using his Talent.

### Prime Targets

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

When you hit a target with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, if the target has not been attacked since the end of your last turn, you deal +1 die of damage.

### Reap Retribution

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

If you take damage from the use of a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), until the end of the encounter you deal an extra 2 points of damage against the creature that used the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against you.

### Sense Primal Force

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who spend time in a diverse wilderness learn the nuances of [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force") at work within a wild ecosystem. When within a natural wilderness area, such as a jungle, a forest, a swamp, or plains, you tap into the vibrant Living Force of the area and can use the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") aspect of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill to detect targets out to a 30-square radius, regardless of [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

### Sentinel Strike

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Any time you attack a [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") opponent (or one who is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against you) with a damage-dealing [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), or attack with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you deal an extra +1d6 points of damage with that attack. This Talent does not affect Force Powers with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, your **Sentinel Strike** damage increases by +1d6 points (maximum +5d6 points of damage).

### Sentinel's Gambit

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate an adjacent opponent with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher as the target of this Talent. The designated opponent loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against your attacks until the end of your next turn.

### Sentinel's Observation

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

If you have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") against a target, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls against that target.

### Steel Resolve

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

When you use a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to make a melee attack, you can take a penalty between -1 to -5 on your attack roll and add twice that value (+2 to +10) as an insight bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). This bonus may not exceed your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). The changes to attack rolls and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") last until the start of your next turn.

### Unseen Eyes

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Clear Mind**, **Force Haze**

Whenever you use the **Force Haze** Talent, allies hidden by the **Force Haze** can reroll any [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, keeping the better of the two results. Additionally, allies hidden by the **Force Haze** gain a +2 bonus on all damage rolls against foes that are unaware of them.
