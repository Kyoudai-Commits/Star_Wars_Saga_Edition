# Strafe

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strafe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strafe) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You can make a [Strafing Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strafing_Attack "Strafing Attack") without a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you make an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack, instead of attacking a 2-square-by-2-square area, you may attack a line 1 square wide and 4 squares long.

**Special:** If you are using a [Jet Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jet_Pack "Jet Pack"), you can use the Strafe feat to make an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack against all squares you fly over.
