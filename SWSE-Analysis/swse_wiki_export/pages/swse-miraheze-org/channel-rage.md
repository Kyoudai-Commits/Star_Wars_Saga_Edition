# Channel Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Channel_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Channel_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You have learned to turn your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") into pure will and focus.

**Prerequisite:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage")

**Effect:** Once per day, instead of entering into a [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), you gain a +5 bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the end of the encounter. This bonus counts as using your [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") ability for that day.
