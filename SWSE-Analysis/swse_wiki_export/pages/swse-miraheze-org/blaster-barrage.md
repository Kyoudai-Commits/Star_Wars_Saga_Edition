# Blaster Barrage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Barrage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Barrage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You and your allies engulf an area with dense blaster fire.

**Prerequisite:** [Coordinated Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack "Coordinated Attack")

**Effect:** When you make an attack with a weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") that deals damage to at least one target within the designated area, you grant a +2 circumstance bonus on any of your allies' [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attacks made against that same target until the beginning of your next turn.
