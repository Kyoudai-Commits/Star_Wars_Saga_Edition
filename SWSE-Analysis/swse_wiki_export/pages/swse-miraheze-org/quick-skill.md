# Quick Skill

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Skill) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Your cautious nature provides a bonus to accomplishing extremely dangerous tasks. You are steady and confident.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when rushed on a single [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") for a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in, unless the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") description explicitly forbids it. Alternatively, you can [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") when using a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in at half the normal time required.

**Normal:** [Taking 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taking_20 "Taking 20") normally requires 20 times the amount of time needed by a regular [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check").
