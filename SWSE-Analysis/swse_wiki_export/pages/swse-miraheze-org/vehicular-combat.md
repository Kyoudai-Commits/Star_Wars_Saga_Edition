# Vehicular Combat

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can avoid attacks made against your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

**Effect:** Once per round (as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")), when you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), you may negate a weapon hit by making a successful [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check. The DC of the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate. In addition, while you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you are considered proficient with Pilot-operated [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").
