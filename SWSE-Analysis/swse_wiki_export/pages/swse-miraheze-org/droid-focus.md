# Droid Focus

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Focus) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

Choose a single degree of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ([1st-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree "1st-Degree"), [2nd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree "2nd-Degree"), [3rd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree "3rd-Degree"), [4th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree "4th-Degree"), or [5th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree "5th-Degree")). You are especially familiar with this class of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), gaining the listed bonuses while working on or against them.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

**Effect:** Select a degree of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ([1st-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree "1st-Degree"), [2nd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree "2nd-Degree"), [3rd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree "3rd-Degree"), [4th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree "4th-Degree"), or [5th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree "5th-Degree")). You gain a +1 bonus to [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks when used on or against a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") of the selected degree, and a +1 bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against attack rolls and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made by [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") of the selected degree.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different degree of [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ([1st-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree "1st-Degree"), [2nd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/2nd-Degree "2nd-Degree"), [3rd-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree "3rd-Degree"), [4th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree "4th-Degree"), or [5th-Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree "5th-Degree")).
