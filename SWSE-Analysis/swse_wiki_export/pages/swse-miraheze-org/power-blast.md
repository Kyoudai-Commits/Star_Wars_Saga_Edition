# Power Blast

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Blast) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can make exceptionally powerful [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks").

**Effect:** As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), before making an attack roll, you can choose to subtract a number from all [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") rolls and add the same number to all ranged damage rolls. This number cannot exceed your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). The penalty on attacks and bonus on damage applies until the start of your next turn but you gain no bonus to damage with [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") or when attacking objects or [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") with this feat.

**Special:** If you do not have a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") score of 13 or higher, you take a -5 penalty to attacks when using this Feat with non-[Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").
