# Veteran Spacer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Veteran_Spacer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Veteran_Spacer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your people are among the oldest explorers to chart the stars, and you benefit from eons of collected knowledge about space travel.

**Prerequisite:** [Duros](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duros "Duros")

**Effect:** You gain a +5 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks made to [Astrogate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogate "Astrogate") aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship").
