# Separatist Military Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Separatist_Military_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Separatist_Military_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have received extensive training from the military of [The Confederacy of Independent Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems "The Confederacy of Independent Systems") and are familiar with its practices.

**Effect:** While you are adjacent to at least one of your allies, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on any one attack roll you make on your turn.
