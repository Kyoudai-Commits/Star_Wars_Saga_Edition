# Perfect Swimmer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfect_Swimmer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfect_Swimmer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are among the best of your kind when swimming and can navigate even the most difficult waters.

**Prerequisite:** [Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan "Gungan")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.
