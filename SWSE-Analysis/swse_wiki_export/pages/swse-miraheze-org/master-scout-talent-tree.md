# Master Scout Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Scout_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Scout_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You are more experienced and talented than the average [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout"). You draw upon your diverse abilities to gain advantages others cannot match.

## Core Talents

### Piercing Hit

*Prerequisites:* **[Acute Senses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acute_Senses "Acute Senses")**, **[Keen Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Keen_Shot "Keen Shot")**

You are skilled at fighting armored characters, taking advantage of their weaknesses. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Binding Hit:* You dislodge an enemy's armor, causing it to hinder the wearer's defenses. Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If you hit and damage your target, the target loses its Armor bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and is [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"). This effect remains until the target spends a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to adjust its armor.
- *Blinding Fire:* Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If the attack hits and damages the target, the target takes a -2 penalty to all attacks until the end of your next turn. Additionally, all other creatures, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from the target until the end of your next turn.
- *Slowing Shot:* Your hit damages your target's armor so that the target's movement is hindered. Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If you hit and damage the target, the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by two squares until the end of your next turn.

### Quicktrap

*Prerequisites:* **[Jury-Rigger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigger "Jury-Rigger")**, **Tripwire**

You can use the **Tripwire** Talent as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Speedclimber

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), **[Long Stride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Long_Stride "Long Stride")**, **[Surefooted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surefooted "Surefooted")**

You do not suffer penalties when using the [Accelerated Climbing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accelerated_Climbing "Accelerated Climbing") application of the [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") skill.

### Surprisingly Quick

*Prerequisite:* [Skill Focus (Initiative)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Initiative) "Skill Focus (Initiative)")

In a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") in addition to the one other [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") normally allowed. If you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

*Normal:* In a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), or a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take no [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions").

### Tripwire

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), **[Jury-Rigger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigger "Jury-Rigger")**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can set up a simple snare or trap across an opening up to 3 squares wide. You must have the required items to set the trap. Make a successful DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to set the trap. Make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to conceal the wire. Compare the result of the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check of the next creature that passes through that square (-10 if it observes you setting the trap).

If the creature fails the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, it takes the indicated use of the trap. If the creature succeeds, it can make a DC 10 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check to avoid the wire. Select one of the following options:

- *Clothesline:* Requires thin wire, set at neck height. Success results in the target falling [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in the trapped square, ending its [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") for the turn and taking 1d6 points of damage.
- *Electronic Tripwire:* Success results in [Mine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mine (page does not exist)") damage (treat as [Frag Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frag_Grenade "Frag Grenade"), 2-square blast radius centered on one end of the wire). Target is automatically hit; roll 1d20+10 against the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of adjacent targets.
- *Tripwire:* Requires thin wire, set at ankle height. Add 2 to the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check result. Success results in the target immediately falling [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in the trapped square and ending its [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") for the turn.
