# Dual Weapon Mastery I

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at fighting with two weapons and double weapons.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you attack with two weapons, or with both ends of a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon") as part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you take a -5 penalty (instead of a -10 penalty) on all attack rolls until the start of your next turn. You only gain this reduced penalty if you are wielding a weapon with which you are proficient. [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") cannot be used in one hand, so this feat has no effect on them.

**Normal:** If you use a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action to make more than one attack on your turn, you take a -10 penalty on all attack rolls for the round.

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat, you can draw or holster two weapons with a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"); you must have both hands free to draw both weapons, and each weapon must be able to be held in one hand.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** I'm curious as to the perceived balance factor of Dual Weapon Mastery versus [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack"). Dual Weapon Mastery I is available sooner, and you can offset its penalties by taking more [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"). [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") works for only one type of weapon, and you can reduce its penalties only with Prestige Class [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). Since both grant an extra attack, why would you want [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")?

**A:** The balancing factor is that Dual Weapon Mastery works only with [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") weapons (that is, weapons that do less damage), but [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") works with [Two-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Two-Handed "Two-Handed") weapons (but only one type of them). For example:

- A Dual Weapon Mastery specialist might use two [Heavy Blaster Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Pistols "Heavy Blaster Pistols"), which deal 3d8 points of damage per weapon, or a potential of 6d8 points from both.
- A [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") specialist might use a [Heavy Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Rifle "Heavy Blaster Rifle"), which deals 3d10 points of damage but has a much better [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). In addition, the weapon is capable of [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), so it can deal up to 5d10 points of damage with [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire").

This also means that [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") can be used with much larger weapons, such as those on a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), while a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") would have no particular reason to use Dual Weapon Mastery because such weapons can't be operated with one hand.

Finally, don't forget that someone can use both Dual Weapon Mastery and [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack"). So even if Dual Weapon Mastery appears more attractive at first, you might want to take [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")/[Triple Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Attack "Triple Attack") later.
