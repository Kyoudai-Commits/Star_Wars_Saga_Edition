# Recall

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recall) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You know a lot of details in your area of knowledge, but don't always remember them immediately.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in at least one [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skill

**Effect:** Once per day, you can reroll any check for a [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skill that you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in, using the better result.
