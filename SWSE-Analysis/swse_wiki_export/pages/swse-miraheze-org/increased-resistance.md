# Increased Resistance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increased_Resistance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increased_Resistance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You remain resilient against attacks that target your vitality, making it difficult for enemies to sap your strength.

**Prerequisite:** [Gamorrean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorrean "Gamorrean")

**Effect:** Whenever an attack or [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") targets your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and fails to equal or exceed it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") until the start of your next turn.
