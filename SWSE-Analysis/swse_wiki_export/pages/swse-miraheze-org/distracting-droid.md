# Distracting Droid

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Distracting_Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Distracting_Droid) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You use lights, holographic projectors, sudden movements, erratic flight, odd noises, or other uniquely Droid techniques to distract an enemy.

**Prerequisite:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

**Effect:** As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of all enemies that are within 6 squares of you and that can see or hear you, as determined by the Gamemaster. If the check equals or exceeds an enemy's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the enemy loses one [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn. If the attack exceeds an enemy's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") by 10 or more, the enemy is also [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
