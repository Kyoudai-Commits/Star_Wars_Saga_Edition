# Exile Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exile_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exile_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You have broken with your family, world, or organization and cannot rely on its aid or support. Whether forced or self-imposed, your exile allows you to adapt in new ways to the galaxy at large.

## Core Talents

### Arrogant Bluster

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

You take on an air of arrogance, allowing you to distract and sway others with your apparent knowledge and experience. When you make a successful [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to change an enemy's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"), the enemy takes a -5 penalty to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the end of your next turn. If you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), the duration is extended to the end of the encounter. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Band Together

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") and [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), **Galactic Guidance**, **Self-Reliant**

You can inspire disparate beings to temporarily ally for a common cause. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Directed Attack:* As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), designate one enemy character or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") as the target of this Talent. Until the end of your next turn, whenever an ally within 12 squares of you hits the target, add 1d6 points of damage to each hit.
- *Strength in Numbers:* Your allies gain confidence in numbers. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you grant all allies within 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") a +5 bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the end of your next turn. You must have a minimum of two allies to use this action.
- *Temporary Allies:* With a successful [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, you can turn a Gamemaster character with an [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") toward you of [Unfriendly](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unfriendly "Unfriendly") or [Indifferent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Indifferent "Indifferent") into an ally willing to aid you and follow your direction for the remainder of the encounter. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you direct the Gamemaster character to attack a target, aid another character, or use a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to aid you or your allies. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Galactic Guidance

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)")

You share your considerable galactic knowledge to inform others' actions and decisions. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), if you succeed on a DC 25 [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") check, you enable one ally within 6 squares of you to reroll a failed [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence")- or [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom")-based [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") (other than [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception")).

### Rant

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

You are a master of voicing your opinion loudly, continuously, and distractingly. If you succeed in making a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") an enemy that is within 6 squares of you and that can hear, see, and understand you, you can deny that enemy the use of a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn, instead of gaining the normal [Intimidation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidation "Intimidation") results. You grant one ally a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to use immediately (the ally uses the [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")).

### Self-Reliant

*Prerequisite:* At least one Talent from the [Inspiration Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree "Inspiration Talent Tree")

You need not rely on others when the going gets tough. Once per encounter, you can use one Talent that you possess from the [Inspiration Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree "Inspiration Talent Tree") on yourself, despite not normally being able to do so.
