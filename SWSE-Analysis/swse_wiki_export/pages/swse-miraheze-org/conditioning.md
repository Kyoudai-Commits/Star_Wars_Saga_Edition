# Conditioning

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditioning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditioning) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are in excellent physical condition, enabling you to extend physical activities. You might not have great strength, but you have a lot of stamina.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13

**Effect:** You can reroll any [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength")- or [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution")-based [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in. The result of the reroll must be accepted even if it is worse. Additionally, once per encounter you can add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"); this bonus lasts until the beginning of your next turn.
