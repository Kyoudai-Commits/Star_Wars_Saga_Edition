# Great Cleave

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Great_Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Great_Cleave) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can wield a melee weapon with such power that you can strike multiple times when you drop your opponents.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack"), [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave")

**Effect:** As the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat, except that you have no limit to the number of times you can use it per round.
