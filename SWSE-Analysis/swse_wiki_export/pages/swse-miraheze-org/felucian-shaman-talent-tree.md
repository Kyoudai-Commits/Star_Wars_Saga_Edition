# Felucian Shaman Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucian_Shaman_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucian_Shaman_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Felucian Shamans learn to manipulate their natural aptitude with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and amplify their inherent powers in a variety of ways. Only Force-users that are part of [The Felucian Shamans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Felucian_Shamans "The Felucian Shamans") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Felucian Charm Beast

You may use a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check when attempting to change the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of an undomesticated creature with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 2 or less. Additionally, you do not take the normal -5 penalty on the check if the creature can't speak or understand your language.

### Felucian Command Beast

*Prerequisite:* **Felucian Charm Beast**

Whenever you manage to shift the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") to Indifferent or Friendly, you may treat that creature as a domesticated animal- but for you only (it remains undomesticated in its response to other creatures). Additionally, you may use this [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") as a mount, as per the [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") skill, provided that it is at least one size category larger than you and has a comfortable place for you to sit.

### Detonating Blast

*Prerequisite:* *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")*

When you use the *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")* Force Power, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of all other characters, creatures, and [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") within 2 squares of your target. If you exceed the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of those secondary targets, they also take damage from the *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")*, and if you fail to exceed their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), they take half damage instead. This is considered to be an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") against the secondary targets (but not against the original target).

### Hive Mind

You can use the [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you automatically succeed (no roll required) if your target is a willing recipient on the same planet.

### Infuse Weapon

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to infuse an unpowered melee weapon (one that does not require an [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell")) with the strength of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), making it resistant to the attacks of other weapons. Infusing the weapon takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). From that point forward, when you wield the weapon, its [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") is doubled, and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") do not ignore the weapon's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"). When you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify the attack roll of an **Infused Weapon**, you also add 2 x the Force Point's result to the damage roll if the attack succeeds.

### Sickening Blast

*Prerequisite:* *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")*

When you use the *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")* Force Power, if your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you can choose to move the target -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Doing so increases your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1.

### Skullblade Mastery

*Skullblade Mastery Talent created by Fandom user LilLiteralist.*

You treat a [Felucian Skullblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucian_Skullblade "Felucian Skullblade") as a [Simple Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Melee) "Simple Weapon (Melee)") instead of as an [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)"). Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with a [Felucian Skullblade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucian_Skullblade "Felucian Skullblade"). This Talent counts as the [Weapon Focus (Felucian Skullblade)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Felucian_Skullblade) "Weapon Focus (Felucian Skullblade)") Feat for the purpose of satisfying prerequisites. If you also have the [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)") Feat, the attack bonus provided by this Talent does not stack with the attack bonus provided by [Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Simple Weapons)").
