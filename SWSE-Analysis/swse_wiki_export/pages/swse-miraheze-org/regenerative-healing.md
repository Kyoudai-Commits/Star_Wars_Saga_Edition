# Regenerative Healing

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Regenerative_Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Regenerative_Healing) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your natural regeneration can help you recuperate from wounds more quickly.

**Prerequisite:** [Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan "Trandoshan")

**Effect:** Once per day when you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you can regain no [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") immediately and instead regain 5 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") at the end of each of your turns until you reach full [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or until the end of the encounter (whichever comes first).
