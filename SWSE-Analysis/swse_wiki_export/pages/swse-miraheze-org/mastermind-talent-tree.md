# Mastermind Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mastermind_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mastermind_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You have the ability to attract loyal **Minions**, and are skilled at redirecting allies on the battlefield.

## Core Talents

### Attract Minion

You attract a loyal **Minion**. The **Minion** is a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character with a Class Level equal to three-quarters of your Character Level, rounded down. You may select this Talent multiple times; each time you select this Talent, you gain another **Minion**. Normally, you can have only one **Minion** with you at a time. Any other **Minions** you have are assumed to be looking after your various interests. If you lose a **Minion**, you can send for another Minion if you have one (although normal travel time still applies).

Each **Minion** that accompanies you on an adventure is entitled to an equal share of the total [Experience Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experience_Points "Experience Points") earned for that adventure. For example, a **Minion** that accompanies a party of five heroes on an adventure receives one-sixth of the XP that the group earns.

### Impel Ally I

You can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to grant one ally the ability to move its normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). The ally must move immediately on your turn, before you do anything else, or else the opportunity is wasted. You can use this Talent up to three times on your turn (spending a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") each time).

### Impel Ally II

*Prerequisite:* **Impel Ally I**

You can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to grant one ally the ability to take a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") or [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). The ally must act immediately on your turn, before you do anything else, or else the opportunity is wasted.

## Additional Talents

### Attract Superior Minion

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisites:* **Attract Minion**, **Impel Ally II**

You attract a particularly skilled and powerful **Superior Minion**. The Minion is a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character with a Class Level equal to your Character Level. This Talent otherwise functions as the **Attract Minion** Talent.

### Bodyguard I

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Attract Minion**

Whenever you are adjacent to a **Minion** gained with the **Attract Minion** Talent, once per turn as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being attacked you can redirect the attack against that **Minion**. Compare the attack roll to the **Minion's** [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") and resolve the attack as normal.

### Bodyguard II

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Bodyguard I**

When you redirect an attack to a **Minion** using the **Bodyguard I** Talent, that minion's relevant [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") gains a bonus equal to half your Class Level.

### Bodyguard III

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Bodyguard II**

When you redirect an attack to a **Minion** using the **Bodyguard I** Talent, that **Minion** can make an immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against your attacker, if the attacker is within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). Additionally, the bonus provided by the **Bodyguard II** Talent increases to your full Class Level.

### Contingency Plan

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Once per encounter, if you fail an attack roll, a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), or the use of a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that requires an opposed check, you can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Impel Ally III

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Impel Ally II**

Once per encounter, you can spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") on consecutive turns to grant one ally the ability to take a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). The ally must act immediately on your turn when the final [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") is spent, before you do anything else, or the opportunity is wasted.

### Inspire Wrath

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Impel Ally II**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can designate a target to be the object of your allies' wrath. While your allies have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to you or until you are unconscious or dead, your allies gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls against the target and a +2 morale bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") against that target. You can designate a new target on any round by using another [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). You can only use this Talent against one opponent at a time.

### Master's Orders

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Impel Ally II**

When an ally uses an [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") granted to him or her by you, the ally can reroll any attack or check made during that [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action"), taking the better result.

### Shelter

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Attract Minion**

Whenever you are adjacent to a **Minion**, you increase any [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by +2.

### Tactical Superiority

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to select two allies. Each ally can move 2 squares as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Tactical Withdraw

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to grant all allies that are in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and within 6 squares of you the ability to use the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") until the start of your next turn.

### Urgency

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Impel Ally II**

Once per encounter, you can spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") on consecutive turns to increase the [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of all allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") of you by 2. The increased [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") lasts until the start of your next turn after the third [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") is spent.

### Wealth of Allies

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Attract Minion**

Whenever one of your **Minions** is killed, he or she is replaced by another **Minion** of the same level. This replacement occurs 24 hours later.
