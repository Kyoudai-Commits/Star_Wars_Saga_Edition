# Aiming Accuracy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming_Accuracy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming_Accuracy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

When you have time to analyze your shot, your shooting accuracy has a precision that only a machine can achieve.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") at a target as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (instead of two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")), you gain a +5 bonus to your next attack made with a weapon with which you are proficient in the following round against that target. The target must remain in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").
