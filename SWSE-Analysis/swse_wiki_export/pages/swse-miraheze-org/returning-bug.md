# Returning Bug

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Returning_Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Returning_Bug) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You have mastered the art of using Yuuzhan Vong [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons") and can cause them to return to your hand.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

**Effect:** When you miss with a [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug") or a [Thud Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bug "Thud Bug"), the weapon returns to your hand immediately.
