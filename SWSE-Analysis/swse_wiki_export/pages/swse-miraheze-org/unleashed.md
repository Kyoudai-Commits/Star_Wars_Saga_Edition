# Unleashed

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You gain access to [Unleashed Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed_Abilities "Unleashed Abilities"). If you have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat, you also gain access to [Force Unleashed Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Abilities?action=edit&redlink=1 "Force Unleashed Abilities (page does not exist)").

**Prerequisites:** Have a [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"), Receive the Gamemaster's Approval

**Effect:** You can activate [Unleashed Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed_Abilities "Unleashed Abilities") by spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"). You must also have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat in order to activate [Force Unleashed Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Abilities?action=edit&redlink=1 "Force Unleashed Abilities (page does not exist)") for [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") and [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents").
