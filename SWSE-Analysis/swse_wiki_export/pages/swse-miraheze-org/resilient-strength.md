# Resilient Strength

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resilient_Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resilient_Strength) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Your brute strength and physical conditioning allow you to shrug off effects that would debilitate others.

**Prerequisite:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier or your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier to determine your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
