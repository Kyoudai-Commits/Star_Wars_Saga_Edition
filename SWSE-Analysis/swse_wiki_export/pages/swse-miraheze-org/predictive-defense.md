# Predictive Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Predictive_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Predictive_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

The sharpness of your mind, not the speed of your reactions, allows you to avoid incoming attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier or your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier to determine your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
