# High Ground Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/High_Ground_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/High_Ground_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You know how to take advantage of the terrain and gain a defensive advantage from it.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Until the start of your next turn, if you are standing on a low object or in [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), any creature that moves adjacent to you provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you. |
| 20 | Until the start of your next turn, if you are standing on a low object or in [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), any creature that moves adjacent to you provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you. Additionally, you gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") (including this one) until the start of your next turn. |
| 25 | Until the start of your next turn, if you are standing on a low object or in [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), any creature that moves adjacent to you provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you. Additionally, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") (including this one) until the start of your next turn. |
| 30 | Until the start of your next turn, if you are standing on a low object or in [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), any creature that moves adjacent to you provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you. Additionally, you gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") (including this one) until the start of your next turn. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Sokan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sokan "Sokan")):** If you have the **[Sokan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sokan "Sokan")** Talent, you deal +1 die of damage on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") 1 square each time you make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") until the start of your next turn. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
