# Commando Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You use advanced combat tactics to take down enemies quickly, shield your comrades, and endure whatever challenges are thrown your way.

## Core Talents

### Battle Analysis

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check. If the check succeeds, you know which allies and opponents in your line of sight are reduced to at least half of their maximum total [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). If you succeed at both a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check and a DC 15 [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, you may use this Talent to determine what [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") are reduced to at least half their maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").

### Cover Fire

*Prerequisite:* **Battle Analysis**

When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") or [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle"), all allies within 6 squares of you when you made the attack gain a +1 bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn. Allies within range don't need to be within your line of sight to gain the bonus.

### Demolitionist

When you use the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill to [Handle Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Handle_Explosives "Handle Explosives"), the explosion deals +2 dice of damage. You may take this Talent multiple times; its effects stack.

### Draw Fire

You can distract opponents and convince them that you are the most tempting (or most dangerous) target in the area. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check and compare the result to the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of all opponents within line of sight. If the check result exceeds an opponent's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that opponent cannot attack any character within 6 squares of you until the start of your next turn as long as you do not have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") against that opponent. (The affected opponent may still attack you, however.)

If you are a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), you may use this Talent to protect allied [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"). You may use it to protect [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") no more than one size category larger than your own.

### Harm's Way

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

Once per round, you may spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to shield a single adjacent ally from attacks, taking the damage and suffering the ill effects in your ally's stead. Until the start of your next turn, any attack made against the protected ally targets you instead. You may elect not to shield your protected ally against a given attack, provided the decision is made before the attack roll is made.

If you are a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), you may use this Talent to protect allied [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"). You may use it to protect [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") no more than one size category larger than your own.

### Indomitable

Once per day as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can move +5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). This does not remove any [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") that may be affecting you. This Talent does not affect the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). You can select this talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, you can use it one additional time per day.

### Tough as Nails

You can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") one extra time per day. If you have this Talent and the [Extra Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind "Extra Second Wind") feat, you can catch your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") a total of three times per day.

## Additional Talents

### Coordinated Effort

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisite:* **Dedicated Protector**

When you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to grant a bonus on attack rolls, if you are aiding the target of your **Dedicated Protector** Talent, that ally also gains a +2 bonus to damage rolls on the attack you aided.

### Dedicated Guardian

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisites:* **Dedicated Protector**

You can use each of the following Actions once per encounter:

- *Blast Shield:* Spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Until the end of your next turn, the ally who is under the effects of your **Dedicated Protector** Talent is treated as having the **[Evasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Evasion "Evasion")** Talent for the purposes of determining damage from an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"). If the ally already has **[Evasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Evasion "Evasion")**, the damage from a successful [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") is reduced by 1 die.
- *Take the Pain:* Whenever your **Dedicated Protector** target would move down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), choose to move the same number of steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") instead (preventing the ally from moving down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track")).
- *Team Effort:* Spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Until the end of your next turn, while you are adjacent to your **Dedicated Protector** target, any enemy that is adjacent to you and to that ally is considered [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked").

### Dedicated Protector

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisite:* **Harm's Way**

Once per encounter, you can designate one ally within 6 squares of you. Until the end of the encounter, that ally gains a +1 morale bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") as long as it remains adjacent to you. Any individual can only be the target of this Talent once per encounter.

### Defensive Position

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisite:* **Battle Analysis**

Whenever you have the benefit of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), you can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to treat it as [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover") until the start of your next turn.

### Hard Target

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Tough as Nails**

You can [Catch a Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catch_a_Second_Wind "Catch a Second Wind") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), instead of as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Keep Them at Bay

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

When you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to [Suppress an Enemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_an_Enemy "Suppress an Enemy"), that enemy takes a -5 penalty on its next attack instead of the normal -2 penalty. Only 1 character may gain the benefits of this Talent against a given target at a time.

### Out of Harm's Way

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Harm's Way**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when you use the **Harm's Way** Talent (which still requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to activate), you can move into the square of the ally you are protecting, and move the ally to any legal square adjacent to you. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
