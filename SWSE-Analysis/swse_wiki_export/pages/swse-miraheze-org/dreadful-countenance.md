# Dreadful Countenance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dreadful_Countenance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dreadful_Countenance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Behind the Threat: The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Behind_the_Threat:_The_Sith "Behind the Threat: The Sith"))*

Marked as Sith, your appearance inspires awe, dread, and fear in all who view it.

**Prerequisites:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, Member of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith")

**Effect:** Whenever you make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check or a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate a Fear effect, you may reroll the check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
