# Fleet-Footed

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fleet-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fleet-Footed) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You gain a burst of speed when you attack while running.

**Prerequisite:** [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack")

**Effect:** If you [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") both before and after you make an attack using the [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack") feat, your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is increased by 2 squares until the end of your turn.
