# Military Engineer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Engineer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Engineer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have become skilled at using and manipulating technology on the battlefield. In fact, your fellow soldiers rely on your technical expertise in the heat of battle.

## Core Talents

### Breach Cover

When you fire or throw a weapon with a [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") or [Splash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Splash "Splash") radius at a target with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), you ignore that [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover").

### Breaching Explosive

You ignore the [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") of doors and walls when using [Mines](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mines "Mines") and fixed (non-[Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade")) [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives").

### Droid Expert

*Prerequisite:* **Repairs on the Fly**

When you [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), you [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") 1 additional [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") for each point by which your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check beats the base DC of 20.

### Prepared Explosive

When you use a [Mine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mine (page does not exist)") or other fixed (non-[Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade")) [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive"), you can choose to have the [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius of the [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive") become [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") after the [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive") has detonated. Alternatively, if you plant a [Mine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mine (page does not exist)") or fixed [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive") in an area of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), you can have the [Explosive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosive "Explosive") deal no damage and instead turn the [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") into normal terrain.

### Problem Solver

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can designate a single [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") whose [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") can hear and understand you. That pilot's [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") ignores [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") until the start of your next turn, and the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") gains a +5 insight bonus on all [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks made to avoid [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards") and [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions") until the start of your next turn.

### Quick Modifications

*Prerequisites:* **Repairs on the Fly**, [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist")

When you create a [Field-Created Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field-Created_Weapon "Field-Created Weapon"), you can choose one weapon modification from the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") feat to apply to the created weapon at the time of creation.

### Repairs on the Fly

You can use the [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") application of the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") skill as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). You can gain the benefits of this Talent only once per day per [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or object [Repaired](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repaired "Repaired").

### Sabotage Device

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can sabotage any object or weapon that is powered by an [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell") or [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") so that it becomes a [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade"). The object or weapon is then considered to be a [Frag Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frag_Grenade "Frag Grenade") in all ways, but it can be turned back into its original form with another [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Tech Savant

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Technology)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Technology) "Knowledge (Technology)")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can increase the [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of one adjacent [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that you occupy by 1 square (applied to any method of [Locomotion System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_System "Locomotion System")) until the end of your next turn. Any [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can only benefit from this Talent once per round.

### Vehicular Boost

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to grant one [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 x your Class Level. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from multiple sources do not stack.
