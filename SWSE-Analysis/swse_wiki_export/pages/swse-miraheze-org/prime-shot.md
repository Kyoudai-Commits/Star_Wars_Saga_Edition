# Prime Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prime_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prime_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know how to handle an enemy when none of your allies is closer.

**Prerequisite:** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** If none of your allies are closer to your target than you are when you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the attack roll. Your target must be at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") or closer to gain this bonus.
