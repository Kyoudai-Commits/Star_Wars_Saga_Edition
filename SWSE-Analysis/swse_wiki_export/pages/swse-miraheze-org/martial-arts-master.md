# Martial Arts Master

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Master) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Most warriors become proficient with a variety of weapons, but Martial Arts Masters strive to master various styles of hand-to-hand combat, shunning blasters and turning their bodies into powerful weapons. Although other combatants are satisfied with learning the basics of unarmed combat, Martial Arts Masters learn the techniques and philosophies of many different styles of martial arts, incorporating different elements and techniques into unique personal styles. They are among the most self-sufficient and the most dangerous fighters in the galaxy.

Martial Arts Masters focus on perfecting their art, but many different paths lead to that goal. Some Martial Arts Masters withdraw from the galaxy, honing their skills in small, isolated enclaves. Others use their prowess in the defense of the helpless and innocent in a manner befitting a Jedi Knight. Some make their lethal skills available to the highest bidder, and still others hold positions of influence with criminal cartels, using their deadly fighting abilities to ruthlessly maintain their rank.

**Examples of Martial Arts Masters in Star Wars:** Anoon Bondara, Arden Lyn, Bultar Swan, the Pike sisters, the Five Masters of Cularin.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Martial Arts Master, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** +7
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II"), [Melee Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense "Melee Defense"), and at least one of the following [Martial Arts Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Feats "Martial Arts Feats"): [Echani Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Echani_Training "Echani Training"), [Hijkata Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hijkata_Training "Hijkata Training"), [K'tara Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27tara_Training "K'tara Training"), [K'thri Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27thri_Training "K'thri Training"), [Stava Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stava_Training "Stava Training"), [Tae-Jitsu Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tae-Jitsu_Training "Tae-Jitsu Training"), [Teräs Käsi Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Training "Teräs Käsi Training"), or [Wrruushi Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wrruushi_Training "Wrruushi Training")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** Any one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from either the
  - [Brawler Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brawler_Talent_Tree "Brawler Talent Tree") or
  - [Survivor Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survivor_Talent_Tree "Survivor Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Martial Arts Masters gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Martial Arts Masters gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Martial Arts Masters gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Martial Arts Master gains an additional [Martial Arts Master Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Master_Talent "Martial Arts Master Talent"). A Martial Arts Master may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Martial Arts Master Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Master_Talent_Trees "Martial Arts Master Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Tough as Durasteel

At 2nd level as part of their training, a Martial Arts Master learned how to ready themselves for the attacks, reducing the effectiveness of the attacker's blows. Whenever a Martial Arts Master damages a target with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") while wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), they gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to their Class Level. The number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") gained with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") increases for every other level you gain in this Prestige Class.
