# Bad Feeling

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bad_Feeling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bad_Feeling) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You have developed an innate ability to sense when things are going badly, and thus can prepare yourself for the worst.

**Effect:** You can always take a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") during a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), even if you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"). If you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can take this [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") in addition to any other Actions you are normally allowed to take in the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round").
