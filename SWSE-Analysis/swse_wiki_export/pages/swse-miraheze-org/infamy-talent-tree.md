# Infamy Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infamy_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infamy_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are wanted in multiple systems for criminal acts, and your manner of doing business has earned you an unsavory reputation in the criminal underworld.

## Core Talents

### Inspire Fear I

Your infamy and reputation are such that any opponent whose level is equal to or less than your Character Level takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls and opposed [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made against you, as well as [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to activate [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") against you. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.

### Inspire Fear II

*Prerequisite:* **Inspire Fear I**

As **Inspire Fear I**, except that the penalty increases to -2.

### Inspire Fear III

*Prerequisite:* **Inspire Fear II**

As **Inspire Fear I**, except that the penalty increases to -5.

### Notorious Crime Lord

Your reputation as a [Crime Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord "Crime Lord") is known throughout the galaxy, even on fringe worlds. When you are not [Disguised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disguised "Disguised"), you may reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks made to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate"), keeping the better of the two results.

### Shared Notoriety

*Prerequisite:* **Notorious Crime Lord**

When your **[Minions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minions "Minions")** invoke your name, others take note. If you have **[Minions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minions "Minions")**, they may reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks made to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") others, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

## Additional Talents

### Fear Me

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **[Attract Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attract_Minion "Attract Minion")**, **Inspire Fear II**

Such is the fear you instill in your minions that when in your presence, they would rather die than disappoint you. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to one of your **[Minions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minions "Minions")** being moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can reduce the number of steps the **[Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minion "Minion")** moves down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by 1. Additionally, the target regains [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to your Heroic Level. If the target is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you cannot use this Talent on that target.

### Frighten

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **[Attract Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attract_Minion "Attract Minion")**, **Inspire Fear I**

Once per encounter, you can designate a **[Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minion "Minion")** as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") to spread fear among your enemies. At any point before the end of the encounter, you can activate this ability to force all enemies adjacent to your **[Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minion "Minion")** to move 1 square away from the **[Minion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minion "Minion")**. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Master Manipulator

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Notorious Crime Lord**, [Skill Focus (Persuasion)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Persuasion) "Skill Focus (Persuasion)")

When you make a successful [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, you can immediately make a second [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the same target, even if it is not normally allowed. The second [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check need not be for the same use of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill as the first. For example, if you successfully [Change Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Change_Attitude "Change Attitude") of the target, you can immediately attempt to [Change Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Change_Attitude "Change Attitude") a second time, or you can attempt to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") the target instead.

### Small Favor

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), **Notorious Crime Lord**

You can call in a small favor from someone who owes you. Once per day, make a DC 25 [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check. If the check is successful, an informant gives you information, granting you a +10 competence bonus to one [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") or [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") check made within the next 24 hours.

### Terrify

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Frighten**, **Inspire Fear II**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against a target that is within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and that is also affected by your **Inspire Fear** Talent. If you equal or exceed the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), then on its next turn the target must spend at least one [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to move away from you. If the target is somehow prevented from doing so, then the penalty from the **Inspire Fear** Talent doubles until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.

### Unsavory Reputation

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Inspire Fear III**, **Notorious Crime Lord**

Any opponent that is reduced to half [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or fewer while within 6 squares of you takes a -2 penalty on all attack rolls and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for the duration of the encounter. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.
