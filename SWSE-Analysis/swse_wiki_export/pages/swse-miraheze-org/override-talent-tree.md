# Override Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Override_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Override_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can take direct control over your [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies for brief periods.

## Core Talents

### Directed Action

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you allow one [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that can hear and understand you to make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion"), [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot"), [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride"), [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury"), or [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check immediately as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can replace its relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier for that check with your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier.

### Directed Movement

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you allow one [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that can hear and understand you to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can make [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), or [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks during this movement, and can replace its own relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier for that check with your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier.

### Full Control

*Prerequisites:* **Directed Action**, **Directed Movement**, **Remote Attack**

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you allow one [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that can hear and understand you to take the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action. The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can replace its relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier to its attack roll with your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier.

### Remote Attack

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you allow one [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that can hear and understand you to make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") can replace its relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier to its attack roll with your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier.
