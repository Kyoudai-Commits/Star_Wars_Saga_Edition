# Yuuzhan Vong Biotech Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_Biotech_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_Biotech_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[Yuuzhan Vong Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_Biotechnology "Yuuzhan Vong Biotechnology") remains available in some parts of the galaxy, although its legally dubious. You have become specialized in its manipulation.

## Core Talents

### Biotech Adept

You can reroll any [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") check, or [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks made for [Biotech Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Repair "Biotech Repair"), but you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Bugbite

You deal +1 die of damage on attacks made with [Razor Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bugs "Razor Bugs") and [Thud Bugs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bugs "Thud Bugs").

### Curved Throw

*Prerequisite:* **Bugbite**

You can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to ignore [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") (but not [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover")) with your next attack with a [Thud Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bug "Thud Bug") or a [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug") made before the end of your turn.

### Surprising Weapons

Whenever you successfully hit an enemy with an [Amphistaff](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Amphistaff "Amphistaff"), [Thud Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thud_Bug "Thud Bug"), or [Razor Bug](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Bug "Razor Bug"), and your attack roll also exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you until the end of your next turn.

### Veiled Biotech

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

You gain a +10 competence bonus on [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") and [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks made to conceal any [Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology") or any [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") you possess. Additionally, you may draw a [Concealed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealed "Concealed") Biotech item or weapon as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"); if you then make an attack with the Biotech weapon before the end of your turn, your opponent loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against the first attack you make with that weapon.
