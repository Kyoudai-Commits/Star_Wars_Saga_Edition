# Warrior Heritage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warrior_Heritage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warrior_Heritage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You come from a proud warrior culture, and your pride fills you with self-assurance.

**Prerequisite:** [Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan "Gungan")

**Effect:** You gain a +2 morale bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") when you wield an [Atlatl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atlatl "Atlatl") or a [Cesta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cesta "Cesta").
