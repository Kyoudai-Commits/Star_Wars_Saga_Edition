# Pincer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pincer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pincer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You use your mechanical strength to restrain and damage enemies.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Claw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claw "Claw") or [Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hand "Hand") Appendage, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin"), [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush")

**Effect:** When you successfully [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") an enemy, you can maintain the [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") beyond 1 round and can make subsequent [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks against the [Pinned](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinned "Pinned") enemy as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). You can apply the [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush") feat whenever your subsequent checks are successful.

**Normal:** [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks are a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). The [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") feat must be rerolled each round as a new [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check.

**Special:** You must have a [Claw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claw "Claw") or [Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hand "Hand") Appendage to use this Feat.
