# Vehicular Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can coax more out of [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") than other [Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilots "Pilots") can, staying in the fight when others might be destroyed.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

**Effect:** If the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of is Colossal or smaller and is reduced to fewer than one-half its maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), once per day as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") gains a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to one-quarter its maximum [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") total. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.
