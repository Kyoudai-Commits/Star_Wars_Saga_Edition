# Plant Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Plant_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Plant_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You reach out with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to entreat the aid of plants, causing them to lash out at your opponents.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 12 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). (There must be one or more plants adjacent to the target, or else this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") fails automatically.)

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**Compare the result of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to the [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") of the target. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn. A creature can escape (restoring its normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed")) by making a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), opposed by your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result. (If the target cannot make [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks, that target cannot escape.)

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to impose a -10 penalty to the target's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks made to escape the *Plant Surge*.

You can maintain *Plant Surge* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Plant Surge* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Plant Surge*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Plant Surge*.
