# Droid Hunter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Hunter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Hunter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to target the delicate parts of your [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") opponents, taking advantage of their mechanical frailties.

**Effect:** While wielding a weapon with which you are proficient, you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls against [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") enemies, or +4 if you are using a weapon that deals [Ion Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Damage "Ion Damage").
