# Sith Alchemy Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemy_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemy_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have learned the alchemical arts of the ancient [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords") and can create objects and creatures that are filled with the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

## Core Talents

### Cause Mutation

*Prerequisite:* **Sith Advanced Alchemy**

You can use your mastery of Sith Alchemy to create mutated abominations. You must have access to a willing (or unconscious) creature to which you will apply the [Sith Abomination Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Abomination_Template "Sith Abomination Template") or the [Chrysalis Beast Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chrysalis_Beast_Template "Chrysalis Beast Template"). You also need a medical lab outfitted for the process, which requires a number of days equal to creature's modified CL. You must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") at the completion of the process to complete the transformation. A creature you have mutated is considered to be a domesticated creature, but for you only (unless it was already a domesticated creature before its mutation).

### Rapid Alchemy

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can perform minor alchemical alterations to a melee weapon you wield. For the remainder of the encounter, you gain a +2 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on attack rolls with that weapon. Additionally, once before the end of the encounter, you can sacrifice this bonus as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") to gain a +5 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on a single damage roll you make with that weapon.

### Sith Advanced Alchemy

Your knowledge of Sith Alchemy allows you to imbue certain objects with the power of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"). You can perform any of the following Alchemical Transformations, though each time you do so, increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1.

- *Create Sith Amulet:* You can create a [Sith Amulet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Amulet "Sith Amulet"). The creation of a [Sith Amulet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Amulet "Sith Amulet") requires 25,000 credits' worth of gems and other raw materials, and the creation process takes 1 week (this work can be spread out over several sessions and need not be consecutive). At the end of the process, you must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to complete the [Sith Amulet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Amulet "Sith Amulet"), after which point it functions exactly as a [Sith Amulet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Amulet "Sith Amulet").
- *Create Sith Armor:* You can transform a suit of normal [Battle Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Armor "Battle Armor") into the [Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Armor "Dark Armor") worn by [Sith Lords](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Lords "Sith Lords"). You must have a suit of [Battle Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Armor "Battle Armor") to transform into the [Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Armor "Dark Armor") ([Light Battle Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Battle_Armor "Light Battle Armor") becomes [Light Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Dark_Armor "Light Dark Armor"), [Battle Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Armor "Battle Armor") becomes [Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Armor "Dark Armor"), and [Heavy Battle Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Battle_Armor "Heavy Battle Armor") becomes [Heavy Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Dark_Armor "Heavy Dark Armor")). The transformation process takes 1 day for [Light Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Dark_Armor "Light Dark Armor"), 2 days for [Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Armor "Dark Armor"), and 3 days for [Heavy Dark Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Dark_Armor "Heavy Dark Armor"). You must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") at the end of the creation process to complete the armor's transformation.
- *Create Sith Talisman:* You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a portable object with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), creating a Sith Talisman that provides offensive strength to a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). Creating the Sith Talisman takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). While you wear or carry the Sith Talisman on your person, you add 1d6 to your damage with [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). You increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1 when you first wear or carry a Sith Talisman. You can have only one Sith Talisman active at any given time, and if it is destroyed, you cannot create another one for 24 hours.
- *Create Sith Weapon:* You can alchemically treat a [Simple Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Melee) "Simple Weapon (Melee)") or an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon"), turning it into a Sith Weapon. You must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") and spend 1 hour imbuing the weapon with the properties of a [Sith Alchemical Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemical_Weapon "Sith Alchemical Weapon"). A [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") does not ignore the Sith Weapon's DR, and characters who are proficient in the weapon's use can treat it as a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") for the purposes of the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**, and **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")** Talents (and any [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") that have these Talents as a prerequisite).
  - Additionally, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), the wielder of a Sith Weapon can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain a bonus equal to their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") to the damage of their next attack made with the weapon before the end of the encounter. This increases the wielder's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1.

### Sith Alchemy Specialist

*Prerequisite:* **Sith Advanced Alchemy**

You can modify an object with Sith Alchemy so that it gains a specific trait. Specific traits are listed in the Sith Alchemy table, below. You can only perform one modification at a time. Unless otherwise noted, you cannot grant more than one benefit to a single object, and you cannot apply the same benefit more than once. You must spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") and devote 1 hour of uninterrupted work to apply a trait to the relevant object, and when you do so, you increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1.
