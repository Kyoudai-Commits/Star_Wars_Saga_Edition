# Critical Master Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Master_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Master_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Some [Elite Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Troopers "Elite Troopers") know exactly where to strike their foes to deal maximum damage, potentially crippling their enemies in the process.

## Core Talents

### Deny Move

*Prerequisite:* **Reduce Mobility**

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), your target cannot [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") on its next turn.

### Extended Critical Range (Heavy Weapons)

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +10, [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)")

When you are using a [Heavy Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapon "Heavy Weapon"), you extend the weapon's critical range by 1 (for example, 19-20 instead of 20). However, anything other than a Natural 20 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll anything other than a Natural 20 and still miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").

### Extended Critical Range (Rifles)

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +10, [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)")

When you are using a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle"), you extend the weapon's critical range by 1 (for example, 19-20 instead of 20). However, anything other than a Natural 20 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll anything other than a Natural 20 and still miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").

### Flurry Attack

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency "Weapon Proficiency") (Chosen Weapon)

Choose a single [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") you are proficient with. When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with a weapon from that group, you can make one immediate extra attack (in addition to the other effects of a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit")) against a single target within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). You may only use this Talent once per turn. You can select this Talent multiple times. its effects do not stack. Each time you take the Talent, it applies to a new [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon").

### Knockback

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") against a target no more than two size categories larger than you are, you can choose to move that opponent 1 square in any direction as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). You cannot use this Talent on an opponent that is being [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), and you cannot move your target into a solid object or into another creature's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space").

### Reduce Defense

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), your target takes a -2 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until it is fully healed (at maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points")).

### Reduce Mobility

When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you reduce the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by half until it is fully healed (at maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points")).
