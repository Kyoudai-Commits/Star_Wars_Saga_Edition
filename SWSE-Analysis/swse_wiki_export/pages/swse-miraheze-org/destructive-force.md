# Destructive Force

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destructive_Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destructive_Force) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You cause so much devastation to [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and objects that you also deal damage to others around those targets.

**Effect:** When you deal damage to an object or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that equals or exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") and reduces the target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you deal 1 die of damage (of the same type as the damage used in the attack) to all targets, including allies, that are adjacent to the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or object.
