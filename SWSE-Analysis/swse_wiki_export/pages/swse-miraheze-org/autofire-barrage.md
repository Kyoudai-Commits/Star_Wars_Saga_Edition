# Autofire Barrage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Barrage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Barrage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Your [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attacks are devastating when focused in a single area for multiple rounds.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** When wielding a weapon for which you possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat, and you target the same area with an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack that you made an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack against on your last turn, the penalty on the [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack is reduced to -2 (-1 if using a [Braced](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Braced "Braced") [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire")-only weapon or the **[Controlled Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Controlled_Burst "Controlled Burst")** Talent) and you deal +1 die of damage on a hit.

**Normal:** A -5 penalty applies to [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack rolls, and there are no bonuses for consecutive rounds of fire in the same squares.

**Special:** This feat cannot be used with the [Autofire Sweep](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Sweep "Autofire Sweep") feat or the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") feat.
