# Inspire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You fill your allies with hope and courage, allowing them to face even the most daunting of odds.

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** All allies within 6 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The targets gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the end of your next turn. |
| 25 | The targets gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the end of your next turn. |
| 30 | The targets gain a +10 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the end of your next turn. |
| 35 | The targets become immune to Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the end of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to grant each target +2d6 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") when *Inspire* is first invoked. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away.

You can maintain *Inspire* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Inspire* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Inspire*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Inspire*.
