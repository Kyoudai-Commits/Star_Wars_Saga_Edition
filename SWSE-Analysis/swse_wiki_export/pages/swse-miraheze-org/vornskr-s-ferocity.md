# Vornskr's Ferocity

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vornskr's_Ferocity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vornskr's_Ferocity) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*, *[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

You walk the thin line between darkness and light as you ferociously attack your foe.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, dealing +1 die of damage if you hit. |
| 25 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, dealing +2 dice of damage if you hit. |
| 30 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, dealing +3 dice of damage if you hit. |
| 35 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target, dealing +4 dice of damage if you hit. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Juyo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juyo "Juyo")):** If you have the **[Juyo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juyo "Juyo")** Talent, this Form Power does not have the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor for you.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Form Power at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge").
