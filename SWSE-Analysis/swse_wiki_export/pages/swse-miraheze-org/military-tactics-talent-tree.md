# Military Tactics Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Tactics_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Tactics_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officers "Officers") study old battles, looking for historical examples of good military tactics. You are an expert at leading troops into battle, and using the battlefield to your advantage.

## Core Talents

### Assault Tactics

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you may designate a single creature or object as the target of an assault. If you succeed on a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check, you and all allies able to hear and understand you deal an additional 1d6 points of damage to the target with each successful [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Deployment Tactics

You can use your tactical knowledge to direct allies in battle. As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check. If the check succeeds, you and any allies that can see, hear, and understand you gain a +1 competence bonus on attack rolls against [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked") opponents, or a +1 dodge bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") (character's choice). The bonus lasts until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect. If you have the **[Born Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Born_Leader "Born Leader")** Talent or the **[Battle Analysis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Analysis "Battle Analysis")** Talent, the bonus granted by this Talent increases to +2.

### Field Tactics

*Prerequisite:* **Deployment Tactics**

You know how to use existing terrain to your best advantage. By using a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check. If the check succeeds, you and all allies within 10 squares of you can use whatever [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") is available to gain a +10 [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (instead of the normal +5 [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus). Allies must be able to hear and understand you to gain this benefit, and the bonus lasts until the start of your next turn. This Talent provides no benefit to anyone who doesn't have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### One for the Team

*Prerequisite:* **Deployment Tactics**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can choose to take one-half or all of the damage dealt to an adjacent ally by a single attack. Similarly, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), an adjacent ally can choose to take one-half or all of the damage dealt to you by a single attack (even if they don't have this Talent).

### Outmaneuver

*Prerequisite:* **Field Tactics**

An [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") learns to counter the tactics of their enemies. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check. If the check succeeds, opponents in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") lose all competence, insight, and morale bonuses on attack rolls, as well as any dodge bonuses to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), until the start of your next turn. If one or more enemy [Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officers "Officers") are within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), the highest level [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") among them can attempt to oppose your [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). If their [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") result is higher than yours, your attempt to **Outmaneuver** your opponents fails.

### Shift Defense I

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can take a -2 penalty to one [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), or [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")) to gain a +1 competence bonus to another [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") until the start of your next turn.

### Shift Defense II

*Prerequisite:* **Shift Defense I**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can take a -5 penalty to one [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), or [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")) to gain a +2 competence bonus to another [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") until the start of your next turn.

### Shift Defense III

*Prerequisite:* **Shift Defense II**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can gain a +5 competence bonus to one [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), or [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")) by taking a -5 penalty to your other two [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses").

### Tactical Edge

You can use the **Assault Tactics**, **Deployment Tactics**, or **Field Tactics** Talent as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), instead of a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") (provided you have the Talent in question).

## Additional Talents

### Commander's Prerogative

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

During the first round of combat in an encounter (after the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if any), you can take your turn before any of your allies, but you must use either the [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") special quality or a Talent from one of the following Talent Trees as part of your turn: [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree"), [Leadership Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leadership_Talent_Tree "Leadership Talent Tree"), or Military Tactics Talent Tree. On the subsequent round, you return to your normal place in the [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order").

### Exploit Weakness

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Assault Tactics**

When you use the **Assault Tactics** Talent on an enemy, the target takes a cumulative -1 penalty to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") each time it is damaged by one of your allies (maximum -5 penalty). This penalty applies until the end of your next turn.

### Grand Leader

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), once per encounter, you can grant [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 5 + one-half your Character Level to allies within 20 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from multiple sources do not stack.

### Irregular Tactics

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") (Any)

Your tactics confuse enemy commanders and tacticians that can see or otherwise observe your forces in action, such as when using sensors. After using the [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") special quality, make a [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). The result replaces the DC of any Talents that use [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") from the Military Tactics Talent Tree used against your or your allies.

### Lead by Example

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") (Any)

If you have already used a Talent in an encounter before granting the same Talent to an ally with your [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") special quality in the same encounter, any character who benefits from [Share Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Share_Talent "Share Talent") gains one of the following bonuses when using that Talent (if the Talent can be affected by more than one effect, the character using the Talent selects the desired effect):

- Reduce the Talent's DC by 5.
- Gain an additional +2 bonus to any bonus to [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense"), attack, or damage used by the Talent.
- Reduce the amount of damage taken by the character through the use of the Talent by 10 points.

### Turn the Tide

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Commander's Prerogative**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

Once per encounter, after the first round of combat, you can make a [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") and compare the result to the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of all enemies with in 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If your check is successful, affected enemies must reroll their [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks at the start of the next round. Allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") can choose whether to reroll their check. Rerolls and other modifiers to the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") skill apply normally to this check for all affected targets.

### Uncanny Defense

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Once per day, you can add one-half your [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") Class Level to all your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") for one round. You must declare that you are using this Talent at the beginning of your turn. The benefits last until the beginning of your next turn.
