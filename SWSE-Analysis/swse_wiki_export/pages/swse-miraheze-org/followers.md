# Followers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Followers are Nonheroic characters that act as your allies and agents during adventures. Unlike other NPC allies, Followers are obtained through [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") an augmented by abilities of your choosing. A player that selects Follower-granting Talents (**[Inspire Loyalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire_Loyalty "Inspire Loyalty")**, **[Reconnaissance Team Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reconnaissance_Team_Leader "Reconnaissance Team Leader")**, **[Commanding Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commanding_Officer "Commanding Officer")**, and **[Akk Dog Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Akk_Dog_Master "Akk Dog Master")**) can choose the role, function, and abilities of those Followers through further Talent selection. Unlike a Crime Lord's **[Minions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Minions "Minions")**, Followers do not gain [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") or [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") as they advance in level, and many of their statistics are tied directly to the hero who spent [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") to gain them. However, Followers go on adventures with you and provide assistance, both in combat and in noncombat encounters, and otherwise function as members of your party. Followers are considered your allies, as well as the allies of your party members.

In a [Clone Wars Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign "Clone Wars Campaign"), Followers give the heroes an opportunity to lead troops into battle, command a crack squad of Republic Commandos, control a group of mercenaries hired to do reconnaissance, or retain a retinue of guards fitting for a individual of stature. Though they serve the same functions as [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") allies, they are different in that their actions are typically under the direct control of one of the heroes. A [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") hero might lead [Clone Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Trooper "Clone Trooper") Followers into battle, while a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") might have a team of loyal protectors and decoys accompanying them as they travel the galaxy. These Followers are not just mindless, faceless drones who obey the hero's every bidding, but they do represent the many background characters that assist heroes in one way or another throughout the *Star Wars* saga.

For an individual hero, the presence of Followers represents a significant part of that character's development: leadership. When a hero takes a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that grants Followers, that hero has become a leader to a single Follower of a group of NPCs. Gamemasters that allow heroes to take Follower-granting [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") should encourage these heroes to act as good leaders should, taking care of their Followers, ensuring they are healthy and prepared, and watching out for their emotional and physical welfare.

Each individual Gamemaster can determine how much or how little time they devote to fleshing out the personalities and mannerisms of a hero's Followers. For some, assigning a name to a Follower is enough, but some Gamemasters might wish to flesh out the Followers as thoroughly as they would major NPCs for a campaign. Gamemasters should also consult with their players to determine how much detail they would like for their Followers. For example, a hero leading a squad of [Republic Clones](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Republic_Clones "Republic Clones") as Followers might not care about their Followers' thoughts and motivations, and Gamemasters should be aware of how much attention their players want paid to Followers' personality details.

## Follower Statistics

A Follower's statistics are determined by your Heroic Level, so when you take a Talent that grants you a Follower (**[Inspire Loyalty](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspire_Loyalty "Inspire Loyalty")**, **[Reconnaissance Team Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reconnaissance_Team_Leader "Reconnaissance Team Leader")**, **[Commanding Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commanding_Officer "Commanding Officer")**, or **[Akk Dog Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Akk_Dog_Master "Akk Dog Master")**), you need not worry about building a character completely from scratch. Similarly, as you gain levels, the Follower's statistics improve, helping your Followers stay active as your campaign progresses. When you gain a Follower, you can build a full stat block for that Follower by using the guidelines presented below.

- **Level:** Your Followers are considered to be the same level as your hero. Followers are considered Nonheroic characters but gain none of the benefits of the [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") class.
- **[Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"):** When you receive a Follower, you can select the Follower's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), applying that Species' Traits to the statistics of the Follower. Your Followers never gain bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") from their Species Traits.
- **[Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"):** Followers do not make [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks. They act on the hero's turn, as the hero must spend [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") to activate them.
- **[Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") and [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points"):** Followers do not receive [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") or [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points"), nor do they have [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies"). Any effect that would give a Follower a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), use a Follower's [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), or otherwise interact with the Follower's [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") and [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") have no effect.
- **[Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"):** Followers have [Dark Side Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Scores "Dark Side Scores") (though they begin with a score of 0), which increases as they commit acts that would warrant an increase normally.
- **[Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages"):** A Follower speaks its native [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages"), plus it has at least one language in common with you (usually Basic).
- **[Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"):** A Follower has a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of 10 + the relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier + your Heroic Level. Some [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and effects allow you to increase these [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), and the basic Follower Templates can also include bonuses for individual [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"). Any bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") from [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") does not stack with the bonus from your Heroic Level. If the Follower is wearing [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), use the higher of the two bonuses.
- **[Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"):** A Follower has a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 10 + your Heroic Level.
- **[Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"):** A Follower's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is equal to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") + any special bonuses from [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") or [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats").
- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** A Follower's Template determines its [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus").
- **[Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple"):** A Follower's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") bonus is calculated by their [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") plus their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") or [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier (whichever is higher), as well as any miscellaneous modifiers from special [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") or [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats").
- **[Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"):** A Follower's [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") are all equal to 10, except as noted under their selected Follower Template.
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** Followers do not gain [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") or [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") unless specified by their Follower Template or by [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") taken by your hero. This means they do not gain bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") from their [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), even if they would normally gain one. *Exception:* All Followers gain the [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)") Feat.
- **[Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"):** Followers do not gain [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), though their [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") skill modifiers are calculated as normal (one-half level + the relevant [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") modifier). Some Follower Templates and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") allow Followers to become [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in certain [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"). Followers do not gain bonus [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") for having a high [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence").
- **Possessions:** All Followers have starting credits equal to the starting credits for your first [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class").

Followers and Dark Side ScoresFollowers and Dark Side Scores

A good leader is responsible for the actions of those who follow them, and when it comes to the lure of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), heroes with Followers are no exceptions. If you command one of your Followers to commit an act that would normally increase one's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"), you increase the [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of both you and your Follower. If a Follower's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equals its [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score, it becomes an NPC under Gamemaster control (as any hero normally would), but you can recruit a new Follower as though yours died (see Follower Death, below).

### Equipping a Follower

Followers gain starting credits and can use those credits to purchase [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). A hero can provide [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") to their Followers, though their limited number of [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") and [Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon "Weapon") proficiencies keep Followers from becoming walking arsenals. Followers must abide by the normal rules for [Carrying Capacity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carrying_Capacity "Carrying Capacity") and [Encumbrance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Encumbrance "Encumbrance"), though they are otherwise unrestricted in the kinds or amounts of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") they can use.

### Follower Death

When a Follower is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") by an attack that exceeds its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), it dies. However, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to prevent the Follower from dying just as you would prevent yourself from dying from a similar wound. An attack that reduces a Follower to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") but does not exceed the Follower's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") causes the Follower to go [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious").

If a Follower [Dies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dies "Dies"), you do not lose the benefits of the [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that granted you the Follower. You can recruit another Follower to replace a dead one, abiding by the normal rules for gaining another Follower at that point. Recruiting a new Follower usually requires 8 hours of searching and recruitment, though Gamemasters can shorten or lengthen this amount of time based on the circumstances. However, Gamemasters should be careful not to delay this recruitment for too long, because until the hero gains a replacement Follower, that hero is at a disadvantage.

## Follower Templates

When you gain a Follower, you must choose one of three Follower Templates for your Follower. These Follower Templates provide unique skills and other mechanical benefits for your Followers that not only increase their usefulness, but also prevent all your Followers from having identical statistics (if you choose different Follower Templates for each Follower).

When you gain a Follower, choose one of the below Follower Templates and apply the mechanical modifications indicated in that Follower Template to the statistics of your Follower. Once chosen, a Follower's Follower Template cannot be changed.

- **[Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower"):** Aggressive Followers are focused on attacks and deleting enemy resources. They are somewhat tougher than other Followers because they put themselves in harm's way more often and are more likely to attack an enemy target.
- **[Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower"):** Defensive Followers specialize in protecting themselves from damage and, similarly, holding a defensive position. These Followers make excellent support fighters, laying down suppression fire or helping to hold a corridor against an invading force.
- **[Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower"):** Utility Followers are typically skilled in several areas, allowing them to contribute to a variety of situations where skill checks are called for. These Followers might be demolitions experts, slicers, saboteurs, or any number of other professions that rely on [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") to excel.

Human FollowersDroid Followers

Since [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Species Traits grant a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") and a bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Followers gain no [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus from the standard Follower generation system. Instead, [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Followers can choose one item from any Follower Template (other than their own Follower Template) and apply that to their own statistics instead. For example, a [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Follower with the [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower") template could choose to gain the bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") from the [Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower") template, or a [Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower") could choose the +2 bonus to [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") from the [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower") template.

Unlike the various alien [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") of the *Star Wars* universe, [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") do not share a basic set of traits that can be used to quickly build a character. Heroes in a [Clone Wars Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign "Clone Wars Campaign") with Droid Followers are likely to be members of the Confederacy (or at least working with the Separatists). Rather than restrict Droid Followers to a single model of Droid, all Droid Followers use some of the same basic rules to cover their game mechanics, leaving the specifics of [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") and appearance to the Gamemaster. The Droids might be Super Battle Droids, Droid Commandos, or MagnaGuards depending on the needs and desires of the individual hero.

However, Droid Followers come with pitfalls that non-Droid Followers do not. It is much easier to upgrade the combat capabilities of a Droid Follower simply by purchasing [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") for it, such as [Hardened Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hardened_Systems "Hardened Systems") or a [Shield Generator System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Generator_System "Shield Generator System"). Gamemasters should be careful when allowing PCs to take Droid Followers, as they can quickly become more powerful than their non-[Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") counterparts. Gamemasters must carefully consider the repercussions of giving heroes too free a hand in modifying their Droid Followers.

Use the following Species Traits when creating a Droid Follower:

- **Ability Scores:** Droid Followers may choose to apply a +2 bonus to any one [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") of their choice (except [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution")).
- **Size:** Droid Followers should be Medium size. Gamemasters that wish to allow Droid Followers of different sizes can do so, but must make adjustments to statistics and [Locomotion Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_Systems "Locomotion Systems") based on the size of the Droid Follower.
- **Speed:** Droid Followers can begin with [Walking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walking "Walking") Locomotion or [Tracked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked "Tracked") Locomotion, each of which confers a [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 6 squares.
- **Droid Traits:** Droid Followers have all of the [Droid Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Traits "Droid Traits"), and follow all the same rules as [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). All Droid Followers are considered to be [4th-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/4th-Degree_Droids "4th-Degree Droids"), though Gamemasters are free to allow a different [Droid Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Degree "Droid Degree"). Gamemasters should keep in mind, however, that [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") of other Degrees typically have limitations placed on the actions they can take (specifically in regards to combat).
- **Starting Equipment:** Droid Followers begin with a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor"), 2 [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages"), and a [Locomotion System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_System "Locomotion System"). They gain normal starting credits as per the Follower rules, but these credits must be spent on non-Droid [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") or the following [Droid Accessories](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Accessories "Droid Accessories"): [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages"), [Communications Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Communications_Systems "Communications Systems"), [Compartment Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Compartment_Space "Compartment Space"), [Sensor Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sensor_Systems "Sensor Systems"), or [Translator Units](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Translator_Units "Translator Units").

## Follower Actions

Unlike normal NPCs, Followers have a limited array of Actions that they can perform on any given round. They depend on you, the hero, to direct them. Followers act on your [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count") and take Actions based on the Actions you choose to give them. Below is a summary of Actions your Followers can take (along with the Action cost to you), in [Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat "Combat"). You must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your Follower or be in communications with your Follower to be able to use any of the Actions below. Other [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") related to Followers might have their own Action or range requirements, as well.

Like a normal hero, Followers that [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") can make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") as normal. However, any [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") made by a Follower counts as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") made by you.

Sharing ActionsSharing Actions

Players and Gamemasters may wonder why Followers, unlike all other NPCs in the galaxy, do not have a full suite of [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") each round. The primary reason is to prevent a single hero who takes the Follower [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from gaining a longer turn than the other players, and also to keep the hero from unbalancing encounters due to the added firepower. Standard assumptions about round length and CL are affected by adding NPCs to the mix, and having each Follower act in concert with its controller reduces the amount of extra work a Gamemaster must put into designing encounters. Therefore, each Follower [Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Tree "Talent Tree") features at least one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that allows the hero to act in concert.

In terms of an in-universe explanation for this discrepancy, a hero needs to direct their Followers, and doing so requires more than just shouting order; they need to assess the situation, determine the best course of action, and effectively communicate instructions to their Followers. When your turn comes up and your Followers takes no [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions"), they aren't just sitting there idle- they are taking cover, assessing the situation, and preparing to execute the plans you set forth for them.

### Full-Round Actions

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can allow your Followers to take the following Action:

- **[Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run "Run"):** When you take a Full-Round Action to Run, each of your Followers can Run up to their normal Run [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.

### Standard Actions

You can give up your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to let one of your Followers take a Standard Action of its own. On any round in which an individual Follower does not take a Standard Action (that is, if you do not grant that Follower a Standard Action from the list below or from a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent")-granted ability), that Follower is considered to be [Fighting Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Defensively "Fighting Defensively").

As a Standard Action, you can cause one of your Followers to take one of the following Actions:

- **Attack:** The Follower makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a legal target. Followers can use weapons set on different settings- such as [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") or [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun")- as normal.
- **[Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another"):** The Follower uses the Aid Another Action as normal.
- **[Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"):** The Follower makes a Charge attack against a single legal target.
- **[Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively"):** The Follower takes the Fight Defensively Action as normal (it can still make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity")).
- **Retrieve an Item:** The Follower retrieves an item concealed with the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill from their person.
- **Use Special Ability:** If you have a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that allows one of your Followers to use a Special Ability as a Standard Action, it can do so.

### Move Actions

As a Move Action, you can allow your Followers to take one of the following Actions:

- **[Draw or Holster a Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_or_Holster_a_Weapon "Draw or Holster a Weapon"):** When you use a Move Action to Draw or Holster a Weapon, each of your Followers can do the same. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers Draw or Holster a Weapon, even if you do not.
- **[Manipulate an Item](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Manipulate_an_Item "Manipulate an Item"):** Each of your Followers can pick up an item, load a weapon, open or close a door, or move a heavy object.
- **Move With You:** When you take a Move Action to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move"), [Stand Up](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stand_Up "Stand Up"), or [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw"), each of your Followers can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), [Stand Up](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stand_Up "Stand Up"), or [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") as normal. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform these Actions, even if you do not.

### Swift Actions

As a Swift Action, you can allow your Followers to take one of the following Actions:

- **[Activate an Item](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Activate_an_Item "Activate an Item"):** When you use a Swift Action to Activate an Item, each of your Followers can do the same. You can use the Action to let one or more of your Followers Activate an Item, even if you do not.
- **[Drop an Item](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drop_an_Item "Drop an Item"):** When you use a Swift Action to Drop an Item, each of your Followers can do the same. You can use the Action to let one or more of your Followers Drop an Item, even if you do not.
- **[Fall Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fall_Prone "Fall Prone"):** When you use a Swift Action to Fall Prone, each of your Followers can do the same. You can use the Action to let one or more of your Followers Fall Prone, even if you do not.
- **[Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover"):** When you use three Swift Actions to Recover in the same round or in consecutive rounds, each of your Followers can do the same. You can use the Actions to let one or more of your Followers Recover, even if you do not.
- **[Switch Weapon Mode](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Switch_Weapon_Mode "Switch Weapon Mode"):** When you use a Swift Action to Switch Weapon Mode, each of your Followers can do the same. You can use the Action to let one or more of your Followers Switch Weapon Mode, even if you do not.

## Followers in Vehicles

A Follower can take on any role on a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), including [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), but the Follower must abide by the same restrictions of that role that any other character would have. For example, Follower [Vehicle Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilots "Vehicle Pilots") must still spend a Move Action [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") their ships. Followers act on your [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count") and take Actions based on the Actions you choose to give them. Below is a summary of Actions your Followers can take (along with the Action cost to you), in [Vehicle Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Combat "Vehicle Combat").

### Full-Round Actions

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can allow your Followers to take the following Action:

- **[All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement"):** When you take a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to move your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") using the All-Out Movement Action, each of your Followers can take the All-Out Movement Action for [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") they are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of as well.

### Standard Actions

You can give up your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to let one of your Followers take a Standard Action of its own. On any round in which an individual Follower in the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") role does not take a Standard Action (that is, if you do not grant that Follower a Standard Action from the list below or from a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent")-granted ability), that Follower is considered to be [Fly Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fly_Defensively "Fly Defensively") as their Standard Action, though normal penalties to the attacks of [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") still apply.

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can cause one of your Followers to take one of the following Actions:

- **Attack:** The Follower can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a legal target, including an attack with a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon").
- **[Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another"):** The Follower uses the Aid Another Action as normal. The normal rules for the Aid Another Action still apply.
- **[Attack Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Run "Attack Run"):** The Follower can make an Attack Run against a legal target, provided that Follower is the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). Followers acting as [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") cannot coordinate their attacks with this Attack Run, since they require a Standard Action to ready or make attacks.
- **[Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"):** The Follower initiates or participates in a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"). When engaged in a Dogfight, your Follower can attack or disengage as normal, but this counts as your one [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") for the round.
- **[Fly Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fly_Defensively "Fly Defensively"):** The Follower takes the Fly Defensively Action as normal; it can still make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). Your Follower must be the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to take this Action.
- **Use Special Ability:** If you have a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that allows one of your Followers to use a Special Ability as a Standard Action, it can do so.

### Move Actions

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can cause your Followers to take the following Action:

- **[Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move"):** When you take a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to Move your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), each of your Followers can Move a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") they are a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of up to their Vehicle's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.

### Swift Actions

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can allow your Followers to take one of the following Actions:

- **[Full Stop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Stop "Full Stop"):** When you take a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to bring a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to a Full Stop, one or more of your Followers [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can take the Full Stop Action as well. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.
- **[Increase Vehicle Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increase_Vehicle_Speed "Increase Vehicle Speed"):** When you take a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to Increase Vehicle Speed, one or more of your Followers [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") can take the Increase Vehicle Speed Action as well. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.
- **[Raise or Lower Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raise_or_Lower_Shields "Raise or Lower Shields"):** When you take a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to Raise or Lower Shields, one or more of your Followers acting as a [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") can Raise or Lower Shields on their [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") as well. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.
- **[Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields"):** When you spend 3 [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round or on consecutive rounds to Recharge Shields, you can also let one or more of your Followers acting as a [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") can make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to increase their Vehicles' [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") by 5, up to the [Vehicle's](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle%27s?action=edit&redlink=1 "Vehicle's (page does not exist)") normal maximum. You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.
- **[Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power"):** When you spend 3 [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round or on consecutive rounds to Reroute Power, you can also let one or more of your Followers acting as an [Engineer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineer "Engineer") can make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to move their [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). You can also use the Action to let one or more of your Followers perform this Action, even if you do not.

*Homebrew Optional Rule data created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

Instead of defaulting to [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") or [Fly Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fly_Defensively "Fly Defensively") when a Follower does not receive commands in a given turn, a hero can instead choose to have their Follower attempt the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to aid the hero's next attack roll or skill check in their turn. A Follower attempting to use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action on any other character, or attempting to [Suppress an Enemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_an_Enemy "Suppress an Enemy"), requires the hero to expend a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), as normal.

This optional rule represents a Follower actively supporting a hero's actions, allowing a more flexible benefit to a character that invests in Follower Talents without extending the length of their turn or unbalancing encounters from the additional utility.
