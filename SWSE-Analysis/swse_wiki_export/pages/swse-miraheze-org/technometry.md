# Technometry

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Technometry](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Technometry) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can tap into and read technological devices and, in some cases, control them.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or electronic device touched.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you discover one piece of information contained within the target's memory as determined by the [Access Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Access_Information "Access Information") application of the [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skill. For every 5 points by which you exceed the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you can learn one additional piece of information.

Alternatively, if you target a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") and succeed at your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you may choose one of the following effects and apply it to the targeted [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"):

- The Droid's senses are jammed, allowing you to make [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks against the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") until the end of your next turn, even though it is aware of you.
- The [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against your next attack made before the end of your next turn.
- On its next two turns, the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") does nothing except flee from you at top speed.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to learn two more pieces of information or extend one of the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")-specific effects of this Force Power by one additional round.
