# Force Shield

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Shield](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Shield) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to create a bubble of telekinetic energy around yourself, protecting you from harm.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain a [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") (SR) of 5 until the beginning of your next turn. unlike other [Shield Ratings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Ratings "Shield Ratings"), this [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is made of pure telekinetic energy and cannot be recharged by any means. |
| 20 | You gain a [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") (SR) of 10 until the beginning of your next turn. unlike other [Shield Ratings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Ratings "Shield Ratings"), this [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is made of pure telekinetic energy and cannot be recharged by any means. |
| 25 | You gain a [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") (SR) of 15 until the beginning of your next turn. unlike other [Shield Ratings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Ratings "Shield Ratings"), this [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is made of pure telekinetic energy and cannot be recharged by any means. |
| 30 | You gain a [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") (SR) of 20 until the beginning of your next turn. unlike other [Shield Ratings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Ratings "Shield Ratings"), this [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is made of pure telekinetic energy and cannot be recharged by any means. |

**Special:**You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to increase the *Force Shield's* [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") by 5.

You can maintain *Force Shield* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Force Shield* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). If the *Force Shield's* [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is ever reduced to 0, the Force Power's effect ends.
