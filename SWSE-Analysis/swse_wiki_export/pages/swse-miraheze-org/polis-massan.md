# Polis Massan

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massan) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/8/8b/Polis_Massan.webp/250px-Polis_Massan.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Polis_Massan.webp)

The small asteroid mining colony of [Polis Massa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massa "Polis Massa") in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)") is the current homeworld of the Polis Massans. Though the Polis Massans evolved on another world in the Subterrel sector, they have been away from their homeworld for so long that they have no living memory of their origins. Instead, they have dedicated themselves to researching a lost civilization, once native to Polis Massa, known as the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)").

The planet once inhabited by the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)") was subject to a great and mysterious cataclysm which caused it to break apart into several million fragments. The [Polis Massa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massa "Polis Massa") asteroid is one of the largest fragments to survive, and many artifacts and traces of the fallen civilization are hidden within its depths. The Polis Massans seek to explore the origins of the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)") in the hopes that they may discover something of themselves in this ancient and extinct Species.

It is a little-known fact that the Polis Massans believe that they are the descendants of the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)") Species. Their ultimate goal is to preserve the memory of the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)") race. They intend to do so using advanced cloning techniques learned from the [Kaminoans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kaminoans "Kaminoans") in an attempt to scientifically resurrect the [Eellayin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eellayin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Eellayin (page does not exist)") Species. No viable genetic material has been recovered from the Polis Massan asteroid, but this does little to stop the Polis Massans from continuing to explore.

## Polis Massan Characteristics

**Personality:** Gentle and compassionate are the two words that often come to mind when describing Polis Massans. They are an extremely inquisitive Species, especially with regard to their own origins. They can be overly protective of their work, especially in regards to treasure hunters seeking wealth within the archaeological digs of [Polis Massa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massa "Polis Massa"). In this regard, they prefer to maintain their secrecy, and will hesitate to call attention to themselves.

**Physical Description:** Polis Massans possess smooth, gray skin. Their faces are featureless with the exception of their eyes, which are universally black. The hands of a Polis Massan are dexterous, with three long fingers and opposable thumbs. They tend to wear formfitting bodysuits decorated with functional accessories, such as [Utility Belts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Belts "Utility Belts"), [Tools](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tools "Tools"), and [Medical Gear](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Gear "Medical Gear"). A typical Polis Massan stands at 1.4 meters tall.

**Homeworld:** The asteroid colony of [Polis Massa](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Polis_Massa "Polis Massa"), located in [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** Polis Massans hum their language of Massan with various vocal pitches.

**Example Names:** Dznori Xam, Osh Scal, Maneeli Tuun, Selif Xam, Utani Xane.

**Adventurers:** [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") and [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts") are common [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") for Polis Massan characters. Given their peaceful nature and respect for life, Polis Massans rarely take up arms as [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers"), though they are perfectly capable of protecting themselves. Polis Massan [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") and [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") are unheard of, though their latent telepathic abilities and even personalities make them excellent candidates for [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") teachings.

## Polis Massan Species Traits

Polis Massans share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Polis Massans receive a +2 bonus to their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"). Polis Massans are thoughtful and ponderous, but their physique is largely lackluster.
- **Small Size:** As Small creatures, Polis Massans gain a +1 size bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +5 size bonus on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. However, their lifting and carrying limits are three-quarters of those of Medium characters.
- **Speed:** Polis Massans have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Polis Massans are known across the galaxy for their skill with medicine. A Polis Massan with [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Treat Injury)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Treat_Injury) "Skill Focus (Treat Injury)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Darkvision:** Polis Massans ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. However, they cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
- **Limited Telepathy:** In conjunction with their sign language and technological methods of communication, Polis Massans are able to employ a weak form [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") that allows them to convey simple feelings and thoughts to any willing recipient within 10 squares.
  - Polis Massans can use the [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") aspect of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") (even without the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat). If the target of the [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") is within 10 squares, no skill check is required to initiate the telepathic contact.
- **Mute:** Polis Massans have no vocal cords, and must rely on a combination of sign language, computers, and [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") in order to communicate with others. When they learn new [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages"), they can understand but not speak them.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Polis Massans can understand, read, and write both Basic and Massan.
