# Jedi Healer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Healer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Healer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have focused your natural ability with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to heal the sick and injured.

### Jedi Treatment

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") Skill for purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check reroll, you can reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Healing Boost

*Prerequisite:* *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*

When healing somebody through *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*, the amount of damage healed increases by 1 point per your Class Level.

### Improved Healing Boost

*Prerequisites:* **Healing Boost**, *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*

When healing somebody through *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*, the amount of damage healed increases by 2 points per your Class Level.

### Soothe

*Prerequisite:* *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*

When using *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")* to heal somebody, you can move the target +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") instead of healing damage. When doing so, you move -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
