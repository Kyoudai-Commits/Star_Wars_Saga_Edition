# Gungan Weapon Master

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan_Weapon_Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan_Weapon_Master) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:49Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to master the ancestral weapons of your people and can penetrate even the staunchest defenses.

**Prerequisite:** [Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan "Gungan")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to an attack roll with an [Atlatl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atlatl "Atlatl") or a [Cesta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cesta "Cesta"), increase the die type by one step (from d6 to d8, or from d8 to d10).
