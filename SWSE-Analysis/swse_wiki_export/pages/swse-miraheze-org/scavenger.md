# Scavenger

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at digging through broken objects and junk piles to come up with the raw material needed for certain tasks.

**Effect:** You can spend 1 hour scavenging materials from other [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") or objects. When you do so, you make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check to determine the value of the parts scavenged. You produce raw materials equal in value to the result of your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check x 30 credits. You must apply these raw materials toward the cost of construction of a single object (see [Build Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Build_Object "Build Object")), and you can scavenge parts for only a single object at any given time. Additionally, you can scavenge raw materials only once for any given object you are attempting to build.
