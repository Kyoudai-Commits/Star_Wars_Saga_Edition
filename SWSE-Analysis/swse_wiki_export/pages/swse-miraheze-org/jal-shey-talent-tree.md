# Jal Shey Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jal_Shey_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jal_Shey_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Jal Shey](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jal_Shey "The Jal Shey") concentrate on studying [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), improving their diplomacy, and applying their teachings to their trappings. Only Force-users that are part of [The Jal Shey](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jal_Shey "The Jal Shey") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Action Exchange

*Prerequisite:* **Force Delay**

Whenever you successfully use **Force Delay**, you can grant one ally within 6 squares, and [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") the ability to trade a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") for another [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") on their next turn.

### Force Delay

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

Jal Shey verbally startle opponents with a little help from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Once per encounter, you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of a target with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 3 or higher, that can understand you, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). If successful, the target loses its [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn. If you choose to spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), the target instead loses its [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Imbue Item

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a specifically crafted weapon, armor, or article of clothing with the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Imbuing the item requires a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), the wearer of such an item can open themselves to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), transferring one of their [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") into the item. You can then use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to spend a stored [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") at any later time, even in the same round that you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") of your own.

You can attune only one item per 24 hours, the item functions only for you, and a given item can only store one [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") at a time. You cannot wear more than one attuned item at a time, and you can spend only up to a maximum of two [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") in a round (one of your own, plus one from an **Imbued Item**).

### Knowledge of the Force

You can use your scholarly knowledge of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to help others reach their full potential. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") ally within 6 squares on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, following the normal rules for the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action as usual.
