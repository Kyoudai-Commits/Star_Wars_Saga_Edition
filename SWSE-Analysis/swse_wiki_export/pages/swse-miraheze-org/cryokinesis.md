# Cryokinesis

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cryokinesis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cryokinesis) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to draw heat away from a target, causing its temperature to drop rapidly.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One unattended object within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The target becomes cold to the touch and more fragile. Reduce the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by 5 until the end of your next turn. Additionally, if the target is mechanical or electronic, it automatically moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). |
| 25 | The target becomes cold to the touch and more fragile. Reduce the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by 10 until the end of your next turn. Additionally, if the target is mechanical or electronic, it automatically moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). |
| 30 | The target becomes cold to the touch and more fragile. Reduce the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by 15 until the end of your next turn. Additionally, if the target is mechanical or electronic, it automatically moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). |
| 35 | The target becomes cold to the touch and more fragile. Reduce the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by 20 until the end of your next turn. Additionally, if the target is mechanical or electronic, it automatically moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the penalty to the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") by an additional 5 points. A target moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") ceases to function until it is repaired, but is otherwise unharmed.

You can maintain *Cryokinesis* from round to round, extending the normal duration of the effects (though the target does not move further down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track")). Maintaining the *Cryokinesis* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Cryokinesis*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Cryokinesis*.

*Cryokinesis* can be used to target a living creature, provided that creature is within 12 squares and [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to you and your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). When used in this way, living creatures reduce their [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), rather than their [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), until the end of your next turn. *Cryokinesis* does not automatically move such creatures down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
