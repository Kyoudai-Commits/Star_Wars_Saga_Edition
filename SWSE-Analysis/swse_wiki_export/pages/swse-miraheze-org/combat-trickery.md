# Combat Trickery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Trickery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Trickery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You excel at deceiving others with your tricks and masterful [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

**Effect:** You can spend two successive [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") on the same turn to make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check against a target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the check is successful, the target is [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against your next attack made before the end of your next turn. Additionally, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to extend your target's penalties until the end of encounter.
