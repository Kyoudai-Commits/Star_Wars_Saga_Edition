# Loyal Protector Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Loyal_Protector_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Loyal_Protector_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

As a result of your station, occupation, or even just your natural charisma, you can attract one or more faithful [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") who protect you from harm.

## Core Talents

### Inspire Loyalty

You gain a single [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower"). Choose either an [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower"), [Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower"), or [Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower"), generating the Follower's statistics based on the rules found in the [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") section. This [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") gains one Armor Proficiency feat of your choice and becomes [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill. The [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") must meet the prerequisites for the Armor Proficiency feat you select.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do, you gain one additional [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") (maximum of 3 [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers")).

### Undying Loyalty

*Prerequisite:* **Inspire Loyalty**

Each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") gains the [Toughness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toughness "Toughness") feat.

### Punishing Protection

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, **Inspire Loyalty**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to you being damaged by an attack or a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), one of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") can make an immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against the target that attacked you. Until the beginning of your next turn, any time you are damaged by an attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), another one of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") can attack that attacking target. This ability can be used once per encounter.

### Protector Actions

*Prerequisite:* **Inspire Loyalty**

You and your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") have learned to work together to great effect, ensuring that you remain safe while allowing them to do their duty. You can use any of the following actions on your turn:

- *Bodyguard:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). Until the end of your next turn, if that target damages you with an attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can choose to redirect the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to an adjacent [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower"); the attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is resolved against that ally as normal.
- *Diversion Attack:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If that target attacks you or one of your allies before the beginning of your next turn, you can move one of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") up to its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") directly toward that target.
- *The Best Defense:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") armed with a ranged weapon and having [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, that target takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls until the beginning of your next turn.
