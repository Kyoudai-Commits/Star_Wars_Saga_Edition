# Jedi Battlemaster Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Battlemaster_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Battlemaster_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Jedi Battlemasters are highly trained in Jedi combat techniques. They serve as the head combat trainers for Jedi organizations, and as Jedi commanders on the battlefield. Many Jedi Battlemasters begin their careers as [Jedi Guardians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Guardian_Talent_Tree "Jedi Guardian Talent Tree").

## Core Talents

### Defensive Circle

*Prerequisites:* **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")**, **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**, **Jedi Battle Commander**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you and any allies affected by your **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")** gain a +2 insight bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), lasting as long as they are affected by your **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")**. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks to **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** and **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** (as per the Talents) for each adjacent ally wielding [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers").

### Force Revive

*Prerequisites:* **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")**, **Jedi Battle Commander**

When an ally affected by your **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")** is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), allowing that ally to take its [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") immediately (though the target still falls unconscious before the [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") is triggered).

### Jedi Battle Commander

*Prerequisite:* **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")**

You are trained to direct Jedi in pitched battles. Your **[Battle Meditation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Meditation "Battle Meditation")** grants a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls instead of the normal +1.

### Slashing Charge

*Prerequisites:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")**, [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

Once per encounter, while making a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you take no cumulative penalty to [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks for each **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** attempt you make during the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"). When performing **Slashing Charge**, you can apply the attack bonus granted by the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") to all **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")** attacks as well. You can declare the use of this ability after you begin the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), but must do so before you make your first **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")** attack.

### Mobile Attack (Lightsabers)

*Prerequisites:* **[Multiattack Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiattack_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Multiattack Proficiency (Lightsabers)")**, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

Immediately after making a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") where you attacked with two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") (or both ends of a [Double-Bladed Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double-Bladed_Lightsaber "Double-Bladed Lightsaber")), you may [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").
