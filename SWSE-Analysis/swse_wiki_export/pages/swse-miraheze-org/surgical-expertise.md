# Surgical Expertise

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgical_Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgical_Expertise) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can perform skillful surgical procedures quickly.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

**Effect:** You can perform [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") in 10 minutes.

**Normal:** Performing [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") typically takes 1 hour.
