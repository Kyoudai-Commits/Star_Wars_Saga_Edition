# Feats

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

A Feat is a special feature that either gives your character a new capability or improves one he or she already has. Unlike [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), your choice of Feats is not restricted by your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Any character can take any Feat as long as the prerequisites are meet.

Acquiring FeatsAcquiring Feats

Choose the Feats you feel best represent your character's interests and capabilities. Each character gets one Feat when the character is created (at 1st level). At 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th level, characters gain another Feat. For [Multiclass](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multiclass "Multiclass") characters, Feats are gained according to total Character Level, not by individual Class Level.

Additionally, [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") get bonus Feats chosen from special lists (see Class Bonus Feats, below). [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") also get a bonus Feat at 1st level, chosen from any Feat the character qualifies for.

## Prerequisites

Some Feats have prerequisites. A character must have the listed Feat, [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score"), [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill"), or [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") in order to select or use that Feat. A character can gain a Feat at the same level at which he or she gains the prerequisite(s).

A prerequisite that contains a numerical value is a minimum; any value higher than the one given also meets the prerequisite. For instance, the prerequisites for the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat are "[Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack")" which itself has the prerequisite of "[Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13." Any character with a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") of 13 or higher and the [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack") feat meets the prerequisites.

You can't use a Feat if you've lost a prerequisite. For example, if your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") drops below 13 for any reason, you can't use the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") or [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack") Feats until your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") returns to 13 or higher.

## Class Bonus Feats

At each even-numbered Class Level (2nd, 4th, 6th, etc.), you gain a Bonus Feat. ([Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") do not receive Bonus Feats.) This Feat must be selected from the [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") list of Bonus Feats, and you must meet any prerequisites for that Feat.

Heroic Class Bonus Feats can be found below:
