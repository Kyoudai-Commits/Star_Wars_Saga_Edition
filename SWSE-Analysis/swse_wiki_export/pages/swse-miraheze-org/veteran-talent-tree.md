# Veteran Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Veteran_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Veteran_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You have been from one side of the galaxy to the other and have fought on more planets than most people visit in a lifetime. Talents from this tree can be used to qualify for the [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") Prestige Class, in addition to those listed in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*.

## Core Talents

### Battlefield Remedy

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

You have learned a variety of different ways to treat combat injuries in the field. When you succeed on a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to administer [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid"), the tended creature also moves +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Grizzled Warrior

*Prerequisites:* **Seen It All**, **Tested in Battle**

You can draw upon your extensive battlefield experience to encourage your comrades and drive your enemies before you. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *Defy the Odds:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). You immediately gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score.
- *Double the Pain:* When you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to provide an ally within 6 squares of you a bonus to his or her attack roll, add one-half your Character Level to the ally's damage roll if the attack is successful.
- *Guarded Assault:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). You gain a +2 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against all attacks until the start of your next turn.

### Reckless

*Prerequisite:* **Tested in Battle**

You know from first-hand experience that victory goes to those willing to take a chance. You can add your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus (minimum +1) to the damage roll when you make a successful [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack.

### Seen It All

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), **Tested in Battle**

You have seen more action in more places than most people know exist, and little in the galaxy gets you rattled. Any character using a Fear effect on you must roll twice, keeping the lower result on any [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and attack rolls.

### Tested in Battle

When you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you move +2 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") in addition to regaining [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").
