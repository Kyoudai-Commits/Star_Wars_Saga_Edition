# Withdrawal Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdrawal_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdrawal_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Your enemies do not escape easily.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

**Effect:** When taking this feat, select a single [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)") or melee [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you are proficient. When wielding that chosen [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)") or melee weapon from the chosen [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"), adjacent opponents may not [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") from squares you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten"). They may still make [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") checks to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") as normal.
