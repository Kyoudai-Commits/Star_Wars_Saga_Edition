# Fortifying Recovery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortifying_Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortifying_Recovery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Even as you reel from attacks, you can get yourself back into the fight and make a difference.

**Prerequisite:** [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13

**Effect:** When you take the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action, you also gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 2 x your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") bonus (minimum 2). Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.
