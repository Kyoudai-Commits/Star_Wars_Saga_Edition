# Nimble Team

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nimble_Team](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nimble_Team) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies train extensively to avoid hazards and to slip through tight spaces, making it difficult to pin your team down.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") skill to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble"), you can [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") 1 extra square.
