# Medical Team

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Team](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Team) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies know the value of keeping soldiers alive and perform as a cohesive healing unit.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to restore [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), your ally restores an extra 4 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to the target.
