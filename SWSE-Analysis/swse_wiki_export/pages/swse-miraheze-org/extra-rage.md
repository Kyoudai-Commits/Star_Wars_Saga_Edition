# Extra Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can fly into a Rage more often.

**Prerequisite:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage")

**Effect:** You can [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") one additional time per day.

**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times; each time you take this feat, you can [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage") one additional time per day.
