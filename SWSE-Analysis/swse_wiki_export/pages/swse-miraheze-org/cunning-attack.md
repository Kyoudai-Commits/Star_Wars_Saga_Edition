# Cunning Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cunning_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cunning_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are skilled at attacking unprepared opponents.

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against a [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") enemy or one who is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
