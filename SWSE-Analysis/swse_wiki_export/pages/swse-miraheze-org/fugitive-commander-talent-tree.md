# Fugitive Commander Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fugitive_Commander_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fugitive_Commander_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

When a fighting force is outmanned, outgunned, and constantly on the run, only the skill and acumen of talented [Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officers "Officers") can keep morale high and prevent desertion or surrender. Fugitive Commanders are skilled in the art of deceiving and eluding their pursuers.

## Core Talents

### Disciplined Trickery

Once per turn as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you allow one ally within 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to reroll one [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") or [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, but the ally must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Group Perception

Whenever you roll a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, all allies within 6 squares of you can do so as well, taking the highest result rolled by you or an ally.

### Hasty Withdrawal

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you target a number of allies equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus (minimum 1). Each targeted ally must be within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Each ally you target can take the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action immediately as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). The normal rules for [Withdrawing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdrawing "Withdrawing") otherwise apply.

### Stalwart Subordinates

When any ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is targeted by a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") against its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the source of that [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") (whether a [Hazard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazard "Hazard"), a creature, a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), or whatever) must roll the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") twice, and take the lowest result.

### Stay in the Fight

*Prerequisite:* **Stalwart Subordinates**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you remove one Mind-Affecting or Fear effect currently affecting an ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). When you do so, you also grant the target a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 10 + your Class Level.

### Stealthy Withdrawal

*Prerequisite:* **Hasty Withdrawal**

When an ally [Withdraws](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraws "Withdraws") as a result of your **Hasty Withdrawal** Talent and ends its [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") or [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from any enemy target, that ally can make an immediate [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").
