# Opportunistic Shooter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Shooter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Shooter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are quick to react when nearby enemies expose themselves to your attacks.

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") with ranged weapons.
