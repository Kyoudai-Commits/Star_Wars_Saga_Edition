# Force Adept

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Force Adept is strong in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), but follows a different path than that of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). To the Force Adept, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and the aid it provides are more mysterious, and more supernatural. A Force Adept comes from a different [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"), with different beliefs and codes of conduct than those of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). There are precious few Force Adepts in the galaxy, and even fewer become adventurers. Some call [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") by its familiar name, while others refer to it by some other term entirely.

Some Force Adepts become adventurers as part of a quest to learn more about [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") (or whatever they choose to call it). Others do so out of necessity. They are forced from their world, hunted by unscrupulous agents, or are interested in what the galaxy has to offer them. Force Adepts might be called wizards, shamans, witches, or prophets, depending on where they come from, and what [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") they follow. Most Force Adepts follow [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side") (or at least a variation of it), but some fully embrace [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") and use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for evil or selfish intent.

Force Adepts usually hail from primitive cultures that revere or fear them for their Force powers. Some strive to keep their powers secret, both because they do not fully understand them, and because they are afraid of how others might treat them.

**Examples of Force Adepts in Star Wars:** Asajj Ventress, Inquisitor Tremayne, Maarek Stele, Sly Moore, Teneniel Djo.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Force Adept, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** At least three [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Force Adepts gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to both their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Force Adepts gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Force Adepts gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Force Adept gains an additional [Force Adept Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent "Force Adept Talent"). A Force Adept may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Force Adept Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent_Trees "Force Adept Talent Trees") or [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Force Techniques

You have learned improved techniques to feel the rhythm of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At every even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, and so on), a Force Adept gains one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") that, once selected, cannot be changed.
