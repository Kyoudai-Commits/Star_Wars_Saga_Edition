# Sharpshooter Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sharpshooter_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sharpshooter_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are an expert at long-range combat, using the inherent advantage of a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") to win the day.

## Core Talents

### Precision Shot

*Prerequisite:* [Far Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Far_Shot "Far Shot")

When using the [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") Action, you gain the benefit of the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") feat against your target, regardless of [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") category.

### Bullseye

*Prerequisites:* **Draw a Bead**, **Precision Shot**, [Sniper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper "Sniper")

Once per encounter, you can designate a single target that you have [Aimed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aimed "Aimed") at and is not within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). When making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") roll against that target, the target is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when determining the effect of your attack.

### Draw a Bead

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +10, **Precision Shot**

Once per round, you can spend a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to designate a single enemy who is not within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). When you make a successful [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") roll that deals damage against the designated enemy, add your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (minimum +1) to the damage roll. This effect lasts until the target is unconscious, dead, or leaves your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You can have only one enemy designated in this manner.

### Pinning Shot

*Prerequisite:* **Precision Shot**

You can keep your target worrying about where the next shot is coming from instead of trying to flee. When you deal damage to an enemy that you have [Aimed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aimed "Aimed") at, the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced to 2 squares, and the target cannot take either a double [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") Action or use the [Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run "Run") Action until the end of your next turn. This is a [Stunning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning "Stunning") effect.

### Harrying Shot

*Prerequisites:* **Pinning Shot**, **Precision Shot**

When you make a successful [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against an enemy that you have [Aimed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aimed "Aimed") at and the attack deals damage, the target cannot use a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to make an attack roll on his or her next turn. This is a [Stunning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning "Stunning") effect.
