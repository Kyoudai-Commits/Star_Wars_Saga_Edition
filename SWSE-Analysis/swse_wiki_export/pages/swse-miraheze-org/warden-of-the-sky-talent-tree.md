# Warden of the Sky Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warden_of_the_Sky_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warden_of_the_Sky_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Wardens of the Sky use a combination of martial arts and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") to help keep the space lanes safe. As a Warden of the Sky, you rely on nothing more than your own body to serve as a weapon against predators. Only Force-users that are part of [The Wardens of the Sky](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Wardens_of_the_Sky "The Wardens of the Sky") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Brutal Unarmed Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Telekinetic Strike**

Whenever you roll the damage for an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you reroll any dice that come up with a result of 1.

### Martial Resurgence

You recover all spent [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), adding them to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") again, whenever you roll a Natural 20 on an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack").

### Rebound Leap

Whenever you reduce an opponent to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can make a [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), moving a distance as determined by the results of your [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check. You can use the *[Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge "Surge")* Force Power as normal to enhance this [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check, increasing the distance you move.

### Simultaneous Strike

*Prerequisite:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make two [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks"), each against different targets.

### Telekinetic Strike

Whenever you make a successful [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can add the result of a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") roll to the damage roll instead of the attack roll.

### Telekinetic Throw

*Prerequisite:* [Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw "Throw")

Whenever you successfully use the [Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw "Throw") feat, your opponent falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in any space you desire up to 3 squares beyond your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").
