# Assault

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assault](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assault) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Saga Edition FAQ](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saga_Edition_FAQ "Saga Edition FAQ") (E2))*

Through focus and raw determination, you can make multiple attacks even after moving.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6, [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") or [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I")

**Effect:** If you are wielding two weapons or a weapon with which you can use the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat, you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to make two attacks as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

The normal penalties due to attacking with two weapons or using [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") still apply, and you cannot make more than two attacks regardless of how many extra attacks you can make during a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action.

**Normal:** You can only make multiple attacks with the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action (a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")).
