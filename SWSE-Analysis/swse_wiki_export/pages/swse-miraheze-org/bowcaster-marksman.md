# Bowcaster Marksman

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster_Marksman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster_Marksman) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are an excellent shot with the [Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster"), and even your most difficult shots are solid hits.

**Prerequisite:** [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to an attack with a [Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster"), if you hit the target, you gain a competence bonus to your damage roll equal to the amount added to the attack.
