# Multi-Grab

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Grab) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You may [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") two opponents at once.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you may make a [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") attack against two targets adjacent to you (roll a separate attack against each target). You must have two empty hands to use this feat.
