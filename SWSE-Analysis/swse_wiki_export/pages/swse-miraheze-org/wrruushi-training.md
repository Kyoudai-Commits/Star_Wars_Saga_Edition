# Wrruushi Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wrruushi_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wrruushi_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the Wrruushi unarmed fighting style of the [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") and feel more invigorated in the heat of battle.

**Prerequisites:** [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee"), [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** Once per round when you make a successful [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you gain a number of [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not stack.

Once per encounter, you can make an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against an enemy's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") instead of the enemy's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the attack is successful, the enemy not only takes damage but also loses any [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") until the end of the encounter. You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain the benefits of this feat.

**Special:** If you have the **[Wrruushi Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wrruushi_Expertise "Wrruushi Expertise")** Talent, once per encounter when you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against an enemy, you can deal normal damage and move the target an additional -2 steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), regardless of the damage result. You must be wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") to gain this special benefit.
