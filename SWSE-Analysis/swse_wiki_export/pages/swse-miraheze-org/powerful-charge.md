# Powerful Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") with extra force.

**Prerequisites:** Medium or larger size, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you gain an additional +2 bonus on your melee attack roll. If your [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") hits, you deal additional damage equal to one-half your level.
