# Camouflage Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Camouflage_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Camouflage_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You learn quickly how to blend in with your environment.

## Core Talents

### Hidden Movement

*Prerequisite:* **Improved Stealth**

You're very good at hiding when mobile. You take no penalty on your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check when moving your normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). This Talent applies to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks made while [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a Starship (see [Starship Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Stealth "Starship Stealth")).

### Improved Stealth

You may choose to reroll any [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. This Talent applies to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks made while [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a Starship (see [Starship Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Stealth "Starship Stealth")).

### Improved Concealment

*Prerequisite:* **Hidden Movement**

Any situation that would give you [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") grants you [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") instead.

## Additional Talents

### Dig In

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

When [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"), you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to gain [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") until the start of your next turn. If you stand up or move, you lose this benefit.

### Extended Ambush

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Improved Stealth**

During a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target that is [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you can [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") at the target as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Ghost Assailant

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

If you start your turn with [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") or [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") from a target, during that turn you can make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), opposed by the target's [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check. If you succeed, the target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you until the end of your turn.

### Hide in Plain Sight

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Hidden Movement**

Once per encounter, when you are within 2 squares of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") or [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), you can move to that [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") or [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") and make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to hide as a single [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

### Hunker Down

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Whenever you benefit from [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), you can spend a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to **Hunker Down** and maximize the benefit of the [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). You increase the benefit of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") to [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover") or [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover") to [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover").

### Shadow Striker

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Hidden Movement**

You excel at fighting from the shadows, hitting your opponents when they least expect it. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *Blinding Strike:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against an opponent within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If you damage the target, you gain [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") against that target until the beginning of your next turn.
- *Confusing Strike:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against an opponent within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If this opponent is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), or if you have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from this opponent, a successful attack also causes the opponent to be able to take only a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn.
- *Unexpected Attack:* Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") against an opponent from whom you have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"). You gain a +2 bonus on this attack roll if you have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), or a +5 bonus if you have [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment").

### Slipping By

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

When you would normally provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") by moving out of a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), you can roll a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, replacing your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") with the results of your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check if it is higher.
