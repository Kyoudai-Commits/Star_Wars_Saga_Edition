# Mounted Combat

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Combat) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You know how to get the most from a mount in combat and in other dangerous situations.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

**Effect:** You can make a DC 20 [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to spur your living mount to move faster than its normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). If the check fails, your mount's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") does not increase and it moves -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). If the check succeeds, your mount's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 2 squares until the start of your next turn. You cannot [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on this check. Your mount must succeed on a DC 20 [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check prior to each subsequent attempt to use this feat in the same encounter.

Additionally, once per round, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when you are riding a mount, you can negate a weapon hit against either you or the mount by making a successful [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") check. The DC of the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") is equal to the result of the attack roll you want to negate.
