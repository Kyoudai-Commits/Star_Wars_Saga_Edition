# Damage Conversion

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Conversion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Conversion) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can make last-minute moves to take hits on more durable portions of your chassis and/or armor, altering the damage dealt to you.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When you take damage from an attack against you (other than an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), or an attack that deals [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") or [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage) and that damage equals or exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you can take an additional 10 points of damage instead of moving down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Each subsequent time you use this feat in a single encounter, the additional damage you take increases by 5 points.
