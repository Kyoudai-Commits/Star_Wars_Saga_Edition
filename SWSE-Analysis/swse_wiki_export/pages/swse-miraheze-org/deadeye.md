# Deadeye

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadeye](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadeye) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are skilled at picking off enemies with well-aimed [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks").

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") and the attack hits, increase the damage you deal by an additional weapon die (3d6 becomes 4d6). A [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") can use this feat, but since they must spend at least one [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") operating their [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), they must split their [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") (two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")) and attack ([Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")) into two separate turns, making it more likely that the target will break line of sight or avoid you.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") or [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feats.
