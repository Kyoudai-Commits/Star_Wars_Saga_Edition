# Ace Pilot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Ace Pilot is to [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") combat what the [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") is to personal weapons combat. Their arms and armor are the weapons and shields of a sleek [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") roaring through space, or an [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") shrieking through the atmosphere. A veteran of countless engagements, the Ace Pilot has proven their skill again and again by virtue of facing the enemy and surviving- and making sure the enemy did not. They have several kills to their credit, and their combat maneuvers are occasionally studied and discussed in training academies for their ingenuity and effectiveness.

For their part, the Ace Pilot only feels truly alive in the cockpit, where they can pit themselves against their peers in a life-and-death contest to see who is the better pilot. For some Ace Pilots, the contest is enough, and they don't care whether they win or simply get away with their lives. For others, the important thing is the kill. The adrenaline surge they feel when finishing off an enemy brings them back again and again. The best Ace Pilots learn to stop enemies without destroying them, but those Ace Pilots are rare, and their exploits are legendary.

Ace Pilots define themselves by the [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") they fly. Some are [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") aces, while others fly [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports"). Ace Pilots can also be found at the controls of a podracer, air taxi, or even a large assault [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") such as an [AT-AT](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AT-AT "AT-AT").

**Examples of Ace Pilots in Star Wars:** Han Solo, Wedge Antilles, Sebulba, Wes Jansen, Tycho Celchu, Baron Fel, Corran Horn.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become an Ace Pilot, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Ace Pilots gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Ace Pilots gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Ace Pilots gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), an Ace Pilot gains an additional [Ace Pilot Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot_Talent "Ace Pilot Talent"). An Ace Pilot may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Ace Pilot Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot_Talent_Trees "Ace Pilot Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Vehicle Dodge

Beginning at 2nd level, you apply a dodge bonus to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of any [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you pilot. The dodge bonus is equal to one-half your Class Level, rounded down. Any condition that makes you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") also makes you lose dodge bonuses. Also, dodge bonuses stack with each other, unlike most other types of bonuses.
