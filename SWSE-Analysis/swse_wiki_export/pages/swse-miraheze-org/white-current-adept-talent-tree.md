# White Current Adept Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/White_Current_Adept_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/White_Current_Adept_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The White Current guides [The Fallanassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fallanassi "The Fallanassi") to use its power to benefit everything and everyone around them, as well as to protect themselves. Those who succeed in channeling the White Current often display powers with surprising effects. Only Force-users that are part of [The Fallanassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fallanassi "The Fallanassi") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Force Immersion

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), **White Current Adept**

You can use the [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak") application of the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill to hide from electronic surveillance and sensors. Any opponent attempting to detect you with sensors or electronic surveillance must beat your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check with a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check. You need only roll a single [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to avoid both electronic notice and notice by conventional means, using the same result as the DC for both [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks made to detect you.

### Immerse Another

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), **White Current Adept**

Whenever you make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak"), you can use your check result in place of one adjacent ally's [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check to [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak") (additionally, if you have the **Force Immersion** Talent, that Talent applies to the designated ally as well). Similarly, you can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result in the place of an adjacent ally's [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to avoid detection by the [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") application of the skill. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to have this ability apply to all adjacent allies instead of just one.

### Ride the Current

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being damaged by an attack or [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") from all targets until the end of your next turn. Additionally, if you have not yet taken your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you can do so immediately as part of this [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Surrender to the Current

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can choose to surrender to the White Current and allow it to flow around you and fuel your [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). Until the end of the encounter, you cannot use [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that do not have "You" as the sole target. However, once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can recover one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that has "You" as the sole target without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), adding that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") back to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").

### White Current Adept

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill. If you are entitled to a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check reroll, you can reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).
