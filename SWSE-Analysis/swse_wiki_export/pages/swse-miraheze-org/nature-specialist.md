# Nature Specialist

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nature_Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nature_Specialist) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know even the most obscure information about the natural world.

**Prerequisite:** [Ithorian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ithorian "Ithorian")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).
