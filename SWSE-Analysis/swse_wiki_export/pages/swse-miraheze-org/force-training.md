# Force Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You learn one or more [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers").

**Prerequisites:** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force").

**Effect:** You add to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") a number of [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum 1). You can add the same [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") more than once.

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, you add to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") a number of new [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") equal to 1 + your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier. If your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier permanently increases, you immediately gain a number of [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") equal to the number of Force Training feats you have taken.

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Improved Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm "Improved Disarm") feat, when you use the *[Force Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disarm "Force Disarm")* Force Power, you gain the +5 bonus from [Improved Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm "Improved Disarm") on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as though you were making an attack roll.
