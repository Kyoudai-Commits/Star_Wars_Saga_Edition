# Turn and Burn

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Turn_and_Burn](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Turn_and_Burn) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can retreat from enemies with additional speed and agility.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"); [Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering "Hovering"), [Flying](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flying "Flying"), [Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled "Wheeled"), or [Tracked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked "Tracked") Locomotion; [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When using the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action, you can move up to 2 squares to escape [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") squares without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), and you can move your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to an enemy ending its movement adjacent to you to [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw").

**Normal:** When using the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action, you can normally move only 1 square to escape a [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") square without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") and can move only up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

**Special:** You must be equipped with [Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering "Hovering"), [Flying](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flying "Flying"), [Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled "Wheeled"), or [Tracked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked "Tracked") Locomotion to use this Feat.
