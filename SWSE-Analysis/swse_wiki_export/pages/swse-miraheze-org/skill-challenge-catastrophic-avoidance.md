# Skill Challenge: Catastrophic Avoidance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Catastrophic_Avoidance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Catastrophic_Avoidance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Your luck is such that catastrophe occurs less frequently for you when you are engaged in stressful situations.

**Effect:** Once per [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") that has a chance of [Catastrophic Failure](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catastrophic_Failure "Catastrophic Failure"), this Failure occurs only if you fail a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") by 15 or more. In addition, when a [Catastrophic Failure](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Catastrophic_Failure "Catastrophic Failure") occurs, you accrue one Failure in the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), not two.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [Skill Challenges](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenges "Skill Challenges").
