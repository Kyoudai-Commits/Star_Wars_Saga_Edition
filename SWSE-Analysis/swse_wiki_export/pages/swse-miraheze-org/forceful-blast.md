# Forceful Blast

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forceful_Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forceful_Blast) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You can position your [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") attacks such that they blast creatures away from the center of the explosion.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

**Effect:** When you damage a Large or smaller creature with a [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") or [Thermal Detonator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thermal_Detonator "Thermal Detonator"), compare the result of your attack roll to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If your result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), you can move the target 1 square in any direction as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

If the [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") deals damage to multiple eligible creatures, you can use this benefit against all of them. You can't move a target that's being [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), and you can't move a target into a solid object or into another creature's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space").
