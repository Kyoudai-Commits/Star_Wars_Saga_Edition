# Rapid Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You can make two quick shots with a ranged weapon as a single attack.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When using a ranged weapon with which you are proficient, you may fire two shots as a single attack against a single target. You take a -2 penalty on your attack roll, but you deal +1 die of damage with a successful attack. This feat works normally with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"). However, if you are using [Missiles or Torpedoes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Missiles_or_Torpedoes "Missiles or Torpedoes") and you miss with your attack roll, the projectile does not gain the benefit of this feat for the second attack roll.

**Special:** Using this feat fires two shots and can only be done if the weapon has sufficient ammunition remaining. The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") feat or the [Deadeye](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadeye "Deadeye") feat. If you do not have a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") score of 13 or higher, increase the penalty to attacks to -5 when using this feat with non-[Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** I think the prerequisites for Rapid Shot and [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") were swapped accidentally. Wouldn't it make more sense for [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), a melee [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), to have the [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") requirement, and for Rapid Shot, a ranged [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), to have the [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") requirement?

**A:** No, this is intentional. For Rapid Shot, you need the higher [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") to control the recoil of firing multiple shots so quickly. For [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike"), you need the higher [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") to have the agility necessary to get two swings for the price of one- catching your target a second time on the backswing, spinning to come around a second time, and so on.

For balance purposes, this also has the effect of giving characters some tough decisions. A ranged specialist won't always want to neglect their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), and a melee specialist won't want to neglect their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). (Sure, such a character could neglect [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") if they have [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse"), but they're spending an extra [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") and restricting themselves to certain weapons for that privilege.)

## Rapid Strike and Whirlwind Attack

**Q:** Reading the rules, it seems that you can use [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") in conjunction with [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack"), gaining an extra die of damage against all targets. Is this correct?

**A:** No, they don't work together, nor will Rapid Shot and [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). The text of both [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") and Rapid Shot mention that they work with a single attack roll against a single target. [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") (such as [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack")) deal with multiple targets, so they are not eligible.
