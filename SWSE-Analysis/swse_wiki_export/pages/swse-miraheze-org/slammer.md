# Slammer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slammer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slammer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You clap two [Hands](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hands "Hands"), [Claws](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claws "Claws"), or [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages") together, delivering a crushing blow to an enemy.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), Small size or larger, 2+ [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages"), [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13

**Effect:** As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you slam two of your [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages") together from either side of an enemy, crushing your target between them. Make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"). If the attack is successful, you deal [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage with double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus. If you also exceed the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), it has a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") that can only be removed with 8 hours of rest or a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check.

**Special:** If you also have the [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush") feat, increase your [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage by 1 die when using Slammer.
