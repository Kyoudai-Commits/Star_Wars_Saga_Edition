# Slicer Team

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicer_Team](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicer_Team) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies are computer specialists, capable of penetrating even the strongest electronic defenses.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist an ally who has this feat with a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, you provide a bonus of +4 instead of +2.
