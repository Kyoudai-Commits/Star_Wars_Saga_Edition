# Autonomy Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autonomy_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autonomy_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are able to resist any attempts to curb your independence and can fight back against anyone that tries to suppress your personality.

## Core Talents

### Defensive Electronics

You defend your independence from all. When someone tries to [Reprogram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogram "Reprogram") you, add your [Independent Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid "Independent Droid") Class Level to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").

### Ion Resistance 10

You gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10 against [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage.

### Soft Reset

You are adept at rerouting your internal electronics. If you are moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any means other than taking damage exceeding your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you automatically move +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") after being [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") for 2 rounds.

### Modification Specialist

You have become skilled at [Reprogramming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogramming "Reprogramming") and modifying your own [Droid Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Systems "Droid Systems"). You do not incur the normal -5 penalty on [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks to [Reprogram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogram "Reprogram") yourself or perform self-modifications.

### Repair Self

When you [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") yourself, you [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") 1 additional [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") for each point by which your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check exceeds the DC.

## Additional Talents

### Just a Droid

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You are adept at passing yourself off as an ordinary [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). You can use each of the following Actions once per encounter.

- *Just Another Droid:* You are skilled at using [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") to sneak past unwary enemies when moving in plain sight. You can use the [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak") application of the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill when in plain sight of an enemy, if the enemy has no reason to doubt that you are just another [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") for this Action.
- *Just a Normal Droid:* You can reroll [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") checks for [Deceptive Appearance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearance "Deceptive Appearance") to make observers believe that you are carrying out a standard function when attempting to do something atypical for your [Droid Model](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Model "Droid Model") or function. You may keep either result.

### Swift Droid

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* Any two Talents from the Autonomy Talent Tree

You move quickly when caught. You can make a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") after failing a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") or [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check.
