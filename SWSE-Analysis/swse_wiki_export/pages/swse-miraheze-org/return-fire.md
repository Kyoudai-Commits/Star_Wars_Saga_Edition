# Return Fire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Return_Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Return_Fire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

When someone takes a shot at you and misses, you retaliate with a shot of your own.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 15, [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw"), [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Ranged Weapon)

**Effect:** Select one [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") for which you possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat. Once per encounter as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with the chosen weapon against an enemy that misses you with a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), provided you have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the enemy.

**Special:** If you have the [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes") feat, you may use this feat a number of times during an encounter equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus, but no more than once during a given enemy's turn.

This feat does not apply to [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") or [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"). You must have your weapon in hand to use this feat. You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon").
