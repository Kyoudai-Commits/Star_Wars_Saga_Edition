# Trample

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trample](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trample) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

When charging, your living mount can flatten smaller targets between you and your target.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

**Effect:** When you use the [Mounted Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Charge "Mounted Charge") Action, your mount can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). While [Charging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging "Charging"), compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of each enemy whose square you [Mounted Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Charge "Mounted Charge") through. Each enemy hit by the attack takes Claw, Gore, or Slam damage from the mount's Natural Weapons plus the mount's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier. You or your mount still can make a regular attack at the end of the [Mounted Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Charge "Mounted Charge").

**Special:** You can use this feat if either you or your mount has taken it. Mounts with this feat do not require the prerequisite.
