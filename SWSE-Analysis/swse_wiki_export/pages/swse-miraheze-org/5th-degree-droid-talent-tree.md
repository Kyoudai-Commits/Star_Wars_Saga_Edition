# 5th-Degree Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[5th-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/5th-Degree_Droids "5th-Degree Droids") are typically Utility Droids with low-level functions. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Cargo Hauler

You gain a +5 bonus on [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength")-based [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"). Additionally, you double your [Carrying Capacity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carrying_Capacity "Carrying Capacity").

### Environmentally Shielded

You gain a +5 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against environmental [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards"), including [Atmosphere](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atmosphere "Atmosphere") Hazards, and [Acid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acid "Acid").

### Power Supply

You have learned to reroute power through your system to act as a [Power Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator "Power Generator"), providing power to an [E-Web Repeating Blaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/E-Web_Repeating_Blaster "E-Web Repeating Blaster") (or similar weapon requiring a [Power Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator "Power Generator")). Additionally, whenever you would normally spend three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to [Recharge Shields](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recharge_Shields "Recharge Shields") or [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power") on a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), you need spend only two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") instead.

## Additional Talents

### Durable

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You are particularly durable and continue to function when a lesser Droid would become [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled"). The first time during an encounter that you would be moved to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any effect, you instead stop at the -4 step (move at half [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"); -10 penalty to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"); -10 penalty on attack rolls, [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks"), and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks")). Additionally, if a single attack causes you to move multiple steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to only move -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Heavy-Duty Actuators

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* Medium or larger size

Your **Heavy-Duty Actuators** allow you to release your power and speed in a quick burst. You can double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus to your melee and [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage rolls.

### Load Launcher

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You are considered proficient with [Improvised Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Thrown_Weapons "Improvised Thrown Weapons") when making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") by throwing an object. Objects up to one size category larger than you can be [Thrown](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown "Thrown") up to a number of squares equal to 2 x your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus (minimum 1 square). Additionally, you add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus to any damage dealt.

### Task Optimization

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

Select a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in. Once per encounter, you can perform any application of that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") as one Action type quicker than normal, but as at least a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") (for example, a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") becomes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") becomes a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") becomes a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")). Tasks requiring more than one round cannot be optimized with this Talent.
