# Metamorph

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Metamorph](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Metamorph) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are a talented shapeshifter, capable of changing your appearance and body mass.

**Prerequisites:** [Shapeshift](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapeshift "Shapeshift"), [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

**Effect:** As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can change your mass when you use your [Shapeshift](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapeshift "Shapeshift") Species Trait, increasing or decreasing your size by one step. You can maintain this form for a number of rounds per day equal to your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score.

- If you reduce your size to Small, you gain a +1 size bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +5 size bonus on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks, but your [Carrying Capacity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carrying_Capacity "Carrying Capacity") is three-quarters of what it was prior to your use of Metamorph.
- If you increase your size to Large, you take a -1 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a -5 penalty on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. However, your [Carrying Capacity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carrying_Capacity "Carrying Capacity") doubles, you gain a +5 size bonus to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), and your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") increases by 1 square.
