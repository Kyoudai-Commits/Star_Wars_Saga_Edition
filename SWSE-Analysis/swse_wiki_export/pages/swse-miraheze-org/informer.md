# Informer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Informer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Informer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at prying into the affairs of others and dealing with those who are most interested in gaining that knowledge.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception")

**Effect:** You can use your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") modifier instead of your [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") modifier when making [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") checks. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") skill. If you are entitled to a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check reroll, you can reroll your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

Additionally, when you have favorable conditions for your [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check (such as from operating on your home planet), you reduce by half the time it takes to make a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check.
