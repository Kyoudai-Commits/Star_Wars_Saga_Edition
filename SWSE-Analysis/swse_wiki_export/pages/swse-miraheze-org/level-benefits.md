# Level Benefits

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Level_Benefits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Level_Benefits) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The *Star Wars Roleplaying Game* uses the terms *Class Level* and *Heroic Level* to mean different things. Class Level pertains to a character's level in a particular Class. Heroic Level pertains to a hero's total experience (but not including the [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") and [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") classes as they are not intended for heroes). So, a character who has only one Class has a Heroic Level and Class Level that are the same. (A 7th-level [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") has a Heroic Level of 7th and a Class Level of 7th.) But for a character with more than one [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), Class Level and Heroic Level are different.

A 4th-level [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout")/3rd-level [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") has a Heroic Level of 7th, with a [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Class Level of 4 and a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Class Level of 3. If a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") refers to your Class Level, but does not specify a Class, it means the combined number of levels in all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") that grant access to that [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent").

## Class Level Benefits

Heroes gain certain benefits from their individual [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Each of these Class Level Benefits progress independently of each other (so a character with Class Levels in multiple Heroic Classes would benefit from the sum of both Class Level Benefits).

### Base Attack Bonus

Your character's Base Attack Bonus applies to all attack rolls. Use the bonus that corresponds to your character's Class Level. If a character has more than one Class, add the Base Attack Bonus for each Class together to determine the character's Base Attack Bonus. [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") and [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers") have a Base Attack Bonus equal to their Class Level, while [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles"), [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels"), and [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts") have a Base Attack Bonus equal to their 3/4 their Class Level (rounded down).

For example a 7th-level [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") has a Base Attack Bonus of +7 while a 4th-level [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout")/3rd-level [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") has a Base Attack Bonus of +5 (+3 Base Attack Bonus for the [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") Class Level, and +2 for the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Class Level).

### Hit Points

After 1st level, a character gains [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") depending on their selected [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). The character always gets at least 1 Hit Point with each new level, regardless of the player's die roll and the character's [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.

### Acquiring Feats

Each character gets one [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") when the character is created (at 1st level). At 3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th level, characters gain another [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). These [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") are not related to Class Bonus Feats, and are tied to a hero's Character Level, not their Class Level.

### Ability Score Improvements

At 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 20th level, a heroic character increases two [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") by 1 point each.

When an Ability Score changes, all attributes associated with that score change accordingly. For example, when Sia-Lan becomes a 4th level [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), she increases her [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") from 15 to 16 and increases her [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") from 13 to 14. Now she's harder to hit, better at using ranged weapons, and all of her [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity")- and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma")-based [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") improve as well.

[Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") increases are retroactive. For example, if you increase your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score from 13 to 14, you immediately gain an additional [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") chosen from your class [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") and an additional [Language](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Language "Language"). Similarly, if a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who with two instances of the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat increased their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") from 13 to 14, they would gain two [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") (for a total of six).
