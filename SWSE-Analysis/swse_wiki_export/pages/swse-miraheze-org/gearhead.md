# Gearhead

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gearhead](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gearhead) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are naturally talented with machines and electronics.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can make [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") and [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks more quickly than normal. A check requiring a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") can be attempted as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a check requiring a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") can be attempted as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and a check requiring a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") can be attempted as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Checks requiring multiple [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") can be reduced by one [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Checks requiring more than a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") can be attempted in one-half the amount of time required at a -10 penalty.
