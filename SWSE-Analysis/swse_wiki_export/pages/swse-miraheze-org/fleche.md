# Flèche

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fl%C3%A8che](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fl%C3%A8che) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You throw yourself at your opponent to make a single precise strike.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Benefit:** Once per encounter, when you [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you can turn any natural attack roll of 17+ into a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").
