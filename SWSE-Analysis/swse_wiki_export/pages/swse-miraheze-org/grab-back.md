# Grab Back

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab_Back](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab_Back) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You are able to counter [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") and [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attacks, redirecting them back on your attacker.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") and [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attacks. If an enemy misses you when making a [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack, you can immediately make your own [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") attack against the same enemy as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

**Special:** If you have a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") that enables you to make a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack (such as [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") or [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip")), you can make a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack instead.
