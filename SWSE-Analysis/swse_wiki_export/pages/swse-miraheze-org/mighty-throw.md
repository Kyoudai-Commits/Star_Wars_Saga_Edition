# Mighty Throw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Throw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are highly accurate with [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons").

**Prerequisite:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13

**Effect:** You can add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier (in addition to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier) to your [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") bonus when using [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons") (including [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades") and grenadelike weapons). Also, you can increase the length of each [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") category by a number of squares equal to your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier.
