# Flash and Clear

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flash_and_Clear](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flash_and_Clear) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can use the smoke, noise, light, and confusion caused by your [Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade "Grenade") attacks to move unseen near your foes.

**Effect:** When you damage a target with a [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") or [Splash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Splash "Splash") weapon, you gain [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") against that target until the beginning of your next turn.
