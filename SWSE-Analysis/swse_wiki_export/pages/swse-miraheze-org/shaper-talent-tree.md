# Shaper Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaper_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shaper_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You manipulate [Yuuzhan Vong Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong_Biotech "Yuuzhan Vong Biotech") in new ways, molding it to your own ends and making it more effective.

## Core Talents

### Biotech Mastery

*Prerequisite:* [Biotech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist "Biotech Specialist")

When using the [Biotech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist "Biotech Specialist") feat to modify [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech"), you are able to make the appropriate modification in half of the normal time for half the normal cost. In addition, you can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check (even when distracted or threatened), but you cannot [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20").

### Expedient Mending

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Shaper**

You can temporarily mend a damaged or disabled [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech") device using the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

### Expert Shaper

You may reroll any [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check made to repair or modify a [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech") object, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

### Master Mender

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Shaper**

Whenever you temporarily mend a [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech") device using the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill, the mended device moves +4 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). In addition, the mended device only moves -3 steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") at the end of the scene or encounter, and is only [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") if this reduction brings it to -5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Skilled Implanter

*Prerequisite:* [Biotech Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Surgery "Biotech Surgery")

Whenever you use the [Biotech Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Surgery "Biotech Surgery") feat to install a [Bio-Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implant "Bio-Implant"), the Bio-Implant's attack bonus against the recipient's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") is halved.
