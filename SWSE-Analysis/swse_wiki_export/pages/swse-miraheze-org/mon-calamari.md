# Mon Calamari

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/a/a8/Mon_Calamari.webp/300px-Mon_Calamari.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Mon_Calamari.webp)

Amphibious land-dwellers, the Mon Calamari share their Outer Rim homeworld with the aquatic [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren"). They tend to be soft-spoken but vigorously defend causes that inspire them. Mon Calamari suffered under great oppression under [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"); thus, it was one of the first worlds to declare support for [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance").

Mon Calamari are widely recognized for their keen analytical and organizational abilities, and they have developed a reputation as being among the foremost ship designers in the galaxy. They see everything they create as a work of art, not just a tool or weapon.

## Mon Calamari Characteristics

**Personality:** Creative, quiet, and inquisitive, the Mon Calamari are dreamers who cherish peace, but aren't afraid to fight for the causes they believe in.

**Physical Description:** The average Mon Calamari stands 1.8 meters tall and weighs 72 kilograms. They have high-domed heads, large eyes, and smooth, mottled skin.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Mon Calamari age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-11 Years | 12-16 Years | 17-40 Years | 41-57 Years | 58-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The watery world of [Dac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dac "Dac").

**Languages:** The Mon Calamari can speak, read, and write both Mon Calamarian and Basic. Most tend to also learn Quarrenese as well.

**Example Names:** Ackbar, Bant, Cilhal, Ibtisam, Jesmin, Oro, Perit, Rekara.

**Adventurers:** Mon Calamari seek their dreams among the stars. They are idealistic and daring, often attaching themselves to causes that seem hopeless or lost right from the start. They strive to prove that even thinkers and dreamers can be brave and daring when the need arises.

## Mon Calamari Species Traits

Mon Calamari share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Mon Calamari receive +2 bonuses to their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"). Mon Calamari are bright and prudent, but they have relatively frail physiques.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Mon Calamari have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Mon Calamari have a base speed of 6 squares. They also have a swim speed of 4 squares.
- **Breathe Underwater:** As amphibious creatures, Mon Calamari can't drown in water.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Keenly perceptive, a Mon Calamari with [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Perception)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Perception) "Skill Focus (Perception)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Expert Swimmer:** A Mon Calamari may choose to reroll any [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. In addition, a Mon Calamari may choose to [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks, even when distracted or threatened.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Mon Calamari ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Mon Calamari can speak, read, and write both Basic and Mon Calamarian.
