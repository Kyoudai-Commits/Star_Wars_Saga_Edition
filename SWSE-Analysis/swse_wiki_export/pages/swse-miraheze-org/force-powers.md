# Force Powers

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Force Powers are special abilities available to anyone who takes the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat. They allow characters to do astonishing things such as play tricks with the minds of others, move heavy objects, see into the future, and even blast foes with terrible arcs of lightning. A character who takes the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat automatically learns a number of Force Powers equal to 1 + their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier (minimum of 1). A character can learn additional Force Powers by taking the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat again or by increasing their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier.

Choosing your Force Powers can be something of an art. If you choose poorly, you might be stuck with a Force Power that you can't use very well. When selecting Force Powers, remember the following:

Like [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), Force Powers cannot be changed once selected (not without your GM's approval, anyway). Also, some Force Powers are inherently more difficult to trigger than others. For example, *[Force Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Stun "Force Stun")* requires a DC 20 (or higher) [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate, which means that a low-level character attempting to use the power will fail more often than they succeed. Don't forget that you can select the same Force Power more than once if you want to be able to have extra uses of an easy-to-trigger power than a new power that is more difficult to activate.

For example, Sen Udo-Mal is a 1st-level [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") with a [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score of 11 (+0 modifier). He takes the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat at 1st level and immediately gains a Force Power. He chooses *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")*. At 3rd level, he takes the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat again, which allows him to learn a new Force Power; he chooses *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")*. At 4th level, Sen Udo-Mal raises his [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score from 11 to 12, thereby increasing his ability modifier from +0 to +1; at the time, he gains two additional Force Powers (one for each instance of the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat), so he chooses *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* and *[Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge "Surge")*.

## Force Power Suite

Your character's Force Powers collectively form a suite. When your character uses a Force Power, it's like playing a card and putting it in the discard pile. The Force Power takes effect, and it's no longer available to the character... at least for a while. When your character uses a Force Power, make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The check result determines the Force Power's effect.

Some Force Powers have all-or-nothing effects. Other Force Powers have multi-tiered effects, and your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result determines the maximum effect you can achieve, although you can always choose a lesser effect. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is too low to activate the Force Power's baseline effect, nothing happens and the action is wasted. Using a Force power removes it from your character's active "suite" of Force powers, regardless of whether the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check succeeds or fails.

You have different ways to regain spent Force Powers so that you can use them again:

- When combat is over and you have a chance to rest for 1 minute, you regain all of your Force Powers.
- If you roll a natural 20 on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate a Force Power, you regain all spent Force Powers at the end of your turn.
- You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") and immediately regain one spent Force Power.
- Some unique abilities (such as the **[Force Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Focus "Force Focus")** Talent) allow you to regain spent Force Powers in other ways.

### Force Power Descriptors

Some Force Powers are more strongly tied to one side of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") than the other. These Force Powers carry either the [*Light Side*] descriptor or the [*Dark Side*] descriptor. Powers that target creatures' minds carry the [*Mind-Affecting*] descriptor, and the [*Telekinetic*] descriptor are those Force Powers that utilize telekinesis.

Dark SideLight SideMind-AffectingTelekinetic

Using a Force Power with the [*Dark Side*] descriptor increases your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1. You cannot use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check when activating a Force Power with the [*Dark Side*] descriptor. Dark Side Powers stem from powerful negative emotions, and include *[Dark Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage "Dark Rage")* and *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")*.

If you have a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher, you cannot use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check when activating a Force Power with the [*Light Side*] descriptor. Light Side Powers are generally beneficial and include *[Sever Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sever_Force "Sever Force")* and *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*.

A Force Power with the [*Mind-Affecting*] descriptor has no effect on creatures that are mindless (that is, creatures with no [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score) or creatures that are immune to Mind-Affecting effects.

A Force Power with the [*Telekinetic*] descriptor uses telekinesis to accomplish its ends. Many telekinetic powers interact with the world physically in similar ways, and are covered as a group by some [Force Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talents "Force Talents"). If a target of a [*Telekinetic*] Force Power has [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), it gains a [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to its relevant [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") or on its opposed check to resist the Force Power.

If the target of a [*Telekinetic*] Force Power has [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), you take a penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate the Force Power unless you have used the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill this turn.

## Lightsaber Form Powers

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The following Force Powers are part of a new group of abilities called Lightsaber Form Powers. These Form Powers are tagged with the [*Lightsaber Form*] descriptor. Each Form Power represents a single technique associated with one of the Jedi [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat forms. Each Form Power uses some [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") manipulation, and most also involve a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack; additionally, each power has a special added benefit that you gain as a result of having the appropriate [Lightsaber Form Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree") for that Form Power. You must be wielding an activated [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to use any Form Power with the [*Lightsaber Form*] descriptor. You cannot *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* a Lightsaber Form Power, with two exceptions: *[Draw Closer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_Closer "Draw Closer")* and *[Pushing Slash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pushing_Slash "Pushing Slash")*.

Lightsaber Form Powers are intended to help Jedi (and Sith) characters make use of special attacks associated with the various Lightsaber Forms well before they are able to access the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree"). They are also a good way for a character to say that he or she is a practitioner of a certain form without necessarily having spent a precious [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") on that form. Many famous Jedi are regarded as students of multiple forms, and with these Form Powers, your heroes can dabble in several different forms, even if they are only considered masters of a small few.
