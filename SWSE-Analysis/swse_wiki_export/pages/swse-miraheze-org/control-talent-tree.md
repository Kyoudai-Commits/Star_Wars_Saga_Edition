# Control Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Control_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Control_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You have learned how to regulate your own body systems, control your emotions, and channel [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

## Core Talents

### Damage Reduction 10

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 10 for 1 minute.

### Equilibrium

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to remove all debilitating [Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditions "Conditions") affecting you and return to a normal state.

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Does the **Equilibrium** Talent move you to the top of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), or does it remove [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") (such as from [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"), [Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disease "Disease"), or serious injury)?

**A:** **Equilibrium** does both of these, removing all [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") *and* moving you to the top of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Force Focus

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you may make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If the check succeeds, you regain one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") of your choice.

### Force Recovery

*Prerequisite:* **Equilibrium**

Whenever you use your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you regain a number of additional [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 1d6 per [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") you possess (maximum 10d6).

## Additional Talents

### Beast Trick

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite: [Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")*

You can use the *[Mind Trick](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Trick "Mind Trick")* Force Power on creatures with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 2 and lower. However, a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") score of 2 or less still cannot perform any complex actions, or understand complex commands it wouldn't otherwise be able to understand. An affected [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") might understand "Attack those sith troopers!" but it would not comprehend, "Break into the command center, and disable the communications array."

### Channel Energy

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")*

Whenever you use the *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")* Force Power to successfully negate the damage from an Energy weapon attack, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to immediately activate any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") currently in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").

### Force Exertion

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

When you select this Talent, choose one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that you have in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can remove any one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") to add an extra use of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") designated by this Talent. Doing so moves you -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). This [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") is removed by resting for 1 minute. You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you choose a different [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to be gained by using this Talent.

### Force Harmony

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Once per encounter, you can activate one [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") that requires a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Force Suppression

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite: [Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")*

If you use the *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* Force Power to attempt to negate or redirect a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") used against you, but fail to overcome your opponent's [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result, you instead lessen the effect of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") by one step. For example, if an opponent attempted to use the *[Slow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slow "Slow")* Force Power on you and rolled a 21, if you fail to *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you only suffer the DC 15 effect, instead of the DC 20 effect. This Talent only affects [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that have variable effects based on your opponent's [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks, and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with static DCs without variable effects (such as *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")*) are unaffected.

### Indomitable Will

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to become immune to all [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects for 1 minute.

### The Will to Resist

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Once per turn, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being targeted by a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or other ability that targets your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and replace your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") with the result of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. After you make this check, until the end of your next turn, you take a -5 penalty on all [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks.

### Telekinetic Stability

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Whenever an attack or effect would normally move you against your will (such as being targeted by a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush"), or being moved by the *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* Force Power), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to negate that movement.
