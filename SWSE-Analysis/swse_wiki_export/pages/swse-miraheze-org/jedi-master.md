# Jedi Master

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Jedi Masters represent the pinnacle of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). They are Jedi who are both strong enough in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and patient enough in life to pass on their skills by teaching a new generation of Jedi. The journey from Padawan Learner to Jedi Master usually takes decades, and many who start upon the path never reach its final destination. Becoming a Jedi Master requires patience, inner strength, wisdom, and a deep connection to, and understanding of, [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Further, a [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") usually doesn't become a Jedi Master until they train a student to completion. Jedi are allowed only one Padawan at a time, and the training of a single Padawan can take years.

The most respected Jedi Masters are invited by their peers (and, in some cases, their former Masters) to join them on the Jedi Council. Those who join the Council use their wisdom and influence to instruct others in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and preserve peace in the galaxy. The Jedi Council is also tasked with identifying Force-sensitive children across the galaxy, and offering them instructions in [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). These lessons are fairly rudimentary- usually intended to teach a student "the basics" until he or she is old enough to receive formal training as a Padawan.

During [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era"), the Jedi Council would confer the title of Jedi Master upon those deemed qualified to teach others in the ways of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). During [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era") and [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era"), Jedi require no such approval; however, they must adhere to the tenets of [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code") to become Jedi Masters.

**Examples of Jedi Masters in Star Wars:** Adi Gallia, Eeth Koth, Luke Skywalker ([The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era")), Kit Fisto, Luminara Unduli, Mace Windu, Obi-Wan Kenobi (Episode III), Plo Koon, Shaak Ti, Yoda.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Jedi Master, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 12th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)")
- **[Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"):** At least one
- **Special:** Must be a member of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Jedi Masters gain a +3 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Jedi Masters gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Jedi Masters gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 7 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") (due to their strong connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), Jedi Masters gain more [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") than most other [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes")).

### Fearless

Jedi Masters are immune to Fear effects.

### Serenity

The Jedi Master may enter a brief meditative state as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). The Jedi Master may remain in this trance as long as they wish, and they are still aware of their surroundings; however, the Jedi Master is otherwise [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") and cannot perform any other [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions"). Upon emerging from the trance (as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")), the Jedi Master's first attack roll or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill check made in the following round is considered to be a Natural 20.

## Serenity and Vehicle Weapons

**Q:** Can you use Serenity to get an automatic Natural 20 when using a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon")? If so, doesn't this make it devastatingly good?

**A:** This isn't clear in the text, but the answer is no.

Serenity says that "your first attack roll... made in the following round is considered to be a Natural 20." This use of "your" was intentional, an artifact left over from earlier phrasing that made this point more clear: any reference to "*your* attack roll" means that it applies only to a personal, hand-held weapon, but a reference to "*an* attack" (or attack roll) is not limited this way.

As described in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, "Your attack roll with a ranged weapon is 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier + [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalty (if any)." However, later, it says that "An attack roll with a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") is... 1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier + [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") modifier." This difference in phrasing was intentional and meant to show that two different processes were occurring. Since an [Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack "Attack") with a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") is never made as "1d20 + [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier + [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalty," it is never technically "your attack roll" (first or otherwise), and thus it can't benefit from Serenity. (The Force Power *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")* is another example of this.)

Originally, this was part of the justification for why Luke had to turn off his targeting computer to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and blow up [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star"). With the targeting computer off, the [Proton Torpedoes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Proton_Torpedoes "Proton Torpedoes") basically became improvised ranged weapons, and he was limited to a very short-range shot (within the same [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale") square) due to the lack of computer assistance. However, Luke could use his personal [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") bonus and thus qualify for using a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") (or even *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")*, which we originally considered making his first [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power")).

Obviously, this explicit distinction was lost while condensing everything to fit in the available page count, but the remnants of it ("your attack roll") are still there.

## "Your Attack"

**Q:** I have a question about your ruling about using Serenity in [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in Jedi Counseling 109. Is the "your attack" wording supposed to apply in every case? If so, wouldn't that make some [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") no longer work with [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles")?

**A:** First, let me apologize for the way I wrote that answer because I made the issue far more confusing than it needed to be. I started by answering the question- "The answer isn't clear in the text, but the answer is no"- and I should have stopped at that point.

Instead, I went into the background of how things were in an earlier draft and what this "your attack" wording was originally supposed to mean. In other words, everything after that first sentence is supposed to explain how things got off track, but discussing the original wording isn't particularly helpful since we'd moved so far from it elsewhere. I further compounded the error by forgetting other cases where this wording would have applied but which had already been clarified to work (such as [Careful Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Careful_Shot "Careful Shot") and [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") in *[Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*).

Originally, I didn't want to call it "errata" because I knew that the actual wording of such errata hadn't been determined for any future printings of the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*. I didn't want to confuse the issue by having errata that might be the subject of later errata. In retrospect, that would have been far less confusing. Given this, let's completely retract that clarification. Instead, we'll just call this errata:

*When using a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"), you cannot apply any effect that causes an automatic Natural 20 or automatic [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") (such as spending a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") or using Serenity), unless a rule specifies that it can be used with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").*

We still might rephrase this a bit, but the meaning shouldn't change substantially.

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Jedi Master gains an additional [Jedi Master Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master_Talent "Jedi Master Talent"). A Jedi Master may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from the [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree") or any [Force Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Tree "Force Talent Tree") they wish (including [Tradition Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tradition_Talent_Trees "Tradition Talent Trees")), but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Force Secrets

You have glimpsed an eternal truth through your contemplation of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At 2nd level, and every level thereafter, the Jedi Master gains one [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret") that, once selected, cannot be changed.
