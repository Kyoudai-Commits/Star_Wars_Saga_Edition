# Accelerated Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accelerated_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accelerated_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Your melee attack is exceptionally quick.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6

**Effect:** Once per encounter, when using only weapons with which you are proficient, you can take a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").
