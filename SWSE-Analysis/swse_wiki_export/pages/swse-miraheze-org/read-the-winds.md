# Read the Winds

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Read_the_Winds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Read_the_Winds) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are so attuned to the wind patterns of your world that you can detect subtle shifts in air pressure around you.

**Prerequisite:** [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor")

**Effect:** You ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") and [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") for the purposes of [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks against targets within 10 squares of you.
