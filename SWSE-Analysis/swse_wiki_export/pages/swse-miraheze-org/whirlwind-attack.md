# Whirlwind Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can strike nearby opponents in an amazing, spinning [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack").

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Melee Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Defense "Melee Defense")

**Effect:** As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with your melee weapon, striking every target within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). This Whirlwind Attack uses the [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") rules; you make one attack roll and apply the result to every target in range.
