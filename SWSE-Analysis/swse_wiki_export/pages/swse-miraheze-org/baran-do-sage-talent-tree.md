# Baran Do Sage Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Baran_Do_Sage_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Baran_Do_Sage_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor") who become Baran Do Sages are contemplative and insightful, using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to unravel the skeins of the past, present, and future to determine the best course of action. They are masters of *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* and other sense-related Force abilities, making them excellent as both investigators and advisors. Only Force-users that are part of [The Baran Do Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages "The Baran Do Sages") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Enhanced Danger Sense

You gain a +10 bonus on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks made to [Avoid Surprise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Avoid_Surprise "Avoid Surprise"). Additionally, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to act in the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), even if you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised").

### Expanded Horizon

Whenever you use the [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, you can sense the consequences of your actions out to 1 hour into the future (instead of the normal 10 minutes). You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you do so to expand this window out to 8 hours, or a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to expand this window out to 24 hours.

### Knowledge and Defense

*Prerequisite:* **Enhanced Danger Sense**

You add your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") whenever your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus would normally be denied to you.

### Planetary Attunement

Whenever you arrive on a new planet, you can spend 10 minutes and a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to acclimate yourself to the planet's unique ebb and flow of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") energy. While on the planet, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against naturally occurring [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards") on the world, your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 1 square, and you can sense what the weather will be like to the immediate area up to 24 hours in advance as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

### Precognitive Meditation

Once per day, you can spend 10 minutes meditating to seek visions of the future. At that time, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a part of this meditation. Once during the rest of the day, whenever you or a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of are the target of an attack, you can choose to negate that attack provided the attack roll is not a Natural 20. At the end of the day, if you did not use this ability, you regain the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") spent on the meditation.
