# Skill Challenge: Recovery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge:_Recovery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You can snatch victory from the jaws of defeat when involved in a [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge").

**Effect:** Once per [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge"), you treat the [Skill Challenge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenge "Skill Challenge") as if it had the [Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recovery "Recovery") effect, even if it does not have that effect.

**Special:** This Feat is allowed only in campaigns that use [Skill Challenges](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Challenges "Skill Challenges").
