# Force Disciple

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Through meditation and contemplation of the deepest mysteries of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), some individuals transcend the dogma in which they were raised as they reach a new and deeper understanding of their connection to the universe around them. The ebb and flow of life- quickening, growing, struggling, dying- becomes a ceaseless chorus of destiny. Some Force Disciples tap into this power, become one with it, and speak its wisdom to others. Others might instead enslave destiny, twist its vision, and corrupt others for the sake of power.

Force Disciples, like [Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts"), exist in every [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition"). Even [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") have had Force Disciples among their ranks, but the dogma of these [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") tends to discourage such an eclectic approach to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). However, in less well-known dominant [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions")- such as [The Baran Do Sages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Baran_Do_Sages "The Baran Do Sages"), [The Witches of Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Witches_of_Dathomir "The Witches of Dathomir"), [The Gand Findsmen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Gand_Findsmen "The Gand Findsmen"), and [The Seyugi Dervishes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Seyugi_Dervishes "The Seyugi Dervishes")- Force Disciples hold great power and influence, shaping their [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") for centuries to come. [Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts") who become Force Disciples may wield power and influence over entire civilizations.

**Examples of Force Disciples in Star Wars:** Kadann, Lord Cronal ("Blackhole"), Rokur Gepta, the Saarai-kaar, Wialu.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Force Disciple, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** Any two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the
  - [Dark Side Devotee Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Devotee_Talent_Tree "Dark Side Devotee Talent Tree"),
  - [Force Adept Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent_Tree "Force Adept Talent Tree"), or
  - [Force Item Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Item_Talent_Tree "Force Item Talent Tree")
- **[Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"):** *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*
- **[Force Techniques](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Techniques "Force Techniques"):** At least one

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Force Disciples gain a +6 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), and a +3 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to both their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Force Disciples gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Force Disciples gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 7 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") (due to their strong connection to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), Force Disciples gain more [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") than most other [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes")).

### Indomitable

Force Disciples are immune to [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Does a Force Disciple's immunity to Mind-Affecting effects also apply to friendly effects, such as many of the [Officer Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer_Talents "Officer Talents")?

**A:** A Force Disciple is immune to such effects, even if the effects would be helpful. Conceptually, think of Force Disciples as having minds that exist on such a transcendental plane of thought that mundane influences simply don't move them enough to make a difference. That said, I don't think it would be unbalancing to allow a Force Disciple to raise or lower this immunity (probably a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), if you decide to use such a variant as a house rule).

### Prophet

Every time you gain a level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class"), you receive two [Destiny Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Points "Destiny Points") instead of the usual one. In addition, you may sacrifice this extra [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") immediately after gaining a Class Level. If you choose to do so, you instead receive a prophetic vision from [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"); the content of this vision is determined by the GM. The vision is instantaneous, so no time is required to exercise this option. Upon seeing the vision, you have the option to choose a new [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"), so long as the new [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") is related in some way to the vision. The GM is the final arbiter of what new [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") (or [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies")) are appropriate.

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Force Disciple gains an additional [Force Disciple Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Disciple_Talent "Force Disciple Talent"). A Force Disciple may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from the [Force Adept Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent_Tree "Force Adept Talent Tree") or any [Force Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Tree "Force Talent Tree") they wish (including [Tradition Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tradition_Talent_Trees "Tradition Talent Trees")), but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Force Secrets

You have glimpsed an eternal truth through your contemplation of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At 2nd level, and every level thereafter, the Force Disciple gains one [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret") that, once selected, cannot be changed.
