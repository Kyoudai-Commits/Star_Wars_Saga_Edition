# Draw Closer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_Closer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_Closer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form"), [Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

You grab an opponent with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), drawing him or her into the path of your weapon.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within 6 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | If your target is Medium or smaller in size and is neither [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") nor [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), you immediately pull it into the nearest unoccupied space adjacent to you (if no space adjacent to you is a legal space for the target due to its size, the power fails). You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. |
| 20 | If your target is Large or smaller in size and is neither [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") nor [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), you immediately pull it into the nearest unoccupied space adjacent to you (if no space adjacent to you is a legal space for the target due to its size, the power fails). You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. |
| 25 | If your target is Huge or smaller in size and is neither [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") nor [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), you immediately pull it into the nearest unoccupied space adjacent to you (if no space adjacent to you is a legal space for the target due to its size, the power fails). You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. |
| 30 | If your target is Gargantuan or smaller in size and is neither [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") nor [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), you immediately pull it into the nearest unoccupied space adjacent to you (if no space adjacent to you is a legal space for the target due to its size, the power fails). You can then make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Niman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Niman "Niman")):** If you have the **[Niman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Niman "Niman")** Talent, you can target a creature within 12 squares (instead of 6 squares).

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to pull the target into any adjacent space.
