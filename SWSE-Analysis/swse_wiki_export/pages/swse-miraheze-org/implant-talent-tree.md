# Implant Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Some [Shapers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapers "Shapers") develop Implants that can be placed quickly and easily, temporarily boosting the physical prowess of their beneficiaries. The following Talents all grant effects that last until the end of the encounter. At the end of the encounter, any target that benefits from one or more of these Talents immediately moves -3 steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), and the [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") becomes [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent"). The [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") can only be removed after 8 hours of rest, or by performing successful [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") on the target.

## Core Talents

### Adrenaline Implant

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can give one adjacent living creature an **Adrenaline Implant**. The target must be willing to receive this Implant, which grants the target 10 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") at the start of each of its turns. These [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") do not accumulate. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from different sources do not stack.

### Precision Implant

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can give one adjacent living creature a **Precision Implant**. The target must be willing to receive this Implant, which grants the target a +1 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on attack rolls until the end of the encounter.

### Resilience Implant

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can give one adjacent living creature a **Resilience Implant**. The target must be willing to receive this Implant, which grants the target a +5 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") until the end of the encounter.

### Speed Implant

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can give one adjacent living creature a **Speed Implant**. The target must be willing to receive this Implant, which increases the target's base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by 2 until the end of the encounter.

### Strength Implant

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can give one adjacent living creature a **Strength Implant**. The target must be willing to receive this Implant, which allows the target to deal +1 die of damage whenever it hits with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") until the end of the encounter.
