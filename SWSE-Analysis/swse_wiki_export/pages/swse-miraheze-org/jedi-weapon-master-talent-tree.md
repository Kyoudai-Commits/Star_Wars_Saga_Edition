# Jedi Weapon Master Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Weapon_Master_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Weapon_Master_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have learned to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to master a variety of weapons, and have expanded your martial prowess to cover more than just [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers").

## Core Talents

### Combat Trance

*Prerequisite:* *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")*

Whenever you use the *[Battle Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battle_Strike "Battle Strike")* Force Power, you gain the Force Power's bonus on attack rolls on your first [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") made each round until the end of the encounter. If you do not attack in a round, this effect ends.

### Improvised Weapon Expertise

|  |
| --- |
| ***This section was titled Improvised Weapon Mastery in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[Improvised Weapon Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapon_Mastery "Improvised Weapon Mastery")."*** |

You take no penalty on attack rolls made with [Improvised Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapons "Improvised Weapons").

### Twin Weapon Style

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), whenever you are wielding two weapons (or a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon")), you can make one attack with each weapon (or each end of a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon")). Each attack must be against a different target.

### Twin Weapon Mastery

*Prerequisite:* **Twin Weapon Style**

Whenever you use the **Twin Weapon Style** Talent, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") 2 squares between each attack. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Shoto Pin

*Prerequisite:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**

Whenever you are wielding a [Light](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light "Light") Lightsaber (typically a [Shoto Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shoto_Lightsaber "Shoto Lightsaber")) and successfully use the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** Talent to negate a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), the attacker can make no further [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") until the start of its next turn or until you are no longer adjacent to it.
