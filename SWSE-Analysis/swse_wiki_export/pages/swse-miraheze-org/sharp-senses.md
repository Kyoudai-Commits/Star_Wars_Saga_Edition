# Sharp Senses

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sharp_Senses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sharp_Senses) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your senses have been honed to the point where you can spot things that others could never see.

**Prerequisite:** [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).
