# Attack Combo (Melee)

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Combo_(Melee)) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

After landing successive blows against a single target, your [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") and [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") become more devastating.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +3

**Effect:** If you hit a single target with two consecutive [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") and/or [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") during the same turn, any additional [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") or [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") you make until the end of your next turn (including [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") and attacks made as [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions")) deal +1 die of damage on a hit. This extra damage stacks with extra damage granted by other [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") or [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").
