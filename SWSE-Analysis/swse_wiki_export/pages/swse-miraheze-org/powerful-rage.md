# Powerful Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Your Rage produces feats of extreme strength.

**Prerequisite:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage")

**Effect:** You gain a +4 bonus to [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") checks and [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength")-based [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") when Raging.
