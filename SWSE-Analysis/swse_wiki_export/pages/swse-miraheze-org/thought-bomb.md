# Thought Bomb

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thought_Bomb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thought_Bomb) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to radiate out harmful waves of telepathy, damaging the minds of nearby foes.

**[*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All opponents within 2 squares of you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to each target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and loses its [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. This is an area effect.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") by an additional +2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.
