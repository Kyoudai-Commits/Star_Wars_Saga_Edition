# Multi-Targeting

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Targeting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Targeting) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

Your targeting system allows you to track multiple targets over time, negating [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonuses.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13

**Effect:** You can use [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") across more than one round to [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") at a target with a weapon with which you are proficient. You can attack different targets prior to the completion of your [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") Action. If the subject of your [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") is out of your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") for any reason, you lose your [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") and must start again.

**Normal:** You must normally use two consecutive [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim").
