# Seyugi Dervish Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Seyugi_Dervish_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Seyugi_Dervish_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Seyugi Dervishes are masters of [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") combat augmented by their control over [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), and they traditionally hire themselves out as contract assassins. Their Force abilities allow them to move unseen and to make devastating, lightning-quick assaults on their targets. Only Force-users that are part of [The Seyugi Dervishes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Seyugi_Dervishes "The Seyugi Dervishes") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Seyugi Cyclone

If you are wielding no weapons (other than [Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves"), a [Stunning Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning_Gauntlet "Stunning Gauntlet"), or [Shockboxing Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing_Gloves "Shockboxing Gloves")), you can use the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") by spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), even if you do not possess the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat. Additionally, this Talent satisfies the prerequisites for the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat.

### Mobile Whirlwind

*Prerequisite:* **Seyugi Cyclone**

Whenever you use the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat, you can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") after the attack is resolved.

### Repelling Whirlwind

*Prerequisite:* **Seyugi Cyclone**

You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against any target hit by your [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") until the start of your next turn.

### Sudden Storm

*Prerequisite:* **Seyugi Cyclone**

Instead of a normal [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to make a [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), provided you are not wielding any weapons (other than [Combat Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Gloves "Combat Gloves"), a [Stunning Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning_Gauntlet "Stunning Gauntlet"), or [Shockboxing Gloves](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shockboxing_Gloves "Shockboxing Gloves")).

### Tempest Tossed

*Prerequisite:* **Seyugi Cyclone**

When you damage a target with a [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack"), you can choose to move that target 1 square in any direction as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). You can't move a target that's being [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), and you can't move the opponent into a solid object or another creature's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"). This forced movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
