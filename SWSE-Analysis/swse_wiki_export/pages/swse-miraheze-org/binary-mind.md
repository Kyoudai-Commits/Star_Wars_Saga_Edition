# Binary Mind

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary_Mind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary_Mind) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

The dual nature of your mind makes it difficult for others to influence you.

**Prerequisite:** [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean")

**Effect:** Whenever an enemy tries to use a Mind-Affecting effect against you, the enemy must roll twice and take the lower result.
