# Master of Disguise

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Disguise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Disguise) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are adept at creating convincing disguises in remarkably short periods of time.

**Prerequisites:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check when creating a [Deceptive Appearance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearance "Deceptive Appearance"). In addition, you can rush the process by taking a -2 penalty to the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check.

**Normal:** When you rush the creation of a [Deceptive Appearance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deceptive_Appearance "Deceptive Appearance"), you take a -10 penalty to your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check.
