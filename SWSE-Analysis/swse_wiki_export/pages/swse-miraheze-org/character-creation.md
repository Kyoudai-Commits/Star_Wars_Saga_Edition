# Character Creation

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Creation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Creation) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Make sure you review the [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities"), and [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") sections before using this overview when creating a *Star Wars* character. Print out a copy of the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet") to use as a record of your character. Characters generally begin play at 1st level and attain additional levels as they complete adventures (refer to [Level Benefits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Level_Benefits "Level Benefits") when leveling up characters).

## Step 1: Generate Ability Scores.

Every character has six [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") that represent the character's basic strengths and weaknesses. These [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities")- [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"), [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma")- affect everything a hero does, from fighting to using [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"). A score of 10 or 11 in an ability is average. Higher scores grant bonuses, and lower scores give penalties. When you create your character, you'll want to put your higher scores into the [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") most closely associated with your character's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class").

Use one of the methods described in the [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") section to generate your six [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"). Record the scores and put them aside for the moment.

## Step 2: Select Your Species

As a *Star Wars* character, you aren't limited to simply being [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human"). There are a variety of [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") available, from [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari") to [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee"). Select the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") (or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) you want to play from those presented in the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") section. Each [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") has its own set of special abilities and modifiers. Record these traits on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet").

## Step 3: Choose Your Class

A [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") provides you with a strong point for your character, a frame upon which you can hang [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and various story elements. Choose a [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") from those presented in the [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") section and write in on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet").

## Step 4: Assign Ability Scores

Now that you know what [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") and [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") you want your character to be, take the scores you generated in Step 1 and assign each to one of the six [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities"): [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"), [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Then make any adjustments to these scores according to the [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") you selected. For guidance, each [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") description indicates which [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") are most important for that [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). You might want to put your highest scores in the [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") that accentuate the natural benefits of the [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class").

Record your [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"). Record your ability modifiers as well.

## Step 5: Determine Combat Statistics

In [Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat "Combat"), you need to know your character's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), and attack bonuses, as well as how many [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") you have to spend.

Hit PointsDefensesDamage ThresholdBase Attack BonusSpeedAttack BonusesForce Points

Each character can withstand a certain amount of damage before falling unconscious or dying. The ability to take damage and keep functioning is represented by the character's [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") determines how many [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") you have at 1st level, as shown below:

| HEROIC CLASS | STARTING HIT POINTS |
| --- | --- |
| [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") | 18 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier |
| [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") | 24 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier |
| [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") | 30 + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier |

For example, if your character belongs to the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") Heroic Class and you have a [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score of 12, you start with 19 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") (18 plus 1 for your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") bonus). Your [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") increase as you gain levels, as described in [Level Benefits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Level_Benefits "Level Benefits").

Determine your character's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") as follows:

- **[Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"):** 10 + Your Heroic Level or Armor Bonus + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") Modifier + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus (if any) + Natural Armor Bonus (if any) + Size Modifier (if any)
- **[Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"):** 10 + Your Heroic Level + [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") Modifier + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus (if any) + [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") Bonus (if any)
- **[Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"):** 10 + Your Heroic Level + [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") Modifier + [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") Bonus (if any)

When you take your first level in a [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), you gain [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonuses on two or more [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), as shown in the [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") section.

If you wear [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), you must substitute your Armor Bonus for your Heroic Level when calculating your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). For example, a 1st level [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") with a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") of 12 while wearing a [Blast Helmet and Vest](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blast_Helmet_and_Vest "Blast Helmet and Vest") (+2 Armor Bonus) has a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 13 (10 + 2 Armor + 1 Dex + 1 Class). Some types of Armor also provide an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") in addition to an Armor Bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (as noted in the [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") section).

Attacks that deal massive amounts of damage can impair or incapacitate you regardless of how many [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") you have remaining. Your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") determines how much damage a single attack must deal to reduce your combat effectiveness or, in some cases, kill you. A Small or Medium character's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is equal to his or her [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). Record this number on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet").

The character's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") (BAB). Apply this bonus to the character's attack rolls. Base Attack Bonuses do not apply to damage rolls. Your character's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") determines your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). Record this number on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"):

| HEROIC CLASS | 1st LEVEL BAB |
| --- | --- |
| [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") | +1 |
| [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"), [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") | +0 |

Your character's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") (or [Locomotion System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_System "Locomotion System") if a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) determines their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). Most [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") have a [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") of 6 squares. [Ewoks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewoks "Ewoks") (among others) have a Speed of 4 squares because they are Small.

To determine your [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") bonus, add your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier to your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). Certain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") might provide additional modifiers, so make adjustments as necessary.

To determine your [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") bonus, add your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier to your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). Certain [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") might provide additional modifiers, so make adjustments as necessary.

Your character begins play with 5 [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points"). Indicate this in the space provided on the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"). If you end up taking the [Force Boon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Boon "Force Boon") Feat, you gain an additional 3 [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points").

If your GM uses the optional [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny") rules, your character begins play with 1 [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point"). Indicate this in the space provided on the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet").

## Step 6: Select Skills

[Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") represent how well a character accomplishes dramatic tasks other than combat, such as disabling a tractor beam generator or climbing a sheer surface. Each [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") comes with a list of [Class Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skills "Class Skills"). From this list, you get to pick a number of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") in which your character is considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained"). The number of [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") your character gets depends on the [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") you've selected and your character's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier. Once you've selected your character's [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"), determine the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") modifier for each [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill").

The [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") modifier for [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills") is one-half your Character Level (rounded down) + the relevant [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") modifier + 5. If you are [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") in a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") modifier is one-half your Character Level (rounded down) + the relevant [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") modifier. (In other words, you get a +5 bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made using [Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills").)

Some [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") cannot be used [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained"). See the [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") section for more information.

## Step 7: Select Feats

[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") are special features that provide a character with new capabilities or improvements. Your character begins play with at least one [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). If you are playing a [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human"), you get a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). In addition, your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") also gives you several starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") that you get for free. Select your [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") from the [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") section and record them on the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"). Some [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") may affect the information you've already recorded, so make adjustments as necessary.

## Step 8: Select a Talent

At 1st level, your character gets a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") (a special class feature). Choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees") available to your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Characters that have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat may take a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from a [Force Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Tree "Force Talent Tree"), or take a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from their [Tradition Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tradition_Talent_Tree "Tradition Talent Tree"). Some [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") have prerequisites that must be met before they can be selected. Select your [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") and record it on the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"). Some [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") may affect the information you've already recorded, so make adjustments as necessary.

## Step 9: Determine Starting Credits and Buy Gear

Your character's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") determines how many credits you start play with. Use your credits to purchase [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") for your character. The [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") section details [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), and [General Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/General_Equipment "General Equipment") you can purchase. Record all purchased [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") and remaining credits on the [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet").

**Q11:** What are the starting credits for a character above 1st level?

**A:** As a very loose guideline, your wealth at any given level should be something like this: (level x [level -1]) x 2,000 credits\*

*\*Double this for a [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") with the **[Wealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wealth "Wealth")** Talent*

This basically assumes that roughly 60% of your total income is spent on upkeep, paying rent, buying food, paying taxes, repairing equipment, getting medical care, etc. (This is a much higher rate than in D&D, where you'd only be expected to "burn" about 10%- but D&D doesn't have an Imperial Revenue Service.)

For example, at 16th level, it should be somewhere around half a million credits.

The reason I say this is a very loose guideline, though, is that at least half of this wealth would tend to be in a non-liquid form (e.g. property, intangibles such as favors and allies, etc.). As a very rough guideline, having someone owe you a favor (i.e. one-time intervention on your behalf, not to exceed one encounter) is worth about 2,000 x CL; having a long-term ally (i.e. someone who will help you out any time they are available) is worth about 40,000 x CL x (% chance that they're available).

## Step 10: Finish your Character

The last details you need to add to your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet") help you to visualize and roleplay your character. You need a name, of course- something that fits your [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), and the *Star Wars* galaxy. You should also determine your character's age, gender, height, weight, eye and hair color, skin color, and any relevant background information you want to provide. (Make sure to run your ideas past your Gamemaster so that he or she can fit them into the campaign.)

The [Heroic Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Traits "Heroic Traits") section provides guidelines that can help you with these details and characteristics.
