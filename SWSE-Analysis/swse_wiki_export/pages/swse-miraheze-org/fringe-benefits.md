# Fringe Benefits

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fringe_Benefits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fringe_Benefits) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are a well-known member of criminal society and can get the best deal possible on illicit goods.

**Prerequisite:** [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian")

**Effect:** Whenever you buy goods on the [Black Market](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Market "Black Market"), reduce the cost multiplier by 2 (for example, from x5 to x3), to a minimum of x1.
