# Pass the Blade

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pass_the_Blade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pass_the_Blade) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You deactivate your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") as your opponent tries to block it, reactivating it just after it passes by their blade.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. Your opponent takes a -2 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** the attack. (This penalty also applies to the **[Primitive Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Block "Primitive Block")** Talent). |
| 20 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. Your opponent takes a -5 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** the attack. (This penalty also applies to the **[Primitive Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Block "Primitive Block")** Talent). |
| 25 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. Your opponent takes a -10 penalty on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** the attack. (This penalty also applies to the **[Primitive Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Block "Primitive Block")** Talent). |
| 30 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. Your opponent cannot make [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks to **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** the attack. (This also applies to the **[Primitive Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Primitive_Block "Primitive Block")** Talent). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Trakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trakata "Trakata")):** If you have the **[Trakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trakata "Trakata")** Talent, you also ignore any deflection bonus the target has from the **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")** Talent.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal +2d6 points of damage with your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack if it hits.
