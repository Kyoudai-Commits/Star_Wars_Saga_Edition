# Weapon Focus

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are especially good at using particular weapons.

**Prerequisite:** Proficient with Chosen Weapon

**Effect:** Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"), you gain a +1 bonus on all attack rolls you make using the selected [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a new [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse") Feat; when you are wielding a single [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") weapon for which you have Weapon Focus, you can treat that weapon as a [Light Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapon "Light Weapon") for the purpose of [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Weapon Focus (and many other weapon-specific abilities) lists "proficient with weapon" among its prerequisites. Can you take that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") if you're proficient with at least one weapon in that group, or do you have to be proficient with all weapons in that group?

**A:** As long as you are proficient with at least one weapon in a group, you are qualified to take Weapon Focus (or any other weapon-specific ability) for that group. The benefits apply only for weapons within that group with which you are proficient.

For example, a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") with the **[Spacehound](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacehound "Spacehound")** Talent is proficient with all [Starship Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Weapons "Starship Weapons") (all of which are [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons")), so they would qualify to take [Weapon Focus (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Heavy Weapons)"). They would gain a +1 bonus on attacks with [Starship Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Weapons "Starship Weapons"), but they wouldn't get that benefit with an [E-Web Repeating Blaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/E-Web_Repeating_Blaster "E-Web Repeating Blaster") or any other [Heavy Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapon "Heavy Weapon") until becoming proficient with them.

**Q:** Does that reasoning apply when a prerequisite says "Weapon Proficiency ([Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"))"?

**A:** No. When the [Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency "Weapon Proficiency") Feat is specified, you must actually have that [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
