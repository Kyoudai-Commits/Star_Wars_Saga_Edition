# K'tara Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K'tara_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K'tara_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the K'tara style of unarmed fighting, which enables you to neutralize enemies before they even know that you are present.

**Prerequisites:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** You roll an extra die of damage against a [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") enemy on one [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") during your turn.

Once per encounter, when you deal damage with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") to a target that has been denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), make a second [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). If successful, you render the target incapable of speech until the end of your next turn. This is a [Stunning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stunning "Stunning") effect.

**Special:** If you have the **[K'tara Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/K%27tara_Expertise "K'tara Expertise")** Talent, you can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to cause a single adjacent enemy to be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against your first attack during the same round.
