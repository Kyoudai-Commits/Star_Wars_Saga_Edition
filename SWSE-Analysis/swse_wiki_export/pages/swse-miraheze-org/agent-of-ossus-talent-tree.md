# Agent of Ossus Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Agent_of_Ossus_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Agent_of_Ossus_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[The Agents of Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Agents_of_Ossus "The Agents of Ossus") are urban Force-users who specialize in using their powers to survive in the cities and towns at the center of civilization. Their proximity to Imperial authority necessitates both discretion and creativity among their members. Only Force-users that are part of [The Agents of Ossus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Agents_of_Ossus "The Agents of Ossus") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Buried Presence

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to become immune to detection by [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") for 1 hour. During this time, you always avoid detection by characters who are using the [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, and you cannot be the target of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power. You may use this as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to another character attempting to locate you with [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") (but before the character locates you). If you make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, this Talent's effect immediately ends.

### Conceal Other

*Prerequisite:* **Buried Presence**

When you use either the **Buried Presence** Talent or the **Vanish** Talent, you affect one other willing adjacent target, granting it the effect of that Talent as well. You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, it increases the number of adjacent willing targets you affect by one.

### Insightful Aim

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)") or [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)")

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to substitute your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") modifier for your [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") bonus when making attacks with a ranged weapon until the beginning of your next turn.

### Vanish

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to vanish from the sight of a single target within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you gain [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") from that target until the beginning of your next turn, or until you make an attack roll or [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") against the target.
