# Anointed Hunter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anointed_Hunter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anointed_Hunter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have been anointed as a hunter for your tribe and possess skills few other [Nelvaanians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nelvaanians "Nelvaanians") do.

**Prerequisite:** [Nelvaanian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nelvaanian "Nelvaanian")

**Effect:** If you end your [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") at least 2 squares from where you started, you gain a +1 competence bonus on attacks made with [Thrown Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapons "Thrown Weapons") until the end of your turn.
