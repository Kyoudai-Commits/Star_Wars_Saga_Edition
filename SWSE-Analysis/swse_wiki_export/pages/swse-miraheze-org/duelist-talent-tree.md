# Duelist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Through rigorous training and experience, you become one of the greatest swordfighters in the galaxy.

## Core Talents

### Force Fortification

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to negate a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") scored against you, and take normal damage instead. You can spend this [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") even if you've already spent a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") earlier in the round.

### Greater Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

You gain a +1 bonus on melee attack rolls with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)") feat.

### Greater Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)

*Prerequisites:* **[Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)")**, **Greater Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)**

You gain a +2 bonus on melee damage rolls with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by the **[Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)")** Talent.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Lightsabers)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") as part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack"), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Severing Strike

When you deal damage with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") that is equal to or greater than both the target's current [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") (that is, when you would deal enough damage to kill your target), you may choose to use this Talent. Instead of dealing full damage, you instead deal half damage to your target and move it -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). In addition, you sever one of your target's arms at the wrist or elbow joint, or one of the target's legs at the knee or ankle joint (your choice).

Severing part of an arm prevents the target from wielding weapons or using tools in that hand, and imposes a -5 penalty on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks") keyed to [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). Severing part of a leg knocks the target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"), reduces the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by half, reduces its [Carrying Capacity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carrying_Capacity "Carrying Capacity") by half, and imposes a -5 penalty on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and [Ability Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Checks "Ability Checks") keyed to [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity").

Because of the severity of such an injury, losing part of a limb causes a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") that can only be removed by having [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") successfully performed on you. A [Cybernetic Prosthesis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cybernetic_Prosthesis "Cybernetic Prosthesis") negates these reductions and penalties.

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Would **Severing Strike** be a [Major Transgression](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Major_Transgression "Major Transgression")? It seems that you'd never learn this [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") unless you intended to maim others.

**A:** No, **Severing Strike**, in itself, is not a [Dark Side Transgression](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Transgression "Dark Side Transgression") because it is designed to protect your target from an otherwise lethal blow. The same would be true for a doctor who has to perform a medical amputation. Although the act is "maiming" in the strictest sense, it is only to protect the target from a more dangerous medical problem.

This doesn't mean that **Severing Strike** can't be used in a blatantly evil way, of course. Cutting off the limbs of an enemy who is at your mercy would still be tantamount to torture no matter how you do it (by [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), scalpel, or angry [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee")).

## Additional Talents

### Improved Lightsaber Throw

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **[Lightsaber Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Throw "Lightsaber Throw")**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to throw your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") at a group of opponents. You make a single ranged attack roll (treating the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") as a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon")) and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of all targets in a 6-square line originating in your square. If your attack roll result exceeds a target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you deal normal [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") damage to that target (dealing half damage if you fail to exceed the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense")). This attack is considered an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

You can pull your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") back to your hand as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") by making a DC 20 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.

### Improved Riposte

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")**

When you successfully make a **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")** attack using the **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")** Talent, you do not count the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** Talent use that triggered the **[Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte "Riposte")** (thus, you take no cumulative penalty to [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks from that **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** attempt). Subsequent **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** attempts before the beginning of your next turn impose these penalties as normal.

### Improved Redirect

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")**

Once per turn, when you successfully Redirect an attack using the **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")** Talent, you do not count the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talent use that triggered the redirected attack (thus, you take no cumulative penalty to [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks from that **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** attempt). Subsequent **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** attempts before the beginning of your next turn impose these penalties as normal.

### Lightsaber Form Savant

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can return any one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*] descriptor to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, you can use it one additional time per encounter.

### Thrown Lightsaber Mastery

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Improved Lightsaber Throw**, **[Lightsaber Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Throw "Lightsaber Throw")**

Any target successfully struck by a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") you [Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw "Throw") moves at half [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (round down) until the beginning of your next turn.

### Shoto Master

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

When you wield both a One-Handed [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") and a Light [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") (typically a [Shoto Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shoto_Lightsaber "Shoto Lightsaber") or a [Guard Shoto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guard_Shoto "Guard Shoto")), you can consider the One-Handed [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to be a [Light Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapon "Light Weapon"). Additionally, if you have the **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")** Talent, you can activate the Talent as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your turn (instead of a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")) whenever you wield both a One-Handed [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") and a Light [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber").
