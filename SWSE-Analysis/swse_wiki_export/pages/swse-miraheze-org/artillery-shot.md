# Artillery Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Artillery_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Artillery_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to use the angle and distance between you and your target to devastating effect.

**Effect:** When you make an attack with a [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") or [Splash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Splash "Splash") weapon with which you are proficient against a target at greater than Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), you can affect two additional squares adjacent to the normal [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius or [Splash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Splash "Splash") area.
