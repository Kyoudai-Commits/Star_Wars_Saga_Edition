# Hunter's Instincts

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hunter's_Instincts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hunter's_Instincts) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have a sixth sense that allows you to spot your prey under almost any circumstances.

**Prerequisite:** [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, you always keep the better result, even if you have multiple reroll abilities.
