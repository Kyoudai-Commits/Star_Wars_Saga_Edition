# Vital Transfer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

You use your own life force to heal another living creature, using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as a conduit.

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Each time you use *Vital Transfer*, you take half as much damage as you heal (rounded down). You may not heal yourself with this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). The result of the check determines the result, if any (you may choose to heal less than the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check allows):

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target heals [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 2 x its Character Level. |
| 20 | The target heals [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 3 x its Character Level. |
| 25 | The target heals [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 4 x its Character Level. |

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to avoid taking any damage when you use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). You may spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to move the target +5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") in addition to healing them normally.

## Optional Rules

You might scale back the benefits of *Vital Transfer* so that groups with multiple Force-users aren't quite so far ahead of the rest. You might want to use either or both of the following variant rules:

Vital SacrificeVital Limits

A character that uses the *Vital Transfer* Force Power to heal another character cannot benefit from the use of *Vital Transfer* in the same 24- hour period.

A character can benefit from *Vital Transfer* only once in a 24-hour period, but you can use a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to overcome this restriction when you activate *Vital Transfer*. (Since you can spend only one [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") per round, you cannot also spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to avoid taking damage from the use of *Vital Transfer*.)
