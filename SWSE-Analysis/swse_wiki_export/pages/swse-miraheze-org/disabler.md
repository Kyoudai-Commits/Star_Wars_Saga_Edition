# Disabler

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabler](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabler) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are skilled in the use of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") weapons and gain certain benefits depending on which weapon you use.

**Effect:** When making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with an [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") weapon with which you are proficient, you gain one of the following benefits, depending on the kind of weapon you use.

- **[Ion Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Grenade "Ion Grenade"):** When you use an [Ion Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Grenade "Ion Grenade"), the Grenade's [Burst](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst "Burst") radius is 3 squares instead of 2 squares.
- **[Ion Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Pistol "Ion Pistol"):** When you use an [Ion Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Pistol "Ion Pistol"), the damage dice increase from d6 to d8.
- **[Ion Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Rifle "Ion Rifle"):** You treat the [Ion Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion_Rifle "Ion Rifle") as an [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon.
