# Brink of Death

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brink_of_Death](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brink_of_Death) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can take an enemy to the Brink of Death without killing them.

**Effect:** When you deal sufficient damage on an attack to kill a target, you may instead choose to reduce the target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), leaving it [Unconscious](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unconscious "Unconscious") but alive. Normal rules for being at 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") apply.
