# Cleave

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can follow through with a powerful [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack").

**Prerequisite:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack")

**Effect:** If you deal an opponent enough damage to reduce its [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to 0, you get an immediate extra [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against another opponent within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). The extra attack is with the same weapon and at the same attack bonus as the attack that dropped the previous opponent. You can use this ability once per round.

**Combined Feat:** If you have the [Follow Through](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follow_Through "Follow Through") feat, you can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") before making the extra [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") granted by the Cleave feat.
