# Spacer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You prowl the space lanes seeking wealth, fame, adventure, or something more. You're also pretty good with [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in general.

## Core Talents

### Hyperdriven

Once per day while aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), you can add your Class Level as a bonus on a single attack roll, [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), or [Ability Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Check "Ability Check"). The decision to add this bonus can be made after the result of the check is known.

### Spacehound

You take no penalty on attack rolls in [Low Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low_Gravity "Low Gravity") or [Zero-Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero-Gravity "Zero-Gravity") environments, and you ignore the debilitating effects of [Space Sickness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Sickness "Space Sickness"). In addition, you are considered proficient with any [Starship Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Weapon "Starship Weapon").

### Starship Raider

*Prerequisite:* **Spacehound**

You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls made while aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"). This bonus applies to attacks made with [Starship Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Weapons "Starship Weapons"), as well as personal weapons used aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship").

### Stellar Warrior

*Prerequisite:* **Spacehound**

Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on an attack roll made aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship"), you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of the encounter, it is lost. This Talent works with both [Starship Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Weapons "Starship Weapons") and personal weapons used aboard a Starship.

## Additional Talents

### Cramped Quarters Fighting

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Starship Raider**

When adjacent to an obstacle or barrier, you gain a +2 [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

### Deep Space Raider

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Starship Raider**

Whether you are taking on military targets on behalf of a resistance movement or you are out for personal gain, you are experienced at raiding Starships and similar targets. You can use each if the following Actions once per encounter:

- *Clear a Path:* Your attack forces an enemy to alter course or move out of your way. You must be fighting aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") to use this Action. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a ranged attack. If the attack hits, on its next turn your target must move its Speed to a square that is not adjacent to you. If the target is an enemy [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") engaged in a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), the target instead must attempt to disengage from the [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight").
- *Covering Fire:* You squeeze off several shots as you escape pursuing enemies. As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), if you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), you may move the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") up to twice its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") and make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with one of the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")-controlled [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems") at any point during that movement. If you hit and deal damage to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") with this attack, the target [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes a -2 penalty to attack rolls against your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") until the end of your turn.
- *Disabling Fire:* Make a ranged attack with your [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"). If you make this attack against a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") and hit and deal damage, you can choose one of the following to take effect until the end of your next turn:
  - One of the target's [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") ceases to function.
  - The target's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") is reduced to 0.
  - The target's [Hyperdrive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrive "Hyperdrive") is disabled.
  - The target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced to 2 squares ([Character Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Scale "Character Scale")).

### Make a Break for It

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Stellar Warrior**

Once per encounter, while on or in a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you can move up to one-half your speed, or move the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") up to one-half its speed if you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
