# Force Item Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Item_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Item_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can imbue weapons and objects with the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

## Core Talents

### Attune Weapon

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to attune a melee weapon. Attuning the weapon takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). From that point forward, whenever you wield the **Attuned Weapon**, you gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on attack rolls. The weapon is attuned to you alone; others who wield the weapon do not gain the [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus.

### Empower Weapon

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to empower a melee weapon. Empowering the weapon takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). From that point forward, the **Empowered Weapon** deals an additional die of damage, but only when wielded by you. For example, an empowered [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") deals 3d8 points of damage, instead of 2d8 points of damage. Others who wield the weapon do not gain the bonus damage die.

### Force Talisman

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a weapon or some other portable object with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), creating a Talisman that provides protection to you. Creating the Talisman takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). While you wear or carry the Talisman on your person, you gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to one of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), or [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")).

You may only have one **Force Talisman** active at a given time, and if your **Force Talisman** is destroyed, you may not create another **Force Talisman** for 24 hours.

### Greater Force Talisman

*Prerequisite:* **Force Talisman**

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a weapon or some other portable object with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), creating a Talisman that provides protection to you. Creating the Talisman takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). While you wear or carry the Talisman on your person, you gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), and [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")).

You may only have one **Greater Force Talisman** active at a given time, and if your **Greater Force Talisman** is destroyed, you may not create another **Greater Force Talisman** (or regular **Force Talisman**) for 24 hours.

## Additional Talents

### Focused Force Talisman

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Force Talisman**

When you create a **Force Talisman**, you can select a single [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") from your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). Whenever you are wearing this **Focused Force Talisman** and activate the selected [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to immediately regain all your expended uses of that spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), adding it to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").

### Force Throw

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Empower Weapon**

You can hurl [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons") or [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons") your size or smaller as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), treating it as a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon"). The [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon") deals normal weapon damage if it hits. If the weapon deals Piercing or Slashing damage, it becomes embedded in your target, remaining there and causing an additional die of damage each round at the end of the target's turn, and also when it is removed (removing the embedded weapon is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and an adjacent ally can remove the embedded weapon for you).

Your target must be within 6 squares of you. The weapon does not automatically return to you, but you can retrieve it with the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power (dealing an additional die of damage in the process, if the weapon is embedded in the target, as above).

### Greater Focused Force Talisman

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Focused Force Talisman**

As the **Focused Force Talisman** Talent, except that a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") spent to immediately recover the selected [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") does not count against the "one per turn" restriction on spending [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points").

### Primitive Block

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Empower Weapon**

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you may negate a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") by making a successful [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The DC of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate, and you take a cumulative -5 penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks to use this Talent for every time you have used **Primitive Block** since the beginning of your last turn.

You must have an **Empowered Weapon** drawn to use this Talent, and you must be aware of the attack and not [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"). You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Talent to negate an attack against an adjacent character. You may use the **Primitive Block** Talent to negate melee [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks"), such as those made by the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat. If you succeed on the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you take half damage if the attack hit, and no damage if the attack missed.
