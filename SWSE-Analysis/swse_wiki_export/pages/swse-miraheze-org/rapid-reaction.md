# Rapid Reaction

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Reaction) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Your lightning reflexes allow you to complete complex actions in response to the actions of others.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can use two different [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions") in response to the same trigger. For example, if you use the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talent and fail to negate the attack, you can also use *[Negate Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Negate_Energy "Negate Energy")* as a response to the same attack.

**Normal:** Without this feat, you can use only one [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") in response to a particular trigger.
