# Stand Tall

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stand_Tall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stand_Tall) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Your courage under fire inspires those around you to acts of bravery.

**Effect:** Once per encounter when you take damage, all allies who are within 6 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") can, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), make a single attack on the target that damaged you.
