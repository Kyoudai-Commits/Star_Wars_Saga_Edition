# Gunslinger Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You never travel anywhere without a blaster (or two), and you know how to handle yourself in a gunfight. The following Talents may only be used with [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") or [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles").

## Core Talents

### Debilitating Shot

If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you move the target character -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") if the attack deals damage. This Talent can be used only against characters, not objects or [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles").

### Deceptive Shot

Select one target in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") within 6 squares. You can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") on the same turn to make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check; if the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against your attacks until the beginning of your next turn.

### Improved Quick Draw

If you are carrying a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") (either in your hand or in a holster), you may draw the [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") and make a single attack during a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), even if you are [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"). If you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised"), you may take any single [Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Action "Action") of your choice, as normal.

### Knockdown Shot

If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), and the attack hits, you knock the target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in addition to dealing damage. You can't use this Talent to knock down targets two or more size categories bigger than you.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Pistols)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") as part of a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack"), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Ranged Disarm

You can [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent using a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"). If your **Ranged Disarm** attack fails, your opponent doesn't get to make a free attack against you.

### Trigger Work

You take no penalty on your attack roll when using the [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feat.

## Additional Talents

### Blind Shot

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You ignore the penalties on your ranged attack rolls when a target has [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") or [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment").

### Damaging Disarm

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisite:* **Ranged Disarm**

If you successfully [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent using a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), the target also takes half damage from the attack.

### Keep Them Honest

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisite:* [Careful Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Careful_Shot "Careful Shot")

When using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to [Suppress an Enemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_an_Enemy "Suppress an Enemy"), the enemy instead takes a -5 penalty to all attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Lingering Debilitation

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Debilitating Shot**

Once per encounter, when you successfully use **Debilitating Shot** to move a target character -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), the target suffers a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") requiring 4 hours of rest or a DC 25 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to remove.

### Mobile Attack (Pistols)

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Multiattack Proficiency (Pistols)**, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Focus (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Pistols) "Weapon Focus (Pistols)")

Immediately after making a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") where you attack with two [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), you may move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Pistol Duelist

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are a master of the elegant, if archaic, custom of dueling with [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"). You can use each of the following Actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *End Game:* You make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") against an opponent within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). The opponent's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is halved (rounded down) for the purpose of this attack.
- *Snap Aiming:* You make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with the benefits of [Aiming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming "Aiming").
- *Stand Ready:* You gain a +4 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn and make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack").

### Ranged Flank

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

If you are within 6 squares of a target and are armed with a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") or a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle"), you can act as though you occupied the nearest square adjacent to the target for purposes of determining whether or not you or any allies are [Flanking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanking "Flanking") that target. You may only be considered to be [Flanking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanking "Flanking") a single target at a time. You must spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on your turn to designate the target you [Flank](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank "Flank") at range.

### Retreating Fire

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

When moving away from a pursuing target, if you either [Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run "Run") or use two [Move Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Actions "Move Actions") during this turn, you can make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a -5 penalty as part of your move action. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to avoid the penalty.

### Slowing Shot

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Debilitating Shot**

If you successfully use **Debilitating Shot**, until the target moves to the normal state on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") or until the end of the encounter, the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 2 squares, and it loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"). These effects occur in addition to the effect of **Debilitating Shot**. If you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 4 squares or half of its normal speed, whichever is the greater reduction.

### Swift Shot

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Once per encounter, you can make a single [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a handheld weapon as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). However, you cannot use your remaining [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") for an attack.
