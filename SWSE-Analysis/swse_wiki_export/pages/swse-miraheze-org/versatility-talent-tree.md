# Versatility Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Versatility_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Versatility_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You are known for your ability to adapt to any situation, heedless of adversity.

## Core Talents

### Adapt and Survive

When an enemy within 24 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") receives a morale or insight bonus of any kind, you also gain the benefits of that bonus until the end of your next turn.

### Defensive Protection

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") and add the results of the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to any one of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), or to one of the [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") of an adjacent ally. This bonus lasts until the beginning of your next turn.

### Quick on Your Feet

Once per encounter, you may [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Ready and Willing

When you [Ready](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ready "Ready") an Action, you can choose at any time before the start of your next turn to take your [Readied Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Readied_Action "Readied Action") at the end of the current turn, after the acting creature, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") completes its Action.

### Unbalancing Adaptation

*Prerequisite:* **Adapt and Survive**

When you use the **Adapt and Survive** Talent, you also deny the target the bonus that triggered the Talent to one enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").
