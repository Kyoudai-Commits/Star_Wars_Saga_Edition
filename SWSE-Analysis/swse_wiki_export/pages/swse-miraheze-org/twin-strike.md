# Twin Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twin_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twin_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You swing both of your [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") in an arc toward the target, slamming them home with great force.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against your target. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to the damage roll. |
| 25 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against your target, with a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to the attack roll. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to the damage roll. |
| 30 | You make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against your target, with a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to the attack roll. If this attack hits, add the base damage of your other [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to the damage roll. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Jar'Kai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jar%27Kai "Jar'Kai")):** If you have the **[Jar'Kai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jar%27Kai "Jar'Kai")** Talent, you triple your deflection bonus from the **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")** Talent instead of doubling it until the end of your next turn.

**Special:** You must be wielding two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") to use this Form Power. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add +1 die of damage to the damage from your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack.
