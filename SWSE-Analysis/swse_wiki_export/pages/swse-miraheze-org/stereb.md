# Stereb

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stereb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stereb) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Dawn of Defiance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dawn_of_Defiance "Dawn of Defiance") ([Sword of the Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sword_of_the_Empire "Sword of the Empire"))*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/2/2c/Stereb.webp/250px-Stereb.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Stereb.webp)

Jokingly called "hairless Wookiees" by the few who have seen them, Stereb have been found on a number of worlds with rocky crusts. Not technologically adept, the Stereb live in harmony with the surfaces of a planet, working elaborate stone passages underground in the belief that they're part of the world's circulatory system.

## Stereb Characteristics

**Personality:** Stereb are deliberate and methodical. Not being very curious, they tend to take things at face value. They are not aggressive and usually fight only if forced into a confrontation.

**Physical Description:** Stereb are tall humanoids with mostly hairless bodies that range from pale red to brown with reddish undertones. They have overly long arms and large hands and feet.

**Homeworld:** The Stereb homeworld is unknown, although small enclaves have been found on [Serroco](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Serroco?action=edit&redlink=1 "Serroco (page does not exist)"), [Prakith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prakith "Prakith"), and a few other lightly populated planets. Given their lack of technology, some suspect that the Stereb were transported to several planets as slave labor far back in their history.

**Languages:** Stereb speak Stereb. They are able mimics, and there are a number of Basic words in the Stereb language.

**Example Names:** Herdr'tui, Eldr'nol, Helr'ven. Stereb names include a Basic-sounding prefix relating to their function in the community (*herdr* means herder, *eldr* means elder, *helr* means healer, and so on).

**Adventurers:** Most Stereb prefer to remain within the safety of their enclaves. The few Stereb who wander abroad are most likely to be [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"). Stereb Force-users are very rare.

## Stereb Species Traits

Stereb share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Stereb receive a +4 bonus to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and a +2 bonus to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"), but suffer -2 penalties to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Stereb are strong and hardy. They are not aggressive, and they might take time to reach a decision, but once they choose a course of action, they are deliberate and steadfast.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Stereb have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Stereb have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Stereb are very adept when working with stone. A Stereb who has [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Mechanics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Mechanics) "Skill Focus (Mechanics)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). However, this bonus applies only when the Stereb character is working with stone.
- **Darkvision:** Stereb ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. However, they cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
- **Primitive:** Stereb do not gain [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"), or [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") at first level, even if their [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") normally grants them.
- **Subterranean:** Stereb are adapted to a subterranean life and can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks when in an underground environment, even when distracted or threatened.
- **Tough:** Like the stone they work with, Stereb are physically hardened, gaining a +5 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") and a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Stereb can speak, read, and write Stereb.
