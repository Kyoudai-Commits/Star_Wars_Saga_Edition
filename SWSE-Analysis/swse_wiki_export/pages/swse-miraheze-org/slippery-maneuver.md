# Slippery Maneuver

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slippery_Maneuver](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slippery_Maneuver) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are adept at finding ways out of tough situations.

**Prerequisite:** [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge")

**Effect:** You can apply the effects of the [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") feat against attacks made from two opponents. In addition, when you use the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action to move away from a target against whom you use the [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") feat, you can move at your full [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). You still provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") if you must move more than 1 square to escape a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area").

**Normal:** The benefit of [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") applies to one target. Once you clear a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), you can continue to move up to a total of half you [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").
