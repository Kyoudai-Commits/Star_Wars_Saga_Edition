# Charging Fire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Fire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are able to make [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") while [Charging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging "Charging").

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4

**Effect:** When you [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you may make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") instead of a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") at the end of your movement. Unlike a normal [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), your momentum does not help you overcome your target, so you gain no bonus on your attack roll. As with a normal [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you still take a -2 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

**Normal:** You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a +2 bonus on your attack roll at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge").

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") feat, you reduce your penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against all opponents to -1 when making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge").
