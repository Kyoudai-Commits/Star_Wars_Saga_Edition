# Exotic Weapon Proficiency

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You understand how to use a type of [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** Choose an [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"), such as a [Bowcaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bowcaster "Bowcaster") or [Atlatl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atlatl "Atlatl"); you make attack rolls with the selected weapon without penalty.

**Normal:** A character who uses a weapon without being proficient with it takes a -5 penalty to attack rolls.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon").
