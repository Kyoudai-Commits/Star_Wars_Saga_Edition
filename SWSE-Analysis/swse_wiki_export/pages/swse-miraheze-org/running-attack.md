# Running Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can move as you attack.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

**Effect:** When making an attack with a melee or ranged weapon, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") both before and after the attack, provided that the total distance moved is not greater than your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") feat, you can add your dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against anyone who makes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against you while using the Running Attack feat.
