# Fortune Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortune_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortune_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Many [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") like to gamble with destiny, putting everything on the line and trusting fate (or [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")) to bring them fortune, fame, and success.

## Core Talents

### Fool's Luck

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain one of the following benefits for the rest of the encounter:

- +1 competence bonus to attack rolls
- +5 competence bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks")
- +1 competence bonus to all your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses")

If you use this Talent to grant yourself a luck bonus to your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), this bonus also applies to the [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") of any [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are on (even if you are not the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")).

### Fortune's Favor

Whenever you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with a melee or ranged attack, you gain a free [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). You must take the extra [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") before the end of your turn, or else it is lost.

### Gambler

You gain a +2 competence bonus on [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") checks when you [Gamble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamble "Gamble"). You can select this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, the competence bonus increases by +2.

### Knack

Once per day, you can reroll a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") and take the better result. You can select this Talent multiple times; each time you select this Talent, you can use it one additional time per day.

### Lucky Shot

*Prerequisite:* **Knack**

Once per day, you can reroll an attack roll and take the better result. You can select this Talent multiple times; each time you select this Talent, you can use it one additional time per day.

## Additional Talents

### Avert Disaster

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Fool's Luck**

Once per encounter, you can turn a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") against you into a normal hit.

### Better Lucky than Dead

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Fool's Luck**

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you gain a +5 luck bonus to any one [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") until the start of your next turn.

### Dumb Luck

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Lucky Shot**

You are possessed of incredible luck and an uncanny ability to succeed where others would fail. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *Elude Enemy:* Make a single melee or ranged attack against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If you damage the target, you gain a +2 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against this target until the beginning of your next turn.
- *Escape:* Make a single melee or ranged attack against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If that target successfully damages you before the start of your next turn, you can immediately move 2 squares as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
- *Make your Own Luck:* Make a single melee or ranged attack against any target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If you miss this target, you gain a +2 bonus on your next attack roll.

### Labyrinthine Mind

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you become immune to all [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects until the end of your next turn (you can choose to ignore this for beneficial effects). Any [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effects currently afflicting you are also removed, though you can choose to retain any beneficial effects you currently have.

### Lucky Stop

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Knack**

A successful hit against you is mitigated by an item you just happen to be wearing or carrying, or glances off your armor or clothing in just the right way. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can negate the damage from a single attack that would normally reduce you to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").

### Ricochet Shot

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Lucky Shot**

When making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), you can choose to reduce the benefit of that target's [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") by one step, from [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover") to [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), or [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") to no [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"). You deal only half damage with this attack.

### Uncanny Luck

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Lucky Shot**

Once per encounter, you can consider any single d20 roll of 16 or higher to be a Natural 20.

### Unlikely Shot

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Lucky Shot**

Once per encounter, you can reroll the damage of one attack, keeping the better of the two results.
