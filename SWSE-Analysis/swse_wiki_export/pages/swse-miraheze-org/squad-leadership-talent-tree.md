# Squad Leadership Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are an expert at small-unit tactics, leading a small group of allies into battle as a cohesive unit.

All Talents in the Squad Leadership Talent Tree apply to other characters in a squad you command. A squad includes yourself plus a number of characters equal to or less than the sum of your [Elite Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper "Elite Trooper") Class Level plus your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum of one other character). These characters must be on foot, must have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to you, and must be able to communicate with you to gain the benefit of your Squad Leadership Talents. As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you designate which characters are in your squad at the beginning of your first turn.

Each squad can benefit from the Talents of only a single squad leader, designated when an encounter begins. A character without a squad leader can join an existing squad if both character and squad leader are willing and if the squad leader has not reached his or her maximum squad size.

## Core Talents

### Fall Back

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can enable each member of your squad to immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") 2 squares. This movement does not provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

### Form Up

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn, as long as they are within 6 squares of another squad member.

### Full Advance

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to damage rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Hold Steady

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

Once per encounter, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you move all members of your squad +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Search and Destroy

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you give all squad members a +2 morale bonus to [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks until the end of your next turn.
