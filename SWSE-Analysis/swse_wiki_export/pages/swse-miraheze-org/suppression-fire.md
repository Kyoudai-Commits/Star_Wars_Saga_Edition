# Suppression Fire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppression_Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppression_Fire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

You can lay down a hail of blaster fire to pin your enemies.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire"), [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)")

**Effect:** When you use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to impose a penalty to an enemy's attack rolls and your attack roll exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that enemy must end its next turn in a position where it has [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from you if possible. Targets whose level is equal to or higher than your Character Level are immune to the effect of Suppression Fire. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.
