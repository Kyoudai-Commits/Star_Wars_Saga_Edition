# Combat Reflexes

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You can respond quickly and repeatedly to opponents who let their guard down.

**Effect:** When opponents leave themselves open, you may make a number of additional [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier.

For example, a character with a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") of 15 can make a total of three [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") in a round: the one [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") every character is entitled to, plus two more attacks because of their +2 [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus. If four [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers") move through the character's [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), they can make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") against three of the four. You still only make one [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") on a single opponent.

With this Feat, you may also make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") while [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

**Normal:** A character without the Combat Reflexes Feat can make only one [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") per round, and can't make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") while [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

**Combined Feat:** You may use the [Return Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Return_Fire "Return Fire") Feat a number of times during an encounter equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus, but no more than once during a given enemy's turn.
