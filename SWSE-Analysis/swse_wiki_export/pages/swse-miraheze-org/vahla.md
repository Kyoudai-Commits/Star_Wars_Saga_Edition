# Vahla

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vahla) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/8/8a/Vahla.webp/300px-Vahla.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Vahla.webp)

The Vahla are a nomadic, [Near-Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Near-Human "Near-Human") Species who roamed the galaxy in search of their homeworld, and are generally regarded as a naturally bright and shrewd people. Their small economy is fueled by petty theft and limited-scale piracy, and, following the Great Jedi Purge, by bounty hunting.

All Vahla are Force-sensitive, and whether aware or not, have ties to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Most Vahla become members of [The Ember of Vahl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ember_of_Vahl "The Ember of Vahl"), a Dark Side cult that worships Vahl, a goddess of fire and destruction. Though not all Vahla are members, they are all expected to follow the orders of a six-priest central coven believed to have been "Chosen" by the goddess.

## Vahla Characteristics

**Personality:** Vahla are a shrewd people, often finding themselves on the darker side of galactic society. Their devotion to their goddess Vahl typically isolated them from other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), and that isolation is mutual due to The Ember of Vahl's need for live sacrifices.

**Physical Description:** Vahla are largely similar to [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") in general build, although being slightly taller and with thick cartilage replacing their bones. The color of Vahla skin varies, with dark gray and dusky brown marking the extremes. Their eyes are often pale blue, gray, or lavender, and their hair color ranges from black to gray to white, with both or all three colors sometimes being present simultaneously. Occasionally, Vahla are born with red hair; those so endowed are often regarded as having the blessing of the goddess Vahl.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Vahla age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-11 Years | 12-15 Years | 16-40 Years | 41-59 Years | 60-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The Vahla are nomads, with no planet to call their own. While it is known that they are searching for their homeworld, it is unknown if that homeworld is where they originated from, or where they intend to settle, or even the planet's name.

**Languages:** Vahla speak their own harsh language of Vahla, with most learning Basic to perform trade in the galactic community.

**Example Names:** Vahe, Viau, Vahnda, Vahni, Vahl.

**Adventurers:** The Vahla are generally vagabonds, constantly plying interstellar space in an ongoing quest to locate their homeworld. They can often be encountered in small-scale acts of piracy, petty thievery, or in bounty hunting. [The Ember of Vahl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Ember_of_Vahl "The Ember of Vahl") are also known for kidnapping and sacrificing individuals to their destructive goddess.

## Vahla Species Traits

Vahla share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Vahla receive a +2 bonus to their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution"). The Vahla are naturally bright and shrewd, but they are physically frail.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Vahla have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Vahla have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Bonus Feat:** Vahla receive the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat at 1st level as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Cartilaginous Skeleton:** Vahla skeletons are primarily made of cartilage instead of bone, giving them a more flexible physique. A Vahla can choose to reroll any [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Dark Side Stigma:** Whether they know it or not, each member of the Vahla Species is forever bound to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Vahla must spend 2 [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") to reduce their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1 (instead of just 1 [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point")).
- **Automatic Languages:** All Vahla can speak, read, and write both Basic and Vahla.
