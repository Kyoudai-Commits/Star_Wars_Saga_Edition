# Zabrak

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/4/4d/Zabrak.webp/300px-Zabrak.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Zabrak.webp)

The Zabrak are an early spacefaring race distinguished by patterns of vestigial horns on their heads. Zabrak hail from many worlds and have been spacefarers for so long that they define themselves and each other according to their colony of origin.

The harshness of [Iridonia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iridonia "Iridonia"), their homeworld, forged in the Zabrak an iron will to survive. Driven to escape their world, they sought to obtain the knowledge of space flight. When [Duros](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duros "Duros") scouts began exploring [The Mid Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mid_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Mid Rim (page does not exist)"), they encountered Zabrak in eight sovereign colonies in five different systems. Though subjugated under the harsh rule of the Empire, the Zabrak colonies eventually regained their independence.

Zabrak posses a great amount of confidence, and they believe that there is nothing they can't accomplish. While this innate attribute could lead to feelings of superiority, most Zabrak don't look down on others. They believe in themselves and are proud and strong, but they rarely project any negativity toward those of other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

## Zabrak Characteristics

**Personality:** Zabrak tend to be dedicated, intense, and focused, sometimes appearing obsessive and single-minded to other [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

**Physical Description:** Zabrak are humanoid, distinguished by patterns of vestigial horns atop their heads. The average Zabrak stands 1.8 meters tall and weighs 75 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Zabrak age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-8 Years | 9-14 Years | 15-44 Years | 45-55 Years | 56-69 Years | 70+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The barren world of [Iridonia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iridonia "Iridonia"), or one of the eight colonies in [The Mid Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mid_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Mid Rim (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** Zabrak speak, read, and write both Zabrak and Basic.

**Example Names:** Aagh Odok, Eeth Koth, Kooth Aan, Bao Dur.

**Adventurers:** Zabrak adventurers love to explore the galaxy. No challenge is too big to deter a Zabrak. They range from [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") on distant colony worlds, to [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") diplomats and merchants, to [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers"), and even [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi").

## Zabrak Species Traits

Zabrak share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** Zabrak have no [Ability Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Score "Ability Score") adjustments.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Zabrak have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Zabrak have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Heightened Awareness:** Having strong survival instincts and quick reactions, a Zabrak may choose to reroll any [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Superior Defenses:** Adapted to a very tough and trying environment, Zabrak gain a +1 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to all their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Zabrak can speak, read, and write both Basic and Zabrak.
