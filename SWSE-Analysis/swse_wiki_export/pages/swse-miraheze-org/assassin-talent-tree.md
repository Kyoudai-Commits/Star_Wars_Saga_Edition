# Assassin Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assassin_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Assassin_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You do not fight fair. If you are ever caught in a straightforward fight, the odds are you are not going to make it, so your best move is to set up your attacks and use every trick at your disposal.

## Core Talents

### Advantageous Positioning

*Prerequisite:* **Shift**

Any opponent that you are [Flanking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanking "Flanking") is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") and is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against you.

### Get Some Distance

*Prerequisites:* **Advantageous Positioning**, **Shift**

Once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target and then move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") away from that target. This movement does not provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

### Murderous Arts I

When your successful attack causes an opponent to move -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), that opponent immediately takes an additional +1d6 damage.

### Murderous Arts II

*Prerequisite:* **Murderous Arts I**

Whenever you successfully hit an opponent that you have [Marked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Marked "Marked"), your [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") and [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") deal an additional +1d6 damage.

### Ruthless

Once per encounter, when you drop a foe to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or push an opponent to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can immediately take a bonus [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Shift

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can move 1 square without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

### Sniping Assassin

When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target that is not at Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), you add half your Class Level to your damage roll.

### Sniping Marksman

*Prerequisite:* **Sniping Assassin**

Once per encounter, when you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target that is not at Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), you can ignore your target's Armor bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

### Sniping Master

*Prerequisites:* **Sniping Assassin**, **Sniping Marksman**

By taking only a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") at a target that is not within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").
