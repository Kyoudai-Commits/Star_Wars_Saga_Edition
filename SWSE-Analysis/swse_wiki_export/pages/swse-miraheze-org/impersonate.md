# Impersonate

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Impersonate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Impersonate) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can alter your appearance to resemble a specific person.

**Prerequisites:** [Shapeshift](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shapeshift "Shapeshift"), [Skill Focus (Deception)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Deception) "Skill Focus (Deception)")

**Effect:** You can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to alter your features to that of a specific person. This feat also allows you to change your voice to match the target's. You always treat impersonating a person as a Moderate [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception").
