# Justice Seeker

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Justice_Seeker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Justice_Seeker) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are willing to take the law into your own hands to bring justice to those who harm your allies.

**Prerequisite:** [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor")

**Effect:** You gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls for attacks against targets that have damaged one of your allies since the end of your last turn.
