# Expert Pilot Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Pilot_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Pilot_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The [Ace Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ace_Pilot "Ace Pilot") relies on finely honed instincts and years of pilot training to outmaneuver and destroy enemy [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships").

## Core Talents

### Elusive Dogfighter

When engaged in a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), any enemy pilot engaged in the same [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight") takes a -10 penalty on attack rolls when you succeed on the opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check.

### Full Throttle

You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks made to [Increase Vehicle Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Increase_Vehicle_Speed "Increase Vehicle Speed"). In addition, when you use the [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") Action, your Vehicle moves up to five times its normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (instead of the normal four times).

### Juke

*Prerequisite:* **Vehicular Evasion**

When you [Fly Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fly_Defensively "Fly Defensively") as the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you may negate a weapon hit on your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") using the [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat") feat one additional time per round.

### Keep it Together

Once per encounter, when a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you're [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") takes damage that equals or exceeds its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") avoids moving down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Relentless Pursuit

You may roll twice for any opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check made to initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), keeping the better result.

### Vehicular Evasion

If the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") is hit by an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), it takes half damage if the attack hits. If the [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") misses your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), it takes no damage. You cannot use this [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") when your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is stationary or disabled.

## Additional Talents

### Blind Spot

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You can fly a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") so close to a target at least two sizes larger than your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that it is difficult for the target to avoid or attack you. You must be adjacent to the target (at [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale")) to use this Talent. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), make an opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check against the target. If you succeed, you move into the same space as your target. You move with your target if it moves (assuming your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") has sufficient [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") to keep up), and you must make another opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check each round as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to stay in its **Blind Spot**.

As long as you stay in the target's **Blind Spot**, any attack you make against the target gains a +2 bonus, and the target takes a -2 penalty on attacks made against you.

### Clip

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When you use the [Ram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ram "Ram") Action, you reduce the size of your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") by two categories for the purposes of taking [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") damage. The [Rammed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rammed "Rammed") Vehicle takes damage appropriate to the actual size of your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

### Close Scrape

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

Whenever you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Colossal size or smaller, you may make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to turn a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") into a normal hit. The DC for the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check is equal to the attack roll total of the [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"). If you are successful, the damage from the attack is not doubled (though it is still considered an automatic hit).

### Improved Attack Run

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You do not have to move in a straight line when using the [Attack Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Run "Attack Run") Action.

### Master Defender

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

When you [Fly Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fly_Defensively "Fly Defensively"), either your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") gains a +5 dodge bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") if you and your [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") take a -2 penalty to attack rolls, or it gains a +10 dodge bonus if you and your [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") take a -5 penalty to attack rolls.

### Renowned Pilot

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Your reputation as a skilled pilot precedes you and bolsters the resolve of your allies. All allies within 6 squares of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of can reroll one [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, keeping the better of the two results. Once an ally has used this ability, that same ally cannot gain this Talent's benefit during the same encounter.

### Roll Out

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Elusive Dogfighter**

You are an expert at escaping [Dogfights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfights "Dogfights"). When making an opposed check to disengage from a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), you can reroll your [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check, taking the better result. If you fail, you remain in the [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), but the [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") on your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") do not take penalties to their attack rolls.

### Shunt Damage

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Once per encounter, if your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes damage, make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check and compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of one adjacent allied [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). If your check result is higher, the allied [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes the damage instead.

### Vehicle Focus

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") 13

Choose a single type of Vehicle from the following list:

- [Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders")
- [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships")
- [Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports")
- [Speeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeders "Speeders")
- [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters")
- [Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers")

When you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") or [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") of that type of [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you gain a +2 bonus to all attack rolls with its [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"), and may [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on any [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks made while [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") that type of [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), even when you are otherwise unable to.

### Wingman

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") 13

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check to assist any allied [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") or [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") within 2 squares at [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale"). If you succeed, the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") gains a +5 bonus on all opposed [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks relating to the [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight") Action until the start of your next turn.
