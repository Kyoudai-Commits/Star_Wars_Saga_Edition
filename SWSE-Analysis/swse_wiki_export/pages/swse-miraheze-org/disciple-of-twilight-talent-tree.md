# Disciple of Twilight Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disciple_of_Twilight_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disciple_of_Twilight_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[The Disciples of Twilight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Disciples_of_Twilight "The Disciples of Twilight") embody shadow, using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to manipulate light and darkness. Only Force-users that are part of [The Disciples of Twilight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Disciples_of_Twilight "The Disciples of Twilight") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Cloak of Shadows

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to bend light around you, veiling yourself in shadows. Until the end of the encounter, whenever you move and end your movement at least 3 squares away from your starting position, you gain [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from all targets until the beginning of your next turn.

### Phantasm

Whenever you successfully use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] descriptor against a target, any time before the end of encounter you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to create illusory phantoms around that target. When you do this, you and all of your allies within the target's [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from the target until the end of your next turn.

### Revelation

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of an enemy that has [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from you or any of your allies. If successful, the enemy is silhouetted by a faint shimmering light that belies their location. Your target loses any [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") bonuses to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn.

### Shadow Armor

*Prerequisite:* **Cloak of Shadows**

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to bend light around yourself, wrapping you in shadows and making it difficult for enemies to tell where you end and the shadows begin. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you grant yourself a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn. You may select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, the [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus it provides increases by +1 (to a maximum of +4).

### Shadow Vision

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you gain [Low-Light Vision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Low-Light_Vision "Low-Light Vision"), allowing you to ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. This benefit lasts for 5 minutes or until the end of the encounter, whichever comes first.
