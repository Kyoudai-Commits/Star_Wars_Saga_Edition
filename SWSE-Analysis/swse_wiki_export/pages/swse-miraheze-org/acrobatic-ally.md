# Acrobatic Ally

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Ally](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Ally) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You move an ally around with ease and accuracy by giving him or her a leg up or a helpful toss.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics")

**Effect:** You can hoist or toss an ally of your size or smaller up to 2 squares. You and your ally must act on the same [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count") and start in adjacent squares. If you succeed on a DC 20 [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), your ally ends up in a square of your choosing up to 2 squares above or away from you. This counts as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") for your ally and ends your turn. Your ally can then continue his or her turn.

If you fail your check, both you and your ally immediately become [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in your square. Your turn is ended, but your ally can take his or her turn normally.

**Special:** This feat can provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") against either character.
