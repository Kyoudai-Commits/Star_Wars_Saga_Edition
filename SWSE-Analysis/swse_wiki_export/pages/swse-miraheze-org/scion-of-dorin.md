# Scion of Dorin

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scion_of_Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scion_of_Dorin) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are accustomed to the harsh environment of your homeworld, [Dorin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dorin "Dorin"), and are tempered against the hazards of other planets.

**Prerequisite:** [Kel Dor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kel_Dor "Kel Dor")

**Effect:** You gain a +5 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against all [Natural](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural "Natural") Hazards.
