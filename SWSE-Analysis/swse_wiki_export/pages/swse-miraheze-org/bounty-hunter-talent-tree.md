# Bounty Hunter Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunter_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunter_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The nature of their work requires [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") to associate with the scum of the universe. You are among the finest [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") in the galaxy, relying on the element of surprise and your hunter's instincts to catch your prey.

## Core Talents

### Hunter's Mark

If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you move the target character -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") if the attack deals damage. This Talent can be used only against characters, not objects or [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles").

### Hunter's Target

*Prerequisite:* **Hunter's Mark**

Once per encounter as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you may designate an opponent. For the rest of the encounter, when you succeed on a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against that opponent, you gain a bonus on damage equal to your Class Level.

### Notorious

Your skill as a Bounty Hunter is known throughout the galaxy, even on fringe worlds. When you are not [Disguised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disguised "Disguised"), you may reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks made to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate"), keeping the better of the two results.

### Nowhere to Hide

You may choose to reroll any [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") checks made to [Locate Individual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locate_Individual "Locate Individual"), but you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Relentless

*Prerequisite:* **Hunter's Target**

This Talent applies only to an opponent you've designated as your **Hunter's Target**. Any attack or effect originating from the target that would normally move you along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") does not, in fact, move you along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Ruthless Negotiator

*Prerequisite:* **Notorious**

When [Haggling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haggling "Haggling") over the price of a bounty, you can reroll your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, keeping the better result.

## Additional Talents

### Detective

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are skilled in locating individuals and using research and surveillance to learn some of their most intimate secrets. When you make a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check to locate an individual, the DC is reduced by 10, and the time and bribery cost are reduced by half.

### Dread

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Hunter's Target**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can instill bone-chilling fear in an opponent whom you selected for your **Hunter's Target**. Make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against your target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you equal or exceed your target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that target takes a -5 penalty to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). This is a [Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting") effect. The penalty remains as long as you have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target and immediately ends if the [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is broken.

### Electronic Trail

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisites:* **Nowhere to Hide**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

Once you have located a target using [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"), you can track its electronic presence. Once per day, you receive a catalog of the target's **Electronic Trail**, which includes the amount and location of credits spent, the routes of any public transportation taken, and the sites viewed on the HoloNet while the target was logged in using its primary identity. To receive this information, you must have access to a computer or [Datapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Datapad "Datapad") plus access to a network or the HoloNet. This **Electronic Trail** does not reveal bank balances or other secret information, which requires a separate [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check.

### Familiar Enemies

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Familiar Foe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Familiar_Foe "Familiar Foe") (+1)

You can apply your [Familiar Foe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Familiar_Foe "Familiar Foe") bonus against a second enemy. If you can see both enemies simultaneously in the same round, you need to spend only a single [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") observing them. Otherwise, you must spend a separate [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") on each enemy.

### Familiar Situation

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Familiar Foe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Familiar_Foe "Familiar Foe") (+1)

You can apply your [Familiar Foe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Familiar_Foe "Familiar Foe") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against attacks and actions taken against you by the target of your [Familiar Foe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Familiar_Foe "Familiar Foe") special quality.

### Fearsome

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Notorious**

Your reputation precedes you, striking fear in your target. Any opponent within 6 squares whose level is equal to or less than your Heroic Level takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls made against you.

### Jedi Hunter

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are skilled at fighting [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") and other Force-users. You gain a +1 insight bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), and deal +1 die of damage against characters who have the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat.

### Nowhere to Run

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Hunter's Target**, **Nowhere to Hide**

Once per turn, whenever an opponent whom you selected for your **Hunter's Target** attempts to [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw"), you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against that target.

### Quick Cuffs

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw")

You are fast with the binders. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), when you successfully use the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") Action against a target, you can use [Binder Cuffs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binder_Cuffs "Binder Cuffs") or similar restraints to bind one of the target's arms to one of your arms or to an adjacent object. You cannot use improvised materials, such as [Mesh Tape](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mesh_Tape "Mesh Tape"), for this Talent, and the binders must be in your hands or readily available. You and the target both take a -2 penalty to attack rolls and [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") while bound together.

### Revealing Secrets

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

*Prerequisite:* **Detective**

Your investigations reveal information that your target though was secret. When you make a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check to [Learn Secret Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Learn_Secret_Information "Learn Secret Information"), the DC is reduced by 10 and the bribery cost is reduced to one-fifth the original cost.

### Signature Item

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are famous for using certain items, and you have become skilled at wielding them. You select a single weapon, suit of armor, [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or other item. While wielding that weapon, wearing that armor, piloting that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), or otherwise using that item, you gain a +2 morale bonus on opposed [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"). You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you choose a new object to be your **Signature Item**. The effects of multiple **Signature Items** are cumulative with one another, increasing this morale bonus by 1 point each time.

### Tag

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Hunter's Target**

Whenever you damage an opponent whom you selected for your **Hunter's Target**, all allies gain a +2 bonus on their next attack roll against that opponent until the start of your next turn.
