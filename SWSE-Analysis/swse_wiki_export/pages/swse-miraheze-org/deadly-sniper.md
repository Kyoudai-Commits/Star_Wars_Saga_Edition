# Deadly Sniper

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadly_Sniper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadly_Sniper) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are adept at staying out of sight when attacking from hidden positions.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +9, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), [Sniper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper "Sniper")

**Effect:** When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target that is unaware of you, you gain a +2 bonus on your attack roll and deal +1 die of damage on the first attack each turn.
