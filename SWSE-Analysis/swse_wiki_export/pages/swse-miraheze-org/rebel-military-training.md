# Rebel Military Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebel_Military_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebel_Military_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You are well versed in the hit-and-run tactics used by [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") and know how to use your mobility to avoid retribution.

**Prerequisite:** [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack")

**Effect:** Whenever you use the [Running Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Attack "Running Attack") feat to move both before and after making an attack, you gain a +2 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.
