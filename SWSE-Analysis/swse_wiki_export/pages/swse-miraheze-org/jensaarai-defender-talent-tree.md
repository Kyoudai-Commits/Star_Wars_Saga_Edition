# Jensaarai Defender Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jensaarai_Defender_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jensaarai_Defender_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai") walk a thin line between light and darkness, using the teachings of both [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") to create their own unique traditions. Only Force-users that are part of [The Jensaarai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jensaarai "The Jensaarai") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Attune Armor

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to attune a suit of armor to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), permanently increasing its Armor bonus by +2. In addition, the maximum [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus of the **Attuned Armor** permanently improves by +1. Only you can benefit from wearing the **Attuned Armor**; the benefits do not apply if someone else dons the armor.

### Force Cloak

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can surround yourself with an invisible bubble of Force power that shields you and anything you're carrying from electronic surveillance. The bubble also blocks all electronic sensors and communications. The Force Cloak lasts for as long as you concentrate (a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")) or until the start of your next turn.

### Force Cloak Mastery

*Prerequisite:* **Force Cloak**

As the **Force Cloak** Talent, except you can expand the bubble to envelop a number of creatures (including yourself) equal to your Character Level.

### Linked Defense

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can take a penalty of up to -5 on your attack roll and add the same number (up to +5) as a [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to an ally's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), provided the ally is within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") when you activate this Talent. The bonus you confer cannot exceed your [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). The changes to your attack rolls and your ally's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") last until the start of your next turn.
