# Relentless Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Relentless_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Relentless_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You ensure the demise of your foe by turning misses into opportunities.

**Prerequisite:** [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** Choose one [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") for which you have the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat. Whenever you miss a target with that weapon, you gain a +2 competence bonus on your next attack roll made before the end of your next turn against the missed target.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you select this feat, it applies to a different [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") or [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon").
