# GenoHaradan Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/GenoHaradan_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/GenoHaradan_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Members of [The GenoHaradan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_GenoHaradan "The GenoHaradan") are skilled not only in performing assassinations but also in manipulating others. An ancient organization dating back to the early days of the Republic, [The GenoHaradan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_GenoHaradan "The GenoHaradan") are a clandestine group of assassins who pick their targets for political reason, though their true motives are rarely evident to those outside the organization.

## Core Talents

### Deadly Repercussions

When you reduce a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or move the target to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), all your opponents within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") of both you and your target take a -2 penalty on attack rolls until the beginning of your next turn.

### Manipulating Strike

Once per turn when you successfully damage a target with a non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), make an [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If successful, you can determine what the target does with its [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on its next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Improved Manipulating Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Manipulating Strike**

Whenever you successfully use the **Manipulating Strike** Talent, you determine what the target does with its [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") on its next turn. You cannot move an opponent into a [Hazard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazard "Hazard") (such as into lava or off a cliff).

### Pulling the Strings

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of a target within 12 squares. If you succeed, you move the target up to half its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") toward you through the safest route, and you make an immediate [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") or [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against the the target if it is within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). You cannot move an opponent into a [Hazard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazard "Hazard") (such as into lava or off a cliff).
