# Tempered Aggression

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tempered_Aggression](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tempered_Aggression) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*, *[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

You throw yourself against an opponent, controlling your aggression to prevent it from getting the better of you.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 30 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. If you beat the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 10 or more, the attack is considered a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"). |
| 35 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. If you beat the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 5 or more, the attack is considered a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"). |
| 40 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the target. If you beat the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 2 or more, the attack is considered a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Vaapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vaapad "Vaapad")):** If you have the **[Vaapad](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vaapad "Vaapad")** Talent, this Form Power does not have the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor for you.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal +2 dice of damage with the attack if it hits. These additional dice of damage are added after damage is doubled, if it is a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").
