# Clawed Subspecies

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clawed_Subspecies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clawed_Subspecies) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You come from a [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren") subspecies that has sharp claws at the tips of its fingers.

**Prerequisite:** [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren")

**Effect:** You have Natural Weapons in the form of claws. When you make an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can use your claws, dealing 1d6 points of Slashing damage with that attack instead of normal [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage. You are always considered armed with your Natural Weapons.
