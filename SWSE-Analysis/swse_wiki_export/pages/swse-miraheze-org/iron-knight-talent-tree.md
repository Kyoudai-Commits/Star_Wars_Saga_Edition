# Iron Knight Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iron_Knight_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iron_Knight_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[The Iron Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights "The Iron Knights") are truly unique, in that they are silicon-based creatures that use [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") as their physical bodies to interact with the galaxy. As a result, they view and utilize [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in ways that seem unusual to [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Most of their powers involve their ability to affect [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") and other machinery. Only Force-users that are part of [The Iron Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Iron_Knights "The Iron Knights") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Droid Duelist

Whenever you are wielding [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to cause an opponent to be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against your next attack made with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") before the end of your turn.

### Force Repair

You can use the [Force Trance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Trance "Force Trance") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill to recover [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") through [Natural Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Healing "Natural Healing"). You can also receive [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") from the *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")* Force Power. Whenever you receive [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from any source, you gain additional [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum +1).

### Heal Droid

*Prerequisite:* *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")*

You can use the *[Vital Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vital_Transfer "Vital Transfer")* Force Power to heal [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") (including [Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shard "Shard")-inhabited [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids")), which are normally immune to this Force Power.

### Mask Presence

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you become immune to the [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, and appear to be nothing more than a regular [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). If you make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check or use any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent"), this effect immediately ends.

### Silicon Mind

Other Force-users have a difficult time knowing how to influence you. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to gain a bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum +1) against all [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks until the end of your next turn.
