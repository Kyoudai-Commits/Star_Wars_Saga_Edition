# Martial Arts III

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_III](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_III) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your Martial Arts prowess is second to none.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6, [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II")

**Effect:** Damage dealt by your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") increases by one die step. In addition, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (which stacks with the dodge bonuses granted by the [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") and [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II") feats).

**Normal:** The amount of damage you deal with a successful [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack is based on your size: Small, 1d3; Medium, 1d4; Large, 1d6.

**Special:** A situation that makes you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (if any) also makes you lose any dodge bonuses. Also, dodge bonuses stack with each other, unlike most other types of [Bonuses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonuses "Bonuses").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Saga Edition FAQ](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saga_Edition_FAQ "Saga Edition FAQ"))*

**Q52:** Do Natural Weapons (claws, bite, etc.) stack with the Martial Arts [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") in some way?

**A:** Martial Arts increases the damage die of a creature's natural attacks, just as it does for a creature's [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks"). For example, A [Cathar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cathar "Cathar") (claw attack, 1d6 damage) with [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I") would deal 1d8 damage with a claw attack whereas a [Cathar](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cathar "Cathar") with Martial Arts III would deal 1d12 damage with a claw attack.
