# Disarming Slash

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming_Slash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming_Slash) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You strike at your opponent's weapon, attempting to rip it from their grasp.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent opponent. If the attack deals damage, you can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result in place of an attack roll to make an immediate [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt against the target. If you succeed at your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt, you can have the weapon drop to the ground or call it to your own hand (assuming you have a free hand with which to hold the weapon). |
| 20 | Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent opponent. If the attack deals damage, you can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result in place of an attack roll to make an immediate [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt against the target, and your target gains only a +5 bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") to resist (instead of a +10 bonus). If you succeed at your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt, you can have the weapon drop to the ground or call it to your own hand (assuming you have a free hand with which to hold the weapon). |
| 25 | Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent opponent. If the attack deals damage, you can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result in place of an attack roll to make an immediate [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt against the target, and your target gains only a +2 bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") to resist (instead of a +10 bonus). If you succeed at your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt, you can have the weapon drop to the ground or call it to your own hand (assuming you have a free hand with which to hold the weapon). |
| 30 | Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent opponent. If the attack deals damage, you can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result in place of an attack roll to make an immediate [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt against the target, and your target does not receive a bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") to resist (instead of a +10 bonus). If you succeed at your [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") attempt, you can have the weapon drop to the ground or call it to your own hand (assuming you have a free hand with which to hold the weapon). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Shii-Cho](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shii-Cho "Shii-Cho")):** If you have the **[Shii-Cho](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shii-Cho "Shii-Cho")** Talent, you can use this Form Power as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being attacked by an adjacent opponent.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal damage to the target's weapon equal to the damage you dealt to the target, provided you successfully [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") that opponent.
