# Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You have refined your knowledge of [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") technique, your blade becoming an extension of your self. Anyone using a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") may use one of these forms, but you have the discipline and patience to become a true master of the form.

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Why are the Lightsaber Form Talents unique to the [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") Prestige Class? Various Sith Lords have been described as masters of a particular form, and not all of them are former Jedi Knights. Therefore, many would be unable to take the [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") associated with the form they have mastered (or possibly invented). Is this a mistake?

**A:** No, it's not a mistake. A Jedi, Sith, or any other [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")-wielding character can use a form- even be considered a master of that form to in-universe observers- without having the associated Lightsaber Form Talent. After all, being a "master of a form" is a label that's completely subjective, in- universe, and not based on game mechanics. Someone is a "master" if others perceive it to be so. Certainly, someone who takes a Lightsaber Form Talent is probably recognized as a master by others, but that doesn't mean that only someone with a Lightsaber Form Talent can be considered a master.

The Lightsaber Form Talents are meant to represent unique benefits that Jedi- with their greater emphasis on meditative and disciplined study, the sublime emptiness of serenity, and patient reflection on the will of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force")- can gain from devoting themselves to a particular form. Sith, meanwhile, are not as meditative, disciplined, serene, or reflective. Instead, they're about passion, rage, strength, and desire, and their [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") (such as **[Dark Scourge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Scourge "Dark Scourge")** and **[Wicked Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wicked_Strike "Wicked Strike")**) reflect this.

In any case, a [Jedi Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight "Jedi Knight") and a [Sith Apprentice](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Apprentice "Sith Apprentice") of the same level, both devoting themselves to [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combat, can learn the same number of [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber")- and combat-related [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), but the specific [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") may vary. In other words, [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") aren't better than [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") when using a particular form; they're just different.

## Core Talents

### Ataru

You may add your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (instead of your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus) on damage rolls when wielding [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"). When you wield a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") Two-Handed, you may apply double your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (instead of double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus) to the damage.

### Djem So

Once per round when an opponent hits you with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to make an immediate attack against that opponent.

### Jar'Kai

*Prerequisites:* **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")**, **Niman**

When you use the **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")** Talent, you gain twice the normal deflection bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when you are wielding two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers").

### Juyo

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +10, [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)"), **[Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)")**

Once per encounter, you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to designate a single opponent in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). For the remainder of the encounter, you may reroll your first attack roll each round against that opponent, keeping the better of the two results.

### Makashi

*Prerequisite:* **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")**

When wielding a single [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") in one hand, the deflection bonus you gain from the **[Lightsaber Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Defense "Lightsaber Defense")** Talent increases by 2 (to a maximum of +5).

### Niman

When wielding [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you gain a +1 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").

### Shien

*Prerequisite:* **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")**

Whenever you redirect a deflected blaster bolt with the **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")** Talent, you gain a +5 bonus on your ranged attack roll.

### Shii-Cho

*Prerequisites:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**

When using the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents, you only take a -2 penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check for every previous **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** attempt since your last turn.

### Sokan

*Prerequisite:* **[Acrobatic Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Recovery "Acrobatic Recovery")**

You may [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") checks to [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble"), even when distracted or threatened. Additionally, each threatened or occupied square that you [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") through only counts as 1 square of movement.

### Soresu

*Prerequisites:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**

You may reroll a failed [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check when using the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** or **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents.

### Trakata

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +12, [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)"), **[Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialization_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)")**

By harnessing the unique characteristics of a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you can catch your opponent off guard by quickly shutting off and reigniting the blade. When wielding [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you may spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") in combat.

### Vaapad

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +12, **Juyo**

When attacking with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") on a natural roll of 19 or 20. However, a natural 19 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll a natural 19 and still miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").
