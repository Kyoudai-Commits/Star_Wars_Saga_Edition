# Shatterpoint

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shatterpoint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shatterpoint) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can see the critical point of something, whether it is a person or object, that would shatter if struck at the right time.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 25 | If your next attack made before the end of the encounter hits, treat the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against the attack as though it were 5 points lower than normal. |
| 30 | If your next attack made before the end of the encounter hits, treat the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against the attack as though it were 10 points lower than normal. |
| 35 | If your next attack made before the end of the encounter hits, treat the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against the attack as though it were 15 points lower than normal. |
| 40 | If your next attack made before the end of the encounter hits, treat the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") against the attack as though it were 20 points lower than normal. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to ignore the target's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") in addition to gaining the normal effects of *Shatterpoint*.
