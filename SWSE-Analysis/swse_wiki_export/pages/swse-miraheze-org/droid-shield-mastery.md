# Droid Shield Mastery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Shield_Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Shield_Mastery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You quickly replenish your onboard shields.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Shield Generator System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Generator_System "Shield Generator System")

**Effect:** You automatically succeed on [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") checks to restore your [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") by 5 points (up to your normal [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating")). Additionally, you replenish your shields in two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions").

**Normal:** Three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") and a DC 20 [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check are normally required to restore lost shield power.
