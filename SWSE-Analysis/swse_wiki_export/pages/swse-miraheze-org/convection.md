# Convection

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Convection](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Convection) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You alter your body chemistry, causing your skin to burn with incredible heat.

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Until the start of your next turn, all your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") deal +1d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage. Any target that takes [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage also catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire"). |
| 20 | Until the start of your next turn, all your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") deal +2d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage. Any target that takes [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage also catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire"). |
| 25 | Until the start of your next turn, all your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") deal +3d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage. Any target that takes [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage also catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire"). |
| 30 | Until the start of your next turn, all your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") deal +4d6 points of [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage. Any target that takes [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage also catches on [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire"). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal the bonus [Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire "Fire") damage to any creature that strikes you with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), or makes a [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack against you, for as long as *Convection* is in effect.

You can maintain *Convection* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Convection* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Convection*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Convection*.
