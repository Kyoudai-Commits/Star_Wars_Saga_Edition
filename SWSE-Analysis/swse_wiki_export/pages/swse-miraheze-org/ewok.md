# Ewok

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewok) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/7/7c/Ewok.webp/300px-Ewok.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Ewok.webp)

Intelligent omnivores native to one of the moons orbiting [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), Ewoks are almost unknown prior to the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"). Ewoks live in tree-dwelling tribes with gender-based division of labor; males hunt, forage, and make weapons, while females raise young and handle other domestic tasks. Ewok culture revolves around complex animistic beliefs involving the giant trees of the forest moon.

Although technologically primitive, Ewoks are clever, inquisitive, and inventive. Skittish and wary when first introduced to machines, their curiosity soon overcomes fear.

## Ewok Characteristics

**Personality:** Ewoks tend to be curious, superstitious, and courageous, though they can be fearful around things that are strange or new.

**Physical Description:** Ewoks average about 1 meter tall and weighs 30 kilograms. Their fur color and pattern varies widely.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Ewoks age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-9 Years | 10-14 Years | 14-29 Years | 30-44 Years | 45-59 Years | 60+ Years |

**Homeworld:** [The Forest Moon of Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Forest_Moon_of_Endor "The Forest Moon of Endor").

**Languages:** Ewoks speak Ewokese. They have no written form of their language. They can learn to speak Basic.

**Example Names:** Asha, Chirpa, Deej, Kneesaa, Latara, Logray, Malani, Nippet, Paploo, Shodu, Teebo, Wicket, Wiley.

**Adventurers:** An Ewok adventurer may be motivated by a love of excitement, a natural inquisitiveness, or a warrior's quest. Collecting powerful "magic" items from fallen opponents is a sure way to earn the respect of the tribe.

## Ewok Species Traits

Ewoks share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Ewoks receive a +2 bonus to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"). Ewoks are very agile but somewhat weak.
- **Small Size:** As Small creatures, Ewoks gain a +1 size bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +5 size bonus on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. However, their lifting and carrying limits are three-quarters of those of Medium characters.
- **Speed:** Ewoks have a base speed of 4 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Ewoks are skilled foragers. An Ewok with [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Survival)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Survival) "Skill Focus (Survival)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Primitive:** Ewoks do not gain [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)"), or [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)") as starting [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") at first level, even if their [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") normally grants them.
- **Scent:** Ewoks have a keen sense of smell. At close range (within 10 squares), Ewoks ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") and [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") for purposes of [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks, and take no penalty from poor visibility while [Tracking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracking "Tracking").
- **Sneaky:** An Ewok may choose to reroll any [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted even if it is worse.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Ewoks can speak Ewokese. They have no written form of their language.
