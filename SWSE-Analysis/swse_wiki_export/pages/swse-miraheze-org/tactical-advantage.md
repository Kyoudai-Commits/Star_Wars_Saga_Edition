# Tactical Advantage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tactical_Advantage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tactical_Advantage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can shift your position to take advantage of openings your opponent offers you.

**Prerequisite:** [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes")

**Effect:** When you successfully damage an opponent using an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you can immediately move 1 square in any direction. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
