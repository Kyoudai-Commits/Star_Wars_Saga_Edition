# Deft Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deft_Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deft_Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

When you [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), it is merely part of a larger attack pattern that you continue to execute.

**Effect:** After you [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you can take [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions"), [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions"), and [Free Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Actions "Free Actions") before your turn ends.

**Normal:** Without this feat, [Charging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging "Charging") ends your turn immediately after the attack is resolved.
