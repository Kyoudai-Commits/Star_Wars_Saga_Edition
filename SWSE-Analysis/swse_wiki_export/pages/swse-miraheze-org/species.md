# Species

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The *Star Wars* galaxy contains a bewildering variety of Species, each with its own unique outlook and civilization. Although [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") dominate the known galaxy, there are many intelligent alien species that can be encountered wherever you travel.

[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") live among [The Core Worlds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core_Worlds?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core Worlds (page does not exist)"), throughout [The Mid Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Mid_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Mid Rim (page does not exist)"), and even on the worlds of [The Outer Rim Territories](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim_Territories?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim Territories (page does not exist)"). Most of the alien Species have their own homeworld and colonies, but individuals can be found anywhere, thanks to the prevalence of Hyperspace travel. The promise of profit and power often lure members of all Species to the same areas, including spaceports and metropolitan cities.

Sometimes it's easy to believe that all [Rodians](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodians "Rodians") are outlaws or all [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") are short-tempered, but the truth is that every member of every Species is unique. Depending on the era in which your campaign is set (see Era Notes: Species, below), nonhuman Species have different advantages or disadvantages, as explained in their individual pages.

Species CharacteristicsSpecies Characteristics

Your character's Species provides plenty of clues as to the sort of person he or she is, how he or she feels about characters of other Species, and what motivates him or her. Remember, however, that the Species descriptions only apply to the majority of people. In each Species, some individuals diverge from the norm, and your character can be one of these. Don't let a description hinder you from detailing your character as you like.

## Ability Adjustments

Find your character's Species on the tables provided further below and apply the adjustments that you see there to your character's ability scores. If the changes raise it above 18 or lower it below 3, that's okay.

For example, a [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") gets a +2 Species bonus on their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") score and a -2 penalty on their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores. Knowing this, the player puts their best score rolled (15) in [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") and sees it increase to 17. They don’t want a [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") or [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") penalty for their character, so they put above-average scores (13 and 12) in [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). These drop to 11 and 10, for neither a bonus nor a penalty.

## Known Languages

The primary language used throughout known space is called Basic. Most characters can speak Basic, and all characters understand it even if they can't speak it. Nonhuman characters can also speak, read, and write the language associated with their species; for example, [Bothans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothans "Bothans") speak, read, and write Bothese as well as Basic.

Characters with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus know how to speak, read, and write other [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") as well. For each point of [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus, a character can speak, read, and write one additional language of their choice. Some Species ([Gamorreans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamorreans "Gamorreans") and [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees"), for example) are incapable of speaking any [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") other than their native tongue, but they can learn to read and write other languages just fine.

## Species Traits

In addition to [Ability](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability "Ability") adjustments and [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages"), each Species has their own set of [Species Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Species_Traits "Category:Species Traits") to differentiate them from each other. These ability range from bonus [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and simple bonuses to entirely new abilities and rerolls to certain [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills"). For ease of use, the [Species Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Species_Traits "Category:Species Traits") category allows a user to find their desired abilities with ease.

### Smaller Characters

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Species come in a great variety of sizes and heights. Usually these differences are not great enough to affect game mechanics. However, it is possible that a character is naturally small enough to qualify for a smaller size modifier. With the Gamemaster's approval, a player can create a character one size category smaller than the one noted in its Species description, gaining all the benefits and penalties of that size.

## Core Species

After you roll your [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") and before you write them on your [Character Sheet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Sheet "Character Sheet"), choose your character's Species. At the same time, you'll want to select your character's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), since Species affects how well the character can do in each class. Once you've decided your character's Species and [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"), assign [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") to particular [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities"). Alter the scores according to the Species' ability modifiers and continue detailing your character.

You can play a character of any Species presented below, but certain Species do better pursuing certain [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes").

## Era Notes: Species

Non-[Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") characters have some restrictions placed upon them, depending on the [Era of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Era_of_Play "Era of Play") in which your campaign is set. These restrictions are described below:

The Old Republic EraThe Rise of the Empire EraThe Rebellion EraThe New Jedi OrderThe Legacy Era

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Relations between Species are more varied in [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"). Some worlds have few conflicts, with each Species considering others more or less equal. On other planets, Species in the minority might be restricted to certain areas or have fewer rights than the dominant Species. This might be true even on planets that consider themselves to be among the most civilized in the galaxy. Non-[Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Species on the planet [Taris](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Taris "Taris"), for instance, are not welcome in the Upper City during certain periods of this era. Characters might find their efforts and adventures hampered by such attitudes, but players might find such conflicts useful points for their character backgrounds.

Any of the common Species available during [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") are suitable for an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign"). However, primitive Species such as [Gungans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungans "Gungans") and [Ewoks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewoks "Ewoks") leave their homeworlds only in rare and unusual circumstances. The remaining Species presented in the *[Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*, along with the Species added in the *[Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*, are all appropriate for an [Old Republic Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Campaign "Old Republic Era Campaign").

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

During [The Rise of the Empire Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rise_of_the_Empire_Era "The Rise of the Empire Era") that saw the erosion of the Republic and the rise of the Empire, including the period around Episodes I-III, all Species participated in a more or less cosmopolitan galaxy. The only restrictions on your choice of alien Species in this time frame refer to species that had not yet "been discovered" by the galaxy at large. [Ewoks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewoks "Ewoks"), therefore, are not available if your campaign is set in this era.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Empire was particularly entrenched with anti-alien sentiments during [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"). During this period of galactic history, most alien Species were either enslaved or subjugated by the Empire. In [The Outer Rim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Outer_Rim?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Outer Rim (page does not exist)"), non-[Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") Species continued to carry on their lives as normally as they were able, but in the more civilized regions, non-[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") were relegated to the status of second-class citizens- or worse. Many aliens joined the Rebellion, fighting alongside [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") in an effort to throw off the shackles of oppression and tyranny. [Ewoks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ewoks "Ewoks") show up late in this era, during the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), and free [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") rarely appear due to their statues as slaves of the Empire.

Late in this era, with the decline of the Empire and the birth of [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"), non-[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") once again take a prominent role in the affairs of the galaxy.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

All alien Species are available without restriction in [The New Jedi Order Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Jedi_Order_Era "The New Jedi Order Era"). The era is marked by The New Republic's battle against the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong") invaders and tainted by growing distrust of the Jedi Knights.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Players in [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era") have many Species open to them, including the dread [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"). With the exception of a few Species that have fallen into obscurity, such as the [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") or the [Rakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rakata "Rakata"), there are few, if any, races, that are inappropriate for [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"). Species that had not yet been discovered or integrated into the galaxy during [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") or the Galactic Civil War have now been present in the galaxy at large for over a century. Moreover, the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong"), who less than a century ago invaded and ravaged the galaxy, are now free to pursue other [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies") (though they remain subject to prejudice).

Under Palpatine's Galactic Empire, many Species were subjected as a part of the Sith Lord's humanocentric campaign. [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees") and [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari") were enslaved, and throughout [The Core](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Core?action=edit&redlink=1 "The Core (page does not exist)") non-[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") were discriminated against as lesser beings. This is not the case during [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), at least not entirely. Although the Empire under [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") imposes tyranny on planets, it treats all Species more or less equally. Species that are singled out, such as the [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari"), are done so only as a punishment for actions against the Empire. In such cases, members of an entire Species pay the price for the rebellion of the few.

Within the Empire ruled by [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") and the Empire-in-exile ruled by Roan Fel, non-[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") can rise to positions of prominence. The Sith Lords themselves hail from a number of Species, and many high-ranking Imperial officers are non-[Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") as well. Despite this general acceptance of all Species within the Empire, a few hard-line traditionalists still hold onto the old prejudices. These Imperial bureaucrats are usually small in number, but they often surround themselves with like-minded allies, leading to small pockets of anti-alien sentiment even within the larger cosmopolitan Galactic Empire.
