# Instinctive Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You naturally know how to avoid attacks and defend your mind and body when [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is with you.

**Prerequisite:** Cannot be a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

**Effect:** On your turn, as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase all of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") by 2 until the start of your next turn.
