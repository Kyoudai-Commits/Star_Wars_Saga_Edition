# Heroic Traits

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Traits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Traits) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

What does your character look like? How old are they? What sort of first impression do they make? What lead them to become a hero?

This section covers a miscellany of topics that apply to your character. It help you establish your character's identity and place in the *Star Wars* universe and made him or her more lifelike- like a main character in a *Star Wars* movie or novel. For many players, this is the true expression of roleplaying: defining the character that you are going to play. When you first play a character, it's fine to leave some of the details sketchy. Over time, as adventures play out, you'll get a better sense of who you want your character to be. You'll develop your character's details much the way an author develops a character over several chapters in a novel or over several novels in a series.

## Name

Invent or choose a name that fits your character's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") and [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). A Species' information page typically has some examples of alien names. A name is great way for you to start thinking about your character's background. For instance, a [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") might be named after a great [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") hunter of the past, and the [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian") may be striving to live up to that heritage. Alternatively, the name could be that of an infamous traitor, and the hero could be bent on proving the he or she is not like their namesake.

A name can also tell a lot about a character and help establish an image in your mind and the minds of the other players. It doesn't have to be descriptive, but you want it to fit the type of character you're going to play. Use the sample names to help you make up a name that has the appropriate *Star Wars* feel.

## Age

Your character's age is pretty much up to you (subject to the Gamemaster's approval). A character reaches 1st level in a [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") at the point when he or she steps out of mundane life and into the drama of the story, either by choice or through circumstances beyond the character's control. That could be as young as a young adult for a padawan learner, as a 20-year-old adult (such as in the case of Luke Skywalker in *A New Hope*), or as an even older character, depending on your character concept.

Each individual [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") page has a table for their stages of age under their characteristics section. As your character ages, his or her physical ability scores ([Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution")) decrease and his or her mental ability scores ([Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma")) increase, as shown in the table below. The effects of each aging step are cumulative. However, none of a character's [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores") can be reduced below 1 in this way.

| AGE GROUP | AGING EFFECTS |

Note that the methods described for determining the scores of an Adult character. For example, when a character reaches Middle Age, their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") scores each drop 1 point, while their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores each increase by 1 point. When they become Old, their physical ability scores all drop an additional 2 points, while while their mental ability scores increase by 1 again. So far they have lost a total of 3 points from their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") scores and gained a total of 2 points to their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"), and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores due to aging.

On the other hand, a Child would start with a total penalty of -4 to [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") and a -2 penalty to all other [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") (adjustments are cumulative for both the Child and Young Adult categories). As he or she advances to Young Adulthood, these penalties would be reduced to -1 for each ability score. He or she would thus "gain" 3 points to both his or her [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") and [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") and 1 point to each of his or her other [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores").

[Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") live longer than the average member of their [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). While a typical [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") lives well into his or her 80s, a [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") might well live into his or her 100s. The upper age limit for a character powerful in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") can be twice as much or more than a typical member of a [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species").

## Appearance

Decide what your character looks like using the descriptions of the various [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") as a starting point. Characters with high [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") tend to be better looking than those with low [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores, though a character with high [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") could have strange looks, giving him or her a sort of exotic beauty. You can use your hero's looks to tell something about their personality and background. For example:

- Deel Surool, the [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek") [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"), always has a smirk on his lips, no matter the situation he finds himself in. He treats life as a joke where only he knows the punchline. He wears the latest fashions and comes off as being mildly superior to everyone around him.
- Vor'en Kurn, the [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"), has a rough, dark look that speaks of the life he has led. His mercenary nature shows through in the way he moves, the way he wears his armor, and the way his twin blasters hang at his sides. His eyes are cold, dead, uncaring. You know he means business and that he's dangerous just by looking at him.
- Sia-Lan Wezz, the [Human](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Human "Human") [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), appears confident and in control. She wears her Jedi Robes and [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") proudly, and her fresh, young face glows with enthusiasm and hope. You know you can trust her, and you know she takes her role very seriously. Perhaps even a bit too seriously.

### Height and Weight

Your character may be of average height and weight, or you can make your character lighter, heavier, shorter, or taller. Think about what your character's [Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Abilities "Abilities") might say about their height and weight. If they are weak but agile, they may be thin. If they are strong and tough, they may be tall or just heavy. Average heights and weights are details on each individual Species' page under their characteristics.

## Personality

Describe how your character acts, what they like, what they want out of life, what scares them, and what makes them angry. Your character's [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") is a good place to start when thinking about personality, but it's a bad place to stop. Make your [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee") (or whatever) different from every other [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee").

Personality is a summary of how your character usually acts. Make sure it's interesting and fun for you to play. Give your character good points and bad points. Think about their code of ethics. Will your character do anything for the right price, or is there a line they just won't cross? Is your character cheerful or dour, optimistic or pessimistic, honorable or dishonorable? These are just some of the factors that could go into your character's personality.

A handy trick for making an interesting personality for your character is including some sort of conflict in their nature. For example, Deel the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") is generally self-centered, but he looks out for his close friends. He may be tempted to help them, even if it goes against his best interests, so long as he can justify doing so. Your character's personality can change over time. Just because you've written some personality notes on your character sheet doesn't mean you can't let your character grow and develop the way real people do.

## Background

Decide what your character's life has been like up until now. Here are a few questions to get you thinking:

- How did they decide to become a hero?
- How did they acquire their class? A [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"), for example, might have been in a planetary militia, they may have come from a family of [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers"), they may have trained in a military school, or they may be a self-taught mercenary.
- Where did they get their starting [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment")? Did they assemble it piece by piece over time? Was it a parting gift from a parent or mentor? Do any items have special significance to them?
- What's the worst thing that's ever happened to them?
- What's the best thing that's ever happened to them?
- Do they stay in contact with their family? What does their family think of them?

Only your GM needs to know all the details of your background. You can tell the other players as much as or as little as you see fit. You can get as complex as you like, or keep your character's background simple. Have they traveled around the galaxy a lot? What's their home planet like? What do they think of the Republic (or Rebellion, or Empire, or whatever)? Do they know any of the other players' characters from before the campaign started? If not, what's their connection to the rest of the team?

## Goals

Your character might have a number of objectives that he or she hopes to accomplish. These are the things that motivate your character. Do they seek wealth or love? Revenge or power? That's up to you and your GM. Goals can be immediate or long-term. They can also change during play, and new goals can be added all the time. Think of goals as what's motivating your character right now, though some long-term goals might fade to the background until circumstances warrant.

## [Old Republic Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Old_Republic_Era_Heroes "Old Republic Era Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era") provides exciting story opportunities for all character types, especially Jedi and other Force-users. This section contains information for creating heroes tailored to this era, including story considerations, new ideas and talents for the standard [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), plus new [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and expanded [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") usable by all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"). These volatile times- when it is difficult to distinguish friend from foe - also call for new heroes to fulfill their destinies.

In the *[Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*, characters are assumed to be good, fighting against the forces of evil. It is a time of wide-ranging open warfare, with shifting fronts and loyalties. Heroes can face off against Mandalorian or Sith invaders. Other enemies might not be so clear-cut, especially if the campaign takes place during [The Jedi Civil War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Civil_War "The Jedi Civil War"). Although it is possible to play darker characters using these rules, the forces of good remain the focus of this book.

## [Clone Wars Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Heroes "Clone Wars Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

During [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), few worlds are untouched by the tide of battle. From these affected worlds rise heroes whose actions shake the foundations of the galaxy. These are your heroes, and your actions can affect how systems, sectors, and even the entire galaxy endures the hardships of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars").

Creating heroes for a [Clone Wars Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign "Clone Wars Campaign") is just like creating characters for any campaign. However, this book provides new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") uses to help tailor your hero to best fit a [Clone Wars Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign "Clone Wars Campaign"). Heroes who come to prominence against the background of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") likely have strong ties to the Separatists or to the Republic, and most have at least a passing familiarity with [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and their powers. Even in the farthest reaches of the galaxy, [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") disturb and destroy the lives of beings that otherwise have no interest in- or knowledge of- the politics and events of the center of the galaxy. Many heroes are thrust into their role by war coming to their homes, and unlikely heroes can be created in the fire fights on distant Outer Rim planets. The mechanics in this chapter help you build heroes who reflect the unique environment and themes of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), but you can use mechanics from other supplements as well.

When you create your character, consider not only how [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") have shaped his or her life but also where your character is going in the future. Your callous smuggler who has become wealthy running weapons for the Separatists behind Republic lines will not be a neutral third party forever. As your hero becomes entangled in the conflicts and plots that surround [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), the hero's station in life, capabilities, and even personality might change accordingly. You might begin your career running weapons with little interest in which side emerges victorious, but when your [Clone Wars Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign "Clone Wars Campaign") begins, you join other heroes and engage in adventures of your own. Think not only about how [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") affect your character at the time of creation but also how it may affect your character five, ten, even twenty levels into the campaign.

## [Dark Times Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Times_Heroes "Dark Times Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") naturally bring new and diverse challenges to all character types. In this chapter, players will find information on creating Dark Times heroes, including story consideration, new uses and [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") for the standard [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), plus new [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") and expanded [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") usable by all classes. A couple of existing [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") gain access to new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and new [Destinies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destinies "Destinies") suited to [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times") are described. Finally, new [Unleashed Abilities](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unleashed_Abilities "Unleashed Abilities") give non-Force-users equally interesting and powerful options similar to *The Force Unleashed*.

In the *Force Unleashed Campaign Guide*, it is assumed that the characters are heroes fighting against the evils of the galaxy. Generally speaking, the heroes are fighting the Empire and its supporters, plus criminal factions that might prove troublesome. Although it is possible to play darker roles using this book, the focus is on the forces of good.

## [Rebellion Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Heroes "Rebellion Era Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

For the heroes of [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era"), fighting against [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") means putting their lives- and the lives of their loved ones- on the line, risking everything to oppose a tyrannical government. Heroes in a [Rebellion Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign "Rebellion Era Campaign") are among the most iconic in all *Star Wars* lore. [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo") is the prototypical [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"); [Princess Leia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Princess_Leia "Princess Leia") is the model for the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble"). They are great heroes not only because they fight against a powerful enemy, but also because they put everything at risk, enduring great triumphs and horrific tragedies to free the galaxy from the oppression of [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine").

The heroes of a [Rebellion Era Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign "Rebellion Era Campaign") often are ordinary citizens who go above and beyond the call of duty to fight the Empire. Although some of these heroes might come from respected families or have prominent histories, many heroes of [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") are simply average folks who have decided that they can no longer live in complacency. Indeed, one of the most interesting aspects of Rebellion Era Heroes is that many lead mundane lives as cogs in the Imperial machine until something spurs them to strike back against the Empire. They undergo a metamorphosis that most players can understand, transitioning from everyday members of society to people whose actions shake the foundations of the galaxy.

Unlike heroes in [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), heroes of [The Rebellion Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebellion_Era "The Rebellion Era") have the support of other like-minded residents of the galaxy. [The Rebel Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Rebel_Alliance "The Rebel Alliance") forms a touchstone around which beings of all walks of life can rally. Indeed, its very existence shows that even the most ordinary of people can come together and achieve amazing results. The Alliance (and other resistance groups not directly affiliated with the Rebellion) gives heroes a place to obtain information and support, share their knowledge and experiences, and reaffirm their dedication to the fight against the Empire.

## [Legacy Era Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Heroes "Legacy Era Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

This chapter provides players with information on creating Legacy Era Heroes. It presents new options for existing [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") and new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") specific to this era. This campaign guide assumes that the heroes are fighting for the cause of good against the tyranny of [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"). The player characters might be agents of [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance"), Jedi struggling to rebuild [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"), mercenaries living on the fringe, or even agents of the Empire loyal to deposed Emperor [Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Roan_Fel "Roan Fel"). One or more of them might be Imperial Knights- neither Jedi nor Sith, but trained Force-users nonetheless.

This section also provides rules for [Legacies](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacies "Legacies"). A [Legacy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy "Legacy") comes into play whenever a character decides to create a character with familial ties to one or more legendary characters from earlier years in the *Star Wars* saga. For example, a player character descended from the Organa family may wish to take advantage of the [Legacy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy "Legacy") rules.

## [Fringe Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fringe_Heroes "Fringe Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Star Wars* explores the conflict between light and dark, but the *Star Wars* universe features numerous examples of antiheroes- individuals who fall somewhere in the middle and who could care less about notions of good and evil, instead forging their own ways, by their own rules. [Lando Calrissian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lando_Calrissian "Lando Calrissian"), [Han Solo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Solo "Han Solo"), [Boba Fett](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Boba_Fett "Boba Fett"), and many others take this role at some time in their careers, standing in that murky zone between hem and villain until circumstance or fate pushes them to one side or the other.

Such antiheroes are some of the most engaging figures in the *Star Wars* universe. Their inexorable slides to evil as well as their efforts to achieve redemption resonate with us all. Thus, characters of ambiguous morals and shady principals are often adopted in the roleplaying game. To this end, this chapter presents new [Character Creation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Creation "Character Creation") material to help you design rogue heroes that are every bit as compelling as any of the reluctant or fallen heroes found in the galaxy.

## [Military Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Heroes "Military Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

For over a thousand generations [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order") has guarded the peace throughout the galaxy. Even so, key eras of the *Star Wars* saga are ravaged by war, offering Gamemasters opportunities for campaigns focusing on warfare and the military. The heroes of a [Military Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Campaign "Military Campaign") fight in battles, but they also can be the characters around which the war turns. In many cases, the actions of such heroes not only decide battles but also determine the fate of entire systems and sectors. Billions of lives hang in the balance, and the heroes' actions can determine the final outcome of entire wars. The heroes of a [Military Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Campaign "Military Campaign") are not simply combatants, they are agents of [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny").

The [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class is well suited for a [Military Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military_Campaign "Military Campaign"). *Galaxy at War* introduces new options specifically geared for characters who take levels in the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class, including those who only have a few levels of the class. Additionally, the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class encompasses a wide variety of archetypes within the military theme: [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers") can be covert-ops warriors, military police, officers-in-training, mercenaries, [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") pilots, [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners"), martial arts masters, and much, much more. *Galaxy at War* helps players and Gamemasters use the [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") class to greater potential, illuminating new paths for combat archetypes.

At the same time, [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers") are not the only participants in warfare. [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") recruit and command troops, [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") raid supply lines and ambush unsuspecting troops; [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts") prepare entire armies for the battles they are about to face; and [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") bring the power of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to combat. Heroes of these [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") can find new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") in this chapter, and elsewhere in *Galaxy at War* can find new material to hive heroes a distinctly military edge. Heroes of all classes are affected by the tides of war, and *Galaxy at War* presents several new options that reflect how involvement in galaxywide war changes those characters.

Much of the discussion in *Galaxy at War* uses the word "military" to represent a general theme of a faction involved in a war. The term is not reserved only for organized factions such as [The Galactic Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Republic "The Galactic Republic") or [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire"), but also covers any paramilitary or combat-oriented group. Highly organized and militant pirate groups, mercenary bands, support forces, and even individual adventuring parties all fall under this banner. Military heroes are not required to be signed up for the armed forces of a major government, although typically they are members of an organized force with a rank structure and protocols of behavior focusing on combat and warfare.

## [Intrigue Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intrigue_Heroes "Intrigue Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Although the *Star Wars* universe is replete with scenes of breathtaking action, dramatic [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") duels, sweeping ship battles, and hair-raising escapes, it is also deeply immersed in intrigue. The galaxy is an enormous place with a huge variety of [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), ideologies, corporations, and other groups jockeying to gain power and to carve their own destinies. A dense, if delicate, web of negotiations, treaties, alliances, and obligations weaves disparate groups together to stop the civilizations of the galaxy from descending into chaos. Sometimes this web keeps the peace, but often stability is threatened or overturned as masterminds scheme for more control. For the heroes, adventure is around every corner and, more often than not, comes looking for them instead of the other way around.

An [Intrigue Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intrigue_Campaign "Intrigue Campaign") has plenty of room for every type of character, although creating heroes for an [Intrigue Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intrigue_Campaign "Intrigue Campaign") requires a bit more thought than it might in other settings. Campaigns that focus on intrigue are less about kicking down doors and tossing thermal detonators (although these is a time and place for that, as well) than about the gradual, but steady, discovery of information and secrets as the heroes unravel plots. This chapter provides more [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), and a new twist on [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") to help your characters fit into a world of backroom deals, clandestine meetings, blackmail, infiltration, and political maneuvering.

At first glance, the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class seems to be the wisest choice in an [Intrigue Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intrigue_Campaign "Intrigue Campaign"). To be sure, with its tremendous amount of [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") and its intrigue-inspired [Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent_Trees "Talent Trees"), the [Noble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Noble "Noble") Heroic Class is well suited for this type of campaign, but the other [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") have just as much to offer. [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") use their silver tongues or dastardly tricks for their own personal gain. [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"), as masters of infiltration, use their resources to break into government facilities or corporate offices to retrieve valuable information. [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers") need not be brutish thugs and can include military intelligence officers or bodyguards for the rich and powerful, picking up vital secrets as they go about their duties. [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi"), known for their role as peacekeepers and negotiators, find themselves wrapped up in densely woven plots and shifting agendas on a daily basis. In short, every [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") has its place in an [Intrigue Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intrigue_Campaign "Intrigue Campaign"), as long as the characters realize that not every situation can be solved with combat. The heroes must rely on their powers of observation and deduction to move the story along, as well.

## [Exploration Heroes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exploration_Heroes "Exploration Heroes")

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Explorers are curious by nature, driven by complex needs and desires. Some love the thrill of discovery. Others enjoy freedom and isolation from the complicated dangerous, and often war-torn galaxy at large. Many are driven by profit, working for interstellar conglomerates or private enterprises, in search of new resources or planets to exploit. Some seek refuge from a hostile government, a vengeful syndicate, or their own troubled past. However, no matter what drives beings to the wildest and least understood areas of the galaxy, they must rely on their own abilities and skills to survive.

When exploring [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"), adaptable and diversely trained heroes perform best. Those trained in survival techniques or adjustable styles of combat do well when physically exploring new worlds. Heroes who have insightful communications abilities might negotiate successfully with a newly encountered [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), perhaps deciphering the basics of its culture. Clever mechanics might adapt or jury-rig broken machinery in the field, far from a store or a machinist's shop. These are useful tools anywhere in the galaxy, but they are vital to heroes operating without support in a strange environment.

This chapter presents several new [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") playable as heroes in *Star Wars* campaigns. Many of these [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") are commonly encountered throughout the galaxy and are not restricted to [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions"). However, their nature or history often ties them to the lesser-known regions of space. Gamemasters can use the information in Chapter 5: [Unknown Regions Threats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Threats "Unknown Regions Threats") for [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") and threats native to [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions").

Although heroes of the [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") class are best suited for standard exploration duties, heroes of all [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") have skills and abilities useful to an exploration team. In this section, all standard [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes") and core [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes") will find new [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") suitable for expanding their exploration options. A new non-Jedi [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") presented at the end of this chapter will interest those who want to search out new non-Jedi [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions") and abilities unique to [The Unknown Regions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Unknown_Regions "The Unknown Regions").
