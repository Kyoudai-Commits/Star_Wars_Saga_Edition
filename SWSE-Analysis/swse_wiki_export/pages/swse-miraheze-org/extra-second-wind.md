# Extra Second Wind

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extra_Second_Wind) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You tend to come back from the edge of oblivion more often.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance")

**Effect:** You can catch your [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") one additional time per day, but you can still only catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") once per encounter.

**Normal:** A hero can normally catch his or her [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") only once per day.

**Special:** You can take this feat multiple times; each time you take this feat, you can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") one additional time per day. A [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character that takes this feat for the first time can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") once per day.
