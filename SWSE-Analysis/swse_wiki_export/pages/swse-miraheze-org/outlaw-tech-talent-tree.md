# Outlaw Tech Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Outlaw_Tech_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Outlaw_Tech_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

[Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") learn a variety of tricks that allow them to squeeze more life out of ailing technology, and they can enhance their gear in ways the manufacturer never intended.

## Core Talents

### Fast Repairs

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

Whenever you [Jury-Rig](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rig "Jury-Rig") an object or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") gains a number of temporary [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to the result of your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check. Damage is subtracted from these temporary [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") first, and temporary [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") go away at the conclusion of the encounter.

### Hot Wire

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

You can use your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check modifier instead of your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check modifier when making [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks to [Improve Access](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improve_Access "Improve Access"). You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skill for the purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check reroll, you may reroll your [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** If my [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") takes both **[Trace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trace "Trace")** and **Hot Wire**, can they make a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check in place of [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") as long as they have access to a computer terminal that they can hot-wire?

**A:** No. **Hot Wire** works only when [Improving Access](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improving_Access "Improving Access") to a computer (that is, when you're trying to improve the computer's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") toward you). Making [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks in place of [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") checks is a completely separate use of the Skill, so [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") can't be substituted in that case.

### Quick Fix

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

Once per encounter, you may [Jury-Rig](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rig "Jury-Rig") an object or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that is not [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled"). All normal benefits and penalties for [Jury-Rigging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigging "Jury-Rigging") still apply.

### Personalized Modifications

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you may tweak the settings, grips, and moving parts of a powered weapon you wield, tailoring it to your needs. For the remainder of a the encounter, you gain a +1 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on attack rolls, and a +2 [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus on damage rolls with that weapon. You can use this Talent only on powered weapons (those that require an [Energy Cell](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cell "Energy Cell") or [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") to operate), including weapons connected to a larger power source (such as [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems")).

The Outlaw Tech Talent TreeThe Outlaw Tech Talent Tree

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements")*

The Outlaw Tech Talent Tree fills some niches left by the existing [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") rules and options for repairing broken pieces of technology. Here's a behind-the-screen look at the design decisions that went into creating these [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").

- **Fast Repairs** is designed to make [Jury-Rigging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigging "Jury-Rigging") more appealing to tech-oriented characters. As it stands now, [Jury-Rigging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigging "Jury-Rigging") an object or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") moves it up the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") but does not provide temporary [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). If your ship is at 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and you jury-rig it, it's still [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") from [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point") loss. This new [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") alleviates that problem, while still making [Jury-Rigging](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rigging "Jury-Rigging") useful for ships pushed down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by other means (such as [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage).
- **Quick Fix** builds on **Fast Repairs** and gives a techie character a way to restore some [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that's still in the fight, but brings with it the threat of becoming [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") at the end of the encounter. This new [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") also gives ship [Engineers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineers "Engineers") something else to do in combat, especially in desperate situations, and it can bring a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") back up to full fighting form very quickly.
- **Hot Wire** is similar to the other [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill")-substitution [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and makes sense for a techie who may not be the best slicer around.
- **Personalized Modifications** is designed to give a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") another means of activating some of their other [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"). For example, many of the Scoundrel's [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the [Misfortune Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talent_Tree "Misfortune Talent Tree") rely on a successful attack roll to activate. Combined with the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") Feat, this new [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") gets the [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") a small boost to attacks that can compensate for the difference between low [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") progression and [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") progression, even at high levels. The [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel") might still need to work closely with their friends against tougher foes, but this [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") is designed to capture the feel of tweaking your own weapon while still synergizing with many of the [Misfortune Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talents "Misfortune Talents").
