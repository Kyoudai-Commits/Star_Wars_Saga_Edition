# Reconnaissance Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reconnaissance_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reconnaissance_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to work well in concert with a team to provide the best reconnaissance and cover as much ground as possible.

## Core Talents

### Reconnaissance Team Leader

You gain a single [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower"). Choose either the [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower"), [Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower"), or [Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower"), generating the follower's statistics using the [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") rules. This [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") gains the [Skill Training (Perception)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training_(Perception) "Skill Training (Perception)") and [Skill Training (Stealth)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Training_(Stealth) "Skill Training (Stealth)") feats. Additionally, whenever you use the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill, all your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") can also make [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks as a part of the same action if they are able to.

You can select this Talent up to three times. Each time you do, you gain one additional [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower").

### Close-Combat Assault

*Prerequisite:* **Reconnaissance Team Leader**

Each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") gains the [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") feat.

### Get Into Their Positions

*Prerequisites:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5, **Reconnaissance Team Leader**

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you can cause one of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") to move up to his or her [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") +2 squares.

### Reconnaissance Actions

*Prerequisite:* **Reconnaissance Team Leader**

You and your reconnaissance team have learned to work together as a cohesive unit and have an established set of tactics, which you have practiced to perfection. You can use any of the following actions on your turn:

- *Forward Scouting:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") armed with a ranged weapon who has [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target, you can grant one ally a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls against the target until the end of your next turn. Thus, if you have multiple armed [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") with [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to the target, you can grant the +2 bonus to multiple allies.
- *Group Sniping:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") armed with a ranged weapon who has [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target, you and each of your followers gains a +1 circumstance bonus to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks until the end of your next turn.
- *Sweep the Area:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). For each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") armed with a ranged weapon who has [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to your target, you and each of your [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") gains a +1 circumstance bonus on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks until the end of your next turn.
