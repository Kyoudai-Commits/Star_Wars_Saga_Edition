# Meat Shield

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Meat_Shield](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Meat_Shield) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are adept at using an opponent's defense to your own advantage.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent attacks you while in [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") provided by another character, creature, or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), you are treated as being in [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from attacks by that opponent.
