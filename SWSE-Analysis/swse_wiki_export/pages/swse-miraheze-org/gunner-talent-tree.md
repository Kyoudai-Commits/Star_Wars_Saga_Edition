# Gunner Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Many Starship [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") are skilled both in and out of the cockpit, and are deadly with ranged weapons of any kind.

## Core Talents

### Dogfight Gunner

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Gunner**

While your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is engaged in a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), you take no penalty on your attack rolls with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"), even if you are not the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot").

### Expert Gunner

You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls made using [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons").

### Quick Trigger

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Gunner**

Whenever an enemy [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") moves out of your square, or an adjacent square, you may make a single attack against that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

### System Hit

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Gunner**

Whenever you deal damage to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") that equals or exceeds its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you move that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") an additional -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

## Additional Talents

### Crippling Hit

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* **System Hit**

Whenever you make an attack that causes a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") to move -1 or more steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you may also cause it to lose one of the following systems: [Hyperdrive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrive "Hyperdrive"), one [Weapon System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_System "Weapon System") or [Weapon Battery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Battery "Weapon Battery"), or communications. The system remains inoperable until the target regains all steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Fast Attack Specialist

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Quick Trigger**

Once per encounter, when [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") of Gargantuan size or smaller, you can make a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Talent one additional time in an encounter.

### Great Shot

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

When firing a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon"), you treat the distance to the target as though it were one [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") category less than it actually is. For example, when targeting an opponent at Short Range, you treat it as though it were at least Point-Blank Range for the purpose of determining bonuses or penalties.

### Overcharged Shot

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Gunner**

You know how to overcharge your Vehicle's weapon to produce additional damage. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can overcharge your Vehicle's energy weapon and deal +1 die of damage on your next attack in the same turn. However, your weapon loses 1 die of damage on its subsequent attacks and cannot be overcharged again, until a full round passes without the weapon firing.

### Synchronized Fire

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Expert Gunner**

Once per encounter, you may ready to fire a single weapon at the same target as an ally, and you coordinate with a single weapon of your ally. If both attacks hit, you add the damage of the two weapons together before applying the target's [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") or [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), and treat it as a single attack for purposes of exceeding the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").
