# Jedi Investigator Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Investigator_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Investigator_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You are one of The Jedi Order's most trusted investigators, tasked with ferreting out truth through a combination of detective work and Force divination.

## Core Talents

### Echoes of the Force

*Prerequisite:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

You can use the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power on a location instead of on an individual creature, peering into the location's past to view events that occurred there. Unlike the normal use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power, you are actually looking into the location's past (at a time you designate), and you must be standing in the location being viewed.

The target DC for your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is 20, +1 for each day into the past that you are attempt to scry. When you look into the past, you need only specify a time in a number of days, as you can sense tremors in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") that focus you visions on meaningful events that day.

### Jedi Quarry

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can designate a single target creature as the focus of your attentions. You gain a +2 bonus to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") any time you spend a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move"), provided that you end your movement adjacent to the target. You retain this bonus (and may not use this Talent again) until your target surrenders, is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), moves to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), or until the encounter ends.

### Prepared for Danger

*Prerequisite:* *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

Whenever you have at least one unspent *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"), you can spend that *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power to regain any one other [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Sense Deceptive Influence

Whenever someone makes a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") or [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill check against your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, replacing your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") with the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check if it is higher.

### Unclouded Judgement

*Prerequisite:* **Sense Deceptive Influence**

Whenever you are the target of a Mind-Affecting [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to negate the effects of that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or [Force Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent "Force Talent") (no [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") required).
