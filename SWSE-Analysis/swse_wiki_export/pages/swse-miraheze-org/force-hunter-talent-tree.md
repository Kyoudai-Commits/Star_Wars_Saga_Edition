# Force Hunter Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Hunter_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Hunter_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Hunting Jedi is big business during [The Legacy Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Legacy_Era "The Legacy Era"), as the Sith-controlled Empire pays well for captured Jedi. However, given their powers and skills, Jedi are among the most dangerous prey for [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") to pursue.

## Core Talents

### Force Blank

You are especially hard to detect using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Attempts to detect you using the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") aspect of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill suffer a -10 penalty.

### Lightsaber Evasion

Whenever an enemy misses you with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you may [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares in any direction. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Precision Fire

The Jedi are skilled at blocking and deflecting [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") with their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), and you are able to compensate for this to some degree by taking careful shots. Whenever you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you increase the difficulty of **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** attempts to negate your attack by +5.

### Steel Mind

If you resist the effects of a [*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*] Force Power, the user of that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") cannot attempt to use the same [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against you for the remainder of the encounter.

### Strong-Willed

You are trained to resist Jedi mind tricks. You add your Class Level to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks.

### Telekinetic Resistance

Whenever you are targeted by a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that moves you, you reduce the distance you are moved by half.
