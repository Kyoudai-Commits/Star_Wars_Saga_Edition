# Close Combat Escape

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Close_Combat_Escape](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Close_Combat_Escape) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When you slip free from your opponent, you can deliver a parting attack.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics")

**Effect:** When you successfully use [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") to escape a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple"), you can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to make a single attack with a melee weapon or an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against the opponent that had [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") you. If the attack hits, it deals normal damage, and your opponent is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") until the start of its next turn.
