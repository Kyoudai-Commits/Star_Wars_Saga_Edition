# Experienced Medic

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experienced_Medic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experienced_Medic) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have spent so much time on the battlefield that patching up wounded allies has become second nature to you.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

**Effect:** You can perform [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery") on a number of creatures equal to your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 2) simultaneously. You make [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks for each individual creature as normal.
