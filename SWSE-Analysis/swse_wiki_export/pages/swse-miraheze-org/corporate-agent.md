# Corporate Agent

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corporate_Agent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corporate_Agent) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Intersteller corporations hold a large amount of power in all [Eras of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Eras_of_Play "Eras of Play"). In some regions, the corporations control enough systems to operate as the de facto government. However, most are spread across countless worlds throughout the galaxy. Their presence varies, ranging from near dominance of a local market, to only having a single office, store, or facility. Major corporations have agents of their own, looking after company interests. These agents might specialize in security, fraud, exploration, administration, or many other aspects of management and production. They operate in the open or covertly, normally restricting their activities to keeping an eye on their own company, but corporations can use agents for corporate espionage, or even sabotaging competitors.

Corporate Agents might wield as much or more power than government agents. Some agents find the company is their life. Indeed, with each promotion, they might find that the company demands more and more of their time in exchange for increased status, power, and pay.

**Examples of Corporate Agents in Star Wars:** Fiolla of Lorrd, Haydel Goravvus, Jana Lorso, Odumin.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Corporate Agent, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information"), [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Skill Focus (Knowledge (Bureaucracy))](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Knowledge_(Bureaucracy)) "Skill Focus (Knowledge (Bureaucracy))")
- **Special:** Must be employed by a major interstellar corporation

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Corporate Agents gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Corporate Agents gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Corporate Agents gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Corporate Agent gains an additional [Corporate Agent Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corporate_Agent_Talent "Corporate Agent Talent"). A Corporate Agent may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Corporate Agent Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corporate_Agent_Talent_Trees "Corporate Agent Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Executive Leadership

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), as many times an encounter equal to half your Corporate Agent level, you can grant an ally within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") a temporary boost to their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), or attack rolls. Until the end of their turn, they gain one of the following benefits:

- Increase their base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by 2 squares.
- Add a +2 morale boost to all their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses").
- Add a +2 morale boost to their attack rolls.
