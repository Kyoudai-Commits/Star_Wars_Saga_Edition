# Hasty Modification

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hasty_Modification](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hasty_Modification) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can tweak and adapt equipment on the fly.

**Prerequisite:** [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist")

**Effect:** You can exchange a Trait you have applied to a piece of [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") for another one by spending 1 minute to make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check. If you succeed, the [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") loses the old Trait and gains a new Trait until the end of the encounter, at which point the device loses all Traits previously acquired through use of the [Tech Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tech_Specialist "Tech Specialist") feat.
