# Long Haft Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Long_Haft_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Long_Haft_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have learned to take advantage of oversized [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), turning them into [Double Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapons "Double Weapons").

**Effect:** When you use a [Lightsaber Pike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Pike "Lightsaber Pike") or a [Long-Handle Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Long-Handle_Lightsaber "Long-Handle Lightsaber"), you can attack with both ends of the weapon, treating it as a [Double Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Weapon "Double Weapon").
