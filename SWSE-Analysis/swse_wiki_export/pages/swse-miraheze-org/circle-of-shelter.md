# Circle of Shelter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Circle_of_Shelter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Circle_of_Shelter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You create a protected area around yourself, through which enemies have difficulty reaching you or your allies.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus all adjacent allies.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | Until the end of your next turn, you and all adjacent allies gain a +1 deflection bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). |
| 25 | Until the end of your next turn, you and all adjacent allies gain a +2 deflection bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). |
| 30 | Until the end of your next turn, you and all adjacent allies gain a +5 deflection bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). |

**Lightsaber Form ([Soresu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soresu "Soresu")):** If you have the **[Soresu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soresu "Soresu")** Talent, you can add the deflection bonus on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to use the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** and **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talents until the end of your next turn.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to also apply the deflection bonus from this Form Power to the [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") of all adjacent allies.
