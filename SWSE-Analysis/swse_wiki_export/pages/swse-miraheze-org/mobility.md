# Mobility

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobility) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at moving past opponents and avoiding opportunistic attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge")

**Effect:** You get a +5 dodge bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") caused when you move out of or into a [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area").

**Special:** A situation that makes you lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (if any) also makes you lose dodge bonuses. Dodge bonuses also stack with each other, unlike most other types of [Bonuses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonuses "Bonuses").
