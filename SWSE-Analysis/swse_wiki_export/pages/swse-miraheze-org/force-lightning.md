# Force Lightning

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You blast an enemy with deadly arcs of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") energy.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 6 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Make one roll and compare the result to the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target takes 8d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage and moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Otherwise, the target takes half damage and does not move along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to move a target an additional -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when you successfully hit it with *Force Lightning*.
