# Deep Sight

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deep_Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deep_Sight) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have spent so much time in the deepest parts of your home planet that you can see even in total darkness.

**Prerequisite:** [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren")

**Effect:** You gain [Darkvision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkvision "Darkvision"), ignoring [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. You cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
