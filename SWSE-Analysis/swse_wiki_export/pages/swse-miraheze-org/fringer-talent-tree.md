# Fringer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fringer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fringer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You're especially skilled at "getting by" on backwater worlds.

## Core Talents

### Barter

You may reroll any [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check made to [Haggle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haggle "Haggle"), but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

### Fringe Savant

Whenever you roll a Natural 20 on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") during an encounter, you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Long Stride

Your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 2 squares if you are wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no armor. If you have a natural Fly, Climb, or Swim speed, it increases by 2 squares as well. You cannot use this Talent if you are wearing [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor") or [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor").

### Jury-Rigger

You may reroll any [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check made to [Jury-Rig](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rig "Jury-Rig"), but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

## Additional Talents

### Flee

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Long Stride**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can designate a single opponent and move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") away from that opponent; this movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") from that opponent, though it might provoke as normal for all other opponents. In addition, your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 2 until the end of your next turn.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Long Stride**

Once per encounter, you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

### Hold it Together

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Jury-Rigger**

Whenever you successfully [Jury-Rig](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jury-Rig "Jury-Rig") a device or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), the item does not move -5 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") at the end of the encounter, though it does move -2 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") steps down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Sidestep

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Long Stride**

You can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to reduce the cost of [Diagonal Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Diagonal_Movement "Diagonal Movement") to 1 until the end of your turn if you are wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no armor. You cannot use this Talent if you are wearing [Medium Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medium_Armor "Medium Armor") or [Heavy Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Armor "Heavy Armor").

### Swift Strider

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Sidestep**

You are skilled at maneuvering on the battlefield thanks to your experience surviving in dangerous places. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Blurring Burst:* As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), and gain a +2 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of the encounter.
- *Sudden Assault:* Make a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") attack against an enemy within range as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"). You take no penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") for this attack.
- *Weaving Stride:* Move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). You gain a cumulative +2 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") for each [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") made against you during this movement. This bonus lasts until the beginning of your next turn.
