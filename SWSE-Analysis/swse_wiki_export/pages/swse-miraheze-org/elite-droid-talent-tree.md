# Elite Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You have become a highly advanced [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") and are among the elite units of your class.

## Core Talents

### Break Program

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

You can use your ability to circumvent [Behavioral Inhibitors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Behavioral_Inhibitors "Behavioral Inhibitors") to temporarily break the programming of a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") that you have a data link with. Make a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check opposed by the Droid's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). Breaking the Droid's programming overrides its [Behavioral Inhibitors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Behavioral_Inhibitors "Behavioral Inhibitors") for a number of rounds equal to your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus.

### Heuristic Mastery

*Prerequisite:* [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") 15

You understand the subtleties and limitations of your [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor"). You can reroll any [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained") skill check (except [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")), but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. Once per encounter, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reroll any [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") ([Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") or [Untrained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Untrained "Untrained")), keeping the better of the two results.

### Scripted Routines

*Prerequisite:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

Your extensive experience allows you to preset specific routines that give you an advantage in some situations. Once per encounter you can use each of the following actions:

- *Attack Script:* You can use a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that modifies your attack roll as one Action less (for example, a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") becomes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") becomes a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") becomes a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") becomes a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")).
- *Defense Script:* You can apply your [Independent Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Spirit "Independent Spirit") bonus a second time during a single encounter.
- *Skill Script:* While in combat, you can apply a bonus equal to one-half of your Class Level to any single [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") that requires a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") or less to use. You must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the chosen [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") to activate this ability.

### Ultra Resilient

You have advanced subroutines that make you more resistant to the effect of damage. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can increase your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") with a bonus equal to your [Independent Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid "Independent Droid") level.
