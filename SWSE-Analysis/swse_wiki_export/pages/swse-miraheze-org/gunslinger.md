# Gunslinger

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Since the first primitive slugthrower was developed thousands of years ago, there have been soldiers of fortune focused on [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") fighting. The tradition grew with the technology, creating a special breed of blaster-wielding freelancers who shoot first, and ask questions later. Often wandering guns-for-hire, bodyguards in fringe areas, professional duelists, or even assassins, these fighters know about [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") of all descriptions.

They can draw faster, shoot faster, and aim more accurately with their weapons of choice than any other category of warrior. Some use their prowess to defend the weak and battle injustice, while others use their skill to gain fast and easy credits. Regardless of motivation, Gunslingers seek to use their focused combat techniques to gain greater fame, and wield that fame as a weapon against less well-known foes.

**Examples of Gunslingers in Star Wars:** Dash Rendar, Gallandro, Han Solo, Jango Fett.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Gunslinger, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot"), [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Gunslingers gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Gunslingers gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Gunslingers gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Gunslinger gains an additional [Gunslinger Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger_Talent "Gunslinger Talent"). A Gunslinger may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Gunslinger Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger_Talent_Trees "Gunslinger Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Trusty Sidearm

Starting at 2nd level, the Gunslinger gains a bonus on [Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage "Damage") rolls equal to one-half their Class Level (rounded down) when wielding a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol"). This damage is in addition to the character's usual level bonus to damage. This damage is doubled on a successful [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"), as normal.
