# Trench Warrior

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trench_Warrior](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trench_Warrior) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have experience fighting in the trenches, and you know how to use [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") to your attack advantage.

**Effect:** When you are adjacent to a wall or object that provides you with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from your target's [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks"), you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on your attack rolls against that target.
