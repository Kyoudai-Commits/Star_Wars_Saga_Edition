# Guardian Spirit Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guardian_Spirit_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guardian_Spirit_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You have a spirit watching over you, providing advice and continuing your training in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Although [Force Spirits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Spirits "Force Spirits") cannot physically aid you, their guidance is critical to your success.

## Core Talents

### Crucial Advice

Once per encounter, when you fail a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), you can reroll the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") with a +2 circumstance bonus. (In the case of [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") with multiple possible results, such as when activating a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with multiple effects, failing a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") is defined as achieving less than the minimum DC for that check.)

### Distracting Apparition

*Prerequisite:* **Manifest Guardian Spirit**

When you have a manifested Guardian Spirit, the Guardian Spirit also discourages your enemies and distracts them from their goals. Any enemy within 3 squares of your Guardian Spirit takes a -2 penalty to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") and a -2 penalty on attack rolls against you.

### Guardian Spirit

You have a **Guardian Spirit** watching over you from the realm of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), providing you with insight and advice. Your **Guardian Spirit** might be an old mentor or an ancient member of your [Force Tradition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Tradition "Force Tradition") who has chosen to guide your [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny").

When you use the [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, you can instead choose to consult your **Guardian Spirit**. When you do so, you learn more than just whether the results of your actions will be favorable or unfavorable; you also learn the nature of any immediate consequences, including potential encounters, and whether or not certain actions will bring you closer to achieving your [Destiny](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny "Destiny"). Additionally, you gain one bonus [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") each day (available after you rest for at least 6 hours). This bonus [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") can only be used to improve a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") or activate a [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") or a [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret"). If you do not spend your bonus [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") in a given day, it is lost at the start of the next day.

### Manifest Guardian Spirit

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to have your Guardian Spirit manifest for the duration of the encounter. Your Guardian Spirit appears in any square within 6 squares of you. A Guardian Spirit occupies a space, but creatures can move through the Guardian Spirit without obstruction. As long as the Guardian Spirit remains within 12 squares of you, you gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, a +2 morale bonus on [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks, and a +2 morale bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). You can move the Guardian Spirit up to 6 squares as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn.

### Vital Encouragement

Once per encounter, your Guardian Spirit offers you **Vital Encouragement**, urging you to press on despite adversity. As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 10 + one-half your Heroic Level. Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter are lost. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from multiple sources do not stack.
