# Saboteur

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saboteur](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saboteur) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Saboteurs aim to disrupt or destroy the physical mechanisms of their enemies, usually covertly and often from within. They might use improvised tools or create specialized devices to carry out certain tasks. They rely on stealth and secrecy to carry out their missions. A Saboteur might work alone in a single installation, or as a part of a small group for complex or widespread attacks. Saboteur missions employ explosives, computer slicing, [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") reprogramming, and social engineering to achieve their goals.

The best Saboteurs focus their attacks and methods for maximum disruption and damage to the systems, objects, or [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") they want to affect. Collateral damage is typically unwanted and counterproductive to their cause, especially to sensitive or nonmilitary targets. Many targets are disabled rather than destroyed, but the results can still be very effective and demoralizing to an enemy. Many Saboteurs must make the most out of limited supplies, which often forces them to make precise attacks to avoid wasting resources.

During [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), Saboteurs are among the most radical and prolific of the anti-Imperial factions. They might appear at any installation, though high security measures make their attacks less likely. Skilled Saboteurs are difficult for Imperials to defend against, and therefore they are ruthlessly pursued by all Imperial organizations, especially the [Imperial Security Bureau](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Security_Bureau "Imperial Security Bureau").

**Examples of Saboteurs in Star Wars:** Kell Tainer, Kyle Katarn (pre-*Jedi Knight*), Wes Janson, Zann Consortium, Defilers.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Saboteur, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Saboteurs gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Saboteurs gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Saboteurs gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, every time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Saboteur gains an additional [Saboteur Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saboteur_Talent "Saboteur Talent"). A Saboteur may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Saboteur Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saboteur_Talent_Trees "Saboteur Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Unexpected Results

Saboteurs occasionally see the results of their work, even at the most at unexpected moments. When an enemy making an attack roll against a Saboteur rolls a Natural 1 on an attack roll using [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons"), [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols"), [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles"), or [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"), that weapon is immediately disabled and ceases to function until it has received [Repairs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repairs "Repairs").

### Destructive

Starting at 2nd level, a Saboteur always deals double damage to unattended objects and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles").

### Quick Sabotage

Saboteurs know how to disable [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") quickly and efficiently. Saboteurs can attempt a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to [Disable Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disable_Device "Disable Device") by using improvised tools if no [Security Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Security_Kit "Security Kit") is available. At 4th level, a Saboteur can attempt to disable Simple Devices as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). At 6th level, a Saboteur can attempt to disable Tricky Devices as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). At 8th level, a Saboteur can attempt to disable Complex Devices using two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions").

### Master Saboteur

When a Saboteur reaches 10th level, they excel at inhibiting and destroying enemy [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment"). 10th level Saboteurs can reroll any [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to [Disable Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disable_Device "Disable Device") or [Handle Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Handle_Explosives "Handle Explosives"), keeping the better of the two results.
