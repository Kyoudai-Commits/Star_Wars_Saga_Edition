# Mind Shard

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Shard](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_Shard) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to splinter the mind of an opponent, wracking it with pain.

**[*[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 12 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 2d8 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Furthermore, the target cannot improve its [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") until the end of your next turn (although other creatures may use abilities to improve the target's [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition")). |
| 20 | Compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 3d8 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Furthermore, the target cannot improve its [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") until the end of your next turn (although other creatures may use abilities to improve the target's [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition")). |
| 25 | Compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 4d8 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Furthermore, the target cannot improve its [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") until the end of your next turn (although other creatures may use abilities to improve the target's [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition")). |
| 30 | Compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes 5d8 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Furthermore, the target cannot improve its [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition") until the end of your next turn (although other creatures may use abilities to improve the target's [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition")). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by an additional +2d8 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.
