# Inborn Resilience

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inborn_Resilience](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inborn_Resilience) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You come from a hardy Species, but some of your people are better than others at avoiding certain kinds of harm.

**Prerequisite:** [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak")

**Effect:** You can reduce your [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to any one [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") to +0 and increase your [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to another [Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense "Defense") to +2. Once you change your [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonuses in this way, they cannot be changed back.
