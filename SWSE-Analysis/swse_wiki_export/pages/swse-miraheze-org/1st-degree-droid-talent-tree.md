# 1st-Degree Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[1st-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/1st-Degree_Droids "1st-Degree Droids") are usually Medical, Analytical, or Scientific Droids. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Dull the Pain

*Prerequisite:* **Medical Programming**

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check on an adjacent living creature to move it +1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Interrogator

You can create an aura of unliving, emotionless, menace that no biological creature can match, and combine it with the cruel application of medical knowledge. You can use your [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") modifier on a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check made to change a creature's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"), or to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate"). You must be adjacent to your target to use this ability, in addition to the normal requirements for these uses of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill.

### Medical Programming

When you use a [Medpac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac "Medpac") to perform [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid") with the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill, the creature gains 2 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") for every point by which your check exceeds the DC (rather than the normal 1 [Hit Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Point "Hit Point")).

## Additional Talents

### Known Vulnerability

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)")

You know the vulnerable spots to hit on most [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"). As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), you make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") check (DC 25 for rare [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), DC 35 for unknown [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), both as determined by the Gamemaster). If the check is successful, until the end of the encounter, whenever you make a successful [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") that deals damage against a target of that [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species"), your target takes a -2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Medical Analyzer

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* **Medical Programming**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)")

You use your medical knowledge and advanced processor to improve your treatment of medical conditions. When making a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to [Treat Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Disease "Treat Disease"), [Treat Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Poison "Treat Poison"), or [Treat Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Radiation "Treat Radiation"), you can also add your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier to your roll.

### Science Analyzer

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") and [Knowledge (Physical Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Physical_Sciences) "Knowledge (Physical Sciences)")

You use your extensive databanks to better analyze scientific data. You can add double your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier to your [Knowledge (Life Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Life_Sciences) "Knowledge (Life Sciences)") and [Knowledge (Physical Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Physical_Sciences) "Knowledge (Physical Sciences)") skill checks.

### Triage Scan

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* **Medical Programming**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check. If the check succeeds, you know if the organic characters within 6 squares and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") are below one half of their [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and at what step they are along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
