# Recurring Success

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recurring_Success](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recurring_Success) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You know how to make your enemies fall for the same trick twice.

**Effect:** When you select this feat, choose one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") or [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") that can normally be used once per encounter. You can use that [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") or [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") one additional time per encounter.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you do so, you must choose a different [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") or [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") to gain the benefits of this feat.
