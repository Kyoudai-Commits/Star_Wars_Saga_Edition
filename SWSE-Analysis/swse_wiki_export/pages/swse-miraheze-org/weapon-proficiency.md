# Weapon Proficiency

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are proficient with a particular kind of weaponry.

**Effect:** Choose one [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"). You are proficient with all weapons of the selected group. [Weapon Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Groups "Weapon Groups") include the following:

- [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons")
- [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons") (including both [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons") and [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"))
- [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers")
- [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols")
- [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles")
- Simple Weapons (including [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)"), [Simple Weapons (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Ranged) "Simple Weapons (Ranged)"), and [Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenades "Grenades"))

**Normal:** If you wield a weapon with which you are not proficient, you take a -5 penalty to your attack rolls.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a different [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"). You cannot select [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons") for this feat; instead you must select the [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency "Exotic Weapon Proficiency") feat to gain proficiency with a specific [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon").

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** If you have the [Quick Draw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Draw "Quick Draw") feat, you can draw and ignite [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
