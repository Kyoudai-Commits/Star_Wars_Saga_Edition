# Luka Sene Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luka_Sene_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Luka_Sene_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Luka Sene](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Luka_Sene "The Luka Sene") build upon the natural [Miralukan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miralukan "Miralukan") sense-based abilities. Only Force-users that are part of [The Luka Sene](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Luka_Sene "The Luka Sene") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Field Detection

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If the check succeeds, you can detect the presence, general strength, and origin (or direction of origin if the source is out of range) of electromagnetic and energy fields within 12 squares of you. You can also determine the type of field, allowing you to detect [Communications Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Communications_Devices "Communications Devices"), [Detection and Surveillance Devices](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Detection_and_Surveillance_Devices "Detection and Surveillance Devices"), and other electronic [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment").

When dealing damage to a person or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") using a personal shield, or to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") with shields active, a successful check allows you to detect minute fluctuations in the shield, reducing their [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating") by -5 against your attacks until the end of your turn.

### Improved Force Sight

*Prerequisites:* [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka"), [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")

Your natural Force Sight is more precise than that of your fellow [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka"). You can use the [Search](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search "Search") application of the [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") skill as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Additionally, you always succeed when using the [Sense Surroundings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Surroundings "Sense Surroundings") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill (no roll required).

### Luka Sene Master

*Prerequisites:* **Field Detection**, *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

You are a Master of Luka Sene techniques and an expert in using sense-related [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"). Once per encounter, you gain a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") that you must spend when activating a Talent from either the [Sense Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Talent_Tree "Sense Talent Tree") or Luka Sene Talent Tree, when using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power, or on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made to use the [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings") or [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") applications of the skill. The [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is lost if not used before the end of the encounter.

### Quickseeing

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"), *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")*

As a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your turn, you can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against a living creature within 12 squares, removing one use of the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power from your active [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") (as though you had activated the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power")). If your check result equals or exceeds the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of the target, you gain a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls against that target until the end of your turn. This counts as using the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power against that target, but this Talent replaces the normal rules and effects of that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").
