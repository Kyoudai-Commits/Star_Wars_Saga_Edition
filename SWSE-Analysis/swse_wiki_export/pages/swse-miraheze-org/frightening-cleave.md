# Frightening Cleave

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frightening_Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frightening_Cleave) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Enemies that see you successfully use the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat become fearful.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave"), [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack")

**Effect:** Immediately after you use the [Cleave](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleave "Cleave") feat, each enemy within 6 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") takes a -1 penalty to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), and attack rolls against you until the end of the encounter. This penalty stacks with itself to a maximum of -5. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
