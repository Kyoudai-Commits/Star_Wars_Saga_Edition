# Awareness Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Awareness_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Awareness_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are exceptionally good at noticing things and avoiding perilous situations.

## Core Talents

### Acute Senses

You may reroll any [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse. This Talent applies to [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks made to [Use Sensors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Sensors "Use Sensors").

### Expert Tracker

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You take no penalty on [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks made to [Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Track "Track") while moving your normal [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

### Improved Initiative

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You may choose to reroll any [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.

### Keen Shot

*Prerequisite:* **Acute Senses**

You take no penalty on your attack roll when attacking a target with [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")).

### Uncanny Dodge I

*Prerequisite:* **Improved Initiative**

You retain your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") regardless of being caught [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") or struck by a hidden attacker. You still lose your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") if you are [Immobilized](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Immobilized "Immobilized"). If you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), this Talent applies to your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

### Uncanny Dodge II

*Prerequisite:* **Uncanny Dodge I**

You cannot be [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked"). You can react to opponents on opposite sides of you as easily as you can react to a single attacker. If you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), this Talent applies to your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

## Additional Talents

### Reset Initiative

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Improved Initiative**, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative")

Once per encounter, at any time after the first round (that is, the first full round after the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), if one occurs), you can set your [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count") to your current Initiative +5.

### Weak Point

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Keen Shot**

Once per encounter, you can use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to ignore the [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") of a single target within your line of sight for the rest of your turn.
