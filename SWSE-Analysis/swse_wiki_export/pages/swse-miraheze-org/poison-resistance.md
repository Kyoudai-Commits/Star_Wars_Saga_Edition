# Poison Resistance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison_Resistance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison_Resistance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are naturally more resistant to the effects of [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison").

**Prerequisite:** [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13

**Effect:** You gain a +5 bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") when attacked by [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"). If the attack succeeds, you take only half damage.
