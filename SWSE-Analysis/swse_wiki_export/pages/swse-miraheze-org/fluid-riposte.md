# Fluid Riposte

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fluid_Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fluid_Riposte) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You smoothly turn aside your opponent's melee attack, stepping in to deliver your own attack.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (when an adjacent opponent makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against you)

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack deals half damage on a hit. You can then immediately move to any other square adjacent to your attacker and make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against it. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

**Lightsaber Form ([Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")):** If you have the **[Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")** Talent, after successfully activating this Form Power, you gain one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") which can only be used to activate the **[Djem So](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Djem_So "Djem So")** talent. If this temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to treat the attacker as [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") for purposes of your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack.
