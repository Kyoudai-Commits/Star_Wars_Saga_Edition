# Jedi Heritage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Heritage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Heritage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You come from a clan that has produced many Jedi, and you have overcome your Species' impulsiveness by becoming a master of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**Prerequisites:** [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek"), [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

**Effect:** Your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score is considered to be 4 points higher for the purpose of determining how many [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") you gain from the [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat, granting you two extra [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") for each [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training") feat.
