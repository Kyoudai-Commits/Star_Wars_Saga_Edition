# Sadistic Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sadistic_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sadistic_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

By dispatching a [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") creature, you terrify opponents around you.

**Effect:** When you deliver a [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") to a [Helpless](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helpless "Helpless") creature, all opponents within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") move -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") until the end of encounter.
