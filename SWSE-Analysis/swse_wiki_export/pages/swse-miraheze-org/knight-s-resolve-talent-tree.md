# Knight's Resolve Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knight's_Resolve_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knight's_Resolve_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You have dedicated your life to serving the rightful Emperor, and your devotion knows no bounds.

## Core Talents

### Knight's Morale

When an ally within 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") hits with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, you gain a +1 morale bonus to all your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the end of your next turn.

### Oath of Duty

When an ally within 12 squares of you and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") hits with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 3 x your Class Level until the end of your next turn. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from different sources do not stack.

### Praetoria Ishu

*Prerequisites:* **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**

You can use the **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")** Talent to negate a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") made against an adjacent ally. In addition, you can use the **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")** Talent to negate a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") made against an adjacent ally.

### Praetoria Vonil

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

You have mastered the offensive [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") style favored by [The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights"). When wielding a single [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") with two hands, you deal +1 die of damage if you move at least 1 square on your turn before making the attack.

### Strength of the Empire

*Prerequisite:* **Knight's Morale**

When an ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") hits with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack, you deal +1 die of damage with the next [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack you make before the end of your next turn.
