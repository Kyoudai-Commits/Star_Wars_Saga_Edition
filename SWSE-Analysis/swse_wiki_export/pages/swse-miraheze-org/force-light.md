# Force Light

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Light](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Light) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can draw [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") into yourself, turning you into a beacon of light that purges the taint of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All creatures with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, within 6 squares of you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The results of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | Until the start of your next turn, any creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, that begins its turn within 6 squares of you, automatically takes 1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls. This is an area effect. |
| 25 | Until the start of your next turn, any creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, that begins its turn within 6 squares of you, automatically takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls. This is an area effect. |
| 30 | Until the start of your next turn, any creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, that begins its turn within 6 squares of you, automatically takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls. This is an area effect. |
| 35 | Until the start of your next turn, any creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1+, that begins its turn within 6 squares of you, automatically takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage, and takes a -1 penalty on attack rolls. This is an area effect. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by an additional +1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage.

You can maintain *Force Light* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Force Light* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Force Light*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Force Light*.
