# Gladiatorial Combat Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gladiatorial_Combat_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gladiatorial_Combat_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You have learned the tricks of fighting in the gladiatorial arena. These Talents represent your knowledge of this specialized combination of combat and showmanship.

## Core Talents

### Brutal Attack

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

Choose a single weapon with which you posses the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat. Attacks with such weapons that deal damage (including doubling damage from a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit")) exceeding an opponent's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") deals +1 die of damage on that attack. You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, it applies to a different weapon.

### Call Out

*Prerequisite:* **Personal Vendetta**

When you use the **Personal Vendetta** Talent, you may designate one target of that Talent to take a -5 penalty to attacks against targets other than you, instead of the normal -2 penalty.

### Distracting Attack

*Prerequisite:* **Brutal Attack** (Chosen Weapon)

When you deal damage to a target with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), compare the attack roll to the targets' [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If the attack roll also meets or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target takes a -2 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn.

### Exotic Weapons Master

*Prerequisite:* [Exotic Weapon Proficiency](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency "Exotic Weapon Proficiency") (Any)

You treat all [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons") as a single [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group"). If you already have [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") or [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") that grant you proficiency with, or augment the use of, one [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon"), those [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") now apply to all [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons"). For example, if you already had [Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Shyarn)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_Proficiency_(Shyarn) "Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Shyarn)"), and [Weapon Focus (Shyarn)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Shyarn) "Weapon Focus (Shyarn)"), you are now proficient with, and possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat for, all [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons").

### Lockdown Strike

When you hit a moving opponent that is one size category larger than you or smaller with an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you immediately end its current movement.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Exotic Weapons)

*Prerequisite:* **Exotic Weapons Master**

Whenever you make multiple attacks with [Exotic Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons "Exotic Weapons") as a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2 points. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2 points.

### Personal Vendetta

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can taunt all opponents within 12 squares and [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"); on their next turn, these opponents take a -2 penalty on attack rolls made against any target other than you. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Unstoppable

You can sometimes shrug off the effect of debilitating attacks. Once per encounter, if you are hit by an attack that would normally knock you down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you can reduce the number of steps you move down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by 1 step (to a minimum of 0).
