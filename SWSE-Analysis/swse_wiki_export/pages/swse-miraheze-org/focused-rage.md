# Focused Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Focused_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Focused_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You retain better focus when enraged.

**Prerequisites:** [Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rage "Rage"), [Controlled Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Controlled_Rage "Controlled Rage")

**Effect:** While Raging, you can use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that require patience and concentration, at a -5 penalty.

**Normal:** While Raging, you cannot use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that require patience and concentration, such as [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force").
