# Sith Military Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Military_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Military_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are trained in Sith military fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in melee combat. This training is generally given to rank and file military members of [The Sith Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith_Empire "The Sith Empire") regardless of their Force training.

**Effect:** Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") when you reduce a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or deal damage that exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you can activate this feat to cause all enemies within 6 squares of that target to take a -2 penalty to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the end of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
