# Contentious Opportunity

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Contentious_Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Contentious_Opportunity) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You seize the moment when your opponent gives you an opening, darting in to strike.

**Time:** [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") (when you make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"))

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | If you hit with your [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you deal an additional +1 die of damage. |
| 25 | If you hit with your [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you deal an additional +2 dice of damage. |
| 30 | If you hit with your [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you deal an additional +3 dice of damage. |
| 35 | If you hit with your [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you deal an additional +4 dice of damage. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Makashi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi "Makashi")):** If you have the **[Makashi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi "Makashi")** Talent, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") (including this one) until the end of your next turn.

**Special:** If you hit with the [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you can immediately spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to reduce the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by 2 squares until the end of its next turn.
