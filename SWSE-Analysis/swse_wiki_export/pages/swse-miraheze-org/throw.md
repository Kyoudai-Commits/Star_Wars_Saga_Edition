# Throw

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Throw) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can Throw a creature that you've [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled").

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip")

**Effect:** If you successfully [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") an opponent with a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack, the opponent falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in any unoccupied space you desire up to 1 square beyond your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") and take Bludgeoning damage equal to your [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack damage. A Thrown opponent is no longer considered [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled").
