# Unified Squadron

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unified_Squadron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unified_Squadron) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies are excellent pilots whose skills shine when you fly together, making you better as a unit than as individuals.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, you always succeed when trying to [Avoid Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Avoid_Collision "Avoid Collision") with [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") piloted by allies who also have this feat (no [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions") occur, even on [Character Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Scale "Character Scale")).
