# Master Privateer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Privateer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Privateer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Books: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide"), [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Governments, large corporations, and other organizations have hired privateers throughout galactic history. Privateers might operate from a single armed ship, or they might control fleets of cruisers and [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"). During [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars"), [The Confederacy of Independent Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems "The Confederacy of Independent Systems") was known to use privateers to bolster its forces, especially late in the war. This might have also been a tactic to induce pirate forces to attack Republic shipping, drawing the pirates away from the CIS's own lucrative mercantile shipping networks. Though privateers are more active during wartime, they also operate during peacetime in more volatile sectors or systems.

Typically, a privateer operates under a letter of marque from an authorizing government or a corporate entity that controls a large region of space. A letter of marque offers a form of legitimacy to justify actions that most would consider nothing more than piracy. Indeed, on the surface, pirates and privateers operate much in the same fashion. They privately own and operate armed vessels that attack merchant shipping and take the cargoes for themselves. Whereas pirates attack whomever they please, privateers are theoretically restricted to ships of specific governments or ownership, as authorized by the letter of marque. Pirates treat prisoners as they see fit, perhaps offering them up for ransom or selling them into slavery. Privateers must treat captured personnel as prisoners of war during wartime, or as specified by their letter of marque at other times.

Master Privateers are experts at the art of stopping and seizing vessels. They are skilled pilots, capable combatants, and shrewd negotiators. They are familiar with people and regions that are likely to be friendly (or at least tolerant) toward privateers and are thus able to obtain letters of marque with some regularity. The line between pirates and privateers is often blurred, and legitimate privateers sometimes ultimately cross the line into piracy.

During [The Dark Times](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Times "The Dark Times"), the Empire considers privateers to be pirates and treats them as such. Thus, active privateers have been forced to the fringes of the galaxy during this time. The future Rebel Alliance would eventually experiment with using privateers, but that would prove to be a very controversial decision, and it did not gain widespread support in the Alliance.

Some Master Privateers are pirates, a motley collection of rogues and cutthroats that prey on merchant ships to plunder them for credits and cargo. An unsavory lot, Master Privateers commander any vessel they capture, taking prisoner the crew and passengers, perhaps letting them go, perhaps tossing them out the airlock depending on the nature of their captain. In all, Master Privateers are criminals and are treated as such by [The Galactic Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Empire "The Galactic Empire") and [The New Republic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_New_Republic "The New Republic"). Although such rogues are a menace to be sure, not all pirates are merciless killers. Many adopt and live by a strict code of conduct that gives them parameters for the types of ships they'll strike and what they do with prisoners. Some pirates release their prisoners, depositing them on more-or-less safe systems where they are likely to be rescued. But for every decent Master Privateer, a dozen more merciless fiends lie in wait.

**Examples of Master Privateers in Star Wars:** Amaiza Foxtrain, Bakken, Bar-Kooda, Cade Skywalker, Captain Dhas Vedij of the Far Orbit, Crimson Jack, Dharus, Drea Renthal, Grov Bricker, Kraaken, Nym, Urias Xhsxin, Zothip.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Master Privateer, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** At least two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from the
  - [Misfortune Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talent_Tree "Misfortune Talent Tree"),
  - [Smuggling Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Smuggling_Talent_Tree "Smuggling Talent Tree"), or
  - [Spacer Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spacer_Talent_Tree "Spacer Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses** At 1st level, Master Privateers gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Master Privateers gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Master Privateers gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Lure of Piracy

Even though Master Privateers are legitimate privateers, piracy is a great temptation. If a Master Privateer is lost to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side"), they drop all pretense of privateering, becoming a known pirate, and may no longer take any further levels of the Master Privateer [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Master Privateer gains an additional [Master Privateer Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Privateer_Talent "Master Privateer Talent"). A Master Privateer may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Master Privateer Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Privateer_Talent_Trees "Master Privateer Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Veteran Privateer

The Master Privateer's experience as a privateer has hardened them to the realities of battle, granting them the ability to subdue their foes with little difficulty. When a Master Privateer makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") roll, as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") they can grant themselves a +2 competence bonus on that attack roll. A Master Privateer can do this a number of times per encounter equal to one-half their Class Level (rounded down).
