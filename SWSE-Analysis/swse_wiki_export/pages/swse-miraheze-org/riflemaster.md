# Riflemaster

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riflemaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Riflemaster) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:54Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are skilled in the use of [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles") and gain certain benefits depending on which weapon you use.

**Prerequisites:** [Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Rifles) "Weapon Proficiency (Rifles)")

**Effect:** When you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") with a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") with which you are proficient, you gain one of the following benefits, depending on the kind of [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") you use.

- **[Blaster Carbine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Carbine "Blaster Carbine"):** You can [Brace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brace "Brace") a [Blaster Carbine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Carbine "Blaster Carbine") set to [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), even though it is not an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire")-only weapon.
- **[Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle"):** You treat the [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle") as an [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapon.
- **[Heavy Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Rifle "Heavy Blaster Rifle"):** When you use a [Heavy Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Blaster_Rifle "Heavy Blaster Rifle"), the damage dice increase from d10 to d12.
- **[Light Repeating Blaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Repeating_Blaster "Light Repeating Blaster"):** You treat the [Light Repeating Blaster](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Repeating_Blaster "Light Repeating Blaster") as if it were Medium instead of Large.
