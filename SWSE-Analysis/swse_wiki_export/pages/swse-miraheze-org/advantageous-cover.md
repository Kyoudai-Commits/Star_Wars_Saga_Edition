# Advantageous Cover

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advantageous_Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advantageous_Cover) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You know how to make the best use of available [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") as shelter from enemy attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

**Effect:** When you have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover"), you take no damage from [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks"), even if the attack roll exceeds your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

**Normal:** You take no damage from [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") when you have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") only if the attack roll misses.
