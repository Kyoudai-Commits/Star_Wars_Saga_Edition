# Crime Lord

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Few societies manage to eradicate crime altogether. In the underworld, someone always rises to the top, either through vision, organization, or sheer intimidation. The life of a Crime Lord isn't for the timid. After conquering an unseen empire, the Crime Lord wages a daily struggle not only to stay on top, but also to stay alive.

Of course, not every Crime Lord is bent on corrupting society and making a profit. Some use their criminal connections to wage lucrative guerrilla wars against tyrants and despots. Indeed, some of the lesser-known heroes of the Rebellion began as smugglers or pirates, and eventually rose through the ranks of their organization to provide a more noble direction for their groups' activities. Although some did so to remove an obstacle to making more money, others recognized that ridding the galaxy of the Empire's evil was a more important long-term goal.

Problematically, Crime Lords are notoriously bad at cooperating with one another. The head of any given criminal empire has as much to fear from a gang war as they do from internal dissent and ambitious underlings. Even those with altruistic intentions sometimes learn the hard way that their troops are more interested in gaining power and wealth than saving the galaxy from greater evils.

**Examples of Crime Lords in Star Wars:** Jabba the Hutt, Prince Xizor, Talon Karrde, Ploovo Two-for-One, Davik Kang, G0-T0.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Crime Lord, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** At least one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from either the
  - [Fortune Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortune_Talent_Tree "Fortune Talent Tree"),
  - [Lineage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lineage_Talent_Tree "Lineage Talent Tree"), or
  - [Misfortune Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Misfortune_Talent_Tree "Misfortune Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Crime Lords gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Crime Lords gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Crime Lords gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At 1st level and each level thereafter, a Crime Lord gains an additional [Crime Lord Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord_Talent "Crime Lord Talent"). A Crime Lord may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Crime Lord Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crime_Lord_Talent_Trees "Crime Lord Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Command Cover

Starting at 2nd level, a Crime Lord can use their allies to shield them from harm. You can gain a +1 [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") for each ally that is adjacent to you, up to a maximum bonus equal to one-half your Class Level (maximum +5 at 10th level).
