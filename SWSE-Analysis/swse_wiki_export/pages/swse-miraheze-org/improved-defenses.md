# Improved Defenses

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Defenses) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are skilled at fending off attacks of many forms.

**Effect:** You gain a +1 bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").

**Special:** This feat does not apply to the [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), even if you are [Piloting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piloting "Piloting") or [Commanding](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commanding "Commanding") it. This is one reason many [Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilots "Pilots") prefer the [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge") feat to Improved Defenses.
