# Unhindered Approach

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unhindered_Approach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unhindered_Approach) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies have trained on worlds where the terrain is unstable, and your team also specializes in vertical insertion into combat zones.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you use the [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") skill, add 1 square to your total jump distance.
