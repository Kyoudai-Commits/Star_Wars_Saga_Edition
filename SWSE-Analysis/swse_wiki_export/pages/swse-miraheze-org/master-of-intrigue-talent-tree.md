# Master of Intrigue Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Intrigue_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Intrigue_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are a skilled manipulator and have mastered the art of working behind the scenes to accomplish your goals. Flash and spectacle are inferior tools, only suitable for those without your unique talents.

## Core Talents

### Advanced Planning

When you roll [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") for combat, choose one willing ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). You and that ally swap [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") results.

### Blending In

You know how to blend into a variety of cultures and groups with ease. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you gain [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment") when adjacent to at least two other creatures. This benefit does not apply to attacks from adjacent creatures.

### Done It All

When you select this Talent, choose two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") (from any non-[Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class")) that you do not possess but for which you meet the prerequisites. Once per turn on your turn, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") to gain the benefits of one of those [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") until the end of your next turn.

### Get Into Position

Once per encounter, at the start of your first turn, choose two allies within 12 squares and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Each ally can immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Master Manipulations

*Prerequisites:* **Advanced Planning**, **Get Into Position**

You are a master of making things happen the way you want them to. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on your turn:

- *Demand Recovery:* Select one ally within 5 squares and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). That ally moves +5 steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") and gains a +2 morale bonus to attack rolls and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") until the end of your next turn.
- *Exceptional Control:* Roll a d20 and note the result. Once before the end of the encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can replace the result of an enemy's or ally's d20 roll with the result you rolled for this ability. The enemy or ally must be within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").
- *Word of Warning:* Select one ally within 5 squares and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Once before the end of the encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to that ally having any [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") targeted by a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") or an attack, you can replace that ally's [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") with your same [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") until the attack or the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") is resolved.

### Retaliation

*Prerequisite:* **Advanced Planning**

Whenever you move down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") as a result of taking damage that equals or exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), the next time you hit and damage a creature or a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") before the end of your next turn, you automatically move the target -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
