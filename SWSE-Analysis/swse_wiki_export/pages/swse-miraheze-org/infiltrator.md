# Infiltrator

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltrator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltrator) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Infiltrators come in many guises, such as spies, moles, special forces operatives, and secret agents. Infiltrators specialize in secretly penetrating enemy strongholds or organizations and carrying out their missions from within.

Infiltrators typically operate individually or in small groups. Their training emphasizes stealth, discreet combat, and covert penetration techniques. They typically have less combat training than [Elite Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Troopers "Elite Troopers") and are better trained and more mission-specific than [Saboteurs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saboteurs "Saboteurs"). Covert Infiltrators operate for extended periods of time within an organization. Aggressive Infiltrators carry out specific combat missions, on their own or in support of commandos or other military units. [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan") Infiltrators are highly effective and a critical part of [The Bothan SpyNet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Bothan_SpyNet "The Bothan SpyNet"). They use the network as much as the network uses them. They gain access to network resources, while they are a resource to the network in and of themselves. They use this arrangement to their advantage whenever possible.

In a [Dark Times Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Times_Campaign "Dark Times Campaign"), Imperial operatives of all sorts might be Infiltrators. Covert spies or ISB agents work to discern any hint of sedition, rebellion, or simply dissent within the New Order. Military Infiltrators are deployed where dissenters are well armed, or to make a public statement. Anti-Imperial operatives might secretly work for dissident Senators, trying to penetrate the workings of the plethora of new organizations that have sprung up within the new Imperial government.

**Examples of Infiltrators in Star Wars:** Lt. Judder Page, Sabé Reccen, Shira Brie, Winter, Bothan SpyNet Operatives.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become an Infiltrator, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Skill Focus (Stealth)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Stealth) "Skill Focus (Stealth)")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** Any two [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from either the
  - [Camouflage Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Camouflage_Talent_Tree "Camouflage Talent Tree") or
  - [Spy Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spy_Talent_Tree "Spy Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Infiltrators gain a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Infiltrators gain 1d8 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Infiltrators gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), an Infiltrator gains an additional [Infiltrator Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltrator_Talent "Infiltrator Talent"). An Infiltrator may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Infiltrator Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infiltrator_Talent_Trees "Infiltrator Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Unarmed Stun

Starting at 2nd level, an Infiltrator can use their [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") to deal [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage. The Infiltrator must designate their intention to [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") their target before the attack is made, and the Infiltrator deals +1 die of damage with their [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), which deals [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage. At 6th level, this extra damage increases to +2 dice, and at 10th level it increases to +3 dice.

### Lead Infiltrator

Starting at 4th level, an Infiltrator becomes an effective leader of infiltration teams. The Infiltrator can make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check for a number of allies within their [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") equal to their [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus (minimum 1), using the check result in place of their allies' [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. At 8th level, the Infiltrator doubles the number of allies they can lead using this ability. Allies must stay within the Infiltrator's [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to retain this bonus.
