# Dark Side Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The path to [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is the quick and easy path, granting amazing power but forever dominating the destinies of those in its grasp. You must have a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") or 1 or higher to select Talents from this Talent Tree; if your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") is ever reduced to 0, you lose access to all Talents in this Talent Tree until your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") increases.

## Core Talents

### Power of the Dark Side

You allow your hatred to fuel your attacks. Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to modify an attack roll, you may choose to roll an additional bonus die and take the best result. However, doing so increases your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1. For example, a 1st-level character with this talent could increase their [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1 to roll 2d6 instead of 1d6, taking the best die result and applying it as a bonus to their attack roll.

### Dark Presence

*Prerequisites:* **Power of the Dark Side**, [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you grant yourself and all allies within 6 squares of you a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the end of the encounter. These bonuses are lost if you fall unconscious or die. Affected allies that move out of range lose the benefits for as long as they remain out of range.

### Revenge

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Presence**

Whenever an ally of equal or higher level than you is killed or reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls until the end of the encounter. (Since [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonuses do not stack, you don't get a higher bonus if more than one ally falls in the same encounter.)

### Swift Power

*Prerequisite:* **Power of the Dark Side**

Once per day, you can use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that normally takes a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") or a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

## Additional Talents

### Consumed by Darkness

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Sometimes your anger consumes you. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can take a -5 penalty to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") to gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on attack rolls. The penalty and bonus last until the beginning of your next turn.

### Dark Preservation

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Power of the Dark Side**

Whenever an effect would normally move you down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") and increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1 to negate that movement down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

### Dark Side Savant

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can return one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") without spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select it, you can use it one additional time per encounter.

### Drain Knowledge

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to drain the knowledge of an opponent you touch; you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC equal to the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense")) to activate this Talent, and if you fail to activate this Talent, you may not try again on the same target for one day. If successful, you immediately become [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in one [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") that the target is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in; if you are already [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"), you instead gain [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill"). This effect lasts for one day. Additionally, your target moves -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), and the [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") can be removed only by resting for 8 hours.

Additionally, you can choose to instead sift through the mind of the target, looking for a specific piece of knowledge or information. Doing so requires you to make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check against the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"); success indicates that you pull the relevant information from the target's mind. When you do so, you do not gain the bonus [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") or [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") normally granted by this Talent, but otherwise the Talent functions as normal. Using this Talent increases your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1.

### Transfer Essence

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equal to [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") Score

When you die, you become a [Dark Side Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Spirit "Dark Side Spirit") until the end of the encounter. You continue to occupy a space in this form, but other creatures can occupy the same space or move through your space without impediment. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can attempt to possess an adjacent target. You must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against an adjacent target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you deal 8d6 points of [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage to the target; if you reduce the target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or move it to the bottom of the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") with this attack, you possess the target as though it were a willing host.

Alternatively, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can transfer your essence into a single adjacent object, such as a [Holocron](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Holocron "Holocron") or a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). If you do so, you lie dormant within the object until another creature attempts to use that object, at which time you can emerge and attempt to possess the creature, as described above. If you do not possess a creature or object within 10 rounds of manifesting as a [Dark Side Spirit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Spirit "Dark Side Spirit"), your spirit dissipates and ceases to exist.

### Wrath of the Dark Side

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Power of the Dark Side**

When you roll a Natural 20 on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that directly deals damage to a target, you can choose not to regain all of your spent [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") as normal and instead the targets damaged by the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") take half that damage again at the start of their next turn. Only Force Powers that directly damage the target are subject to this Talent, including *[Corruption](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Corruption "Corruption")*, *[Force Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast "Force Blast")*, *[Force Grip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Grip "Force Grip")*, *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")*, *[Force Slam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Slam "Force Slam")*, *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* (only when spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point")), and *[Repulse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repulse "Repulse")* (only when spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point")).
