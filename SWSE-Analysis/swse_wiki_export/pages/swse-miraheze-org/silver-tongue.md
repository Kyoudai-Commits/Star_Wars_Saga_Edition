# Silver Tongue

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Silver_Tongue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Silver_Tongue) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You are adept at quickly changing a creature's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") toward you.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

**Effect:** You can [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") or [Change Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Change_Attitude "Change Attitude") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

**Normal:** To [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") or [Change Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Change_Attitude "Change Attitude") requires a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").
