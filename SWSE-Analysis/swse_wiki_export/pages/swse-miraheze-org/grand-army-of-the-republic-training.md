# Grand Army of the Republic Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Army_of_the_Republic_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Army_of_the_Republic_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have received basic training from the Grand Army of the Republic and know how to turn even bulky armor into an asset.

**Prerequisite:** [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)")

**Effect:** If you wear [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") that provides an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and you are proficient with that [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), you also apply the Armor's [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
