# Unhindered Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unhindered_Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unhindered_Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You move quick to your opponent, ignoring [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and obstacles.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") towards an enemy, ignoring [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and low obstacles. If you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your enemy, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 20 | You can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +2 squares, towards an enemy, ignoring [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and low obstacles. If you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your enemy, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 25 | You can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +4 squares, towards an enemy, ignoring [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and low obstacles. If you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your enemy, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |
| 30 | You can move up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +6 squares, towards an enemy, ignoring [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and low obstacles. If you end your movement within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") of your enemy, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against that target. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Sokan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sokan "Sokan")):** If you have the **[Sokan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sokan "Sokan")** Talent, you gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") made against you during this movement.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to move an additional 2 squares.
