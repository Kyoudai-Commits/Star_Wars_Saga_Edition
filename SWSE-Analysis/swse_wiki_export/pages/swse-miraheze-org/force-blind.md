# Force Blind

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You hurl dirt, dust, and debris at your foe, affecting its sight.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** One creature within 12 squares of you, and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Make one roll and compare the result to the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the result equals or exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"), and all other creatures have [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") from your target until the start of your next turn. Creatures that do not rely on eyesight to perceive (such as [Miraluka](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Miraluka "Miraluka")) are immune to this effect.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), rather than a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
