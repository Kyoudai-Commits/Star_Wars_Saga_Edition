# Implant Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You have acclimated your mind and body to the presence of a cybernetic implant.

**Prerequisite:** Must possess an [Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant "Implant")

**Effect:** You are not moved an extra step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when you would normally be moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").

**Normal:** Characters with an [Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Implant "Implant") move one extra step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") for any reason, and take a -2 penalty to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") without this feat.
