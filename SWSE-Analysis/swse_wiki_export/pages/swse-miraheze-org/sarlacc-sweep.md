# Sarlacc Sweep

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sarlacc_Sweep](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sarlacc_Sweep) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You lash out at multiple enemies, striking at them with a sweeping motion.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") (the primary target), one other enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") (the secondary target).

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the primary target. The attack does normal damage to the primary target and 5 points of damage to the secondary target if the attack hits. |
| 20 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the primary target. The attack does normal damage to the primary target and 5 +1d6 points of damage to the secondary target if the attack hits. |
| 25 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the primary target. The attack does normal damage to the primary target and 5 +2d6 points of damage to the secondary target if the attack hits. |
| 30 | You can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against the primary target. The attack does normal damage to the primary target and 5 +3d6 points of damage to the secondary target if the attack hits. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Shii-Cho](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shii-Cho "Shii-Cho")):** If you have the **[Shii-Cho](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shii-Cho "Shii-Cho")** Talent, you can use this Form Power in place of a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"). You gain the benefits of the [Powerful Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge "Powerful Charge") feat on the initial attack only if you use this Form Power in that way.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to affect two secondary targets, provided both enemies are within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach") and adjacent to the primary target of your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack.
