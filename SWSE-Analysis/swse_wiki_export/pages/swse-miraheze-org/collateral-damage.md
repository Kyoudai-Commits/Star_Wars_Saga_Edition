# Collateral Damage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collateral_Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collateral_Damage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Your hail of fire can catch secondary targets in your firing arc.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +6, [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot")

**Effect:** When you deal damage with a single, non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") using the [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") feat, once per turn on your turn, you can immediately make a second attack at a -2 penalty against a second target within 2 squares of the first. If this attack hits, you deal half the original attack's damage to that target.
