# Naval Officer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naval_Officer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naval_Officer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are skilled at commanding [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") and small fleets.

## Core Talents

### Combined Fire

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you may designate a single creature, [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or object within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") as the target of **Combined Fire**. Any [Weapon Batteries](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Batteries "Weapon Batteries") attacking the target deal an extra die of damage for every 2 points by which their attack roll exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") (instead of every 3 points).

In addition, when using the [Tactical Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tactical_Fire "Tactical Fire") option for a [Capital Ship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ship "Capital Ship"), you may designated a weapon or [Weapon Battery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Battery "Weapon Battery") to make a single attack.

### Fleet Deployment

*Prerequisite:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

As a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can designate a number of [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") equal to your [Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officer "Officer") Class Level and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). Those [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") may immediately move a number of squares equal to their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

### Fleet Tactics

*Prerequisites:* [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, **Fleet Deployment**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you may designate a single [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") as the target of a large-scale assault. If you succeed on a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check, all allied [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") deal 1 additional die of damage to the target with each successful [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") until the start of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### It's a Trap!

You are skilled at sensing the plans of enemy naval [Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officers "Officers"), and counteracting them. Once per encounter as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can grant the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of any single [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") (including a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you are commanding) an immediate [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

### Legendary Commander

*Prerequisites:* [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13, [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13, **[Born Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Born_Leader "Born Leader")**

When you are the [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander") of a [Capital Ship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ship "Capital Ship"), calculate its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") using your Heroic Level plus one-half the ship's armor bonus (rounded down), the Vehicle Pilot's Heroic Level, or the ship's Armor bonus, whichever is highest.

In addition, all [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") on your ship add one-half your Heroic Level, or one-half their Heroic Level, whichever is more, to damage rolls with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"). Finally, you treat any generic crew as being one quality level higher (maximum of Ace, see [Crew Quality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew_Quality "Crew Quality")).
