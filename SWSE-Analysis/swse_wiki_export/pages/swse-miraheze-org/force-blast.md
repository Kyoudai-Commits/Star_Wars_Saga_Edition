# Force Blast

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Blast) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to create a ball of compressed air and debris that you can hurl at enemy targets.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of the target; if you exceed the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you deal damage with the *Force Blast*. The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The *Force Blast* deals 2d6 points of damage to the target. |
| 20 | The *Force Blast* deals 3d6 points of damage to the target. |
| 25 | The *Force Blast* deals 4d6 points of damage to the target. |
| 30 | The *Force Blast* deals 5d6 points of damage to the target. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add one-half your Heroic Level as a damage bonus on a *Force Blast* just as you would with a normal weapon.
