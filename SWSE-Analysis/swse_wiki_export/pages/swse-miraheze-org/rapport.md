# Rapport

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapport](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapport) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:39Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at working with certain individuals, able to anticipate their next move.

**Effect:** When using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action, you grant an additional +2 insight bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") and attack rolls to the character you are assisting. This bonus does not stack with any bonus provided by the Noble's **[Coordinate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinate "Coordinate")** Talent.
