# Staggering Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Staggering_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Staggering_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:42Z.

Scum and VillainyGalaxy at War

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can deliver nasty attacks that leave your opponents gasping.

**Prerequisite:** **[Sneak Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak_Attack "Sneak Attack")** or [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") or [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike")

**Effect:** Any time you would deal additional damage from a [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") or [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") that grants one or more extra dice of damage, you can forgo the extra damage to move the target 2 squares per extra die sacrificed. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You can deliver punishing blows to your enemies, distracting them and keeping them from focusing their efforts.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When using a melee weapon with which you are proficient, you can make an attack that disrupts an enemy's concentration. You take a -2 penalty on your attack roll, and if your attack deals damage, the target takes a -2 penalty to [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") until the end of your next turn. Alternatively, you can take a -5 penalty to your attack roll, and if your attack deals damage, the target takes a -5 penalty to [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") until the end of your next turn.
