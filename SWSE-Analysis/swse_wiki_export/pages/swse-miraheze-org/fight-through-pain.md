# Fight Through Pain

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Through_Pain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Through_Pain) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Through sheer willpower, you can keep yourself at peak fighting performance, ignoring physical pain and negative effects.

**Effect:** You can use your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") instead of your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") when determining your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").
