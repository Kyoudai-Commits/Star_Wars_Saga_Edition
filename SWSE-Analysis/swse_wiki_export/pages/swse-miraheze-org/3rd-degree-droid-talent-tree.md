# 3rd-Degree Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

[3rd-Degree Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/3rd-Degree_Droids "3rd-Degree Droids") are typically Protocol Droids and Replica Droids. Only they can select Talents from this Droid Talent Tree.

## Core Talents

### Etiquette

When you succeed on a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to change a character's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"), you adjust the target's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") by one additional step.

### Helpful Droid

Once per turn you can use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist any adjacent ally on a [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action").

### Protocol

You always succeed on attempts to [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") on [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge"), and [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks (no roll required).

## Additional Talents

### Nuanced

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You are skilled in the nuances of diplomatic speech and gestures. Once per encounter, you can add your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus to a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check in addition to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus.

### Observant

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You enhance your persuasiveness by applying data obtained through observation. Whenever you would fail a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check, you can roll a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), with a DC equal to the DC of the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check. If you succeed, add +5 to the original [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check result.

### Supervising Droid

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisites:* **Observant**, Any Talent from the [Influence Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Influence_Talent_Tree "Influence Talent Tree"), [Inspiration Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspiration_Talent_Tree "Inspiration Talent Tree"), or [Leadership Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leadership_Talent_Tree "Leadership Talent Tree")

You are programmed to oversee other Droids. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter:

- *Combat Support*: As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you automatically [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") on an allied Droid's attack roll, provided you are capable of using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to assist that ally. If you also have [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat, you increase the bonus provided by the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action from +2 to +3.
- *Director*: As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you automatically [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Droid with a [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in. If you also have [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") in that skill, you increase the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") bonus from +2 to +5.
- *Instant Action*: As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you grant one ally a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") that it can use immediately as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

### Talkdroid

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion")

You know how to subtly massage translations. When one of your allies is attempting to use the [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") skill to change the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of a creature that does not understand your ally, you add +2 to the ally's [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check results if you perform the translation.
