# Sever Force

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sever_Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sever_Force) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can block another Force-user's access to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), preventing them from spending [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") and making it difficult for them to use [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers").

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One Force-using creature with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 1 or higher that is within 12 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). (This [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") has no effect on targets with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") of 0.)

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the effect (if any) is determined by your check result:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 25 | The target cannot spend [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") for a number of hours equal to its [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"). |
| 30 | The target cannot spend [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") for a number of hours equal to its [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"). In addition, the target moves -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") each time they use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") in the same time frame. |
| 35 | The target cannot spend [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") for a number of hours equal to its [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"). In addition, the target moves -2 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") each time they use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") in the same time frame. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to double the duration of the effect. Alternatively, you can spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to increase the duration to a number of days equal to the target's [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score").
