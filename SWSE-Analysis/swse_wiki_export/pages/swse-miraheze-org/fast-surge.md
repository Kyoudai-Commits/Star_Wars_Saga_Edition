# Fast Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fast_Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fast_Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can return to the fight in the blink of an eye, giving your enemies no time to find an opening.

**Effect:** On your turn, you can catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") instead of a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
