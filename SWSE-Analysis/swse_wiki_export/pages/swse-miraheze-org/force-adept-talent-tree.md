# Force Adept Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adept_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:05Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[Force Adepts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Adepts "Force Adepts") use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to survive on fringe worlds, and they often have signature [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") that they use particularly well.

## Core Talents

### Force Power Adept

You are skilled at using a particular [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). Select one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") you know. When using that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), you have the option of spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to make two [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks, keeping the better result. This Talent may be selected multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you select this Talent, you must choose a different [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").

### Force Treatment

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill. If you are entitled to a [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check reroll, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). In addition, you can administer [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid"), [Treat Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Disease "Treat Disease"), [Treat Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Poison "Treat Poison"), and [Treat Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Radiation "Treat Radiation") without the requisite [Medical Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Kit "Medical Kit") or [Medpac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac "Medpac").

### Fortified Body

*Prerequisite:* **[Equilibrium](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equilibrium "Equilibrium")**

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") shields you against ailments, toxins, and radiation poisoning, making you immune to [Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disease "Disease"), [Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison "Poison"), and [Radiation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Radiation "Radiation").

## Additional Talents

### Instrument of the Force

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You are particularly in tune with [The Living Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Living_Force "The Living Force"). When you successfully use [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings"), you gain a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") that must be used before the end of the encounter. If you use this [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") in a manner that would end in unfavorable results (per [Search Your Feelings](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Search_Your_Feelings "Search Your Feelings")), you raise your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 1. If you use the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") in an action that would normally raise your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"), you raise your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") by 2 instead.

### Long Call

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Mystical Link**

When using the [Telepathy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telepathy "Telepathy") ability of [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force"), you reduce the DC of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check by half, as do those Force-users for whom you are a willing telepathic recipient. When attempting to contact an unwilling target, you can reroll and take the better result. By spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), you can simultaneously contact a number of targets equal to your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier (minimum two) with a single [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.

### Mystical Link

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") guides you in unexpected ways. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a DC 30 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If the check is successful, you gain one of the following benefits, as selected by the Gamemaster:

- One [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is returned to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").
- Gain one [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") that is lost if it is not spent before the end of the encounter.
- Gain an additional use of a Force-related [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") or [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") normally restricted to once per encounter.
- Roll an additional die when making a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and select the highest die rolled.
