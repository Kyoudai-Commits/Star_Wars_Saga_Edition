# Linguist

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Linguist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Linguist) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You pick up [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") quickly and easily.

**Prerequisite:** [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13

**Effect:** You gain a number of additional [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") equal to 1 + your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 1).

**Special:** You can take this feat more than once. Each time you take this feat, you gain a number of additional [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") equal to 1 + your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 1). If your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier increases so does the number of [Languages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Languages "Languages") granted by this feat.
