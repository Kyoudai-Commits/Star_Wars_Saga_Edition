# Overwhelming Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Overwhelming_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Overwhelming_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You know how to get your attacks past enemy defenses, making sure they strike true.

**Effect:** You must spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") in the same round to activate this feat. If your target attempts to use a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"), [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), or other ability to negate your attack (such as **[Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Block "Block")**, **[Deflect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflect "Deflect")**, or [Vehicular Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicular_Combat "Vehicular Combat")), the target takes a -5 penalty to any attack rolls or [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") it makes to negate the attack. This effect applies to the next attack you make before the end of the same round.
