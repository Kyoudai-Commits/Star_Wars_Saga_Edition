# Survivor Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survivor_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survivor_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

As an explorer of dangerous places, you are trained to react to danger swiftly and adroitly, as well as navigate [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), and reduce damage.

## Core Talents

### Evasion

If you are hit by an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"), you take half damage if the attack hits you. If the area attack misses you, you take no damage. If you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"), this talent applies to your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

### Extreme Effort

You can spend two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to gain a +5 bonus on a single [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check or [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength")-based [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") made during the same round.

### Sprint

When you use the [Run](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run "Run") Action, you can move up to five times your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") (instead of up to four times).

### Surefooted

Your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is not reduced by [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain").

### Wilderness Medic

*Wilderness Medic Talent created by Miraheze user Chellewalker.*

You can use your [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check modifier instead of your [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") modifier when making [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks. If you are entitled to an [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check reroll, you can reroll your [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). You are considered to be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury").
