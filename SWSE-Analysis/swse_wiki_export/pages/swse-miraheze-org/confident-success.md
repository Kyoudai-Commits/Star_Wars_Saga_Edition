# Confident Success

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Confident_Success](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Confident_Success) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have confidence in your ability to learn information that others consider secret, and in doing so, boost your confidence in other areas.

**Prerequisite:** [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan")

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully use the [Learn Secret Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Learn_Secret_Information "Learn Secret Information") application of the [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") skill, you gain 1 [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). You cannot gain more than 3 [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") each level, and you cannot have more [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") than you gained when you reached your current level.
