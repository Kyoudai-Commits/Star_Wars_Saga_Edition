# Detonate

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Detonate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Detonate) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can perceive points of weakness within an object and use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to telekinetically press on one of those points, shattering the object.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One unattended object within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The object takes 4d6 points of damage. |
| 25 | The object takes 6d6 points of damage. |
| 30 | The object takes 8d6 points of damage. |
| 35 | The object takes 10d6 points of damage. |

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to increase the damage dealt to the object by +2d6.
