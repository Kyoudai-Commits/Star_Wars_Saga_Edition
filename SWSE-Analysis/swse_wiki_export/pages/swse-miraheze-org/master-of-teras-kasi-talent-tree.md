# Master of Teräs Käsi Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

Teräs Käsi is a powerful and deadly martial arts form that enables its wielder to deal devastating amounts of [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage.

## Core Talents

### Teräs Käsi Basics

*Prerequisite:* [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

You deal an additional die of damage with your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks").

### Ignore Damage Reduction

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

When you make an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against a target that has [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), and you deal more damage than the target's DR, you ignore the target's DR completely.

### Teräs Käsi Mastery

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II"), [Martial Arts III](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_III "Martial Arts III")

If you make only [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") during a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you can take the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Can a character who has the **Teräs Käsi Mastery** Talent and the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") Feat combine these with the [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") Feat? Specifically, could I use two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to get the benefit of [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") and then use my [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to make a [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")? If so, would the extra die of damage from [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") apply to both attacks from the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") or only to the first attack?

**A:** Yes, you can use **Teräs Käsi Mastery**, [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack"), and [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") at the same time, using two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") for [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") and then your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") for an unarmed [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack").

However, [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") specifies that its extra damage applies only to your *next* attack in the same round, so the second attack from [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") (or any other source of extra attacks) would not gain this benefit.

### Unarmed Counterstrike

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, **Unarmed Parry**, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II")

When you successfully parry a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with the **Unarmed Parry** Talent, you can immediately make an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") against that target.

### Unarmed Parry

*Prerequisites:* **Teräs Käsi Basics**, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), [Martial Arts II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_II "Martial Arts II")

When you [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively"), as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") you can negate a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") by making a successful [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack roll. If your attack roll equals or exceeds the attack roll of the incoming [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), the attack is negated. You must be aware of the attack, and not be [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed"), and you take a cumulative -2 penalty to all attack rolls for each attack roll made since the beginning of your last turn.
