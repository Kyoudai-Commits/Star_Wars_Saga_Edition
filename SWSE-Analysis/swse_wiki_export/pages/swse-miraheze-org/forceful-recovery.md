# Forceful Recovery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forceful_Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forceful_Recovery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is strong with you, and it gives you focus and vitality.

**Prerequisites:** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

**Effect:** Whenever you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), choose one expended [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") and return that power to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").
