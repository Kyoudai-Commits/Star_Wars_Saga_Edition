# Trandoshan

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandoshan) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/e/e5/Trandoshan.webp/250px-Trandoshan.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Trandoshan.webp)

The reptilian Trandoshans are known for their great strength, and warlike natures. Many of these beings dedicate themselves to martial training, and some follow the path of the hunter on their native world. A few have even become renowned (or infamous) bounty hunters in galactic society. Trandoshans have a long-standing enmity with [Wookiees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiees "Wookiees"), and the two [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") have fought often over the centuries.

Trandoshans (who refer to themselves as "T'doshok") have supersensitive eyes that can see into the infrared range. They shed their skin several times in the span of their lives and can even regenerate lost limbs, but their clawed hands have difficulty with fine manipulation of objects.

## Trandoshan Characteristics

**Personality:** Violent, brutal, and driven, Trandoshans love to compete but can show compassion and mercy as the situation warrants.

**Physical Description:** Trandoshans range from 1.8 to 2.1 meters tall and typically weigh 80 kilograms. Their scaly hides offer additional defense against attacks.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Trandoshans age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-11 Years | 12-14 Years | 15-34 Years | 35-49 Years | 50-59 Years | 60+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The jungle world of [Trandosha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandosha "Trandosha").

**Languages:** Trandoshans speak, read, and write both Dosh and Basic.

**Example Names:** Bossk, Fusset, Krussk, Ssuurg, Tusserk.

**Adventurers:** A Trandoshan adventurer craves the thrill of battle. Some leave [Trandosha](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trandosha "Trandosha") to become bodyguards or mercenaries. Others set out to find new places to hunt and explore. A few use their warrior traditions to become [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers"), some even developing into [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") as time goes by.

## Trandoshan Species Traits

Trandoshans share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Trandoshans receive a +2 bonus to their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). Trandoshans are powerful but lack agility.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Trandoshans have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Trandoshans have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Bonus Feat:** Rugged and resilient, Trandoshans receive the [Toughness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toughness "Toughness") feat at 1st level as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Darkvision:** Trandoshans ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. However, they cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
- **Limb Regeneration:** A Trandoshan regrows a lost limb in 1d10 days. At the end of that time, all [Persistent Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Conditions "Persistent Conditions") associated with the loss of the limb are removed.
- **Natural Armor:** Trandoshans have thick scales that provide a +1 Natural Armor bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). A Natural Armor bonus stacks with an Armor bonus.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Trandoshans can speak, read, and write both Basic and Dosh.
