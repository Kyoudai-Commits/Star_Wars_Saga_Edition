# Rising Whirlwind

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rising_Whirlwind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rising_Whirlwind) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You swing your [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") around your body, creating a brilliant whirlwind of deadly light.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Two enemies within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks, each against a different target. If both attacks hit, you gain 10 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points"). Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any remaining [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") disappear at the end of the encounter. |
| 20 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks, each against a different target. If both attacks hit, you gain 15 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points"). Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any remaining [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") disappear at the end of the encounter. |
| 25 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks, each against a different target. If both attacks hit, you gain 20 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points"). Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any remaining [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") disappear at the end of the encounter. |
| 30 | You can make two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") melee attacks, each against a different target. If both attacks hit, you gain 25 [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points"). Damage is subtracted from [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any remaining [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") disappear at the end of the encounter. |

**Lightsaber Form ([Jar'Kai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jar%27Kai "Jar'Kai")):** If you have the **[Jar'Kai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jar%27Kai "Jar'Kai")** Talent, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to 2 squares between the two attacks. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"),

**Special:** You must be wielding two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") to use this Form Power. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal +1d6 damage to each target you hit.
