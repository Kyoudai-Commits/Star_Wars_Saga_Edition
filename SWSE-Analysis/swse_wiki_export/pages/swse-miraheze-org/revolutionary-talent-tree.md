# Revolutionary Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revolutionary_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You seek to overthrow the system and are adept at being a monkey wrench in the cogs of society, but often at the cost of others' rights, freedoms, and lives.

## Core Talents

### Bomb Thrower

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

You are skilled in making and handling impromptu [Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosives "Explosives"). You gain a +5 bonus to [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks to [Handle Explosives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Handle_Explosives "Handle Explosives"). In addition, you can spend a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to craft the equivalent of a [Frag Grenade](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frag_Grenade "Frag Grenade") from spare parts you have on hand. You must have access to the appropriate supplies, such as an old blaster, a [Tool Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Kit "Tool Kit"), or materials found inside a hangar bay.

### For the Cause

*Prerequisite:* **Make an Example**

Whenever you or an ally within 6 squares of you takes damage that exceeds that character's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you and all allies within 6 squares of you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls until the end of your next turn.

### Make an Example

Whenever you hit with an attack and deal enough damage to exceed a target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), that target takes a -5 penalty to attack rolls against you until the end of your next turn. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Revolutionary Rhetoric

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can do or say something that causes an enemy to doubt its motives. Choose one enemy within 12 squares and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), and make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the target’s [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you succeed, the target can take only [Move Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Actions "Move Actions") and [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") until the end of your next turn. This effect ends if you attack the target. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
