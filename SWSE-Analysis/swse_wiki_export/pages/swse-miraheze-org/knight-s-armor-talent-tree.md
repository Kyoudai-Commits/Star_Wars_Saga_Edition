# Knight's Armor Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knight's_Armor_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knight's_Armor_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You are a skilled master of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") who knows how to get the most out of your [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").

## Core Talents

### Armored Augmentation I

*Prerequisite:* Proficient with Armor

Once per encounter, you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to augment your own ability to withstand damage by imbuing the [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") you are wearing with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). This allows you to add your Armor bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") to your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") until the end of the encounter.

### Armored Augmentation II

*Prerequisites:* **Armored Augmentation I**, Proficient with Armor

Whenever you use the **Armored Augmentation I** Talent, you also gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") equal to 2 x your Armor's [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").

### Cortosis Defense

You are adept at using a [Cortosis Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlet "Cortosis Gauntlet") to parry [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attacks. You gain a +2 bonus when making an opposed [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") melee attack roll against a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack.

### Cortosis Retaliation

*Prerequisite:* **Cortosis Defense**

Whenever you successfully use a [Cortosis Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlet "Cortosis Gauntlet") to parry an attack made with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you may make an immediate [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against the attacker.

### Knight Armor Mastery

*Prerequisite:* Proficient with Armor

When calculating your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you may add your Heroic Level plus one-half your Armor bonus (rounded down), or your Armor bonus alone, whichever is higher. This Talent counts as both the **[Armored Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armored_Defense "Armored Defense")** Talent and the **[Improved Armored Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Armored_Defense "Improved Armored Defense")** Talent for the purposes of prerequisites.
