# Sure Climber

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sure_Climber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sure_Climber) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can navigate steep cliffs and caverns as though climbing were second nature to you.

**Prerequisite:** [Sullustan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullustan "Sullustan")

**Effect:** Whenever you are not distracted or threatened, you gain a natural Climb Speed of 4 squares.

**Normal:** Without a Climb Speed, you must succeed on a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check to climb.
