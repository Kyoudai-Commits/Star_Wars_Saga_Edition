# Battering Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battering_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battering_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You know how to use your strength to batter an opponent to the ground, knocking him or her [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone").

**Prerequisites:** [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush"), [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip")

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully move a creature with the [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") feat, you also knock that target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone").
