# Brigand Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brigand_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brigand_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:18Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You use your experience operating on the wrong side of the law to give you an advantage in a fight, fair or otherwise.

## Core Talents

### Cheap Trick

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

When you make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check to [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") against an enemy within 6 squares of you, you can roll twice, keeping the better of the two results.

### Easy Prey

*Prerequisite:* **Cheap Trick**

When you make an attack as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") and successfully hit an enemy, you can choose to reduce the damage you deal by half. That target is then denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against your attacks until the end of your next turn.

### Quick Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Cheap Trick**

During the initial round of combat, if you successfully damage an enemy who has not yet acted in the combat, you can make an immediate attack as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") against a different target within 6 squares of the first target.

### Sly Combatant

*Prerequisites:* **Cheap Trick**, **Easy Prey**

You quickly move about the battlefield, taking advantage of the chaos of battle. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *Distracting Injury:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against any enemy within your range. If the attack successfully hits, that enemy takes a -2 penalty to his or her attack rolls and damage rolls until the end of the encounter.
- *Make Them Bleed:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against any living creature within your range. If you successfully deal damage as a result of the attack, that enemy gains a [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") that can be removed only with a successful DC 25 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check to perform [Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgery "Surgery").
- *Strength in Numbers:* Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against an enemy who is adjacent to one or more of your allies. If you successfully hit that enemy, you gain a +2 bonus to damage for each ally that is adjacent to the target.
