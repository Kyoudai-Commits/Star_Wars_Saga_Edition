# Force Storm

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Storm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Storm) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:24Z.

Force Unleashed Campaign GuideJedi Academy Training Manual

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to create a swirling whirlwind of [Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side") energy around yourself.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*, *[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** You create a vortex of debris and dark energy around yourself that deals 2d6 points of damage to all adjacent targets at the end of your turn. The result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check determines the duration of the effect, if any.

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The *Force Storm* manifests for a duration of 2 rounds, following the user's movement. |
| 25 | The *Force Storm* manifests for a duration of 4 rounds, following the user's movement. |
| 30 | The *Force Storm* manifests for a duration of 6 rounds, following the user's movement. |
| 35 | The *Force Storm* manifests for a duration of 8 rounds, following the user's movement. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to deal an extra 2d6 points of damage with a *Force Storm.*

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can create a storm that draws upon [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), focusing its malicious intent on a certain area.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One designated area within 12 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** All squares within the *Force Storm* are filled with driving acid rain that provides [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment"), and deals 2d6 points of [Acid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acid "Acid") damage to any creature that enters the *Force Storm*, or begins its turn within the area. The *Force Storm* also imposes a -5 penalty on [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks to listen, as the *Force Storm* howls loudly. The *Force Storm* lasts until the end of your next turn. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"). The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You create a *Force Storm* that covers a 3x3 square area. At least one square within that area must be within the given range of this Force Power. |
| 25 | You create a *Force Storm* that covers a 4x4 square area. At least one square within that area must be within the given range of this Force Power. |
| 30 | You create a *Force Storm* that covers a 5x5 square area. At least one square within that area must be within the given range of this Force Power. |
| 35 | You create a *Force Storm* that covers a 6x6 square area. At least one square within that area must be within the given range of this Force Power. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the damage dealt by the acid rain by an additional +2d6 points of [Acid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acid "Acid") damage.

You can maintain *Force Storm* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Force Storm*[Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Force Storm*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Force Storm*.
