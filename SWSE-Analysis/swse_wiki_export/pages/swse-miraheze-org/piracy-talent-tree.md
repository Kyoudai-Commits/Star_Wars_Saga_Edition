# Piracy Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piracy_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Piracy_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You use devious tactics to capture ships and victims intact.

## Core Talents

### Bloodthirsty

You can perform a [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Whenever you successfully perform a [Coup de Grace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coup_de_Grace "Coup de Grace") Action and kill the target, all allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls for the duration of the encounter.

### Fight to the Death

*Prerequisite:* **Bloodthirsty**

Once per encounter, as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can fill your companions with renewed vigor. All allies within 6 squares of you heal damage equal to your Heroic Level.

### Keep Them Moving

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). If the attack hits, you deal no damage, but your target must [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") or [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") away from you on its next turn.

### Raider's Frenzy

Once per round, when one of your allies within 6 squares successfully damages a target, you grant all your allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") a bonus to damage rolls against that target equal to one-half your Class Level until the end of your next turn.

### Raider's Surge

Once per encounter, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") or [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check (your choice) against each enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If the check result equals or exceeds the enemy's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that enemy must [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") on its next Action or take a -1 penalty on its attack rolls until the end of the encounter. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.

### Savage Reputation

*Prerequisite:* **Bloodthirsty**

You have cultivated a **Savage Reputation**, and when you are recognized, you instill fear in your enemies. All opponents within 6 squares of you take a -1 penalty to all their attack rolls. This is a Mind-Affecting Fear effect.

### Take Them Alive

Whenever you or any of your allies within 6 squares of you reduces a target to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you can choose to treat that opponent as though they had been reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") by [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun") damage (and thus, remain stable).
