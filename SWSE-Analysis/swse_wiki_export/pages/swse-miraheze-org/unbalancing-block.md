# Unbalancing Block

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unbalancing_Block](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unbalancing_Block) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You catch an opponent's weapon on your blade before deactivating your blade momentarily, causing them to stumble.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (when an adjacent enemy makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against you)

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack deals half damage on a hit. Additionally, if your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") until the end of your next turn.

**Lightsaber Form ([Trakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trakata "Trakata")):** If you have the **[Trakata](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trakata "Trakata")** Talent, you deal +2 dice of damage with the first attack you make on your next turn against your attacker.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to take no damage from the incoming attack if your check equals or exceeds the attack roll of the incoming attack.
