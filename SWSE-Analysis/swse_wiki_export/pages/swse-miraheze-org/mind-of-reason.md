# Mind of Reason

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_of_Reason](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind_of_Reason) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have learnt to consider all sides of an issue carefully before making a decision, allowing you to reason out information that may not be readily apparent.

**Prerequisite:** [Cerean](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cerean "Cerean")

**Effect:** You may use your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") bonus instead of your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") bonus for all [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence")-based [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks").
