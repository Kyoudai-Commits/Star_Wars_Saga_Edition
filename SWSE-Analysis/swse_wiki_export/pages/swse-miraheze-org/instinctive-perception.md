# Instinctive Perception

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instinctive_Perception) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can sense the presence of danger naturally, giving you and edge even when you fail to spot trouble the first time.

**Prerequisite:** [Zabrak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zabrak "Zabrak")

**Effect:** Whenever you reroll a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check and take the second result, if your second result is lower than your first result, you gain a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). This [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") can be spent only to add to the result of a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check, and if it is not spent before the end of the encounter, it is lost.
