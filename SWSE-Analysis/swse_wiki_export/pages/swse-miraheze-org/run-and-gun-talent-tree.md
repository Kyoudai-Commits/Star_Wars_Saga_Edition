# Run and Gun Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run_and_Gun_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Run_and_Gun_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels") find ways to slip through fights of any size, even when the odds are against them.

## Core Talents

### Cheap Shot

*Prerequisite:* **Opportunistic Strike**

Once per encounter, you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against an opponent that takes the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action to [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") from a space [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") by one of your allies within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

### No Escape

*Prerequisite:* **Opportunistic Strike**

Whenever an opponent uses the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action to leave your [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area"), that opponent is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you until the end of your next turn.

### Opportunistic Strike

Once per encounter, you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against an opponent within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") (even using a ranged weapon), if that opponent provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from one of your allies.

### Slippery Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Strike and Run**

Once per encounter, you can designate an opponent you have just damaged as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"); that opponent cannot make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") against you until the end of your next turn. You may use this in conjunction with the **Strike and Run** Talent, allowing you to benefit from both Talents as a single [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Strike and Run

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") after successfully damaging an opponent with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").
