# Desperate Gambit

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Desperate_Gambit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Desperate_Gambit) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You can draw upon your inner reserves to make a shot or strike count when it matters most.

**Effect:** Once per turn, when you miss on an attack roll, you reroll the attack, but you take a -2 penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn. If your first attack missed because you rolled a Natural 1, you can still use this feat, but you take a -5 penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") instead. You must accept the result of the second roll, even if it's worse than the first.
