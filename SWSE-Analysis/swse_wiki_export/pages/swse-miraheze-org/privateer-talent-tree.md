# Privateer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Privateer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Privateer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are a veteran of privateering and have learned the secrets of fighting aboard [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships").

## Core Talents

### Armored Spacer

You can use [Armored Space Suits](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armored_Space_Suits "Armored Space Suits") as if you had the [Armor Proficiency (Heavy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Heavy) "Armor Proficiency (Heavy)") feat.

### Attract Privateer

You attract a loyal Privateer Lieutenant. The Lieutenant is a [Nonheroic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nonheroic "Nonheroic") character who has a Class Level equal to three-quarters of your Character Level, rounded down.

You can select this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you gain another **Privateer**. Each **Privateer** who accompanies you on an adventure is entitled to an equal share of the total [Experience Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experience_Points "Experience Points") earned for the adventure. For example, a **Privateer** who accompanies a party of five heroes on an adventure receives one-sixth of the XP that the group earns.

### Blaster and Blade I

*Prerequisites:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Advanced_Melee_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)")

When you make a single attack with an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can immediately make an attack with a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), provided you have both the [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") and the [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol") in your hands when the melee attack is made. You apply the normal penalties for fighting with two weapons to both of these attacks.

### Blaster and Blade II

*Prerequisites:* **Blaster and Blade I**, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Advanced_Melee_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)")

When you are wielding both an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") and a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol"), you treat the [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") as though you were wielding it two-handed (including doubling your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus on damage rolls).

### Blaster and Blade III

*Prerequisites:* **Blaster and Blade I**, **Blaster and Blade II**, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Advanced_Melee_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)"), [Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Pistols) "Weapon Proficiency (Pistols)")

When you are wielding both an [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") and a [Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistol "Pistol"), you can make a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), provided you attack with both weapons.

### Boarder

You are skilled at boarding hostile vessels. You ignore [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") (but not [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover")) with your [Character Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Character_Scale "Character Scale") ranged attacks while aboard a [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship") or [Space Station](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Station "Space Station").

### Ion Mastery

You know the typical weaknesses of [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and you know how to preserve such targets for capture rather than destroying them. When attacking with [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") weapons, you gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and deal +1 die of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Advanced Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapon "Advanced Melee Weapon") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (see [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack")), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Preserving Shot

When you deal damage with a [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") that is equal to or greater than both the target Vehicle's current [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") and the target Vehicle's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") (that is, when you would deal enough damage to destroy the target [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle")), you can choose to use this Talent. Instead of dealing full damage, you instead deal half damage to your target and move it -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). In addition, you disable the ship's sublight engines and [Hyperdrive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrive "Hyperdrive"). The ship cannot [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") or make a jump to lightspeed until it receives repairs.
