# Category : Vehicles

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Vehicles) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:22Z.

The term "Vehicle" encompasses both Planetary Vehicles (such as Airspeeders, Landspeeders, and Walkers), and Starships (such as Starfighters, Space Transports, and Capital Ships). The major difference between Planetary Vehicles and Starships is that Planetary Vehicles generally do not travel in the vacuum of space. Rules that pertain only to Planetary Vehicles or Starships are called out specifically.

Vehicle StatisticsVehicle Statistics

Some statistics are exclusive to Vehicles, or require more elaboration:

- **Challenge Level (CL):** Heroes gain [Experience Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experience_Points "Experience Points") (XP) for disabling, destroying, or otherwise overcoming the Vehicle based on the Vehicle's CL. However, they don't gain additional XP for incidental crew or passengers killed as a result of the Vehicle's destruction.
  - For example, heroes who blow up a shuttle don't also receive XP for its crew; however, if the shuttle was transporting an Imperial Moff that the heroes were hired to eliminate, they should receive XP for the Imperial Moff as well.
- **Vehicle Initiative:** The Pilot's [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") modifier (or the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill modifier if you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained")) + Vehicle's size modifier + Vehicle's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier.
- **Perception:** Character's normal [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") modifier.
- **Reflex Defense:** The Vehicle's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") is equal to 10 + size modifier + Armor bonus (or Pilot's Heroic Level) + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus.
- **Fortitude Defense:** The Vehicle's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") is equal to 10 + [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus.
- **Damage Threshold:** The Vehicle's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is equal to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") + size modifier.
- **Maximum Velocity:** The Maximum Speed the Vehicle can achieve after using [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") for at least one full round. Maximum Velocity is never used in Starship Scale.
- **Attack Rolls:** Gunner's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier + miscellaneous bonuses ([Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus"), etc.) +2 (if [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") and firing [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")-controlled [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems") from the Pilot's position).
- **Base Attack Bonus:** The Gunner's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus").
- **Grapple:** The Vehicle's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") modifier is mainly used to resist attempts to restrain the Vehicle physically (usually via [Tractor Beam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tractor_Beam "Tractor Beam")). A Vehicle's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") modifier is equal to its Pilot's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + the Vehicle's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier + the Vehicle's special size modifier;
  - Large +5,
  - Huge +10,
  - Gargantuan +15,
  - Colossal +20,
  - Colossal (Frigate) +25,
  - Colossal (Cruiser) +30,
  - Colossal (Station) +35.
- **Fighting Space:** The [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") that the Vehicle occupies at Character Scale, Starship Scale, or both.
- **Cover:** The amount of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") the Vehicle grants to its crew and passengers, listed as none, +5 (Normal [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover")), +10 ([Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover")), or [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover").
- **Skills:** Character's appropriate [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") modifiers (+ Vehicle size modifier + Vehicle [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier in the case of the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") and [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skills).
  - If the ship's computer is Friendly or Helpful towards you, you also add the ship's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier as an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks.
- **Crew:** The number and quality of the crew (see [Crew Quality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew_Quality "Crew Quality")). The crew modifiers are already included in all other statistics in the Vehicle description.
- **Passengers:** The number of passengers (including troops) that the Vehicle can carry, in addition to its crew.
- **Cargo Capacity:** The amount of cargo the Vehicle can carry.
- **Carried Craft:** Other Vehicles that are usually carried aboard this Vehicle.
- **Payload:** The Vehicle's complement of Grenades, Rockets, Missiles, and Torpedoes. If this Vehicle has no Payload, this line is absent.
- **Hyperdrive:** The multiplier used when calculating hyperspace travel times (see [Astrogation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogation "Astrogation")). If the Starship has a backup Hyperdrive, its multiplier is listed in parentheses. Hyperdrive is never used in Character Scale. If the Vehicle has no Hyperdrive, this line is absent.
- **Availability:** Whether or not the Vehicle is a [Restricted Item](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted_Item "Restricted Item").

#### Planetary Vehicles

Planetary Vehicles are further subdivided into Ground Vehicles and Air Vehicles.

- **[Speeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeders "Speeders"):** Speeders are common sights on the technologically advanced worlds. Equipped with repulsorlift drive technology, they hover above the ground at a height of anywhere from a few centimeters, to several meters, and can achieve remarkable speeds. Speeders ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain").
- **[Tracked Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked_Vehicles "Tracked Vehicles"):** Tracked Vehicles have treads or tracks looped around a large number of wheels, providing great traction, but making them less maneuverable at higher speeds- and extremely loud at any speed. Tracked Vehicles ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), and take half damage from [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions") with obstacles at least one size category smaller than them.
- **[Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers"):** Walkers move about on two or more legs, literally walking over the terrain. Walkers retain their balance by means of finely tuned gyroscopes, and offer a fairly smooth ride. They rely on even footing, however, and when they topple, the results can be disastrous. Walkers ignore obstacles that are three or more sizes smaller than them.
- **[Wheeled Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled_Vehicles "Wheeled Vehicles"):** Wheeled Vehicles move on one or more wheels (most commonly two to four). Faster and more maneuverable than [Tracked Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked_Vehicles "Tracked Vehicles") or [Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers"), wheels are a cheap alternative to repulsorlift technology for light and fast Vehicles.
- **[Weapon Emplacements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Emplacements "Weapon Emplacements"):** Weapon Emplacements are a staple of ground warfare. They typically fall under two categories: antipersonnel and antivehicle. Weapon Emplacements are treated as Vehicles for statistical purposes, but they are usually immobile and do not require a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") to function. Instead, [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") control all the functions of the Weapon Emplacement.
- **[Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders"):** Airspeeders are repulsorlift Vehicles that can travel anywhere up to about 300 kilometers above ground level, but they are incapable of true space flight. Because they fly so high above the ground, they ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and obstacles. Of all Planetary Vehicles, Airspeeders are the most maneuverable. Airspeeders can also be used to form [Fighter Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighter_Groups "Fighter Groups").

#### Starships

Starships are Vehicles capable of interplanetary and interstellar travel. They are further subdivided into Starfighters, Space Transports, Capital Ships, and Space Stations.

- **[Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"):** Starfighters are small, agile Starships of Gargantuan size or smaller. Though they can travel in atmosphere, they excel at space combat. Starfighters can function with as few as one crewmember (the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")). Starfighters can also be used to form [Fighter Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighter_Groups "Fighter Groups").
- **[Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports"):** Space Transports are mid-sized Starships of Colossal or Colossal (Frigate) size, with fewer than 200 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Most Space Transports are designed for moving passengers or cargo, but some are used as gunships, drop ships, or assault shuttles.
- **[Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships"):** Capital Ships include all Starships of Colossal (Frigate) size or larger. Capital Ships usually carry hundreds, if not thousands of crewmembers and passengers, and some are large enough to house other Capital Ships within their hangar bays. Some very large transports and colony ships fall into this category, despite not being designed for combat.
- **[Space Stations](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Stations "Space Stations"):** Though not technically modes of transportation, Space Stations have statistics like other Starships. A Space Station can have a population in the thousands or millions, depending on its size. Most Space Stations are immobile, [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star") being a notable exception.
