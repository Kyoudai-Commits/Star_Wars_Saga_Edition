# Impetuous Move

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Impetuous_Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Impetuous_Move) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You have an uncanny ability to call upon your reserves when you most need them.

**Prerequisite:** [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 13

**Effect:** When you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you can choose to regain only half of the [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") you normally would with a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") and immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to half of your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
