# Point-Blank Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at making well-placed shots with ranged weapons at Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

**Effect:** You get a +1 bonus on attack and damage rolls with ranged weapons against opponents within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

**Special:** This Feat works normally with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons"). However, if you are using [Missiles or Torpedoes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Missiles_or_Torpedoes "Missiles or Torpedoes") and you miss with your attack roll, the projectile does not gain the benefit of this feat for the second attack roll.

Point-Blank Shot, Rangefinders, and Targeting ScopesPoint-Blank Shot, Rangefinders, and Targeting Scopes

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

Under most circumstances, a character with the Point-Blank Shot Feat receives a bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls only when the target is within his or her weapon's Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

The use of a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope") does not change how far your target is from you, so taking an [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") Action with a [Targeting Scope](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeting_Scope "Targeting Scope") on a [Blaster Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Rifle "Blaster Rifle") to attack a target that is 50 squares away does not give you the +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls that you would receive if the target were within 30 squares. This is the same for the [Rangefinder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rangefinder "Rangefinder") upgrade, which negates the penalty for attacking a target that is at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").
