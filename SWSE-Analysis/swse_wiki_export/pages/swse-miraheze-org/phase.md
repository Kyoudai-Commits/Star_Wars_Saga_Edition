# Phase

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Phase](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Phase) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

*See also: [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers")*

You can pass through solid objects, such as walls and doors.

**Time:** [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** When moving using your Phasing Speed, you can move through the spaces of your enemies, as well as through [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), walls, large objects, and other obstructions, but must end your movement in a legal, unoccupied space. You retain this Phasing Speed until the beginning of your next turn. You may move up to your Phasing Speed as a part of the activation of this Force Power. The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 25 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 2 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). |
| 30 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 4 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). |
| 35 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 6 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). |
| 40 | You gain a Phasing Speed of 8 squares; your Phasing Speed can never exceed your own base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase your Phasing Speed by 2 squares.

You can maintain *Phase* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Phase* Force Power is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and whenever you maintain this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") you can move up to your Phasing Speed. If you take damage while maintaining *Phase*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Phase*.
