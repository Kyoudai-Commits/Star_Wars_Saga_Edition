# Trip

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at tripping [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") foes.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** If you succeed on a [Grappling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappling "Grappling") attack and your opponent fails the opposed [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check, it falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in its space and is no longer considered [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"). A [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") opponent takes a -5 penalty on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls. [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") made against a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") target gain a +5 bonus, while [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") made against a [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") target take a -5 penalty.

**Special:** You cannot use the [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") and Trip feats in the same round.
