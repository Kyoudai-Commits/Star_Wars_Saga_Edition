# Maniacal Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Maniacal_Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Maniacal_Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") your enemies like a battle-crazed berserker.

**Effect:** When using the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") Action, you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate") your target and each enemy you pass within 1 square of. When your check succeeds against an enemy you pass, it cannot make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against you. When your check succeeds against your target, it loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus and is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** You cannot use this feat with the [Intimidator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidator "Intimidator") feat.
