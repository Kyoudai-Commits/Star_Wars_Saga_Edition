# Covert Operatives

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Covert_Operatives](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Covert_Operatives) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:56Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies specialize in undercover missions in which stealth and subtlety are paramount.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you move more than your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") or more than twice you [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), reduce the penalties to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks by 2.
