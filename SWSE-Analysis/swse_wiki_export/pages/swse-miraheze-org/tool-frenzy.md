# Tool Frenzy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Frenzy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Frenzy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You use all your built-in [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages"), [Claws](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Claws "Claws"), [Probes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Probes "Probes"), [Instruments](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Instruments "Instruments"), and [Tool Mounts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mounts "Tool Mounts") at once to slash, pierce, burn, or cut your enemy in a frenzied [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack").

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), Small size or larger, 2+ [Tool Mounts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mounts "Tool Mounts")

**Effect:** As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), using [Appendages](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendages "Appendages") not normally considered [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons"), you can make several strikes as a single attack against a single target. Make an [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") melee attack with a +2 bonus; until the end of your next turn, you take a -2 penalty to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). Use the damage die of the highest rated [Appendage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Appendage "Appendage") when determining damage.

**Special:** True melee or ranged [Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") are not considered [Tool Mounts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tool_Mounts "Tool Mounts") for the purposes of this feat.
