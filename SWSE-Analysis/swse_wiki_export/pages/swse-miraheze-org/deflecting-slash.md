# Deflecting Slash

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflecting_Slash](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deflecting_Slash) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You use the momentum from your effort to deflect a projectile to strike at an adjacent opponent.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (when targeted by a non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") ranged attack)

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |

**Lightsaber Form ([Soresu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soresu "Soresu")):** If you have the **[Soresu](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soresu "Soresu")** talent, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to redirect the incoming attack (as per the **[Redirect Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Redirect_Shot "Redirect Shot")** Talent) at any other target within 6 squares of you.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to take no damage from the incoming attack if your check equals or exceeds its attack roll.
