# Kissai

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kissai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kissai) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/6/6a/Kissai.webp/300px-Kissai.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Kissai.webp)

The [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") and Kissai are two subspecies of the original Sith race of [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"). The Kissai are the priests and spiritual leaders who indoctrinate their comrades in the teaching of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith").

The Kissai are just as ruthless as the [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi"), but they are more intelligent and observant. Their strict cultural standards demand that they serve their masters well, a trait instilled in them by the Dark Jedi who first enslaved their Species. When the [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") and Kissai flee [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban") (well before the arrival of the eventual Sith Lords) to resettle on [Ziost](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ziost?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ziost (page does not exist)"), the Kissai oversee the rebuilding efforts.

The Kissai serve The Sith directly, but the Sith Lord [Naga Sadow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naga_Sadow?action=edit&redlink=1 "Naga Sadow (page does not exist)") has other ideas for the [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") caste. The Kissai are largely left on [Ziost](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ziost?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ziost (page does not exist)") as the servants of other Sith Lords, but Sadow uses **[Sith Alchemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Alchemy "Sith Alchemy")** to transform the [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") into his hulking, brutish soldiers. His [Massassi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi "Massassi") crews labor under his command on [Yavin 4](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yavin_4 "Yavin 4"), building temples and providing the means for him to hide his ship and practice his dark arts. After Sadow's death, they become a primitive, tribal society worshiping one of Sadow's creations as a dark god.

## Kissai Characteristics

**Personality:** Kissai are loyal and capable allies who prefer to work with others, making them excellent aides and assistants. They respond well to instruction and are adaptable despite great adversity.

**Physical Description:** Kissai have glowing yellow eyes and crimson hides. They are humanoid with distinctly sharp, predatory features. A typical Kissai stands 1.8 meters tall and weighs 90 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Kissai age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 Years | 11-15 Years | 16-30 Years | 31-42 Years | 43-59 Years | 60+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The ancient Sith world of [Korriban](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korriban "Korriban"), resettled later to [Ziost](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ziost?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ziost (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** Kissai speak and read the ancient language of the Sith, and most tend to learn Basic to better serve their Sith masters.

**Example Names:** Drophan, Kalgrath, Uburluh, Zythmnr.

**Adventurers:** Most beings in the galaxy would not recognize a Kissai at first glance, though their unique appearance tends to draws attention to themselves. Kissai do not become [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") during [The Old Republic Era](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Old_Republic_Era "The Old Republic Era"), and instead most adventurous Kissai become [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles") or [Scoundrels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrels "Scoundrels").

## Kissai Species Traits

Kissai share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Kissai receive a +2 bonus to their [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"), but suffer a -2 penalty to their [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom"). Kissai are charming and pleasant, but their devotion to [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") blinds them to the suffering that follows.
- **Medium Size:** As medium creatures, Kissai have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Kissai have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Inspired:** Kissai make the best use of any inspiration or information provided to them. Whenever a Kissai receives a morale or insight bonus from any source, the bonus increases by +1.
- **Warrior's Awareness:** Kissai grow up in a violent and warlike society, so they constantly watch for threats. Once per encounter as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), a Kissai can gain the benefit of the **[Uncanny Dodge I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Uncanny_Dodge_I "Uncanny Dodge I")** and **[Uncanny Dodge II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Uncanny_Dodge_II "Uncanny Dodge II")** Talents until the beginning of the Kissai's next turn.
- **Weapon Familiarity:** Kissai treat the [Massassi Lanvarok](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Massassi_Lanvarok "Massassi Lanvarok") as a [Simple Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapon_(Ranged) "Simple Weapon (Ranged)") instead of an [Exotic Weapon (Ranged)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Ranged) "Exotic Weapon (Ranged)").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Kissai can speak, read, and write both Basic and Sith.
