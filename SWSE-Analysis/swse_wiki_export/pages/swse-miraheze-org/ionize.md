# Ionize

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ionize](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ionize) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You call upon [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to overload electrical systems and [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), damaging or even destroying the unit.

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One target within 6 squares and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**Compare the result of the check to the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the check equals or exceeds the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target sustains damage as determined by the results of your check:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | The target takes 4d6 points of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage. |
| 25 | The target takes 5d6 points of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage. |
| 30 | The target takes 6d6 points of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage. |

**Special:**You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage dealt by this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") by an additional 2d6 points of [Ion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ion "Ion") damage.
