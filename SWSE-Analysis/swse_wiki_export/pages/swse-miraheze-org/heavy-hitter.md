# Heavy Hitter

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Hitter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Hitter) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You know how to pound targets when firing heavy-weapons emplacements and vehicle weaponry.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Focus (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Focus (Heavy Weapons)")

**Effect:** When making an attack roll using a [Weapon Emplacement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Emplacement "Weapon Emplacement") or a [Weapon System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_System "Weapon System"), you add an additional 1 point of damage for every 5 points you roll above the target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If you exceed the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), then on its next turn the target cannot attack and its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 2 squares (1 square [Starship Scale](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship_Scale "Starship Scale")).
