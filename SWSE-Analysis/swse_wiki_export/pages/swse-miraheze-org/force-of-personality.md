# Force of Personality

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_of_Personality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_of_Personality) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

As a result of your self-confidence and conviction, your will is strong.

**Prerequisite:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

**Effect:** You can use either your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") modifier or your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier to determine your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
