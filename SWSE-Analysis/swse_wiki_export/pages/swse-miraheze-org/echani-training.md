# Echani Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Echani_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Echani_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Books: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide"), [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in Echani unarmed fighting techniques, giving you an advantage in hand-to-hand combat.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** You can double the damage bonus provided by your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier to [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks") (minimum +1) provided you make only one [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack this turn.

Once per encounter when you deal damage to a target with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") you may make an immediate [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"); if successful, you knock your opponent [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in their current square, provided they are no more than one size larger than you.

**Special:** Targets gain a bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against being knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") based on their size: Large +5, Huge +10, Gargantuan +20, Colossal +50. Unusually stable creatures (such as those with four legs) gain a +5 bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against being knock [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") as well.

If you have the **[Echani Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Echani_Expertise "Echani Expertise")** Talent, whenever you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), the target is immediately knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in its current square, provided that the target is no more than one size larger than you.
