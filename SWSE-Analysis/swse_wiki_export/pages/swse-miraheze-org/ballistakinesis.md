# Ballistakinesis

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ballistakinesis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ballistakinesis) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:21Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to spray an area with dangerous debris.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All targets within a 2x2 square area (the entire area must be within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight")).

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of each target in the affected area. If the check result equals or exceeds a target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), the target takes 3d8 points of Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage (your choice) and takes a -2 penalty on all attack rolls until the start of your next turn. This is an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack").

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power, in which case targets whose [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") you fail to exceed with your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check take half damage.
