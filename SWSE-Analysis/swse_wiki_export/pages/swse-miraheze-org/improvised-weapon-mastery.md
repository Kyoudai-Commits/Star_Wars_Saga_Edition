# Improvised Weapon Mastery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapon_Mastery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapon_Mastery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You use [Improvised Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapons "Improvised Weapons") with true skill.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Simple_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Simple Weapons)")

**Effect:** You treat all [Improvised Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Weapons "Improvised Weapons") as [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"), and you can use them without penalty and with [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") and [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats") designed for use with [Simple Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons "Simple Weapons"). You also deal an additional 1d6 points of damage on a successful hit.
