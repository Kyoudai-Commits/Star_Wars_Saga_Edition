# Hyperspace Explorer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperspace_Explorer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperspace_Explorer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:08Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are an experienced deep-space explorer who has learned a few tricks along the way that help you survive both in space, and planet-side.

## Core Talents

### Deep-Space Gambit

Once per encounter, when you or a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy are the target of an attack roll, you can force your opponent to reroll the attack. The opponent must keep the worse of the two results.

### Guidance

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception")

You know how to guide others through treacherous terrain. You may use a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to point out the path of least resistance to an ally within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") who can see, hear, and understand you. The ally ignores the effect of [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") on its next turn. You cannot use this Talent on yourself.

### Hidden Attacker

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

Your shots seem to come from nowhere. Whenever you use the [Snipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Snipe "Snipe") application of the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill, you do so as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") instead of a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

### Hyperspace Savant

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

You can substitute your [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill for any [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check made to [Astrogate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogate "Astrogate") or [Use Sensors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Sensors "Use Sensors") while you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle").

### Vehicle Sneak

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

You know how to fly and operate your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") in order to hide its approach visually, decrease the noise it produces, and minimize its sensor signature. Treat your ship as two size categories smaller when attempting [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks.

## Additional Talents

### Silent Movement

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth")

You know how to move silently in almost any environment. You never suffer from [Unfavorable Circumstances](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unfavorable_Circumstances "Unfavorable Circumstances") from environmental effects associated with noise when you [Sneak](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sneak "Sneak") using the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill. Once per round, when you make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, you can automatically use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action on one ally's [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check.
