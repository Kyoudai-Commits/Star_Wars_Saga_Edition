# Force Boon

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Boon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Boon) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:30Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You are able to draw upon [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") more often than less skilled users.

**Prerequisite:** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

**Effect:** You gain three additional [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") at each level.

**Special:** If you are using the [Daily Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Daily_Force_Points "Daily Force Points") optional rule, you gain one additional [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") per day instead of the feat's normal effect.
