# Rapid Strike

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You make two quick strikes with a melee weapon as a single attack.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When using a melee weapon with which you are proficient, you may make two strikes as a single attack against a single target. You take a -2 penalty on your attack roll, but deal +1 die of damage with a successful attack.

**Special:** The effects of this feat do not stack with the extra damage provided by the [Mighty Swing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mighty_Swing "Mighty Swing") feat. If you do not have a [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") score of 13 or higher, increase the penalty to attacks to -5 when using this feat with non-[Light Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapons "Light Weapons").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** I think the prerequisites for [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") and Rapid Strike were swapped accidentally. Wouldn't it make more sense for Rapid Strike, a melee [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), to have the [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") requirement, and for [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), a ranged [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"), to have the [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") requirement?

**A:** No, this is intentional. For [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot"), you need the higher [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") to control the recoil of firing multiple shots so quickly. For Rapid Strike, you need the higher [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") to have the agility necessary to get two swings for the price of one- catching your target a second time on the backswing, spinning to come around a second time, and so on.

For balance purposes, this also has the effect of giving characters some tough decisions. A ranged specialist won't always want to neglect their [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength"), and a melee specialist won't want to neglect their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"). (Sure, such a character could neglect [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") if they have [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse"), but they're spending an extra [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") and restricting themselves to certain weapons for that privilege.)

## Rapid Strike and Whirlwind Attack

**Q:** Reading the rules, it seems that you can use Rapid Strike in conjunction with [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack"), gaining an extra die of damage against all targets. Is this correct?

**A:** No, they don't work together, nor will [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") and [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). The text of both Rapid Strike and [Rapid Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Shot "Rapid Shot") mention that they work with a single attack roll against a single target. [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") (such as [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack")) deal with multiple targets, so they are not eligible.
