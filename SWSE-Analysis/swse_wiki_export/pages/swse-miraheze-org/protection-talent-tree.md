# Protection Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protection_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protection_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You are specially trained to protect those around you, either by taking the brunt of attacks that are aimed at them, or by defusing dangerous situations before they occur.

## Core Talents

### Armored Guard

*Prerequisite:* **Ward**

When you use the **Ward** Talent, your ally's [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") is increased by one-half the Armor bonus of any Natural Armor you possess as well as any [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor") you are wearing.

### Bodyguard's Sacrifice

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can interfere with any successful attack against an adjacent ally. You can choose to take any or all of that attack's damage, and the remainder is dealt to the target as normal. Once you use this Talent, you may not use it again until the end of your next turn.

### Guard's Endurance

*Prerequisite:* **Ward**

Whenever you begin your turn adjacent to the target of your **Ward** Talent, you gain [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your Character Level until the start of your next turn. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from different sources do not stack.

### Lifesaver

*Prerequisite:* **Bodyguard's Sacrifice**

Once per encounter as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when an ally takes damage that equals or exceeds its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") or reduces it to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") provided you end your movement adjacent to that ally. This movement provokes [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") as normal, you take all of the damage that triggered this Talent's use, and your ally takes no damage.

### Out of Harm

As a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), you allow one ally within 6 squares of you to move up to its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), provided the ally ends its movement adjacent to you. This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Roll With It

*Prerequisites:* **Bodyguard's Sacrifice**, **Take the Hit**

Whenever you take damage on behalf of an ally through the use of a Talent (including **[Harm's Way](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Harm%27s_Way "Harm's Way")**), you gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") equal to your Class Level until the end of your next turn.

### Take the Hit

*Prerequisite:* **Bodyguard's Sacrifice**

Whenever you take damage on behalf of an ally through the use of a Talent (including **[Harm's Way](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Harm%27s_Way "Harm's Way")**), your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") is increased by 5 points.

### Ward

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), designate one adjacent ally. Until the end of your next turn, as long as that ally remains adjacent to you, you are considered to be providing that ally with "soft" [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") against all attacks. You cannot be designated as the target of this Talent (such as, when it is used by an ally) if you have used this Talent since the start of your last turn, and you cannot use this Talent if you are currently designated as another ally's **Ward**.
