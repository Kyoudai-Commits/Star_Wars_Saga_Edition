# Brilliant Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brilliant_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brilliant_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You think quickly enough on your feet to stay alive in dangerous situations.

**Prerequisite:** [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 13

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can add your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"); this benefit lasts until the start of your next turn.
