# Improved Charge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Charge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:31Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") around obstacles.

**Prerequisite:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge"), [Mobility](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mobility "Mobility")

**Effect:** You can make a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") without having to move in a straight line, and you can alter your direction when making a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") to avoid obstacles. All other [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") rules apply.

**Normal:** A character must [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") in an unobstructed straight line.
