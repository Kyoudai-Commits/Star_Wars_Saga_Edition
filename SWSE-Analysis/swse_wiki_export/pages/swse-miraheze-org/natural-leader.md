# Natural Leader

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Leader) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:38Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are a natural leader, and you founded your own [Organization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organization "Organization").

**Prerequisite:** [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 13

**Effect:** You become the leader of an [Organization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organization "Organization") of your design. The [Organization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organization "Organization") has a scale equal to one-half your Heroic Level plus your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") bonus and continues to grow in scale as you gain levels. You automatically begin with a +10 bonus to your [Organization Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organization_Score "Organization Score") for your new [Organization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organization "Organization").
