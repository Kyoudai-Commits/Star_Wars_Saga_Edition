# Steadying Position

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Steadying_Position](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Steadying_Position) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You know how to fire from the optimal position, using the ground to steady your aim.

**Prerequisite:** [Careful Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Careful_Shot "Careful Shot")

**Effect:** When you are [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") and [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), your target does not benefit from its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
