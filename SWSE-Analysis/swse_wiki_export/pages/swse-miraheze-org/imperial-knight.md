# Imperial Knight

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Knight) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights") are [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") who dedicate themselves to the preservation of [The Fel Empire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Fel_Empire "The Fel Empire") and the defense of [Emperor Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Roan_Fel "Emperor Roan Fel")- not unlike the [Royal Guards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Royal_Guards "Royal Guards") that once protected [Emperor Palpatine](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Palpatine "Emperor Palpatine"), though their training in [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") distinguishes them from that organization. Despite opinions to the contrary, Imperial Knights are every bit as capable in the use of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") as their Jedi counterparts, though their training has considerably more martial focus. During the height of [The Sith-Imperial War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith-Imperial_War "The Sith-Imperial War"), [The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights") were forbidden from participating in the fighting. Instead, they served as the Emperor's personal guard.

At any given time, there are as few as a dozen Imperial Knights or as many as three score. Casualties within the Order have been high since [Darth Krayt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darth_Krayt "Darth Krayt") declared himself Emperor. The remaining Imperial Knights continue to serve [Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Roan_Fel "Roan Fel") closely, with many fighting an insurgent war against [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") and their Imperial forces.

Recruitment within [The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights") is limited to individuals who demonstrate both Force potential and loyalty to [Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Roan_Fel "Roan Fel"). Like [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), Imperial Knights are permitted to take on apprentices, but only one at a time and only once they have mastered their own skills to a sufficient degree. Apprenticeships are personally approved by the Emperor, as are promotions within the structure of the Order. Only the highest ranking Imperial Knight is designated as a "Master," subject to Roan Fel's approval.

Imperial Knights reject the use of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), yet true [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") still feel that [The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights") lack a balanced approach to their use of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Controlling fear and managing one's emotions in the face of adversity is paramount to the ideology of [The Imperial Knights](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Imperial_Knights "The Imperial Knights"), as is loyalty to their Emperor. They are warriors first and foremost, not negotiators, and they make no excuses for this fact.

**Examples of Imperial Knights in Star Wars:** Sigel Dare, Antares Draco, Marasiah Fel, Mohrgan Fel, Roan Fel, Ganner Krieg, Azlyn Rae, Treis Sinde, Elke Vetter.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become an Imperial Knight, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** +7
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)"), [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)"), [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)")
- **Special:** Must be a sworn defender of [Emperor Roan Fel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Emperor_Roan_Fel "Emperor Roan Fel").

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Imperial Knights gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Imperial Knights gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Imperial Knights gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), an Imperial Knight gains an additional [Imperial Knight Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Knight_Talent "Imperial Knight Talent"). An Imperial Knight may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Imperial Knight Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_Knight_Talent_Trees "Imperial Knight Talent Trees") or [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Force Techniques

You have learned improved techniques to attune yourself to [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At every even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, and so on), an Imperial Knight gains one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") that, once selected, cannot be changed.
