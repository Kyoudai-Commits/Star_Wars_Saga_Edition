# Sensor Link

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sensor_Link](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sensor_Link) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:58Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You can broadcast your sensor input to an ally, such as another [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or a Friendly computer system.

**Prerequisite:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or [Cyborg Hybrid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cyborg_Hybrid "Cyborg Hybrid") with [Subcutaneous Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Subcutaneous_Comlink "Subcutaneous Comlink")

**Effect:** As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can broadcast the input from your audio, visual, and special sensors to a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally, [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink"), communications system, or holographic receiver within 24 squares.

That ally is aware of anything you are aware of, and can use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to aid on your [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks even if you do not have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to one another. If both you and your ally have the Sensor Link feat, you share information simultaneously, granting a +2 bonus to [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") checks.

**Special:** [Cyborg Hybrids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cyborg_Hybrids "Cyborg Hybrids") with built-in [Subcutaneous Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Subcutaneous_Comlink "Subcutaneous Comlink") can also take this feat.
