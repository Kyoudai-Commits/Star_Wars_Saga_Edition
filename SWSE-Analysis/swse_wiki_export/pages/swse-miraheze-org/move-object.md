# Move Object

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You telekinetically move a target up to 6 squares in any direction using [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force").

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One character or object within 12 squares, and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the maximum size of the target you can lift. If the target is a creature that resists your attempt, your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check must also exceed the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). You can hurl the target at (or drop it on) another target in range if your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check exceeds the second target's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). Both targets take damage determined by your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Can move object up to Medium size (deals 2d6 points of damage). |
| 20 | Can move object up to Large size (deals 4d6 points of damage). |
| 25 | Can move object up to Huge size (deals 6d6 points of damage). |
| 30 | Can move object up to Gargantuan size (deals 8d6 points of damage). |
| 35 | Can move object up to Colossal size (deals 10d6 points of damage). |

**Q:** What happens if you use *Move Object* against a door? How about *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")*?

**A:** If you use *Move Object* against a door, you deal normal damage to it as the door strains against its frame. (Don't forget to apply the effects of the door's [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction").) If the damage is sufficient to reduce the door to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), it becomes [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled") and the door is pulled free, making the doorway passable.

If you use *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")* against a door, compare your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result to the door's [Break DC](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Break_DC "Break DC"). If successful, you have forced the door open. If not, the door remains intact and functional.

For those who are interested, an object's [Break DC](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Break_DC "Break DC") is calculated as if it were a character resisting a [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") or *[Force Thrust](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Thrust "Force Thrust")*. Take its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus, add 10 (the average result on a roll of 1d20, rounded down), and- in most cases- add 5 more for being exceptionally stable. (Some particularly fragile objects may deviate on this last detail, but that is fairly rare.)

**Special:** You may maintain your concentration on the targeted object to continue to move it from round to round. Maintaining the *Move Object* power is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you suffer damage while maintaining *Move Object*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue concentrating. If you deal damage with the *Move Object* Power, you cease to be able to maintain it.

If you use *Move Object* against a hovering or flying target (such as a [Speeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder "Speeder") or [Starship](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starship "Starship")), the target can oppose your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check with a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). If the target wins the opposed check, you are unable to move the target.

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the maximum size of the object by one category and deal an additional 2d6 points of damage (maximum size Colossal (Frigate), 12d6 damage). Alternatively, you may spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to increase the maximum size of an object by three categories and deal an additional 6d6 points of damage (maximum size Colossal (Station), 16d6 damage).

The rules for the *Move Object* Force Power allow a character to hurl objects at other targets, dealing damage to them. By the strict interpretation of the rules, the size of the object and the damage dealt by throwing the object are independent of one another; for example, you can throw a Medium object with a DC 25 check and still deal 6d6 points of damage.

As an optional rule, Gamemasters can link object size to the damage dealt with the power, meaning that the object thrown deals damage equal to the damage listed on the line corresponding to that object's size; for example, Medium objects always deal 2d6 damage, Large objects always deal 4d6 damage, Huge objects always deal 6d6 damage, and so forth.

However, Gamemasters should be prepared to make exceptions in situations where players show creativity or when the situation seems to warrant higher damage for a smaller object, such as using the power to drive a Large metal spike through the cockpit of an [AT-ST](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/AT-ST "AT-ST").

## Move Object Reality Check

When you use the *Move Object* Force Power to hurl an object at one or more targets, it doesn't cease to take up space on impact. For particularly large objects, you as the Gamemaster have several options. First, you can describe the object as breaking up on impact, crumbling into several smaller objects and (at your discretion) creating [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") without displacing anyone in the affected squares. For objects that are less likely to break up on impact, you can rule that the object now occupies a certain amount of space, and any creature occupying a space taken up by the object automatically moves to the nearest unoccupied space adjacent to the object (without provoking [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity")).
