# Slicer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You move like a ghost through the HoloNet, and can hack into enemy mainframes and computer systems with astonishing grace.

## Core Talents

### Gimmick

You can [Issue Routine Command](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Issue_Routine_Command "Issue Routine Command") to a computer as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").

### Master Slicer

*Prerequisite:* **Gimmick**

You may choose to reroll any [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check made to [Improve Access](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improve_Access "Improve Access") on a computer, keeping the better of the two results.

### Trace

You can substitute your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skill for any [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check as long as you have access to a computer network.

## Additional Talents

### Electronic Forgery

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

You can use your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") modifier in place of your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") modifier to create a deceptive appearance with forged electronic documents.

### Electronic Sabotage

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")

You excel at causing havoc with computers and electronics. As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can lock down a computer terminal by making a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, making it potentially difficult for anyone else to access. That computer is considered Unfriendly to anyone other than you who attempts to use it, and the result of your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check replaces the computer's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") on an attempt to change its [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer"). This effect ends if anyone else succeeds in adjusting the computer's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") to Indifferent. You cannot [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on this [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check.

### Security Slicer

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

You are an expert in electronic security. When you make a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to [Disable Device](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disable_Device "Disable Device"), you can do so without the help of a [Security Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Security_Kit "Security Kit"). Additionally, something goes wrong only when you fail the [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check by 10 or more.

### Virus

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisites:* **Electronic Sabotage**

You can substitute a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check for a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check when disabling a computerized device. The effort takes 1 minute and the DC is equal to the computer's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). In addition, whenever anyone accesses the afflicted computer using a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") or another computer, that computer or Droid's [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") immediately becomes Unfriendly.
