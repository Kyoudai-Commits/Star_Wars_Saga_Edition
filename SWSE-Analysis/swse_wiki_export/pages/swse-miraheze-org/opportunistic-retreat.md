# Opportunistic Retreat

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Retreat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Opportunistic_Retreat) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:41Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

When your opponent lets down his guard, you can better position yourself to press your advantage.

**Prerequisite:** [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes")

**Effect:** Whenever an opponent provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you, once per turn you can sacrifice the attack to [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") a number of squares equal to one-half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). Moving in this way does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
