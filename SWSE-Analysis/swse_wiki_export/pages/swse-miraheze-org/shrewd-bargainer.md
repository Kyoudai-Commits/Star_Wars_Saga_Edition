# Shrewd Bargainer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shrewd_Bargainer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shrewd_Bargainer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know how to break down the defenses of your opponents, ensuring that you gain the upper hand in any negotiation.

**Prerequisite:** [Quarren](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quarren "Quarren")

**Effect:** Whenever you make [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks against a target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), the target does not receive the benefit of insight or morale bonuses to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
