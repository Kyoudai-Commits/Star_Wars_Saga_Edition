# Brute Squad Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brute_Squad_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brute_Squad_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Your brutal fighting techniques give you an upper hand when operating in small groups.

## Core Talents

### Gang Leader

Once per encounter, when you make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check to [Intimidate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intimidate "Intimidate"), you gain a +1 bonus on the check for every ally within 6 squares of you and in the target's [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") (maximum +5 bonus).

### Melee Assault

When you make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target that has one or more of your allies adjacent to it, compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as well as its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the attack hits both [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), the attack deals +1 die of damage and the target is knocked [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone").

### Melee Brute

When you make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target that has one or more of your allies adjacent to it, compare the result to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as well as its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). If the attack hits both [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"), the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 2 squares and it takes a -2 penalty to its [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn.

### Melee Opportunist

Once per encounter, when an ally makes a successful [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target adjacent to you, you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against that target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), with a +2 bonus on the attack roll.

### Squad Brutality

When you succeed on a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against a target that has one or more of your allies adjacent to it, you may reroll your damage roll, keeping the better of the two results.

### Squad Superiority

Whenever you and at least two allies are adjacent to the same target, that target is considered [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against you.
