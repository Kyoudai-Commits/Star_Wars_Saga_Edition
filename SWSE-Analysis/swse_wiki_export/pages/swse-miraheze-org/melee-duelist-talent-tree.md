# Melee Duelist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Duelist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are lethal when fighting with one or more melee weapons.

## Core Talents

### Advantageous Strike

You take advantage of your opponent's haste. You gain a +5 bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") with melee weapons you are proficient with.

### Dirty Tricks

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

You are not above using a few dirty tricks to win. You can use the [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") application of the [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill as two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") against an opponent you [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten").

### Dual Weapon Flourish I

*Prerequisites:* [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

When wielding only two [Light Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapons "Light Melee Weapons") or two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), whenever you make a single attack as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") with one weapon, you can make a single attack with the other weapon as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") against the same target. You apply the normal penalties for fighting with two weapons with this attack.

### Dual Weapon Flourish II

*Prerequisites:* **Dual Weapon Flourish I**, **Master of Elegance**, [Dual Weapon Mastery I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_I "Dual Weapon Mastery I"), [Dual Weapon Mastery II](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dual_Weapon_Mastery_II "Dual Weapon Mastery II"), [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

When wielding only two [Light Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapons "Light Melee Weapons") or two [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), once per turn on your turn, you can make a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), rather than a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), provided you attack with both weapons during the attack. You apply the normal penalties for fighting with two weapons to both of these attacks.

### Master of Elegance

*Prerequisites:* **Dual Weapon Flourish I** or **Single Weapon Flourish I**, [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

You may add your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (instead of your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus) on damage rolls when wielding a [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon"). When you wield a [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon") two-handed, you may apply double your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (instead of double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus) to the damage.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Advanced Melee Weapons)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with [Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons") as a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2 points. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2 points.

### Out of Nowhere

*Prerequisites:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception"), [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

Once per encounter, as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") on your turn, you can make an attack with a [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon") or [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") after a successful [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint").

### Single Weapon Flourish I

*Prerequisites:* [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") ([Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons"), an [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)"), or [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers")), [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

When you wield only a single [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon"), or a single [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), and use the [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action, once per turn; you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") at any time during your turn.

### Single Weapon Flourish II

*Prerequisites:* **Master of Elegance**, **Single Weapon Flourish I**, [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") ([Advanced Melee Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Melee_Weapons "Advanced Melee Weapons"), an [Exotic Weapon (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon_(Melee) "Exotic Weapon (Melee)"), or [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers")), [Weapon Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse "Weapon Finesse")

When you wield only a single [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon"), or a single [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), once per turn on your turn, you can make a [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack") Action as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").
