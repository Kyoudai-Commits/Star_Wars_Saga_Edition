# Darkness Dweller

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkness_Dweller](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Darkness_Dweller) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have lived underground for so long that you can spot sneaky enemies easily and make it hard for them to slip past your allies.

**Prerequisite:** [Sullustan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sullustan "Sullustan")

**Effect:** Any enemy that makes a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check within 10 squares of you takes a -2 penalty to the check. This penalty does not stack with the same penalty imposed by other characters with this feat.
