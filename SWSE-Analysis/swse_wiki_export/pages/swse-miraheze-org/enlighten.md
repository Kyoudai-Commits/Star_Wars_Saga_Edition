# Enlighten

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enlighten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enlighten) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:23Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You reach out to an ally telepathically, sharing visions of the near future to give the ally an edge or to protect the ally from harm.

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*, *[Mind-Affecting](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mind-Affecting "Mind-Affecting")*]**

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") or [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** One ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The target may use the check result instead of one attack roll, [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), or opposed check it makes before the start of your next turn, or the target can use the check result in place of one of its [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this Force Power to prolong the effect until the end of your next turn.
