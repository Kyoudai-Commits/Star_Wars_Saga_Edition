# Master Tracker

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Tracker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_Tracker) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:51Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You can follow a shadow on a moonless night and survive in the wilderness with nothing but your wits to protect you.

**Prerequisite:** [Rodian](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rodian "Rodian")

**Effect:** Whenever you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to add to a [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check, increase the die type by two steps (from d6 to d10, or from d8 to d12).
