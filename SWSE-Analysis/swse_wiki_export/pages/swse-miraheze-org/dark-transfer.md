# Dark Transfer

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Transfer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Transfer) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

Legacy Era Campaign GuideWeb Enhancements

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to restore vitality to a living ally.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One other living creature within reach.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to your Character Level. |
| 20 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 2 x your Character Level. |
| 25 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 3 x your Character Level. |
| 30 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 4 x your Character Level. |

**Special:** Each time you use *Dark Transfer*, you move -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). You must rest for 8 hours to remove this [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition"). You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to avoid moving down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when you use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Star Wars Miniatures Previews](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Miniatures_Previews "Star Wars Miniatures Previews"))*

You use [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to heal another living creature.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature touched.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the result, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 2 x your Character Level. |
| 20 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 3 x your Character Level. |
| 25 | The target heals a number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to 4 x your Character Level. |

**Special:** Each time you use *Dark Transfer*, you move -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). You must rest for 8 hours to remove this [Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition "Condition").

You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to avoid moving down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") when you use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). You may spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to revive a creature that has just died. You must use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") within 1 round of the creature's death to revive it, and you must succeed on a DC 25 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If successful, the creature is unconscious instead of dead and does not heal any [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") from the use of *Dark Transfer*.
