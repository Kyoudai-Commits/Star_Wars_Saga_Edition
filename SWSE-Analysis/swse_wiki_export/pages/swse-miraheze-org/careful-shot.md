# Careful Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Careful_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Careful_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are particularly skilled at [Aiming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aiming "Aiming") your attacks.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +2, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** If you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you gain a +1 bonus on your attack roll.

**Special:** A [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") can use this feat, but since they must spend at least one [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") operating their [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles"), they must split their [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") (two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")) and attack ([Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")) into two separate turns, making it more likely that the target will break line of sight or avoid you.
