# Mounted Defense

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mounted_Defense) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:35Z.

*Reference Book: [Threats of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threats_of_the_Galaxy "Threats of the Galaxy")*

You are able to react to incoming attacks, using your mount or [Speeder Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bike "Speeder Bike") to absorb attacks against you.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") or [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride")

**Effect:** When riding a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") as a mount or on a [Speeder Bike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeder_Bike "Speeder Bike") (as a passenger or a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")), once per encounter you can redirect any attack made against you at your [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") instead. The decision to redirect the attack is made after the result of the attack roll is determined but before damage or other effects are resolved.
