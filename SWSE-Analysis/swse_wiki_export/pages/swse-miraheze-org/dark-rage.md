# Dark Rage

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Rage) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You become enraged as [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") flows through you.

**[*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*]**

**Time:** [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") Check.** The result of the check determines the following effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You gain a +2 Rage bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls and melee damage rolls until the end of your turn. |
| 20 | You gain a +4 Rage bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls and melee damage rolls until the end of your turn. |
| 25 | You gain a +6 Rage bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls and melee damage rolls until the end of your turn. |

**Special:** While consumed by a *Dark Rage*, you cannot use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") or perform tasks that require patience or concentration. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to extend the duration of your *Dark Rage* until the end of the encounter.
