# Nikto Survival

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto_Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto_Survival) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You gain an additional boost to your [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") checks, based on your [Nikto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto "Nikto") subspecies.

**Prerequisite:** [Nikto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto "Nikto")

**Effect:** Select a [Nikto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto "Nikto") subspecies: Kajain'sa'Nikto (Desert), Kadas'sa'Nikto (Forest), Esral'sa'Nikto (Mountains), Gluss'sa'Nikto (Ocean), M'shento'su,Nikto (Arctic). In addition to the usual [Nikto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nikto "Nikto") Species Traits, when operating in an environment advantageous to your native subspecies, you can reroll any [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check, taking the better result.
