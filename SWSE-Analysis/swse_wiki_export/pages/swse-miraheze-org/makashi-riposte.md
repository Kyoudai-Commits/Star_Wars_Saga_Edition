# Makashi Riposte

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi_Riposte](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi_Riposte) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

**[*[Lightsaber Form](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Form "Lightsaber Form")*]**

You are trained in a **[Makashi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi "Makashi")** technique that allows you to slightly change the angle of an opponent's attack before striking with a decisive riposte of your own.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") (when an adjacent opponent makes a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against you)

**Targets:** Self, plus one enemy within [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the results of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check against the attack roll of the incoming attack. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the incoming attack roll, the attack deals half damage on a hit. Additionally, you can make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against your target after the damage is resolved. You deal half damage to the target on a miss.

**Lightsaber Form ([Makashi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi "Makashi")):** If you have the **[Makashi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Makashi "Makashi")** Talent, until the end of your next turn, the target provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you if it makes an attack and is within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to take no damage from the incoming attack if your check equals or exceeds the attack roll of the incoming attack.
