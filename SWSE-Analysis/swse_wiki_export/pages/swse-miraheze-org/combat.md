# Combat

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:22Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy"), [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Saga Edition FAQ](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saga_Edition_FAQ "Saga Edition FAQ") (Q154))*

The galaxy is a dangerous place, and sometimes you have to fight to survive. Whether the enemy takes the form of Battle Droids or Stormtroopers, a Dark Force-user or a rampaging [Rancor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rancor "Rancor"), you need to be able to defend yourself. Using blasters, [Vibroblades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibroblades "Vibroblades"), and [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers"), heroes regularly get caught up in blazing firefights, wild cantina brawls, and mesmerizing duels. You can try to bluff your way out of a tough situation, attempt to sneak away when your opponent is distracted, or even dazzle an enemy with your charming personality. But when all else fails, nothing beats having a good blaster at your side.

This article and tangential articles detail the combat rules, covering the basics first. The back end of the section looks at some of the more unusual strategies that heroes can employ, including using [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") in combat. Many special abilities and forms of damage that affect combat are covered in [Gamemastering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamemastering "Gamemastering").

## Basics of Combat

All forms of Combat in the *Saga Edition* system use "rounds" to simulate time in a mechanical sense, with "actions" representing the things done in those rounds. The following are the core mechanics of an form of Combat heroes might encounter.

- **[Combat Sequence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Sequence "Combat Sequence"):** Combat takes place through a series of rounds, allowing every character involved in the encounter a chance to perform their turn in the [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order"). Certain events may also affect just how an encounter begins.
- **[Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions"):** Every round, on your character's turn, you may take a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") (in any order). These Actions represent everything a character can do in Combat, with [Bonuses and Penalties](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonuses_and_Penalties "Bonuses and Penalties") applied to tasks that a given situation might be easier or harder to accomplish.
- **[Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"):** A character can target an opponent that they see, which is to say, any opponent within their Line of Sight. If a line can be drawn from any point in the attacker's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") to any point in the target's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space"), they have Line of Sight.
- **[Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"):** If an opponent moves out of a square adjacent to you, or performs an action that forces them to let down their guard, you can make a single, immediate attack against that opponent (even if you've already acted during the round).

## Base Statistics

When determining an individual character, creature, or any other entity acting in Combat, several key statistics determine their capabilities.

- **[Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks "Attacks"):** Attacking (whether it be [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") or [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks")) is how characters primarily inflict damage on their opponents. If their roll of a 1d20 and their appropriate modifiers is high enough, the Attack can harm their enemies in Combat.
- **[Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses"):** Your Defenses represent your ability to avoid taking [Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage "Damage") and overcome attacks to the body and mind. You have three Defense Scores; Reflex (how hard you are to hit in combat), Fortitude (your ability to resist debilitating effects), and Will (your willpower and ability to resist mental attacks).
- **[Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"):** Your Speed tells you how far you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") with a single [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"). Sometimes you might have special types of movement (a Climb Speed, Fly Speed, Swim Speed, etc) that are also part of your Speed.
- **[Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"):** Hit Points (abbreviated "HP") represent two things in the game world: the ability to take physical punishment and keep going, and the ability to turn a serious blow into a graze or near miss.

## Vehicle Combat

These rules come into play whenever Vehicles figure prominently on the battlefield- whether that battlefield happens to be on a planet's surface, or in the dark void of space, the movement and combat rules presented below cover all types of Vehicles, from planetary Vehicles, to Starships. Unless noted otherwise, climbing aboard a Vehicle is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and powering up a Vehicle requires a second [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

There are two options for determining [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order") in Vehicle Combat. First, each character can make separate [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks. This is probably the best method if most or all characters are aboard the same Vehicle, but it can result in a lot of [Delayed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Delayed "Delayed") or [Readied](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Readied "Readied") Actions, as passengers wait for [Pilots](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilots "Pilots") to perform maneuvers. An alternative is to make an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check for each Vehicle, using the Pilot's [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check modifier. This is particularly appropriate when characters are in separate Vehicles, since it allows all Crew Positions aboard the same Vehicle to act more or less simultaneously.

**Special:** If you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill, you can choose to make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check instead of an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check to determine your place in the [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order"). In any event, you must apply the Vehicle's size modifier to your check.

In the event that the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") is incapacitated, or abandons a Vehicle before using the [Full Stop](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Stop "Full Stop") Action, the Vehicle's emergency systems automatically kick in. On the Pilot's [Initiative Count](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Count "Initiative Count"), the Vehicle moves in a straight line a number of squares equal to its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), and then comes to a complete stop. A [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") without a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") cannot land itself while flying, so after coming to a complete stop, it will fall as if it had been [Disabled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disabled "Disabled").

### Combat Scale

The below rules use two scales: Character Scale, and Starship Scale. If the encounter involves both Vehicles and characters on foot, use Character Scale. If the scene involves Vehicles only, use the Starship Scale.

Character ScaleStarship Scale

Character Scale is identical to the standard movement scale. Combat is carried out on a grid, in which each square equals 1.5 meters (about 5 feet).

In Character Scale, most Vehicles are large enough to occupy multiple squares on the battle map. How many squares a Vehicle occupies is determined by the Vehicle's size (and the same holds true for characters and creatures). Vehicles of Colossal (Frigate) size or greater are not placed on the battle map; they are either off the map (perhaps providing fire support), or the battle takes place inside them. In Character Scale, more than one surface Vehicle cannot occupy the same space on the battle map, and a [Collision](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collision "Collision") occurs whenever a Vehicle enters a square occupied by a creature, obstacle, or another Vehicle.

In Starship Scale, Each square of the battle map is abstract, representing a variable amount of space, depending on the Vehicles involved. In most cases, one square is hundreds or even thousands of meters wide. In Starship Scale, the battle map itself represents relative movement, not absolute movement, so several Starships in orbit might be drifting together at many kilometers per second, even as they move around one another. At Starship Scale, a Vehicle can pass through squares occupied by allies, but not squares occupied by opponents.

### Crew Positions

A character in a Vehicle fills one of several possible roles, which determines what the character can do. A character can fill several roles at once, but most roles may only be filled by one character at a time. For example, an [X-Wing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/X-Wing "X-Wing") Pilot also acts as the Vehicle's Commander and Gunner, while their Astromech Droid usually acts as a Copilot, System Operator, and Engineer. On the other hand, an [*Imperial I*-Class Star Destroyer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Imperial_I-Class_Star_Destroyer "Imperial I-Class Star Destroyer") with thousands of crewmembers still only has one Pilot, one Commander, and so forth. You can change roles from round to round, but you can only start filling a particular role if no other crewmember has filled that role since your last turn.

In the case of a generic Vehicle, its crewmembers are represented by their [Crew Quality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew_Quality "Crew Quality").

The 6 basic roles in a Vehicle can be further explored below:

- **[Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot"):** Controls the movement and speed.
- **[Copilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Copilot "Copilot"):** Aids the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") in basic systems.
- **[Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner"):** Operates one of the [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems").
- **[Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander"):** Coordinates other crewmembers while analyzing the battle.
- **[System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator"):** Manages the shields, sensors, and communications.
- **[Engineer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineer "Engineer"):** Keeps the craft in good order, even during the heat of battle.

Other crewmembers can fill many supporting roles, coordinating troops or [Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"), administering medical care, guarding sensitive areas, and providing general maintenance. These crewmembers can assist others on some [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"); for example, the members of a repair team may assist the Engineer in their duties on [Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships").

All other personnel aboard the Vehicle are considered Passengers. Passengers have no specific role in the Vehicle's operation, but may take [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") aboard the Vehicle, or replace crewmembers as needed.

### Vehicle Types

The term "Vehicle" encompasses both Planetary Vehicles (such as Airspeeders, Landspeeders, and Walkers), and Starships (such as Starfighters, Space Transports, and Capital Ships). The major difference between Planetary Vehicles and Starships is that Planetary Vehicles generally do not travel in the vacuum of space. Rules that pertain only to Planetary Vehicles or Starships are called out specifically.

Vehicle StatisticsVehicle Statistics

Some statistics are exclusive to Vehicles, or require more elaboration:

- **Challenge Level (CL):** Heroes gain [Experience Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Experience_Points "Experience Points") (XP) for disabling, destroying, or otherwise overcoming the Vehicle based on the Vehicle's CL. However, they don't gain additional XP for incidental crew or passengers killed as a result of the Vehicle's destruction.
  - For example, heroes who blow up a shuttle don't also receive XP for its crew; however, if the shuttle was transporting an Imperial Moff that the heroes were hired to eliminate, they should receive XP for the Imperial Moff as well.
- **Vehicle Initiative:** The Pilot's [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") modifier (or the [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skill modifier if you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained")) + Vehicle's size modifier + Vehicle's [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier.
- **Perception:** Character's normal [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") modifier.
- **Reflex Defense:** The Vehicle's [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") is equal to 10 + size modifier + Armor bonus (or Pilot's Heroic Level) + [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus.
- **Maximum Velocity:** The Maximum Speed the Vehicle can achieve after using [All-Out Movement](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/All-Out_Movement "All-Out Movement") for at least one full round. Maximum Velocity is never used in Starship Scale.
- **Attack Rolls:** Gunner's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + Vehicle's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier + miscellaneous bonuses ([Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus"), etc.) +2 (if [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") and firing [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")-controlled [Weapon Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Systems "Weapon Systems") from the Pilot's position).
- **Base Attack Bonus:** The Gunner's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus").
- **Grapple:** The Vehicle's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") modifier is mainly used to resist attempts to restrain the Vehicle physically (usually via [Tractor Beam](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tractor_Beam "Tractor Beam")). A Vehicle's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") modifier is equal to its Pilot's [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") + the Vehicle's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier + the Vehicle's special size modifier;
  - Large +5,
  - Huge +10,
  - Gargantuan +15,
  - Colossal +20,
  - Colossal (Frigate) +25,
  - Colossal (Cruiser) +30,
  - Colossal (Station) +35.
- **Fighting Space:** The [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") that the Vehicle occupies at Character Scale, Starship Scale, or both.
- **Cover:** The amount of [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") the Vehicle grants to its crew and passengers, listed as none, +5 (Normal [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover")), +10 ([Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover")), or [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover").
- **Skills:** Character's appropriate [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") modifiers (+ Vehicle size modifier + Vehicle [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier in the case of the [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") and [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") skills).
  - If the ship's computer is Friendly or Helpful towards you, you also add the ship's [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier as an [Equipment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Equipment "Equipment") bonus to your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks.
- **Crew:** The number and quality of the crew (see [Crew Quality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew_Quality "Crew Quality")). The crew modifiers are already included in all other statistics in the Vehicle description.
- **Passengers:** The number of passengers (including troops) that the Vehicle can carry, in addition to its crew.
- **Cargo Capacity:** The amount of cargo the Vehicle can carry.
- **Carried Craft:** Other Vehicles that are usually carried aboard this Vehicle.
- **Payload:** The Vehicle's complement of Grenades, Rockets, Missiles, and Torpedoes. If this Vehicle has no Payload, this line is absent.
- **Hyperdrive:** The multiplier used when calculating hyperspace travel times (see [Astrogation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogation "Astrogation")). If the Starship has a backup Hyperdrive, its multiplier is listed in parentheses. Hyperdrive is never used in Character Scale. If the Vehicle has no Hyperdrive, this line is absent.
- **Availability:** Whether or not the Vehicle is a [Restricted Item](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted_Item "Restricted Item").

#### Planetary Vehicles

Planetary Vehicles are further subdivided into Ground Vehicles and Air Vehicles.

- **[Speeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speeders "Speeders"):** Speeders are common sights on the technologically advanced worlds. Equipped with repulsorlift drive technology, they hover above the ground at a height of anywhere from a few centimeters, to several meters, and can achieve remarkable speeds. Speeders ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain").
- **[Tracked Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked_Vehicles "Tracked Vehicles"):** Tracked Vehicles have treads or tracks looped around a large number of wheels, providing great traction, but making them less maneuverable at higher speeds- and extremely loud at any speed. Tracked Vehicles ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain"), and take half damage from [Collisions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collisions "Collisions") with obstacles at least one size category smaller than them.
- **[Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers"):** Walkers move about on two or more legs, literally walking over the terrain. Walkers retain their balance by means of finely tuned gyroscopes, and offer a fairly smooth ride. They rely on even footing, however, and when they topple, the results can be disastrous. Walkers ignore obstacles that are three or more sizes smaller than them.
- **[Wheeled Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled_Vehicles "Wheeled Vehicles"):** Wheeled Vehicles move on one or more wheels (most commonly two to four). Faster and more maneuverable than [Tracked Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tracked_Vehicles "Tracked Vehicles") or [Walkers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walkers "Walkers"), wheels are a cheap alternative to repulsorlift technology for light and fast Vehicles.
- **[Weapon Emplacements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Emplacements "Weapon Emplacements"):** Weapon Emplacements are a staple or ground warfare. They typically fall under two categories: antipersonnel and antivehicle. Weapon Emplacements are treated as Vehicles for statistical purposes, but they are usually immobile and do not require a [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") to function. Instead, [Gunners](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunners "Gunners") control all the functions of the Weapon Emplacement.
- **[Airspeeders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeders "Airspeeders"):** Airspeeders are repulsorlift Vehicles that can travel anywhere up to about 300 kilometers above ground level, but they are incapable of true space flight. Because they fly so high above the ground, they ignore penalties for [Difficult Terrain](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Difficult_Terrain "Difficult Terrain") and obstacles. Of all Planetary Vehicles, Airspeeders are the most maneuverable. Airspeeders can also be used to form [Fighter Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighter_Groups "Fighter Groups").

#### Starships

Starships are Vehicles capable of interplanetary and interstellar travel. They are further subdivided into Starfighters, Space Transports, Capital Ships, and Space Stations.

- **[Starfighters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighters "Starfighters"):** Starfighters are small, agile Starships of Gargantuan size or smaller. Though they can travel in atmosphere, they excel at space combat. Starfighters can function with as few as one crewmember (the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot")). Starfighters can also be used to form [Fighter Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighter_Groups "Fighter Groups").
- **[Space Transports](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Transports "Space Transports"):** Space Transports are mid-sized Starships of Colossal or Colossal (Frigate) size, with fewer than 200 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). Most Space Transports are designed for moving passengers or cargo, but some are used as gunships, drop ships, or assault shuttles.
- **[Capital Ships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Capital_Ships "Capital Ships"):** Capital Ships include all Starships of Colossal (Frigate) size or larger. Capital Ships usually carry hundreds, if not thousands of crewmembers and passengers, and some are large enough to house other Capital Ships within their hangar bays. Some very large transports and colony ships fall into this category, despite not being designed for combat.
- **[Space Stations](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Space_Stations "Space Stations"):** Though not technically modes of transportation, Space Stations have statistics like other Starships. A Space Station can have a population in the thousands or millions, depending on its size. Most Space Stations are immobile, [The Death Star](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Death_Star "The Death Star") being a notable exception.

## Mass Combat

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

[The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") are all about the massive battles that transform the galaxy for decades- even centuries- to come. No conflict on the scale of [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") is complete without sweeping battles that cover entire planets. In most cases, these battles serve as the backdrop for adventures, with either predetermined outcomes planned in advance by the Gamemaster or outcomes based on the results of smaller encounters, as described in the guidelines for [Large-Scale Conflict](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Large-Scale_Conflict "Large-Scale Conflict"). However, sometimes the GM might want the players to act as commanders and tacticians on a higher level. In these cases, Jedi Generals can truly be generals in command of entire battalions, and all the heroes can participate in these large-scale battles. [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") feature battles deep in the jungles of [Felucia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Felucia "Felucia"), on the ash-choked streets of [Mygeeto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mygeeto?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mygeeto (page does not exist)"), and deep in the sinkholes of [Utapau](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utapau "Utapau"). Picking an exciting setting for a Mass Combat battle is an important step- just as important as choosing the two (or more) opposing forces.

The following rules govern massive ground combat, like that seen in the *Star Wars* movies and *The Clone Wars* animated series. In many ways, these rules mirror Vehicle Combat- and for good reason. [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions")- the basic organizational name for a large number of troops- function like Vehicles in that heroes can join the Battalion and take Actions to control and manipulate that Battalion. These Mass Combat rules represent the chance to put thousands of soldiers into play during a single encounter. This section also includes rules for integrating both individual Vehicles as well as groups of smaller Vehicles, allowing Gamemasters more diversity than just having two armies firing at one another across an open plain. Like designing interesting character-scale and Vehicle encounters, designing interesting Mass Combat battles is a matter of choosing compelling opponents, providing an engaging setting, and adding existing [Hazards](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hazards "Hazards") and challenges.

Everything in a Mass Combat encounter takes place at Starship Scale. As in Starship Scale, each square is abstract, representing however much terrain the Gamemaster needs it to. In a densely packed battle, the squares might be relatively small, forcing the members of each [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") to crowd together (as in the Battle of [Geonosis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Geonosis "Geonosis")). In other cases, the battle might be spread out over several kilometers. As a result, the actual time taken up by the combat round is also abstracted, and to a certain extent, so are the Actions of each [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion").

### Starting Mass Combat

There are two options for determining [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order") in Mass Combat. First, each character can make separate [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks. This is probably the best method if most or all characters are part of the same [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"), but it can result in excessive [Delayed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Delayed "Delayed") or [Readied](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Readied "Readied") Actions as [Attack Leaders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leaders "Attack Leaders") wait for [Field Commanders](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commanders "Field Commanders") to move [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") into position. An alternative is to make an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check for each [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"), using the Battalion's [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check modifier. This is particularly appropriate when the characters are parts of different [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions"), since it allows everyone in the same [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") to act more or less simultaneously.

**Special:** If a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") or a Battalion's [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)"), you can choose to make a [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check instead of an [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check to determine that Battalion's place in the [Initiative Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative_Order "Initiative Order").

Resolving Mass Combat with OrganizationsResolving Mass Combat with Organizations

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You can use the Organization [Power Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Check "Power Check") rules to resolve Mass Combat. Though the Gamemaster might have an outcome of battle in mind when Mass Combat takes place, the [Power Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Check "Power Check") rules make it easy to resolve large-scale battles without having to script them entirely.

When the heroes participate in a large battle, such as the Battle of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo") or the Battle of [Endor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endor "Endor"), they have the chance to make a difference and turn the tide of combat. Typically, a massive fight will take place in the background while the heroes engage in a small number of more focused encounters. If you want to use this system to resolve Mass Combat, design adventures using massive battles as the backdrop for a number of encounters.

#### Disbanding

When a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"), or when the [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") of the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") orders it so, the Battalion Disbands. Any heroes in a Battalion that Disbands are no longer part of the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"), and they are then considered individual targets. Two or more adjacent Disbanding Battalions can recombine into a single [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"). The newly formed Battalion is of the same type as the lowest CL Disbanding Battalion; thus, a CL 8 Battalion and a CL 10 Battalion can Disband and reform as a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") of the same type as the CL 8 Battalion.

The [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") of the Disbanding Battalions are added together, and become the new Hit Point total of the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") (up to the Battalion's maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points")). If the CLs of the Disbanding Battalions have a difference of 4 or more, the Gamemaster can give the new Battalion an Advantaged [Battalion Template](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion_Template "Battalion Template"). However, this is solely at the Gamemaster's discretion, and might be affected by the circumstances surrounding the formation of the new [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"). A Disbanding Battalion that does not reform with another Disbanding Battalion leaves the battlefield.

### Battalion Positions

A character in a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") fills one of several possible roles, determining not only the Actions a character can take but also how the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") behaves in Mass Combat. Mass Combat allows several distinct roles, but a single character can fill multiple roles at the same time. You can change roles from round to round, but you can fill a particular role only if no other character has filled that role since the end of your last turn. Characters within [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") cannot be specifically targeted by effects or attacks, just as one cannot target specific vehicle systems in most circumstances.

Heroes contribute to the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") by their leadership and ability to affect the whole [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"), not their individual combat prowess. A single creature or character within a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") cannot be targeted; all attacks target the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"), effectively providing all heroes in a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") with [Total Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Cover "Total Cover") from outside attacks. Unless noted otherwise, joining a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

- **[Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander"):** A Field Commander controls the Battalion's movement and can also fill every other Battalion Position not currently filled by another character. A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can have only one Field Commander at a time.
- **[First Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Officer "First Officer"):** A First Officer can perform all the same Actions that the [Field Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Commander "Field Commander") hasn't performed since the end of the First Officer's last turn. A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can have only one First Officer at a time.
- **[Attack Leader](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_Leader "Attack Leader"):** The Attack Leader coordinates all the Battalion's attacks. An Attack Leader can order attacks and use special tactics. A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can have only one Attack Leader at a time.
- **[Communications Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Communications_Officer "Communications Officer"):** The Communications Officer is responsible for coordinating all the orders within the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") and coordinating with other [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions"). A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can have only one Communications Officer at a time.
- **[Medical Officer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Officer "Medical Officer"):** The Medical Officer coordinates a team of combat physicians who oversee the general welfare of the [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion"). A [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") can have only one Medical Officer at a time.

#### Abilities in Mass Combat

Characters filling the various roles in a [Battalion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalion "Battalion") might have [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers"), and other special abilities that they can use in Mass Combat. As a general rule, special abilities that function in Character Combat have too small an effect to be noticeable in Mass Combat. However, some abilities, especially those used by [Officers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Officers "Officers") and [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles"), can give [Battalions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Battalions "Battalions") a big advantage through superior leadership. [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), [Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"), and special abilities can be used in Mass Combat only if they meet certain criteria.

- **All Targets who can See, Hear, and Understand You:** Abilities that require your targets to be able to see, hear, and understand you function only for targets within your same square in Mass Combat.
- **All Targets in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"):** Abilities that affect enemies, allies, or targets in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") function in Mass Combat. They affect all targets of the appropriate type within your same square.
- **Single Target, Limited Number of Targets, or Nearby Targets:** Abilities that affect a single target, a limited number of targets, or targets within a certain number of squares of you have no effect during Mass Combat.
