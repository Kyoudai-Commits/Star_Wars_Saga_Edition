# Bantha Rush

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can shove your opponents around the battlefield to gain a tactical advantage.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** After making a successful [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an opponent up to one size category larger than you, you can choose to move that opponent 1 square in any direction as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). You can't Bantha Rush an opponent that's being [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") or [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), and you can't Bantha Rush your opponent into a solid object or another creature's [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space").
