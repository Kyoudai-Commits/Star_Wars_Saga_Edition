# Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:06Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is the chosen weapon of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"). Not as clumsy or random as a blaster, the [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is an elegant weapon for a more civilized combatant. The following talents allow you to improve your prowess with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber").

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** Why are **Block**, **Deflect**, and **Redirect Shot** three separate [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents")? All Jedi- even the younglings that [Yoda](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yoda "Yoda") trained- seem to be capable of this basic ability, so why isn't it a single [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") (or even a built-in class feature) that you get at 1st level?

**A:** First, there's a difference between cinematically using a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to deflect a shot or block an incoming blow, and mechanically gaining a specific benefit from deflecting or blocking an attack. All characters, [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") or otherwise, avoid attacks however they can- dodging, ducking, weaving, parrying, blocking, or even deflecting- and all of these things are built into a character's Reflex Defense. If you're proficient with and wielding a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), at least some of the attacks that miss you are deflected, parried, or blocked in a cinematic sense. You aren't gaining any mechanical benefit from it, though, because you haven't managed to hone that ability enough to make a difference until you take the **Deflect** or **Block** Talents. Once you take those [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), you gain the ability to defend yourself even more effectively, making a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to nullify an attack that beats your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

As for why these abilities are separated into multiple [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), we decided that the abilities were so useful that they would be a "no-brainer" choice if they were combined into a single [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). In other words, almost every [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") would take this at 1st level, no matter what. No other choice would be competitive unless you were playing a character who was not combat oriented at all. In addition, making them separate [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") also gives each [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") more individuality.

Are you more of a duelist, specializing in one-on-one combat against dark Jedi and Sith apprentices? If so, then **Block** is your best choice.

Are you more of a diplomat or even an infiltrator who'd like a nice defensive ability in the event that you have to make a hasty retreat? **Deflect** is a great choice, allowing you to avoid [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") from an ambush or pursuers.

Are you a battlefield commander leading a platoon of [Clone Troopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Troopers "Clone Troopers") against waves of blaster-wielding Battle Droids? Both **Deflect** and **Redirect Shot** are outstanding choices, allowing you to defend yourself during the battle and add to the ranged offensive capabilities of your unit, turning the enemy's fire against them.

In the end, abilities like these are extremely useful, and, in our opinion, it's worth spending a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") for any one of them. Certainly, a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") might take all three (and many do), but it isn't necessary to take all three for every character concept, and some [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") may choose to take none at all.

Even without the extra mechanical benefits of these [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), you can still describe your [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") parrying blaster bolts out of the air or locking sabers with a Sith Lord. Game mechanics are meant to enhance the cinematic flair, not restrain it.

## Core Talents

### Block

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you may negate a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") by making a successful [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The DC of the check is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate, and you must take a cumulative -5 penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check for every time you have used **Block** or **Deflect** since the beginning of your last turn.

You may use the **Block** Talent to negate melee [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks"), such as those made by the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat. If you succeed on the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you take half damage if the attack hits, and no damage if the attack misses. You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Talent to negate an attack against an adjacent character.

You must have a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") drawn and ignited to use this Talent, and you must be aware of the attack and not [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

Jedi CounselingJedi Counseling

*Reference Book: [Web Enhancements](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Web_Enhancements "Web Enhancements") ([Jedi Counseling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Counseling "Jedi Counseling"))*

**Q:** The **Deflect** Talent can reduce the damage from an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack. Can **Block** reduce the damage from [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") (which is also resolved as an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack"))?

**A:** **Block** versus [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") (or any other melee [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") that might exist in the future) is supposed to work the same as **Deflect** versus [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the attack roll, you take half damage if the attack was a hit, and you take no damage if the attack was a miss. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is less than the attack roll, resolve the [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") normally.

Message From the Faculty: Saber LocksMessage From the Faculty: Saber Locks

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Many times two [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") combatants will strike at one another, locking their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") together in a test of strength and skill as their blades crackle with contact. As an optional rule, whenever a [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") with the **Block** Talent rolls their [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to negate the attack and the [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") result is exactly equal to the incoming attack roll, the attack is negated and a Saber Lock occurs. Both characters lock their [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers") together, and both the original attacker and the blocking Jedi make an opposed [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") check. The character with the higher result may make an immediate [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack against the other character as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), at which point the Saber Lock ends.*

### Deflect

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you may negate a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") by making a successful [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. The DC of the check is equal to the result of the attack roll you wish to negate, and you take a cumulative -5 penalty on your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check for every time you used **Block** or **Deflect** since the beginning of your last turn. You may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this Talent to negate an attack against an adjacent character.

You can use this Talent to deflect some of the barrage of shots fired from a ranged weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), or the *[Force Lightning](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Lightning "Force Lightning")* Force Power. If you succeed on the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you take half damage if the attack hits, and no damage if the attack misses.

You must have a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") drawn and ignited to use this Talent, and you must be aware of the attack and not [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed").

This Talent cannot be used to negate attacks made by Colossal (Frigate) or larger-size [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") unless the attack is made with a [Point-Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Defense "Point-Defense") Weapon System.

### Lightsaber Defense

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") you can use your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") to parry your opponents' attacks, gaining a +1 deflection bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn. You must have a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") drawn and ignited to use this Talent, and you don't gain the deflection bonus if you are [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") or otherwise unaware of the incoming attack.

You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, the deflection bonus increases by +1 (maximum +3).

### Weapon Specialization (Lightsabers)

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Focus (Lightsabers)")

You gain a +2 bonus on melee damage rolls with [Lightsabers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsabers "Lightsabers").

### Lightsaber Throw

You can throw a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), treating it as a [Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thrown_Weapon "Thrown Weapon") (without this Talent, a thrown [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is considered an [Improvised Thrown Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improvised_Thrown_Weapon "Improvised Thrown Weapon")). You are considered proficient with the thrown [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), and you apply the normal [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") penalties to the attack roll. The thrown [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") deals normal weapon damage if it hits.

If your target is no more than 6 squares away, you can pull your [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") back to your hand as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") by making a DC 20 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.

### Redirect Shot

*Prerequisites:* **Deflect**, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

This Talent allows you to redirect a deflected blaster bolt along a specific trajectory, so that it damages another creature or object in its path. Once per round when you successfully **Deflect** a blaster bolt, you can make an immediate ranged attack against another target with which you have line of sight.

Apply the normal range penalties to the attack roll, not counting the distance the bolt traveled to reach you. If the attack succeeds, it deals normal weapon damage to the target. Only single blaster bolts can be redirected in this manner. Barrages from [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") weapons and other types of projectiles can't be redirected.

## Additional Talents

### Cortosis Gauntlet Block

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)")

You have received additional training in the use of [Cortosis Gauntlets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlets "Cortosis Gauntlets"). You can use the **Block** Talent, even when not armed with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), provided you are wearing a [Cortosis Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlet "Cortosis Gauntlet"). If you successfully **Block** an attack with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") while wearing a [Cortosis Gauntlet](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cortosis_Gauntlet "Cortosis Gauntlet"), the attacking [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") is deactivated.

### Precise Redirect

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisite:* **Redirect Shot**

Whenever you successfully Redirect a blaster bolt and hit your target, the redirected attack deals +1 die of damage.

### Precision

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a melee attack with a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") against an adjacent opponent. If the attack hits, it deals normal damage and also reduces the target's speed to 2 squares until the end of your next turn.

### Riposte

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* **Block**, [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") once per encounter, you can make a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") attack against a being whose attack you successfully negated using the **Block** Talent. Only non-area melee attacks can be **Riposted** in this manner; you cannot use this Talent when negating the damage from melee [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") (such as those made with the [Whirlwind Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Whirlwind_Attack "Whirlwind Attack") feat).

### Shoto Focus

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

Whenever you wield both a one-handed [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber") and a [Short Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Short_Lightsaber "Short Lightsaber") (or [Guard Shoto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guard_Shoto "Guard Shoto")), you gain a +2 competence bonus on attack rolls made with the [Short Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Short_Lightsaber "Short Lightsaber") (or [Guard Shoto](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Guard_Shoto "Guard Shoto")).
