# Intercept

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intercept](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intercept) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

Force PowerStarship Maneuver

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to telekinetically hurl a small object in the path of an incoming projectile, preventing it from striking you.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")

**Targets:** One incoming [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") targeting you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check equals or exceeds the attack roll result of the incoming [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") (including all modifiers), you negate the attack against you.

**Special:** You can use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to negate an object being used as a projectile weapon with the [Move Light Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Light_Object "Move Light Object") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill, or the [*Move Object*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object") Force Power. In both cases, the DC for your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check is equal to the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check made by the opponent targeting you with the projectile. If you are successful, the object being used as a projectile weapon does not hit you, and deals no damage to you. Additionally, if the object was moved with the [*Move Object*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object") Force Power, the activator of that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") can no longer maintain the [*Move Object*](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object") Force Power on that object.

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to use this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to *Intercept* some of the barrage of shots from a ranged weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"). If you succeed on the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you take half damage if the [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack hits, and no damage if the [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack misses.

*Reference Book: [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

This maneuver allows a ship to fire thrusters and intercept a passing target, engaging in a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight").

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot") check.** If an enemy [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") or [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") moves into a square up to 2 squares away from you, you my activate this maneuver to attempt to initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"). The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You may initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight") as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target up to 2 squares away. If successful, you immediately move 1 square toward your target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). |
| 25 | You may initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), with a +1 maneuver bonus, as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target up to 2 squares away. If successful, you immediately move 1 square toward your target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). |
| 30 | You may initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), with a +2 maneuver bonus, as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target up to 2 squares away. If successful, you immediately move 1 square toward your target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). |
| 35 | You may initiate a [Dogfight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dogfight "Dogfight"), with a +5 maneuver bonus, as an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against a target up to 2 squares away. If successful, you immediately move 1 square toward your target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). |

**Special:** You activate this maneuver as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to a [Starfighter](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starfighter "Starfighter") or [Airspeeder](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Airspeeder "Airspeeder") moving into a square up to 2 squares away from you.
