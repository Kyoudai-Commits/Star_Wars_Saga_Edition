# Wary Defender

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wary_Defender](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wary_Defender) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Even in the heat of battle you can bolster yourself against nearly anything that comes your way.

**Effect:** When you use the [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") Action, you gain a +2 competence bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the beginning of your next turn.
