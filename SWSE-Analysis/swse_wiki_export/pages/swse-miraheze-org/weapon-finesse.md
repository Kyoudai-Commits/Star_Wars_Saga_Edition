# Weapon Finesse

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Finesse) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You are especially skilled at using weapons that can benefit as much from [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") as [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength").

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When using a [Light Melee Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Melee_Weapon "Light Melee Weapon") or a [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), you may use your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier instead of your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier on attack rolls.

**[Combined Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combined_Feat "Combined Feat"):** When you are wielding a single [One-Handed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Handed "One-Handed") weapon for which you have [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus"), you can treat that weapon as a [Light Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Weapon "Light Weapon") for the purpose of Weapon Finesse.
