# Repulse

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repulse](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repulse) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to clear an area around yourself.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** All adjacent targets.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The targets make opposing [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") checks, adding their [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"). If you exceed a target's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check, you push it back 1 square plus 1 additional square for every 5 points by which you exceeded the target's check result. If you push the target into a larger object, the target takes 1d6 points of damage.

The target adds a size modifier to its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check: Colossal, +20; Gargantuan, +15; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium, +0; Small, -5; Tiny, -10; Diminutive, -15; Fine, -20. In addition, it gets a +5 stability bonus if it has more than two legs or is otherwise exceptionally stable.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to apply a -5 penalty to the target's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check to resist your *Repulse*. Additionally, after spending a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"), if you successfully push the target into a larger object, you deal an extra 2d6 points of damage from the extreme force of the pulse.
