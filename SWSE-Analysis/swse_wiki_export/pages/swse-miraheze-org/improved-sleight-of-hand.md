# Improved Sleight of Hand

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Sleight_of_Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Sleight_of_Hand) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You manipulate small objects quickly, and they seemingly disappear with a flick of your wrist. You excel at hiding objects when observed, even at close range.

**Prerequisites:** [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 15, [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), [Skill Focus (Deception)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Deception) "Skill Focus (Deception)")

**Effect:** When using the [Sleight of Hand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sleight_of_Hand "Sleight of Hand") application of the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") skill, you can use your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") skill to improve your chances of success. Prior to making the [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, you can make a [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). The opposed [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check of anyone attempting to notice your action must exceed both your [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check and your [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check.

Additionally, you can, as a single Action, use Improved Sleight of Hand to simultaneously draw and palm a weapon two sizes smaller than you. Make a [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") when drawing your weapon to successfully conceal it in a position in your hand (or similar appendage), where you can use it on a following Action. Once the weapon is used, you can attempt to palm it again as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action").
