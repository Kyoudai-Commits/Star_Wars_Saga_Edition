# Risk Taker

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Risk_Taker](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Risk_Taker) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You're not afraid to make great leaps or climb treacherous slopes, and you know how to put forth the extra effort to make the impossible seem routine.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") or [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump")

**Effect:** You fall only if you fail a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check by 10 or more. Additionally, if you make a [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check and fail to land on a safe or solid surface (such as if you fall into a pit or fail to clear a stream of lava), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") and add the result of the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") roll to your distance jumped. However, you must land in the first available safe square.

**Normal:** You fall if you fail a [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") check by 5 or more.
