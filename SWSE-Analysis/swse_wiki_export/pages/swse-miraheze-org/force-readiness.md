# Force Readiness

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Readiness](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Readiness) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") is always with you, and you have a knack for succeeding even when the odds are against you.

**Effect:** You can spend [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), even if it is not your turn. All other restrictions to [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") use still apply.

**Normal:** Unless otherwise specified, you can spend [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") only on your turn, preventing you from enhancing attacks and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made as [Reactions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reactions "Reactions").
