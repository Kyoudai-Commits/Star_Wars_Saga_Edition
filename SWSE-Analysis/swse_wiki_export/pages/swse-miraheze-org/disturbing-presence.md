# Disturbing Presence

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disturbing_Presence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disturbing_Presence) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Your presence is such that enemies let you move past them freely.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception")

**Effect:** If you succeed on a DC 15 [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") check, you can move through the [Threatened Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened_Area "Threatened Area") or [Fighting Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fighting_Space "Fighting Space") of an enemy as part of your [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") Action without provoking an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"). Each [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") space or occupied space that you move through in this manner counts as 2 squares of movement.

**Normal:** You can [Tumble](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tumble "Tumble") through an occupied enemy square only by using the [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics") skill.
