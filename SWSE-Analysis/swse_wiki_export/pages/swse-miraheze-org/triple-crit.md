# Triple Crit

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Crit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triple_Crit) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:34Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Choose one type of weapon, such as a [Blaster Pistol](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Pistol "Blaster Pistol"), [Vibrodagger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vibrodagger "Vibrodagger"), or [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"). You deal more damage on a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with that weapon.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +8

**Effect:** Choose one weapon with which you are proficient. When you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") with the selected weapon, you deal triple damage. You may select "[Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack")" as a weapon for purposes of this feat.

**Normal:** A [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") normally deals double damage to the target.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. The effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different weapon. It's possible to take Triple Crit with a specific [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon").
