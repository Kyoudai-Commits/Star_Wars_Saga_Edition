# Jedi Knight

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Among [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi"), those of the Order who have proven capable, wise, self-sufficient, and stable are given the rank of Knight. A Jedi apprentice must learn to use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") without instability or strain, build their own [Lightsaber](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber "Lightsaber"), and show an understanding of [The Jedi Code](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Code "The Jedi Code") (though such understanding may shift from era to era, as the nature of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") differs somewhat in each [Era of Play](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Era_of_Play "Era of Play")). During most eras, a Jedi must pass a series of tests before becoming a Jedi Knight, which may be as simple as fulfilling a single important mission assigned by a [Jedi Master](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Master "Jedi Master"), or as complex as a series of trials to test the Jedi's mettle, ethics, and dedication. In a few of chaos and uncertainty, Jedi apprentices are forced by circumstance to push themselves past the normal boundaries of their training and become Knights in function, if not in name, with no formal testing. In these rare cases, the ruling Jedi generally acknowledge the Jedi's status as a Knight as soon as circumstances allow.

Jedi Knights are trusted to teach apprentices, carry out missions with little aid or support, and use their best judgment when handling the unexpected. A Jedi Knight is expected to deal with any problem in a manner that reflects well on [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") as a whole, and carry out the Jedi goals of defending justice and maintaining peace. Jedi are the guardians of the galaxy, and the Jedi Knight is a skilled individual expected to uphold that trust.

**Examples of Jedi Knights in Star Wars:** Anakin Skywalker (Episode III), Barriss Offee, Bastila Shan, Kyle Katarn, Luke Skywalker (Episode VI), Nomi Sunrider, Ulic Qel-Droma.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Jedi Knight, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** +7
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force")
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity"), [Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Lightsabers) "Weapon Proficiency (Lightsabers)")
- **Special:** Must be a member of [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and have completed [Lightsaber Construction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Construction "Lightsaber Construction")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Jedi Knights gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Jedi Knights gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Jedi Knights gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Jedi Knight gains an additional [Jedi Knight Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight_Talent "Jedi Knight Talent"). A Jedi Knight may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Jedi Knight Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Knight_Talent_Trees "Jedi Knight Talent Trees") or [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Force Techniques

You have learned improved techniques to feel the rhythm of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). At every even-numbered level (2nd, 4th, 6th, and so on), a Jedi Knight gains one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") that, once selected, cannot be changed.
