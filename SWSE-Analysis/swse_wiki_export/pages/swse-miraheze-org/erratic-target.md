# Erratic Target

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erratic_Target](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erratic_Target) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You bob, swerve, dip, and dive to avoid incoming shots and other attacks.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering "Hovering") or [Flying](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flying "Flying") Locomotion, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge")

**Effect:** You can reduce your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by up to 2 squares to increase your dodge bonus by 1 for each square of [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") you give up, until the start of your next turn. You must move at least 2 squares to gain this benefit.

**Special:** To use this feat you must be equipped with and using [Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering "Hovering") or [Flying](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flying "Flying") Locomotion.
