# Pin

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:32Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are skilled at immobilizing [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled") foes.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** If you succeed on a [Grappling](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappling "Grappling") attack and your opponent fails the opposed [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check, your opponent is automatically Pinned until the start of your next turn. A Pinned creature can't move or take any [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") while Pinned, and it loses its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus (if any) to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

**Special:** You cannot use the Pin and [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip") feats during the same round. You can use the Pin and [Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush "Crush") feats in the same round, however.
