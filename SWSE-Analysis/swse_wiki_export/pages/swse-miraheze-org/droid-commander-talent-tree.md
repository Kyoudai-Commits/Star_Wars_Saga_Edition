# Droid Commander Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have learned the quirks and intricacies of commanding [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"), and you can get the most out of even the least sophisticated basic programming.

## Core Talents

### Automated Strike

*Prerequisite:* [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack")

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check. If successful, all [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies able to hear and understand you gain the benefits of the [Double Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Attack "Double Attack") feat for one [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you are proficient until the end of your next turn.

### Droid Defense

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can transmit tactical information to all [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies that can hear and understand you, granting them a bonus equal to your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier to one of their [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") (your choice) until the beginning of your next turn.

### Droid Mettle

*Prerequisite:* **Droid Defense**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can designate a single [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). That [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally gains [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to 10 + your Class Level.

### Expanded Sensors

If you or any of your [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies has [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to, and is aware of, a target, all [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies that can hear and understand you are also considered to have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") (but not necessarily [Line of Effect](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Effect "Line of Effect")) to that target.

### Inspire Competence

*Prerequisite:* **Expanded Sensors**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can grant one [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") a competence bonus on its next attack roll made before the start of your next turn equal to half your Class Level. Additionally, any [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") designated as the target of your [Network Mind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Network_Mind?action=edit&redlink=1 "Network Mind (page does not exist)") Class Feature is considered to have a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor") whenever it is beneficial, even if it does not actually have a [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor").

### Maintain Focus

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can grant all [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") the ability to take the [Recover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recover "Recover") Action as two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") (instead of as three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions")) until the start of your next turn.

### Overclocked Troops

*Prerequisite:* **Droid Defense**

You push the limits of the [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") under your command. You can spend a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn to allow each of your Networked allies (see the [Network Mind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Network_Mind?action=edit&redlink=1 "Network Mind (page does not exist)") Class Feature) to immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to their [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").

### Reinforce Commands

*Prerequisite:* **Droid Defense**

When you use an ability that grants a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") ally a morale or insight bonus, increase the value of that bonus by 1.
