# Mon Calamari Shipwright

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari_Shipwright](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari_Shipwright) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:50Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You have intimate knowledge of the workings of [Starships](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships "Starships") and other [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles") and can keep them running no matter what.

**Prerequisite:** [Mon Calamari](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mon_Calamari "Mon Calamari")

**Effect:** You need spend only two [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to move a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") you occupy +1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). In addition, you automatically succeed on [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks to [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power").

**Normal:** It takes three [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") to use the [Reroute Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reroute_Power "Reroute Power") Action.
