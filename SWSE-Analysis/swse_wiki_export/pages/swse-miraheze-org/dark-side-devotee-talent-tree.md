# Dark Side Devotee Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Devotee_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Devotee_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Your powerful negative emotions allow [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") to flow through you, giving you great power.

## Core Talents

### Channel Aggression

If you succeed on an attack against a [Flanked](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanked "Flanked") opponent, or any target that is denied its [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") to deal additional damage to the target equal to 1d6 per Class Level (maximum 10d6).

### Channel Anger

*Prerequisite:* **Channel Aggression**

You let your anger swell into a Rage. As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to gain a +2 Rage bonus on melee attack rolls and melee damage rolls for a number of rounds equal to 5 + your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier. At the end of this duration, you move -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). While Raging, you cannot use [Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skills "Skills") that require patience and concentration, such as [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), or [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force").

### Crippling Strike

*Prerequisite:* **Channel Aggression**

Whenever you score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit"), you may spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to also reduce the target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by half until they are fully healed (that is, restored to maximum [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points")).

### Embrace the Dark Side

*Prerequisites:* **Channel Anger**

Whenever you use a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, but you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse. Upon choosing this Talent, you can no longer use [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor.

## Additional Talents

### Dark Side Talisman

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a weapon or some other portable object with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), creating a Talisman that grants you protection from [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side"). Creating the **Dark Side Talisman** takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). While you wear or carry the Talisman, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to one of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") ([Reflex](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex "Reflex"), [Fortitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude "Fortitude"), or [Will](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will "Will")) against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor.

You can only have one **Dark Side Talisman** active at a given time (though you can have both a **Dark Side Talisman** and a **[Force Talisman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talisman "Force Talisman")** active at the same time), and if your **Dark Side Talisman** is destroyed, you cannot create another one for 24 hours.

### Greater Dark Side Talisman

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Side Talisman**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to imbue a weapon or some other portable object with [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), creating a Talisman that grants you protection from [The Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Light_Side "The Light Side"). Creating the **Greater Dark Side Talisman** takes a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"). While you wear or carry the Talisman, you gain a +2 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all of your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") against [Force Powers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Powers "Force Powers") with the [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor.

You can only have one **Greater Dark Side Talisman**, or **Dark Side Talisman**, active at a given time (though you can have both a **Greater Dark Side Talisman** and a **[Greater Force Talisman](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Greater_Force_Talisman "Greater Force Talisman")** active at the same time), and if your **Greater Dark Side Talisman** is destroyed, you cannot create another one for 24 hours.
