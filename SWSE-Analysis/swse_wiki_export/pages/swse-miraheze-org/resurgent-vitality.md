# Resurgent Vitality

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resurgent_Vitality](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resurgent_Vitality) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

Though your Species heals quickly over time, you can push through your pain in the short term and return to fighting form.

**Prerequisite:** [Wookiee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wookiee "Wookiee")

**Effect:** Whenever you catch a [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), you regain an additional number of [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to twice your [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") bonus (minimum +2).
