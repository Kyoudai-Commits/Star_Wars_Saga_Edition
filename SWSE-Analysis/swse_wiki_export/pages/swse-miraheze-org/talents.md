# Talents

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:20Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

Talents are a unique ability that allow a character to perform extraordinary deeds. These deeds cannot be done by just anyone, and are primarily based on that particular creature's [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class"). Every Talent can be found in a Talent Tree, which is a collection of Talents with similar functions. Talent Trees, in general, can be divided into two primary types: Class Talent Trees, and [Force Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Talent_Trees "Force Talent Trees"). Class Talent Trees are Talent Trees that are only available to specific [Heroic Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Classes "Heroic Classes"), or [Prestige Classes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Classes "Prestige Classes"), while Force Talent Trees are available to anyone with the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat ([Tradition Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tradition_Talent_Trees "Tradition Talent Trees") are a subset of Force Talent Trees, available only to members of particular [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions")).

At 1st level and every odd-numbered level taken in a particular [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") thereafter (3rd, 5th, 7th, etc.), a character gains a Talent from any one of the Talent Trees available to the [Heroic Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heroic_Class "Heroic Class") or [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class"). Any Talent in the Tree may be chosen, but the prerequisites (if any) must be met. No Talent can be chosen more than once unless expressly indicated.
