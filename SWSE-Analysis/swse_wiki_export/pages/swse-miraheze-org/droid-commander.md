# Droid Commander

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

All armies need an established hierarchy to ensure that objectives are achieved successfully. Even Battle Droids, with their network connection to a central command computer, must sometimes have specific movements dictated to them by a separate [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") designated as a field commander. The Droid Commander, through the course of its career as a soldier for its organization, proves itself through its actions and is promoted to lead others of its kind to support the unit's mission and the organization's cause.

Droid Commanders are easy to spot on the battlefield. They bear command colors and are positioned within the ranks to receive maximum protection from the enemy. Although they might be easy to find, they are rarely easy to eliminate. Because these [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") are prized by their superiors, they are augmented and improved to increase the likelihood of their survival and continued service to their masters. Better weapons, armor and equipment in addition to upgraded systems make the Droid Commander a formidable foe on its own. Its ability to effectively lead troops on the battlefield make the Droid Commander and its squad a force to be reckoned with.

**Examples of Droid Commanders in Star Wars:** OLR-4, OOM-9, OOM-14.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become a Droid Commander, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 7th
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)"), [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** Any one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from either the
  - [Leadership Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Leadership_Talent_Tree "Leadership Talent Tree") or
  - [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree")
- **Special:** Must be a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Droid Commanders gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Droid Commanders gain 1d10 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").
- **Force Points:** Droid Commanders gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, every time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), a Droid Commander gains an additional [Droid Commander Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander_Talent "Droid Commander Talent"). A Droid Commander may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Droid Commander Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Commander_Talent_Trees "Droid Commander Talent Trees") they wish (or [Droid Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Talents "Droid Talents") from their [Droid Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Degree "Droid Degree")), but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Networked Mind

A Droid Commander can designate a number of its Droid Allies equal to one-half its Class Level as being networked with it. A Networked Droid Ally can receive beneficial Mind-Affecting effects from the Droid Commander. Once the Droid Commander designates a Droid Ally as the recipient of this benefit, it cannot change the [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") beneficiary until after the end of the encounter, and designating a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") to benefit from this Class Feature is a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action").

Additionally, the Droid Commander is considered to have [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to a Networked Droid Ally as long as they both have an active [Comlink](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlink "Comlink") (if the [Comlinks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Comlinks "Comlinks") are subject to communications jamming, it loses this benefit).
