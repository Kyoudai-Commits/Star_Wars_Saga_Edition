# Enforcement Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enforcement_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enforcement_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:11Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are a trained investigator who can locate and apprehend a quarry with exceptional speed and prowess.

## Core Talents

### Cover Bracing

You can [Brace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brace "Brace") a weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") (instead of two) if you are adjacent to an object (including walls, barriers, and [Vehicles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles "Vehicles")) that provides you with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from all of the target squares.

### Intentional Crash

*Prerequisite:* [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pilot "Pilot")

You know how to intentionally crash an opponent's moving [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"). When you successfully deal damage to a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") by [Ramming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ramming "Ramming") it, your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes half damage from the [Ram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ram "Ram"). Additionally, if the target [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") is the same size as your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") or smaller, that [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") cannot [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") in the following round.

### Nonlethal Tactics

When you are using a ranged weapon set to [Stun](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun "Stun"), [Stun Grenades](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun_Grenades "Stun Grenades"), [Nets](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nets "Nets"), or [Stun Batons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun_Batons "Stun Batons"), you gain a +1 bonus on your attack roll and deal +1 die of [Stun Damage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stun_Damage "Stun Damage").

### Pursuit

*Prerequisite:* [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13

When [Running](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running "Running"), you are not restricted to a straight line; and you can reroll [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") checks, keeping the better of the two results, while [Running](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running "Running").

### Respected Officer

You have a reputation that causes allies and opponents to treat you with respect. You automatically improve the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of an [Indifferent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Indifferent "Indifferent") character to [Friendly](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Friendly "Friendly") with no check required.

### Slowing Stun

When you move a target at least -1 step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") with an attack, its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is halved until all [Conditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Conditions "Conditions") are removed.

### Takedown

When you successfully make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") and deal damage at the end of a [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge"), you knock your target [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") as well, provided your opponent is no more than one size category larger than you.
