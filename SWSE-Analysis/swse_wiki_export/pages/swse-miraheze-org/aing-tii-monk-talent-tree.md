# Aing-Tii Monk Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii_Monk_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aing-Tii_Monk_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

[The Aing-Tii Monks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks "The Aing-Tii Monks") have an understanding of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") that is different from [The Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi "The Jedi") and most other [Force Traditions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Traditions "Force Traditions"). [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") has many more aspects than just a Light Side and a Dark Side, and it is most useful for facilitating freedom of travel. Only Force-users that are part of [The Aing-Tii Monks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Aing-Tii_Monks "The Aing-Tii Monks") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Aura of Freedom

All allies within 6 squares of you gain a +5 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") checks made to escape [Grapples](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapples "Grapples") or other [Immobilizing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Immobilizing "Immobilizing") Hazards. Additionally, whenever an ally within 6 squares of you is moved against its will (such as by the *[Move Object](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Object "Move Object")* Force Power or the [Bantha Rush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bantha_Rush "Bantha Rush") feat), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to negate the forced movement entirely.

### Folded Space Mastery

*Prerequisite:* *[Fold Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space "Fold Space")*

While you are the [Vehicle Pilot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Pilot "Vehicle Pilot") of a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you can use the *[Fold Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fold_Space "Fold Space")* Force Power to move the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") across long distances. If your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate the Force Power is sufficient to move an object the same size as your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") (or larger), you can use the Force Power to move your [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") and all of its occupants safely to the desired destination.

You can use your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check result instead of a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, as though performing [Astrogation](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Astrogation "Astrogation"). This otherwise uses the normal rules for hyperspace travel, though travel is instantaneous and requires no [Hyperdrive](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hyperdrive "Hyperdrive").

### Liberate

*Prerequisite:* **Aura of Freedom**

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to designate one ally within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") that is currently [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed"), [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled"), or [Immobilized](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Immobilized "Immobilized"); that target automatically escapes from the [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") or [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") (or the [Immobilization](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Immobilization "Immobilization") effect is removed), and the target can move up to half its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") immediately as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Many Shades of the Force

*Prerequisite:* [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

Choose one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] or [*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*] descriptor in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). That [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is no longer considered to have that descriptor for you.

### Spatial Integrity

While you are aboard a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") taking damage; you make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and reduce the damage the [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") takes by the check result. This reduction occurs after both DR and SR are applied to the damage.
