# Kaleesh

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kaleesh](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kaleesh) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:36Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/3/37/Kaleesh.webp/250px-Kaleesh.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Kaleesh.webp)

The Kaleesh are a nomadic Species with a tribal culture. They are spiritual beings who worship their ancestors as deities, and they consider their ancestral burial grounds to be sacred lands. The Kaleesh believe that anyone who dies in honorable service to their people becomes a god upon death.

The Kaleesh come to a momentary prominence in the galaxy when they fight off an invasion by an alien [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") known as the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)"). Having already subjugated and exploited the natural resources of several worlds, the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)") capture millions of Kaleesh and sell them into slavery. However, the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)") invasion of [Kalee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kalee "Kalee") is short lived. Under the leadership of a hero who eventually comes to be known as Grievous, the Kaleesh repel the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)"). They doggedly pursue their enemies into space, and the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)") are completely overcome by the Kaleesh. The hunters become the hunted. The [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)") request assistance from the Republic, and through a number of underhanded dealings, they receive it. As a result, the Republic subjects the Kaleesh to serve economic sanctions, and the Kaleesh are marginalized by galactic society.

## Kaleesh Characteristics

**Personality:** Kaleesh are a warlike Species, but they reserve their ire for those who provoke them. They are protective of their territory- especially their ancient burial grounds. Kaleesh hold their families and tribes in high esteem, and do anything to protect them. These sentiments sometimes extend to non-Kaleesh who prove themselves to be trustworthy companions, but such a distinction is rare. Honor and sacrifice at the expense of oneself are highly valued qualities in their society.

**Physical Description:** The Kaleesh are tall, reptilian humanoids. Their scaly skin varies from from deep red to dull yellow. Their black hair is worn in long braids, or tied up in a queue or topknot. The long faces of the Kaleesh are framed on either side by two sharp tusks that extend outward from their jawbones, and their nostrils are two long slits that stretch toward their yellow eyes. However, few non-Kaleesh have seen the faces of these enigmatic aliens, for they wear masks made from the skulls of some of Kalee's most vicious predators. The Kaleesh are also distinguished by their hands, which each feature four long fingers, two of which are fully opposable. A typical Kaleesh male stands at 1.8 meters tall and weighs 80 kilograms, while a typical Kaleesh female stands at 1.6 meters tall and weighs 75 kilograms.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Kaleesh age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-9 Years | 10-24 Years | 25-40 Years | 41-55 Years | 56-79 Years | 80+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The craggy world of [Kalee](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kalee "Kalee"), located in [Wild Space](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wild_Space?action=edit&redlink=1 "Wild Space (page does not exist)").

**Languages:** The Kaleesh speak their own sophisticated language of Kaleesh, and after the [Huk](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Huk?action=edit&redlink=1 "Huk (page does not exist)") invasion, most also learn Basic.

**Example Names:** Qymaen jai Sheelal, Ronderu lij Kummar, Bentilais san Sk'ar.

**Adventurers:** The Kaleesh excel in the roles of [Scout](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scout "Scout") and [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier") (especially in [The Kolkpravis](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Kolkpravis "The Kolkpravis")). Given their inherent sense of honor and dignity, they also make excellent [Nobles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nobles "Nobles"), but rarely take on the role of a [Scoundrel](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scoundrel "Scoundrel"). Although no Kaleesh [Jedi](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi "Jedi") are known, Kaleesh spiritual traditions view [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") as a boon granted by their ancestors.

## Kaleesh Species Traits

Kaleesh share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** All Kaleesh receive a +2 bonus to both their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") scores, but suffer a -2 penalty to both their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") and [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") scores. Kaleesh are sturdy and work well with others, but lack sophistication and precision.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Kaleesh have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Kaleesh have a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Conditional Bonus Feat:** Kaleesh are a nomadic people who have learned to live off the land. A Kaleesh with [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") as a [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") gains [Skill Focus (Survival)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Survival) "Skill Focus (Survival)") as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
- **Darkvision:** Because of the thermoreceptor glands next to their eyes, Kaleesh ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (including [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness. However, they cannot perceive colors in total darkness.
- **Driven:** Kaleesh are focused individuals who maintain sight of their goals even under adverse conditions. Because of this single-minded drive, they gain a +5 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting effects.
- **Persistent:** A Kaleesh can reroll any [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted, even if it is worse.
- **Automatic Languages:** All Kaleesh can speak, read, and write both Basic and Kaleesh.
