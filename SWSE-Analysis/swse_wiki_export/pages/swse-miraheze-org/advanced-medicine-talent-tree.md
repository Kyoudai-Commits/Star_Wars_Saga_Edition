# Advanced Medicine Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Medicine_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advanced_Medicine_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:10Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You are highly trained in medical procedures for all conditions.

## Core Talents

### Battlefield Medic

*Prerequisite:* **Steady Under Pressure**

You can use the [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid") application of the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill on a creature as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") instead of a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action").

### Bring Them Back

You can use the [Revivify](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Revivify "Revivify") application of the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill on a target that has died anytime within a number of rounds equal to one-half your Heroic Level.

### Emergency Team

You are skilled at working on and managing an emergency medical team. Allies automatically succeed on [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") attempts when assisting you with [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks.

### Extra First Aid

You can use the [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid") application of the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") skill one additional time per day on a target that has already received [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid") for the day.

### Medical Miracle

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check on an adjacent target. If the check is successful, that target immediately uses its [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), even if it is above half [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points"). If the target has already expended all of its [Second Winds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Winds "Second Winds") for the day, this Talent has no effect.

### Natural Healing Techniques

Your extensive knowledge of [Natural Healing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Natural_Healing "Natural Healing") allows you to make [First Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/First_Aid "First Aid"), [Treat Disease](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Disease "Treat Disease"), and [Treat Poison](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Poison "Treat Poison") checks without a [Medical Kit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Kit "Medical Kit"), if you have access to appropriate natural substitutes (as determined by the Gamemaster).

### Second Chance

*Prerequisite:* **Steady Under Pressure**

If you fail your [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check, your patient does not take any additional damage, nor does it die, even if the failed check would normally require it.

### Steady Under Pressure

You can choose to reroll any [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check, keeping the better of the two results.
