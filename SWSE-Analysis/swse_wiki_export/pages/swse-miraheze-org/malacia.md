# Malacia

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malacia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Malacia) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You create dizziness and nausea by disrupting your target's equilibrium.

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One living creature within 6 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result to your target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target moves -1 step down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). Additionally, until the end of its next turn, the target takes a -5 penalty to its [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the penalty to the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") to -10.

You can maintain *Malacia* from round to round, extending the duration of the [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") penalty. Maintaining the *Malacia* Force Power is a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Malacia*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Malacia*.
