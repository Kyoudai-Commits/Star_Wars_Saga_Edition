# Resolute Stance

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resolute_Stance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Resolute_Stance) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:00Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

By standing your ground, you focus your mind to repel any assault.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively"), you gain a +2 morale bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If you make no attacks until your next turn, you gain a +5 morale bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") until the start of your next turn.

**Normal:** When you [Fight Defensively](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_Defensively "Fight Defensively") you only get a dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
