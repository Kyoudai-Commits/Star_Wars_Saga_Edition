# Sith Commander Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Commander_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sith_Commander_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

[The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") use terror to lead their troops, and Sith Commanders have few compunctions about making examples of underlings. Therefore, it is not surprising that [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") use anger and fear of their subordinates to their own terrible advantage.

## Core Talents

### Desperate Measures

*Prerequisite:* **Focus Terror**

Desperation stems from fear. Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can instill desperation in all allies within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), allowing each of them to make an immediate attack at a -5 penalty.

### Focus Terror

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can harness the fear felt by your allies and transform it into a powerful motivational tool. All allies within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") move +2 steps along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), but suffer a -2 penalty on attack rolls and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") for a number of rounds equal to your Character Level.

### Incite Rage

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can channel your anger and hatred into your allies. All allies within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") gain a +1 Rage bonus on attack rolls, but take a -2 penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"). This effect lasts until the encounter ends, or until you are knocked unconscious or killed.

### Power of Hatred

*Prerequisite:* **Incite Rage**

Once per encounter as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can inflame the passions of your allies. Each ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") who has fewer than half its normal [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") gains [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") equal to your Character Level. Damage is subtracted from the [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") first, and any [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") remaining at the end of the encounter go away. [Bonus Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Hit_Points "Bonus Hit Points") from multiple sources do not stack.
