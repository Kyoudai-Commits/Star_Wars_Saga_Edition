# Halt

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Halt](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Halt) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:02Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

When making an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), you can stop an enemy in its tracks.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +8, [Trip](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trip "Trip"), [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** If you hit with an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), while wielding a weapon for which you possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat, against an enemy of up to one size larger than you, compare the same die roll against the target's [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check. If the die roll succeeds against the [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check, the target immediately halts all movement and falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") in its current square. The target can spend any remaining [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") normally.

If your attack exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), the target loses any remaining [Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Actions "Actions") and ends its turn immediately. Apply damage normally to the target regardless of the results of this feat.

**Special:** If the target is using the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") Action when moving past you, its [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") is ended if you successfully knock it [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone"), and the target cannot [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") again on this turn.
