# Cornered

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cornered](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cornered) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

You are vicious when cornered.

**Effect:** Whenever you are [Threatened](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threatened "Threatened") by an opponent and unable to take the [Withdraw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Withdraw "Withdraw") Action, you gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against opponents that [Threaten](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Threaten "Threaten") you.
