# Ascension Specialists

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ascension_Specialists](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ascension_Specialists) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You and your allies train on the sheer cliffs of [Teth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Teth "Teth") and the sinkholes of [Utapau](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utapau "Utapau") as a matter of course.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb")

**Effect:** You gain a +3 competence bonus on [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb") checks. At the time you make a check, for each ally within 12 squares of you who also has this feat, the competence bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +7).

Additionally, when you climb, you climb at half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") (or at your normal speed as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action")).
