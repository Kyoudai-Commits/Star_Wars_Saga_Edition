# Unwavering Resolve

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unwavering_Resolve](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unwavering_Resolve) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You are not easily swayed by the words of others, and can see through deceptions thanks to your keen mind.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception")

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") and [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks.
