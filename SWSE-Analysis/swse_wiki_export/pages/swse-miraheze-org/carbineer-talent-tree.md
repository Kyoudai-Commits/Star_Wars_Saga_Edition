# Carbineer Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carbineer_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Carbineer_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

While [Pistols](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pistols "Pistols") offer great versatility and mobility, their [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") is a significant limiting factor. Thus, many [Gunslingers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslingers "Gunslingers") choose to specialize in long arms, especially [Blaster Carbines](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blaster_Carbines "Blaster Carbines").

## Core Talents

### Blowback

When you make an attack with a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") that deals damage in excess of your target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), you can choose to push the target 1 square away from you.

### Close Contact

The Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") of any [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") or carbine you use is increased by 5 squares. Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") for the weapon begins 5 squares later, but still ends at the same distance. You can take this Talent up to two times; each time you take this Talent, you increase the Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range") of any [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") or carbine you use by an additional 5 squares, up to a maximum of 10 squares.

### Multiattack Proficiency (Rifles)

Whenever you make multiple attacks with any type of [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") (see [Full Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full_Attack "Full Attack")), you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by 2. You can take this Talent multiple times; each time you take this Talent, you reduce the penalty on your attack rolls by an additional 2.

### Old Faithful

The [Trusty Sidearm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trusty_Sidearm "Trusty Sidearm") Class Feature of the [Gunslinger](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunslinger "Gunslinger") Prestige Class also applies to any [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") or carbine that you use.

### Opportunity Fire

You gain a +2 bonus on [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") made with [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles").

### Rifle Master

You treat all [Rifles](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifles "Rifles") as though they were [Accurate](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Accurate "Accurate") weapons, taking no penalty when firing at targets at Short [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

### Shoot from the Hip

You can always use a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") to make [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").

### Snap Shot

You do not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity") while using the [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") Action with a [Rifle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rifle "Rifle") or carbine with its [Retractable Stock](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Retractable_Stock "Retractable Stock") extended.
