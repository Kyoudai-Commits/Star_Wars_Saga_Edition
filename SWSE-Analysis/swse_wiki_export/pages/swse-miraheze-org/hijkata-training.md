# Hijkata Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hijkata_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hijkata_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the Hijkata style of unarmed fighting and can quickly turn enemies' attacks against them.

**Prerequisites:** [Combat Reflexes](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combat_Reflexes "Combat Reflexes"), [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** Once per round, when an adjacent enemy successfully damages you with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack"), you can make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack") at a -5 penalty against that enemy, even if the enemy's attack normally would not provoke an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity").

Once per encounter, when an enemy provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you and you successfully damage that enemy with an Unarmed [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), that enemy takes a penalty to attack rolls equal to your [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") modifier (minimum +1, for a minimum penalty of -1) until the end of your next turn.

**Special:** If you have the **[Hijkata Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hijkata_Expertise "Hijkata Expertise")** Talent, once per encounter, you can spend a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") to designate a single adjacent ally. Until the start of your next turn, any enemy adjacent to you or the designated ally that makes an attack roll against that ally provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from you. If the ally moves during his or her turn, you can immediately move a number of squares up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"); however, you must end your movement in a square that is adjacent to the designated ally.
