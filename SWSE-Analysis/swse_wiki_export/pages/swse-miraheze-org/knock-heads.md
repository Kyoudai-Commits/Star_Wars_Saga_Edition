# Knock Heads

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knock_Heads](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knock_Heads) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You knock two opponents' heads together in unarmed combat.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 13, [Multi-Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Grab "Multi-Grab")

**Effect:** After successfully using the [Multi-Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Multi-Grab "Multi-Grab") feat against two targets that are both adjacent to you and each other, you can immediately knock their heads together, dealing automatic bludgeoning damage to each creature equal to 1d6 plus your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier. When comparing the damage to each creature's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), treat their [Damage Thresholds](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Thresholds "Damage Thresholds") as if they were 5 points lower. Both creatures are still considered [Grabbed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grabbed "Grabbed") at the end of using this [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat").
