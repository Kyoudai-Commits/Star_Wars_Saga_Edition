# Sniper Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You make a precision attack at the expense of your own defense.

**Prerequisite:** Proficient with [Ranged Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Weapon "Ranged Weapon") used (other than [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons"))

**Effect:** When wielding only weapons with which you are proficient, you can gain a +2 bonus on all your [Ranged Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attacks "Ranged Attacks") until the beginning of your next turn, but you take a -5 penalty to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

**Special:** This [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat") cannot be used with [Vehicle Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapons "Vehicle Weapons") or [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons").
