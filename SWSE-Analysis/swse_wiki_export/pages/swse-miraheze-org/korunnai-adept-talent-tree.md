# Korunnai Adept Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korunnai_Adept_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Korunnai_Adept_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:14Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have learned to use *Pelekotan* to survive in the jungles of [Haruun Kal](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Haruun_Kal?action=edit&redlink=1 "Haruun Kal (page does not exist)") and have bonded with an Akk Dog, which serves you faithfully. Only Force-users that are part of [The Korunnai](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Korunnai "The Korunnai") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Akk Dog Master

You gain an Akk Dog [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower"). Choose either an [Aggressive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aggressive_Follower "Aggressive Follower"), [Defensive Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defensive_Follower "Defensive Follower"), or [Utility Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Utility_Follower "Utility Follower"), and generate the Akk Dog Follower's statistics using the Akk Dog Follower Template. The Akk Dog Follower automatically gains the [Power Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Attack "Power Attack") Feat as a bonus [Feat](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat "Feat"). Additionally, any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") you activate that targets you can target your Akk Dog Follower instead, at your discretion. An Akk Dog counts toward the total number of [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") you have, just like [Followers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Followers "Followers") gained from other [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").

Akk Dog Follower TemplateAkk Dog Follower Template

When a character gains an Akk Dog Follower, the [Follower](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Follower "Follower") gains the Species Traits of the Akk Dog described below. Akk Dogs are considered [Beasts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beasts "Beasts").

- **Ability Scores:** Akk Dogs have set [Ability Scores](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ability_Scores "Ability Scores"). They use the following statistics: [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 16, [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 8, [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") 12, [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") 10, [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") 2, [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") 8.
- **Large Size:** Akk Dogs take a -1 size penalty to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") and a -5 size penalty to [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") checks. Their lifting and carrying capacities are double those of Medium creatures.
- **Speed:** An Akk Dog has a base speed of 6 squares.
- **Natural Armor:** Akk Dogs gain a +2 Natural Armor bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").
- **Natural Weapons:** When an Akk Dog makes an [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") attack, it can use its Natural Weapons, dealing 1d6 points of Slashing damage (plus its [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier) with that attack instead of normal [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage.

### Akk Dog Trainer's Actions

*Prerequisite:* **Akk Dog Master**

You and your Akk Dog have bonded through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") and can fight in concert. You can use any of the following actions on your turn:

- *Attack in Concert:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If your Akk Dog Follower is adjacent to the target and your attack hits, the target also takes Piercing damage equal to 1d6 + the Akk Dog's [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier.
  - This additional damage is considered part of your attack for the purposes of resolving damage, [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"), and overcoming [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").
- *Fall Upon Prey:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"), and your Akk Dog can take the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") Action against a target within its [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). However, both you and your Akk Dog take a -5 penalty on your attack rolls (this replaces the bonus to attack rolls granted by the [Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge "Charge") Action).
- *Paired Maul:* As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target in [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If the attack hits, your Akk Dog Follower gains a +2 competence bonus on its next attack roll against that target.

### Akk Dog Attack Training

*Prerequisite:* **Akk Dog Master**

Your Akk Dog Follower gains the [Powerful Charge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powerful_Charge "Powerful Charge") Feat.

### Protective Reaction

*Prerequisite:* **Akk Dog Master**

Whenever you are targeted by an attack made by an enemy adjacent to your Akk Dog Follower, that attacker provokes an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") from your Akk Dog Follower.
