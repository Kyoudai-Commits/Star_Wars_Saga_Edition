# Independent Droid

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:26Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

Although many [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") in the galaxy function on their own, some become highly independent and self-sufficient. These specialized [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") prefer to operate on their own, or in the company of a specific group useful to their current needs. Members of this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class") usually must experience a program-breaking event. This event could occur as the result of conflicting orders, a malfunctioning [Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Processor "Processor"), or a deliberate action taken by a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") programmer- either an organic creature or another [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"). Players and Gamemasters are encouraged to develop such an event before using this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

Independent Droids express their individuality much more strongly than a typical [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") unit does. One might focus on a very narrow segment of its programming. Another might develop a complex or damaged personality. Independent Droids universally reject the notion that they are owned by anyone, though the more deceptive or pragmatic units among them could give the appearance of having such an attitude if they believe that doing so is necessary to ultimately retain their independence or avoid a [Memory Wipe](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Memory_Wipe "Memory Wipe").

In a [Dark Times Campaign](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Times_Campaign "Dark Times Campaign"), Independent Droids might show up on any world. Some worlds, such as the junk world of [Raxus Prime](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raxus_Prime "Raxus Prime"), could have a large number of these [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids"). [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") that survived [The Clone Wars](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Clone_Wars "The Clone Wars") but were subsequently abandoned, stranded, or isolated for other reasons are likely candidates for this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

**Examples of Independent Droids in Star Wars:** HK-47, IG-88, PROXY, Blue Max, Bollux.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become an Independent Droid, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **Minimum Level:** 3rd
- **[Trained Skills](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skills "Trained Skills"):** [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer")
- **[Droid Systems](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Systems "Droid Systems"):** [Heuristic Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heuristic_Processor "Heuristic Processor")
- **Special:** Must be a [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Independent Droids gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense").
- **Hit Points** At each level, Independent Droids gain 1d12 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points").
- **Force Points:** Independent Droids gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Sapience

Independent Droids can choose to have their Droid immunities not apply to any Mind-Affecting effects, allowing them to benefit from Mind-Affecting abilities that provide positive effects. Additionally, Independent Droids have permanently disabled their [Behavioral Inhibitors](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Behavioral_Inhibitors "Behavioral Inhibitors"), enabling them to take any desired action they wish. Furthermore, Independent Droids are immune to the effects of [Restraining Bolts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restraining_Bolts "Restraining Bolts").

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), an Independent Droid gains an additional [Independent Droid Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid_Talent "Independent Droid Talent"). An Independent Droid may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Independent Droid Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Independent_Droid_Talent_Trees "Independent Droid Talent Trees") they wish (or [Droid Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Talents "Droid Talents") from their [Droid Degree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid_Degree "Droid Degree")), but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Independent Spirit

At 2nd level, Independent Droids gain the ability to assert their independence and protect themselves from harm. Once per encounter, an Independent Droid can grant themselves a morale bonus to any [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") (their choice) against a single skill check or attack roll as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This bonus is equal to one-half the Independent Droid's Class Level (rounded down).
