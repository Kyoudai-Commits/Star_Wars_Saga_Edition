# Teräs Käsi Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:55Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You are trained in the Teräs Käsi unarmed fighting style and can send an enemy reeling with a single punch.

**Prerequisites:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 13, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** Once per round when you make a successful [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you reduce the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") by 5 when determining the effect of the attack.

**Special:** If you have the **[Teräs Käsi Basics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ter%C3%A4s_K%C3%A4si_Basics "Teräs Käsi Basics")** Talent, you are considered to be one size category larger when determining the damage of your [Unarmed Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attacks "Unarmed Attacks").
