# Unswerving Resolve

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unswerving_Resolve](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unswerving_Resolve) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:46Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You stand your ground in the face of terrifying or domineering opposition.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +2

**Effect:** Whenever you are targeted by a Fear effect or Mind-Affecting effect that fails to affect you, you gain a temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point"). If this [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not used before the end of your next turn, it goes away. If you negate the contingent effect in any way, you do not gain the benefit of this feat.
