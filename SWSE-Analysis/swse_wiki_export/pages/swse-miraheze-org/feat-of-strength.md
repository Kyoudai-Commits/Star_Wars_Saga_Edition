# Feat of Strength

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat_of_Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feat_of_Strength) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You draw on inner reserves of physical power to perform great feats of strength, such as lifting heavy objects, leaping great distances, or hauling yourself up a cliff while weighed down by a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load").

**Prerequisite:** [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") 15

**Effect:** Once per encounter as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action"), you can [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on a [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") check or [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength")-based [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check") (you must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill")), even if distracted or threatened.

**Normal:** You may not [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") when distracted or threatened, or in a single round.

**Special:** After using this feat for the first time in a given encounter, make a DC 15 [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") check (as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")). If the check succeeds, you can use this feat once more during the same encounter.
