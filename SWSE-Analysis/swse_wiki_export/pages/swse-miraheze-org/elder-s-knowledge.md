# Elder's Knowledge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elder's_Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elder's_Knowledge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:01Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You have gained the wisdom of elders, whether through your own experiences or by learning from their instruction.

**Prerequisite:** [Skill Focus (Knowledge (Galactic Lore))](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore)) "Skill Focus (Knowledge (Galactic Lore))") or [Skill Focus (Knowledge (Social Sciences))](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus_(Knowledge_(Social_Sciences)) "Skill Focus (Knowledge (Social Sciences))")

**Effect:** Once per encounter, you can substitute a [Knowledge (Galactic Lore)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Galactic_Lore) "Knowledge (Galactic Lore)") or [Knowledge (Social Sciences)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Social_Sciences) "Knowledge (Social Sciences)") check for a [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") or [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom")-related check.
