# Force Whirlwind

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Whirlwind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Whirlwind) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You call upon [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to surround an enemy in a swirling vortex of Force energy. The whirlwind lifts them about half a meter off the ground, spinning them in the air and buffeting them with Force energy.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") within 12 squares, and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** Compare the result of the check to the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"). If the check result equals or exceeds the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), the target is captured within the whirlwind and [Immobilized](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Immobilized "Immobilized") until the beginning of your next turn. Any attack rolls or [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") the target attempts while within the whirlwind are at a -5 penalty. The target falls [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone") when the effect ends. The target also sustains damage at the end of your turn as determined by the results of your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target takes 1d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage. |
| 20 | The target takes 2d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage. |
| 25 | The target takes 3d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage. |
| 30 | The target takes 4d6 points of [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") damage. |

The target adds a size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") for purposes of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium and smaller, +0.

**Special:**You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the penalty to the target's attack rolls and [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") while inside the *Force Whirlwind* from a -5 penalty to a -10 penalty.

You can maintain *Force Whirlwind* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Force Whirlwind* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), and you must make a new [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check each round. If you take damage while maintaining *Force Whirlwind*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Force Whirlwind*. If a target within a *Force Whirlwind* is attacked or otherwise damaged or moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by you or one of your allies, the effects of the *Force Whirlwind* end immediately.
