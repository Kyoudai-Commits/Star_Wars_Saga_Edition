# Targeted Area

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeted_Area](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Targeted_Area) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

Your [Area Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attacks "Area Attacks") deal more damage to one target within the area of effect.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +5

**Effect:** When you make a successful [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with a weapon with which you are proficient, select a target hit by the attack. That target takes an additional 5 points of damage from the attack, before applying the effect of the **[Evasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Evasion "Evasion")** Talent.
