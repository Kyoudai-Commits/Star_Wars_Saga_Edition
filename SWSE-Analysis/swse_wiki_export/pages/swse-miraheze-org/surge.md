# Surge

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surge) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") enables you to jump great heights and distances as well as move quickly.

**Time:** [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effect, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | You gain a +10 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks and your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 2 squares until the start of your next turn. The [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |
| 15 | You gain a +20 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks and your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 4 squares until the start of your next turn. The [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |
| 20 | You gain a +30 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks and your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") increases by 6 squares until the start of your next turn. The [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks includes the adjustment for increased speed. |

**Special:** Using the *Surge* Force Power counts as a [Running Start](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Running_Start "Running Start") for determining a [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") DC. You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the Force Power's bonus on [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") checks by 10 and increase your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by an additional 2 squares. You may spend a [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to gain an additional 4 squares of movement; when you do so, you may also use any and all movement for the round as a part of a [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") (no [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check required).
