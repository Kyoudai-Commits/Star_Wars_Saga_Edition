# Expert Droid Repair

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Droid_Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert_Droid_Repair) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You can repair multiple [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") simultaneously.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** You can [Repair](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair "Repair") a number of [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") equal to your [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") modifier (minimum 2) simultaneously. You make [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") checks for each [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") as normal.
