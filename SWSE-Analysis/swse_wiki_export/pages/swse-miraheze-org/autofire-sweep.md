# Autofire Sweep

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Sweep](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Sweep) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Your can sweep an area with your [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") weapon, spraying fire in a 180-degree arc in front of you.

**Prerequisite:** [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") (Chosen Weapon)

**Effect:** When making an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack with a weapon with which you possess the [Weapon Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Focus "Weapon Focus") feat, you can attack all targets in a 6-Square [Cone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cone "Cone"). The origin square of the cone can be any square in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") and within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

**Special:** This feat cannot be used with the [Autofire Barrage](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire_Barrage "Autofire Barrage") feat or the [Burst Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Burst_Fire "Burst Fire") feat. It can be used with the **[Improved Suppression Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Suppression_Fire "Improved Suppression Fire")** Talent.
