# Coordinated Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coordinated_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Starships of the Galaxy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starships_of_the_Galaxy "Starships of the Galaxy")*

You are skilled at coordinating your attacks with your allies.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +2

**Effect:** You are automatically successful when using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to aid an ally's attack, or [Suppress an Enemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_an_Enemy "Suppress an Enemy") as long as the target is adjacent to you or within Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range").

You may use this feat to aid an ally's [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") attack. You can't use the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") action to suppress a [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), but you can suppress an individual [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") (such as the lead [Gunner](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gunner "Gunner") for a single [Weapon Battery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Battery "Weapon Battery")). You may use this feat even if you are using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action as a [System Operator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/System_Operator "System Operator") using a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check or a [Commander](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commander "Commander") using a [Knowledge (Tactics)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Tactics) "Knowledge (Tactics)") check.

**Normal:** You must make an attack roll against a [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of 10 to gain the benefits of the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") action.
