# Disgrace Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disgrace_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disgrace_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Scandal fouls your family name, forcing you to live among the fringes of society and teaching you that honor is overrated when victory is on the line.

## Core Talents

### Ambush

*Prerequisite:* **Dirty Tactics**

During a [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"), before combat begins, if you are not [Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") you can give up your [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") to allow all non-[Surprised](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprised "Surprised") allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to take an extra [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") during the [Surprise Round](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surprise_Round "Surprise Round"). Allies can spend this [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to instead reroll their [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative") checks and take the better of the two results as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action") before combat begins.

### Castigate

You deliver a scathing rebuke against a target to erode its will and fill it with doubt. Make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") against the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"). If successful, you impose a -2 penalty to all the target's [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses") until the end of your next turn. You can use this ability only against targets that can clearly hear and understand your language.

### Dirty Tactics

Once per encounter, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can grant a tactical advantage to all allies within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). When an ally [Flanks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanks?action=edit&redlink=1 "Flanks (page does not exist)") an opponent, that ally gains a +4 [Flanking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanking "Flanking") bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls instead of the normal +2 bonus. Allies lose this benefit immediately if [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is broken or if you are unconscious or dead, or at the end of the encounter.

### Misplaced Loyalty

*Prerequisite:* **Dirty Tactics**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") once per turn, you can make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of all opponents within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). If successful, a target cannot attack you if one of your allies is within 6 squares of you. You may not gain the benefit of this Talent if another character within 6 squares of you has used this talent since the end of your last turn. You may not use this Talent in the same round as the Soldier's **[Draw Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Draw_Fire "Draw Fire")** Talent.

### Two-Faced

*Prerequisites:* **Dirty Tactics**, **Misplaced Loyalty**

You have mastered the art of saying one thing and doing another, allowing you to deceive your enemies to keep your machinations hidden. You can use each of the following actions once per encounter as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"):

- *False Security*: Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). At any time before the beginning of your next turn, you can make a single attack against that target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") if that target attacks you.
- *Nonthreatening*: Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within your [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). Until the beginning of your next turn, that opponent cannot make any attacks against you except for [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity"). This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
- *Tricky Target*: Make a single [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within your range that has not attacked you since the end of your last turn. You gain a +2 bonus on your attack roll and damage roll for this attack.

### Unreadable

You gain a +5 bonus to your [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks") made to read your emotions and influence your [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude"). In addition, whenever you successfully [Feint](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feint "Feint") a target in combat, that target is [Flat-Footed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flat-Footed "Flat-Footed") against all your attacks until the end of your next turn.
