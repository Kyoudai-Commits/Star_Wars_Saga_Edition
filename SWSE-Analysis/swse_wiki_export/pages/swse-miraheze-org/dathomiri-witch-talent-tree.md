# Dathomiri Witch Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomiri_Witch_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomiri_Witch_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:04Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

The Force-using witches of the planet [Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dathomir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dathomir (page does not exist)") are primitive yet powerful beings. They use ancient training handed down for generations as the basis of their powers. Only Force-users that are part of [The Witches of Dathomir](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Witches_of_Dathomir "The Witches of Dathomir") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Adept Spellcaster

You may use any [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that normally requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action"), or [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action") as a [Full-Round Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Full-Round_Action "Full-Round Action") instead. If you choose to do so, you may reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to activate that power, but you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse.

### Dathomiri Charm Beast

You may use a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check when attempting to change the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of an undomesticated creature with an [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of 2 or less. Additionally, you do not take the normal -5 penalty on the check if the creature can't speak or understand your language. This Talent is identical to the **[Beastwarden Charm Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beastwarden_Charm_Beast "Beastwarden Charm Beast")** Talent, and both are considered to be the same for the purposes of satisfying prerequisites.

### Dathomiri Command Beast

*Prerequisite:* **Dathomiri Charm Beast**

Whenever you manage to shift the [Attitude](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attitude "Attitude") of a [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") to Indifferent or Friendly, you may treat that creature as a domesticated animal- but for you only (it remains undomesticated in its response to other creatures). Additionally, you may use this [Beast](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beast "Beast") as a mount, as per the [Ride](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ride "Ride") skill, provided that it is at least one size category larger than you and has a comfortable place for you to sit.

### Flight

*Prerequisite:* **Adept Spellcaster**

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to fly. You gain a Fly Speed equal to your base [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), and you can ascend at half speed or descend at double speed. The **Flight** lasts until the start of your next turn; if you're still airborne at that time, you fall.
