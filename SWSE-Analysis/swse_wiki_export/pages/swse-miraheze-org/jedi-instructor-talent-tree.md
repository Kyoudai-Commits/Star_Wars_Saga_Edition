# Jedi Instructor Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Instructor_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Instructor_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:17Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

The role of teacher is vital to the success and strength of [The Jedi Order](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Jedi_Order "The Jedi Order"). Though all [Jedi Masters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Masters "Jedi Masters") take the time to instruct their apprentices, a certain few excel at the art of instruction.

## Core Talents

### Apprentice Boon

Whenever an ally within 12 squares with a lower [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill bonus than you makes a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to add to that [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. Use your level to determine how many dice to roll for the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point").

### Share Force Secret

*Prerequisite:* At least 1 [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret")

When you take this Talent, choose one [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret") that you know. Once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can grant the use of this [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret") to one ally within 12 squares of you who is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill. The target gains the benefit of this [Force Secret](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Secret "Force Secret") until the end of your next turn.

### Share Force Technique

*Prerequisite:* At least 1 [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique")

When you take this Talent, choose one [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") that you know. Once per turn as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can grant the use of this [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") to one ally within 12 squares of you who is [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill. The target gains the benefit of this [Force Technique](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Technique "Force Technique") until the end of your next turn. You cannot choose [Force Point Recovery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point_Recovery "Force Point Recovery") for this Talent.

### Share Jedi Talent

| --- |

*Prerequisite:* At least 1 [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"), [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree"), or [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree")

Choose a Talent that you already possess. The Talent you select must be from the [Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Combat_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Combat Talent Tree"), the [Duelist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Duelist_Talent_Tree "Duelist Talent Tree"), or the [Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightsaber_Forms_Talent_Tree "Lightsaber Forms Talent Tree"). Once per day, as a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to impart the benefits of the chosen Talent to one or more allies, effectively granting them the Talent (even if they don't meet the prerequisites).

An ally must be within 12 squares of you, and must be able to see and hear you to gain the Talent; once gained, its benefits last until the end of the encounter. You can share the Talent with a number of allies equal to one-half your Class Level (rounded down). Only allies who are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill can gain the benefits of the **Shared Jedi Talent**.

You can take this Talent multiple times. Each time you do so, you must select a different Talent to share with this ability. You can share each Talent with your allies only once per day.

### Transfer Power

*Prerequisite:* [Force Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Training "Force Training")

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can spend any one use of a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") currently in your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"), adding a use of that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to the [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") of any ally [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill. The ally must be within 12 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). When your ally uses that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"), it disappears from his or her [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). If the ally does not use the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") before the end of the encounter, it is permanently removed from his or her [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite").
