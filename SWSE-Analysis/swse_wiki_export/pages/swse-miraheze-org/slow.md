# Slow

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slow](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slow) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:28Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

[The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") enables you to slow your targets as if they are encumbered by an extremely heavy load, making it difficult for them to move.

**[*[Telekinetic](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Telekinetic "Telekinetic")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") within 12 squares, and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If your result equals or exceeds your target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense"), they take the following effects as determined by your check result:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | Target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 1 square until the beginning of your next turn. | 20 | Target's [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") is reduced by 2 squares until the beginning of your next turn. The target is also [Encumbered](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Encumbered "Encumbered"), as though carrying a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load"), taking a -10 penalty on [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"), [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), and [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks. |

The target is also [Encumbered](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Encumbered "Encumbered"), as though carrying a [Heavy Load](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Load "Heavy Load"), taking a -10 penalty on [Acrobatics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatics "Acrobatics"), [Climb](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Climb "Climb"), [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance"), [Initiative](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Initiative "Initiative"), [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump"), [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth"), and [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks. |

The target adds a size modifier to its [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") for purposes of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"): Colossal, +50; Gargantuan, +20; Huge, +10; Large, +5; Medium or smaller, +0.

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to impose a -5 penalty to your target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") against the use of this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power").
