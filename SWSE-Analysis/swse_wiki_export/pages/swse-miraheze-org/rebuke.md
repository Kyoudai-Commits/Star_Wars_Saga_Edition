# Rebuke

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:27Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You harmlessly absorb or deflect one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") used against you, perhaps even turning it against its creator.

**Time:** [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")

**Targets:** One [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") directed at you.

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** If your result equals or exceeds the check result of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") directed at you, you harmlessly redirect it and suffer no ill effects. If your result exceeds the check result of the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") directed at you by 5 or more, you may choose to turn the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") against its creator, who suffers the effect based on the creator's original [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.

**Special:** If you successfully reflect a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") back at its originator, the originator may attempt to *Rebuke* the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") as well, expending a use of the *Rebuke* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") and using your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check as its target DC. If they reflect it back again, both you and the originator are affected by the [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power"). You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to suffer no effects from a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") that has been *Rebuked* twice- once by you and once by the Force Power's originator.
