# Lasting Influence

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lasting_Influence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lasting_Influence) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:48Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

When you penetrate the mind of your enemy, you can keep your barbs there for a long time.

**Prerequisite:** [Bothan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bothan "Bothan")

**Effect:** Whenever you make a successful [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against a target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), you gain [Favorable Circumstances](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Favorable_Circumstances "Favorable Circumstances") on future [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") checks against that target for the next 24 hours.
