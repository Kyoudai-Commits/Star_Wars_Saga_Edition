# Martial Arts Forms Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Forms_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_Forms_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:19Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You have advanced training in one or more martial art forms, turning your body into a lethal weapon. Although any character can practice these forms, you have the dedication and perseverance to go beyond what most beings are capable of.

## Core Talents

### Echani Expertise

*Prerequisite:* [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +10

When making an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you extend your critical threat range by 1 (for example, 19-20 instead of 20). However, anything other than a Natural 20 is not considered an automatic hit; if you roll anything other than a Natural 20 and still miss the target, you do not score a [Critical Hit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit").

### Hijkata Expertise

When you damage a creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), the target takes a penalty to its next attack roll equal to your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus.

### K'tara Expertise

Once per turn, when you damage a creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can make an attack to [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") as a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). Also, you do not take the -5 penalty to your attack roll if the target is wielding a weapon with more than one hand.

### K'thri Expertise

Any enemy that begins its turn adjacent to you takes damage equal to your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") modifier (minimum 1 point) if you are able to make an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity") against them. You can use this Talent only while wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").

### Stava Expertise

When you successfully [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") an enemy, he or she must make an opposed [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab") check to break free of your [Grab](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grab "Grab"). If you are initiating a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple"), you can reroll your [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") check. However, you must accept the result of the reroll, even if it is worse. You can use this Talent only while wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").

### Tae-Jitsu Expertise

Once per turn, when you damage a creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), compare your attack roll to the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"). If your attack roll equals or exceeds the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold"), the target is moved -1 step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"), regardless of the damage result of your attack.

### Wrruushi Expertise

Once per turn, when you damage a creature or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid") with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), you can make an attack against the target's [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"). If that attack is successful, the target can only take a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on their next turn. You can use this Talent only while wearing [Light Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Armor "Light Armor") or no [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor").
