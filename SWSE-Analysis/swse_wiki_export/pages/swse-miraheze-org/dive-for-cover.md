# Dive for Cover

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dive_for_Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dive_for_Cover) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:53Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

You can leap behind cover, even when caught out in the open.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump")

**Effect:** Once per turn, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to being targeted by a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can make a [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") check to [Long Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Long_Jump "Long Jump"). If you land in a square that provides you with [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") from your attacker, you gain the normal [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") bonus against the attack, even if you didn't have [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") at the time the attack was made. When you use this feat, you always land [Prone](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prone "Prone").
