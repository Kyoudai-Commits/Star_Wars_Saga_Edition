# Wilderness First Aid

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wilderness_First_Aid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wilderness_First_Aid) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:03Z.

*Reference Book: [Unknown Regions Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Campaign_Guide "Unknown Regions Campaign Guide")*

You know how to treat wounds in the wilderness, using only the natural materials around you.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival").

**Effect:** Once per day, you can make a DC 20 [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check. If the check is successful, when you use the [Basic Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Basic_Survival "Basic Survival") application of the [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") skill, you are considered to have a [Medpac](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medpac "Medpac") for any [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks made until the end of the day.

**Special:** The Gamemaster can adjust the DC for the current environment, raising it for harsh or desert climates or lowering it for diverse locales.
