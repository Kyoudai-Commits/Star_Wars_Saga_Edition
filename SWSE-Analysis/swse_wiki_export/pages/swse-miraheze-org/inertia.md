# Inertia

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inertia](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inertia) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:25Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can use [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") to shift your body's inertia, allowing you to perform impossible stunts.

**Time:** [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action")

**Targets:** Self

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.**The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | You can move up to half your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") along a wall, as though it were the ground, ignoring the normal effects of [Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity "Gravity") for the duration of this movement. If you are not on the ground when you end this movement, you [Fall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fall "Fall") to the ground. |
| 20 | You can move up to your full [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") along a wall, as though it were the ground, ignoring the normal effects of [Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity "Gravity") for the duration of this movement. If you are not on the ground when you end this movement, you [Fall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fall "Fall") to the ground. |
| 25 | You can move up to your full [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +2 additional squares, along a wall, as though it were the ground, ignoring the normal effects of [Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity "Gravity") for the duration of this movement. If you are not on the ground when you end this movement, you [Fall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fall "Fall") to the ground. |
| 30 | You can move up to your full [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), +4 additional squares, along a wall, as though it were the ground, ignoring the normal effects of [Gravity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity "Gravity") for the duration of this movement. If you are not on the ground when you end this movement, you [Fall](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fall "Fall") to the ground. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") you move along the wall by an additional +2 squares.
