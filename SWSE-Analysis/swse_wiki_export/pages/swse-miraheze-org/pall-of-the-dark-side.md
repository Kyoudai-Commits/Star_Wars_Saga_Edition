# Pall of the Dark Side

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pall_of_the_Dark_Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pall_of_the_Dark_Side) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You are adept at hiding yourself from Force-users, thanks to the haze of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side").

**Prerequisite:** [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") 1+

**Effect:** You can add one-half your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") (minimum 1) to any [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") checks made to resist attempts to detect you with the [Sense Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sense_Force "Sense Force") application of the [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") skill.
