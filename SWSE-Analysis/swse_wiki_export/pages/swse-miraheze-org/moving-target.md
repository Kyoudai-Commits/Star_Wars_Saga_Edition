# Moving Target

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moving_Target](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moving_Target) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:47Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know how to stay mobile, making it harder for your enemies to hit you with their attacks.

**Prerequisite:** [Dodge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dodge "Dodge")

**Effect:** If you end your turn at least 3 squares away from where you started, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the start of your next turn.
