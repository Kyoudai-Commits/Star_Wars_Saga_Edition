# Flood of Fire

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flood_of_Fire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flood_of_Fire) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:43Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You fill the air with weapon fire, keeping your opponents from escaping the barrage.

**Effect:** When you make an [Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") with a weapon set on [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire"), and you are proficient with that weapon, all targets in the area lose all dodge or deflection bonuses to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") against the [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack.
