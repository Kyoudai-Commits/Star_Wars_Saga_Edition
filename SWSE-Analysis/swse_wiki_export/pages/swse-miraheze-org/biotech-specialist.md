# Biotech Specialist

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Specialist) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can make custom modifications to [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech")-based armor, weapons, vehicles, and similar devices.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics")

**Effect:** You can modify a [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech") device, suit of armor, weapon, or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle") so that it gains a specific trait. You can perform only one modification at a time. Unless noted otherwise, you cannot grant more than one benefit to a single device, suit of [Armor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor "Armor"), [Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon "Weapon"), or [Vehicle](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle "Vehicle"), and you cannot apply the same benefit more than once.

Before beginning the modification, you must pay one tenth the cost of the item you wish to modify or 1,000 credits, whichever is more. Completing the modification requires one day per 1,000 credits of the modification cost. At the end of this time, make a DC 20 [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check; you can't [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") or [Take 20](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_20 "Take 20") on this check. If the check succeeds, the modification is completed successfully, and the object gains the desired Trait. If the check fails, you lose all credits spent making the modification, and the object doesn't gain the desired Trait. However, you may start over if you wish.

Due to the specialized nature of [Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology"), only other characters with the Biotech Specialist feat can assist you, reducing proportionately the time needed to complete the modification. At the end of the modification process, they can make a [Mechanics](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanics "Mechanics") check to aid your check. The market value of a modified item is equal to the base cost of the item + double the cost of the modifications made to it (not including credits wasted on failed attempts).

**Special:** This feat allows you to make [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") checks on [Biotechnology](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotechnology "Biotechnology") without the normal -5 penalty.
