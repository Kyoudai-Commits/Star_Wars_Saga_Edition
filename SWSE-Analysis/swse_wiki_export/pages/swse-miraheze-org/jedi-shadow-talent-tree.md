# Jedi Shadow Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Shadow_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Shadow_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:09Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

Jedi Shadows are often elite [Jedi Sentinels](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Sentinel_Talent_Tree "Jedi Sentinel Talent Tree") who specialize in abilities that aid them in pursuing Dark Side users in all forms and locations. These Talents are also utilized by [Jedi Covenanters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Covenanters "Jedi Covenanters").

## Core Talents

### Dark Deception

You can cloak your intentions with a veil of anger and hate. When another character attempts to sense you through [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force") in any way, you can choose to act as though your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equals your [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom") score. Additionally, [Deception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Deception "Deception") is now a [Class Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class_Skill "Class Skill") for you.

### Improved Sentinel Strike

*Prerequisite:* **[Sentinel Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sentinel_Strike "Sentinel Strike")**

Increase the damage dice of your **[Sentinel Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sentinel_Strike "Sentinel Strike")** to d8, instead of d6.

### Improved Sentinel's Gambit

*Prerequisite:* **[Sentinel's Gambit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sentinel%27s_Gambit "Sentinel's Gambit")**

You can use **[Sentinel's Gambit](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sentinel%27s_Gambit "Sentinel's Gambit")** an additional number of times equal to half your Class Level (minimum 1).

### Rebuke the Dark

*Prerequisite:* *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")*

When using the *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* Force Power against a [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor, roll two dice for the *[Rebuke](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebuke "Rebuke")* attempt, keeping the better of the two results.

### Taint of the Dark Side

*Prerequisite:* **Dark Deception**

Add one [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor to your [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite"). Once per encounter you can use that [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") with the [*[Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side "Dark Side")*] descriptor without increasing your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score"). If you spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") or [Destiny Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Destiny_Point "Destiny Point") to modify this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") in any way, you increase your [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") as normal.
