# Crush

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crush) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You can deal damage to a target that you've [Grappled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grappled "Grappled").

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin")

**Effect:** If you successfully [Pin](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pin "Pin") an opponent with a [Grapple](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grapple "Grapple") attack, you can immediately deal Bludgeoning damage to it equal to your [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") damage or claw damage, whichever is greater.
