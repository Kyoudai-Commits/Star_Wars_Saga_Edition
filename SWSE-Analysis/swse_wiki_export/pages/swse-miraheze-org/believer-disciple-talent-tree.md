# Believer Disciple Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Believer_Disciple_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Believer_Disciple_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

[The Believers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Believers "The Believers") follow the teachings of [The Sith](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Sith "The Sith") from ancient times and strive to extend the influence of [The Dark Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Dark_Side "The Dark Side") of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"). Only Force-users that are part of [The Believers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Believers "The Believers") may select [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") from this tree.

## Core Talents

### Believer Intuition

As a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when an opponent successfully attacks you, make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check and compare the result to your opponent's attack roll. If the check equals or exceeds the result of the attack roll, you can add your [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma") modifier to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense").

### Defense Boost

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If the check succeeds, you gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") until the end of the encounter. Before you make your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check, you can increase the target number to DC 20 to gain a +1 [Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force "Force") bonus to all your [Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defenses "Defenses").

### Hardiness

You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to reduce the number of [Swift Actions](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Actions "Swift Actions") it takes you to move +1 step along to [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by one.

### High Impact

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), make a DC 15 [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check. If your check succeeds, double your [Strength](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strength "Strength") bonus to the next melee damage roll you make before the end of your turn.

### Sith Reverence

You gain a +1 morale bonus on your attack rolls while you are within 20 squares and in [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") of an ally with a [Dark Side Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Side_Score "Dark Side Score") equal to or greater than your own.
