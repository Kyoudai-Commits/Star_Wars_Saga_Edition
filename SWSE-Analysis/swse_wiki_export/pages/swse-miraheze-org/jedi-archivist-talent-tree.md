# Jedi Archivist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Archivist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Archivist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:13Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

You have dedicated your life as a Jedi to preserving knowledge and applying it in times when it is needed.

## Core Talents

### Direct

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can return one spent [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to the [Force Power Suite](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power_Suite "Force Power Suite") of any ally within 6 squares of you and in your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). The [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") must have been spent by the ally you designate.

### Impart Knowledge

*Prerequisite:* **[Skilled Advisor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skilled_Advisor "Skilled Advisor")**

You can [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") on the [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") checks of an ally within 6 squares of you as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") for [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skills you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in.

### Insight of the Force

You can make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check in place of a [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") check for any [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skill you are not [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in that [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skill for the purposes of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") check reroll, you can reroll your [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Master Advisor

*Prerequisite:* **[Skilled Advisor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skilled_Advisor "Skilled Advisor")**

When you use the **[Skilled Advisor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skilled_Advisor "Skilled Advisor")** Talent, the ally you aid gains one temporary [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") at the end of their next turn. If the [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") is not spent before the end of the encounter, it is lost.

### Scholarly Knowledge

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can reroll a [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") check and keep the better of the two results. This can be used with any [Knowledge](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") skill you are [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in.
