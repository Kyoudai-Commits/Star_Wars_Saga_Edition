# Outlaw Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Outlaw_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Outlaw_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

As an [Outlaw](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Outlaw "Outlaw"), you live under a shadow, and everywhere you go, any time you reveal yourself, you risk being noticed and captured. As a result, you develop a number of abilities that help you escape pursuers, even when hopelessly outnumbered.

## Core Talents

### Confounding Attack

*Prerequisites:* **Tangle Up**, **Uncanny Instincts**

Once per encounter, whenever you would use **Uncanny Instincts**, you can forgo the movement to make an immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against the opponent that hit you. If your attack is a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") that hits and deals damage, you and your opponent immediately switch places; assuming both you and your opponent can end in a legal space.

### Double Up

*Prerequisites:* **Find an Opening**, **Seize the Moment**

Once per encounter, whenever you would use **Seize the Moment**, you can forgo the extra [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to make an immediate [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against the damaged opponent. If your attack is a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") that hits and deals damage, you treat the damage dealt by you and your ally as though it was one attack for purposes of overcoming [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction"), [Shield Rating](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shield_Rating "Shield Rating"), and determining whether the damage exceeded the target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold").

### Find an Opening

*Prerequisite:* **Seize the Moment**

Whenever you would use **Seize the Moment**, you can forgo the [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") to be able to [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") as a single [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") on your turn.

### Opportunistic Defense

*Prerequisite:* **Uncanny Instincts**

Once per encounter, whenever you would use **Uncanny Instincts**, you can forgo this extra movement and instead increase your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") by 5 until the end of your next turn.

### Preternatural Senses

Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can add one-half your Class Level to the [Defense Score](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Score "Defense Score") of your choice.

### Seize the Moment

Once per round, whenever an ally successfully damages an opponent, you can take a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction").

### Tangle Up

*Prerequisite:* **Uncanny Instincts**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), you can make a non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") melee or ranged attack against an opponent within [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). If the attack hits, you deal half your normal damage (minimum 1 point), but your opponent loses its next [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action").

### Uncanny Instincts

Once per round whenever an opponent successfully deals damage to you, you can move 1 square as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"). This movement does not provoke [Attacks of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attacks_of_Opportunity "Attacks of Opportunity").
