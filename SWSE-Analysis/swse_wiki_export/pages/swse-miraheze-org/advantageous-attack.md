# Advantageous Attack

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advantageous_Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Advantageous_Attack) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:37Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You know how to take advantage of slow-moving enemies in combat.

**Prerequisite:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1

**Effect:** When you make a successful attack against an enemy who has not yet acted in combat, you add your full Heroic Level to damage rolls.

**Normal:** Typically you add only one-half your Heroic Level to damage rolls.
