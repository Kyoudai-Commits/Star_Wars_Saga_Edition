# Valor

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Valor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Valor) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:29Z.

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

You call upon the strength of [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), reaching out to your ally and sharing your strength with them.

**[*[Light Side](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Side "Light Side")*]**

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One ally within 12 squares of you, and within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | The target gains a +1 bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the start of your next turn. |
| 20 | The target gains a +2 bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the start of your next turn. |
| 25 | The target gains a +5 bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the start of your next turn. |
| 30 | The target gains a +10 bonus to their [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against Mind-Affecting and Fear effects until the start of your next turn. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") when you activate this [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") to have the bonus apply against all effects that target [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") (not just Mind-Affecting and Fear effects).

You can maintain *Valor* from round to round, extending the normal duration. Maintaining the *Valor* [Force Power](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Power "Force Power") is a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"). If you take damage while maintaining *Valor*, you must succeed on a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check (DC = 15 + damage taken) to continue maintaining the *Valor*. You may have multiple instances of *Valor* active at once (affecting different allies), but maintaining each one requires a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action") each round, and each one must be activated by a separate use of *Valor*.
