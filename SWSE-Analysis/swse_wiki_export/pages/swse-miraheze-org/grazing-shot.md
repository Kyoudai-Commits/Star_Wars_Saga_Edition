# Grazing Shot

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grazing_Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Grazing_Shot) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

Using precise aim, you can hit two targets with a single shot.

**Prerequisite:** [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot")

**Effect:** When you successfully make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a single target, you can make a second attack roll against an additional target that is in direct [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"), and no farther than 6 squares from the original target. If the second attack roll succeeds, make a single damage roll, and divide the damage equally between the two targets. If the second attack roll fails, you deal no damage to either target.
