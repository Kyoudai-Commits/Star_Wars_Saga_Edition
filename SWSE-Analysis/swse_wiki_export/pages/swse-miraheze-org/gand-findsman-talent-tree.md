# Gand Findsman Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gand_Findsman_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gand_Findsman_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

[The Gand Findsmen](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Gand_Findsmen "The Gand Findsmen") are an ancient order of Force-sensitive [Bounty Hunters](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounty_Hunters "Bounty Hunters") that interpret the signs and portents they receive as a result of the complex ceremonies they perform. Typically, only [Gand](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gand "Gand") can take these [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"), though under some circumstances a Gamemaster may allow a Force-sensitive member of another [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") to take these [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents") as the character's story permits. Regardless, a character must posses the [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity") feat to select any of the following [Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents").

## Core Talents

### Findsman Ceremonies

*Prerequisite:* [Force Sensitivity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Sensitivity "Force Sensitivity")

Once per day, you can spend 10 minutes performing rituals that enhance your connection with [The Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Force "The Force"), receiving visions and portents as a result. At that time, you can spend any number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") in the performance of the ritual, up to the total number you have remaining.

For the remainder of the day, whenever you make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") or [Stealth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stealth "Stealth") check, make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check to use the *[Farseeing](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Farseeing "Farseeing")* Force Power, or make an attack roll, you can choose to reroll that check, but must accept to the results of the reroll, even if it is worse. You may do this a number of times per day equal to the number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") you spent during the casting of the ritual. At the end of the day, you regain [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to the number of rerolls you have remaining.

### Findsman's Foresight

*Prerequisite:* **Findsman Ceremonies**

The visions you receive sometimes provide clues about dangerous situations. Whenever you make a [Perception](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perception "Perception") check to [Avoid Surprise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Avoid_Surprise "Avoid Surprise"), you may roll twice, keeping the better of the two results.

### Omens

*Prerequisite:* **Findsman Ceremonies**

You see **Omens** in both success and failure. Whenever an ally within 10 squares and [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") of you rolls a Natural 1 or a Natural 20 on an attack roll, you gain a +2 insight bonus to either your next attack roll made before the end of your next turn, or a +2 insight bonus to your [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn (your choice).

### Target Visions

*Prerequisite:* **Findsman Ceremonies**

You have visions that tell you what your enemies are likely to do even before they do it. Once per encounter, when an enemy creature moves within 6 squares of you, you may make a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against that target as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to their movement.

### Temporal Awareness

*Prerequisite:* **Findsman Ceremonies**

Timing is crucial to a Findsman. Once per encounter, as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction") to any enemy's attack, you can [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed").
