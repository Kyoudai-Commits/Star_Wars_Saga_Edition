# Unstoppable Force

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unstoppable_Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unstoppable_Force) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:44Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

Your constant exposure to Force-users has taught you methods of physically resisting their effect on you in battle.

**Effect:** You gain a +5 insight bonus to [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") and [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") against any attack or effect requiring a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.
