# Survivor of Ryloth

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survivor_of_Ryloth](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survivor_of_Ryloth) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:52Z.

*Reference Book: [Rebellion Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rebellion_Era_Campaign_Guide "Rebellion Era Campaign Guide")*

You know how to survive on both the night and day sides of your homeworld, allowing you and your allies to weather the harsh environments of the galaxy.

**Prerequisite:** [Twi'lek](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Twi%27lek "Twi'lek")

**Effect:** Once per hour when you and your allies are in an environment of [Extreme Heat or Cold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extreme_Heat_or_Cold "Extreme Heat or Cold"), you can make a [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check. You and up to 10 allies can use the results of the [Survival](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Survival "Survival") check in place of your [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense") when the [Extreme Heat or Cold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extreme_Heat_or_Cold "Extreme Heat or Cold") makes its hourly attack roll against your [Fortitude Defenses](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defenses "Fortitude Defenses").
