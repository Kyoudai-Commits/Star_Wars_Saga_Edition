# Wicked Attack

| ***This section was titled Wicked Strike in its official source, but has been renamed to distinguish it from the similarly named "[Wicked Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wicked_Strike "Wicked Strike")."*** |

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Your melee attacks can catch bystanders.

**Prerequisite:** [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike")

**Effect:** When you damage a target with a single, non-[Area Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Area_Attack "Area Attack") using the [Rapid Strike](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Strike "Rapid Strike") feat, once per turn on your turn, you can immediately make a second attack at a -2 penalty against a second target within your [Reach](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reach "Reach"). If this attack hits, you deal half of the original attack's damage to that target.
