# Cut the Red Tape

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cut_the_Red_Tape](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cut_the_Red_Tape) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:59Z.

*Reference Book: [Galaxy of Intrigue](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_of_Intrigue "Galaxy of Intrigue")*

You know how to work the bureaucratic system to get information you need.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)")

**Effect:** You can use your [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)") modifier instead of your [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") modifier when making [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") checks. If you are entitled to a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check reroll, you can reroll your [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations). You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") skill for the purposes of this check.
