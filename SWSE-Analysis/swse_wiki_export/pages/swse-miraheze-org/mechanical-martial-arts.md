# Mechanical Martial Arts

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanical_Martial_Arts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mechanical_Martial_Arts) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:57Z.

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

You know how to inflict pain using your metal appendages when fighting in [Unarmed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed "Unarmed") combat against organic enemies.

**Prerequisites:** [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid"), [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +1, [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I")

**Effect:** When you successfully damage an enemy with an [Unarmed Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unarmed_Attack "Unarmed Attack"), the enemy takes a -5 penalty to all [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls and damage rolls until the start of your next turn.

**Special:** If you strike an organic enemy while using this feat during an [Attack of Opportunity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Attack_of_Opportunity "Attack of Opportunity"), the penalty lasts until the start of the enemy's next turn.
