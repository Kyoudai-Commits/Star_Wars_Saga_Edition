# Biotech Surgery

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Surgery](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech_Surgery) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

You can perform the surgical procedures necessary to graft [Biotech](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biotech "Biotech") components onto living flesh, similar to those used by the [Yuuzhan Vong](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Yuuzhan_Vong "Yuuzhan Vong").

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury")

**Effect:** You can install [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") onto a living being. The surgical procedure takes 1 hour of uninterrupted work, after which you must make a DC 20 [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check. If the check succeeds, the prosthesis is installed correctly. If the check fails, the prosthesis is not installed properly; however, you can try again after another uninterrupted hour of surgery.

**Special:** You can install [Bio-Implants](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implants "Bio-Implants") on yourself, but you take a -5 penalty on the [Treat Injury](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treat_Injury "Treat Injury") check. If you have the [Surgical Expertise](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Surgical_Expertise "Surgical Expertise") feat, you can install a [Bio-Implant](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio-Implant "Bio-Implant") in 10 minutes instead of 1 hour.
