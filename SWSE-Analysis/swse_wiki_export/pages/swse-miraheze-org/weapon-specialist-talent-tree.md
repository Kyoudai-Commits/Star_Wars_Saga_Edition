# Weapon Specialist Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialist_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialist_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:07Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are highly trained at using specific weapons.

## Core Talents

### Devastating Attack

Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you are proficient. Whenever you make a successful attack against a target using such a weapon, you treat your target's [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") as if it were 5 points lower when determining the result of your attack. If you select [Heavy Weapons](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Weapons "Heavy Weapons") as the [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") this Talent applies to, you may also use the Talent with [Vehicle Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicle_Weapon "Vehicle Weapon") attacks. You may select this Talent multiple times. Each time you select this Talent, it applies to a different [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group").

## Additional Weapon Specialization Talents

### Autofire Assault

*Reference Book: [Galaxy at War](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galaxy_at_War "Galaxy at War")*

*Prerequisite:* [Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Proficiency_(Heavy_Weapons) "Weapon Proficiency (Heavy Weapons)")

When making an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") attack, you can [Brace](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brace "Brace") a Weapon that is not restricted to [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire")-only. You must be proficient with the weapon being used to activate this Talent.

### Crushing Assault

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Weapon Specialization**

You use your attacks to beat down your opponents' defenses. When you successfully damage an opponent using a Bludgeoning weapon that you have the **Weapon Specialization** Talent for, your next attack against that opponent made before the end of the encounter gains a +2 bonus on the attack roll and to the damage roll. The effects of multiple **Crushing Assaults** do not stack.

### Disarming Attack

*Reference Book: [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide")*

*Prerequisites:* [Improved Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Disarm "Improved Disarm"), **Weapon Specialization**

Choose a single [Exotic Weapon](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapon "Exotic Weapon") or [Weapon Group](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Group "Weapon Group") with which you are proficient. You ignore a target's Armor bonus to [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") when [Disarming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarming "Disarming") with such a weapon. Additionally, as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action"), once per encounter, you can grant yourself a +10 bonus on your attack roll when attempting to [Disarm](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disarm "Disarm") an opponent while using such a weapon.

### Impaling Assault

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Weapon Specialization**

You can hit your opponents to slow them down. Whenever you successfully damage an opponent using a Piercing weapon that you have the **Weapon Specialization** Talent for, your opponent reduces its [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed") by 2 squares until the end of your next turn. The effects of multiple **Impaling Assaults** do not stack.

### Improved Suppression Fire

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

When you successfully [Suppress an Enemy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_an_Enemy "Suppress an Enemy") using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action, that enemy takes a -5 penalty on its attack rolls until the start of your next turn. When targeting an area with an [Autofire](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Autofire "Autofire") weapon, each enemy in the attack area takes a -2 penalty on its attack rolls until the start of your next turn, regardless of whether your attack hits.

### Stinging Assault

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

*Prerequisite:* **Weapon Specialization**

You can deliver nasty injuries that leave your opponents reeling. Whenever you successfully damage an opponent using a Slashing weapon that you have the **Weapon Specialization** Talent for, your opponent takes a -2 penalty on [Melee Attacks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attacks "Melee Attacks") against you until the start of your next turn. The effects of multiple **Stinging Assaults** do not stack.
