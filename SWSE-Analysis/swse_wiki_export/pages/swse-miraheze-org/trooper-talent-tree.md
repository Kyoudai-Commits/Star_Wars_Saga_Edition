# Trooper Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trooper_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trooper_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:15Z.

*Reference Book: [Clone Wars Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Clone_Wars_Campaign_Guide "Clone Wars Campaign Guide")*

In war, [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers") stand side by side against deadly foes, and you have learned how to keep yourself and your comrades alive in battle.

## Core Talents

### Comrades in Arms

Whenever you are within 3 squares of an ally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on all [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") rolls.

### Focused Targeting

*Prerequisite:* **Comrades in Arms**

When you damage a target with a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") or [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), all allies within 3 squares gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls against that target until the beginning of your next turn.

### Phalanx

*Prerequisite:* **Watch Your Back**

Whenever you provide "soft" [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") to an ally within 3 squares, you are considered to be providing [Improved Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Improved_Cover "Improved Cover").

### Stick Together

*Prerequisite:* **Comrades in Arms**

You can spend a [Move Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move_Action "Move Action") to activate this Talent. Until the beginning of your next turn, if an ally moves you can immediately [Move](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Move "Move") up to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"), provided you end your movement within 3 squares of that ally.

### Watch Your Back

If you are adjacent to at least one ally, enemies gain no benefit from [Flanking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flanking "Flanking") you or any adjacent allies.
