# Sniper

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:33Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

You are particularly adept at hitting the right target in a crowd.

**Prerequisites:** [Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus") +4, [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot"), [Precise Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precise_Shot "Precise Shot")

**Effect:** You always ignore "soft" [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") (that is, [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") provided by a character, creature, or [Droid](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droid "Droid")) when you make a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack").

**Normal:** You can only ignore [Cover](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cover "Cover") if you [Aim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aim "Aim") before making a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack").
