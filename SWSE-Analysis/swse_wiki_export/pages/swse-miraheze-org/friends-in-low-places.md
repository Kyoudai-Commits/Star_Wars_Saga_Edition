# Friends in Low Places

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Friends_in_Low_Places](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Friends_in_Low_Places) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:40Z.

*Reference Book: [Scum and Villainy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scum_and_Villainy "Scum and Villainy")*

Given enough time, you can dig up the right tool for the job.

**Prerequisite:** [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information")

**Effect:** Whenever you would acquire a license for a [Restricted](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Restricted "Restricted") or [Military](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Military "Military") object, you can substitute a [Gather Information](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gather_Information "Gather Information") check for a [Knowledge (Bureaucracy)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knowledge_(Bureaucracy) "Knowledge (Bureaucracy)") check. Reduce the [Black Market](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Market "Black Market") cost multiplier of such items by 1.
