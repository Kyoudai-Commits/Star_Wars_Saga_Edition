# Gungan

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gungan) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:30Z.

*Reference Book: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook")*

[![](//static.wikitide.net/swsewiki/thumb/3/3b/Gungan.webp/300px-Gungan.webp.png)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/File:Gungan.webp)

The Gungans are omnivorous humanoids native to the swamps of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo"). Technologically advanced, they rely mostly on biotech, growing instead of building their homes and production facilities. While their culture is peaceful, it evolved from a series of long, bloody clan wars, and Gungans still admire strength and cunning. Most communities are devoted to farming or manufacturing goods that are then traded to other Gungan settlements, but they will reluctantly trade with the [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo").

## Gungan Characteristics

**Personality:** Gungans tend to be inquisitive, cautious, and suspicious.

**Physical Description:** Gungans range from 1.6 to 2 meters tall and weigh about 70 kilograms, with two eyestalks, floppy ears, and long tongues.

**[Age Groups](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Age_Groups "Age Groups"):** Gungans age at the following stages:

| CHILD | YOUNG ADULT | ADULT | MIDDLE AGE | OLD | VENERABLE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-12 Years | 13-15 Years | 16-35 Years | 36-54 Years | 55-64 Years | 65+ Years |

**Homeworld:** The serene world of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo").

**Languages:** Gungans speak, read, and write both Gunganese and Basic.

**Example Names:** Fassa, Jar Jar, Rugor, Tarpals, Toba, Tobler Ceel, Yoss.

**Adventurers:** Many Gungans leave their underwater cities to explore as [Scouts](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scouts "Scouts"). Since the treaty with the [Humans](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Humans "Humans") of [Naboo](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Naboo "Naboo"), some Gungans- perhaps driven by an impulse to regain the warrior spirit of old as [Soldiers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldiers "Soldiers")- have taken a larger step into galactic society.

## Gungan Species Traits

Gungans share the following Species Traits:

- **Ability Modifiers:** Gungans have a natural +2 bonus to their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity"), but suffer -2 penalties to their [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") and [Charisma](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma "Charisma"). Gungans are agile, but aren't particularly witty or astute.
- **Medium Size:** As Medium creatures, Gungans have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Gungans have a base speed of 6 squares. They also have a swim speed of 4 squares.
- **Expert Swimmer:** A Gungan may choose to reroll any [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") check, but the result of the reroll must be accepted even if it is worse. In addition, a Gungan may choose to [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") on [Swim](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swim "Swim") checks even when distracted or threatened.
- **Hold Breath:** Gungans are at home either in air or in water. A Gungan can hold his or her breath for a number of rounds equal to 25 times their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") score before they need to make [Endurance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Endurance "Endurance") checks to hold their breath.
- **Lightning Reflexes:** Gungans gain a +2 [Species](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Species "Species") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), accounting for their uncanny ability to avoid danger.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Gungans ignore [Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Concealment "Concealment") (but not [Total Concealment](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Total_Concealment "Total Concealment")) from darkness.
- **Weapon Familiarity:** Gungans treat the [Atlatl](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atlatl "Atlatl"), [Cesta](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cesta "Cesta"), and [Electropole](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Electropole "Electropole") as [Simple Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Simple_Weapons_(Melee) "Simple Weapons (Melee)") instead of [Exotic Weapons (Melee)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exotic_Weapons_(Melee) "Exotic Weapons (Melee)").
- **Automatic Languages:** All Gungans can speak, read, and write both Basic and Gunganese.
