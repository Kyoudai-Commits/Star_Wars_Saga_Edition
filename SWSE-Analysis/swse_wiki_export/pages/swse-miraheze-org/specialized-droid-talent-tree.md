# Specialized Droid Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Specialized_Droid_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Specialized_Droid_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:12Z.

*Reference Book: [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

You have become more specialized than other [Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Droids "Droids") of the same or a similar model.

## Core Talents

### Computer Language

*Prerequisite:* [Binary](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Binary "Binary")

You can use your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") modifier instead of your [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") modifier when making [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") checks. You are considered [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") skill for the purpose of using this Talent. If you are entitled to a [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check reroll, you can reroll your [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check instead (subject to the same circumstances and limitations).

### Computer Master

You can reroll any opposed [Use Computer](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_Computer "Use Computer") check, keeping the better of the two results.

### Enhanced Manipulation

*Prerequisite:* [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") 15

You have improved appendage manipulation routines. You can [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10") when making any [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity")-based [Skill Check](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Check "Skill Check"), even if you are threatened or would not normally be able to [Take 10](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Take_10 "Take 10").

### Hotwired Processor

You gain temporary processing power, enhancing your mental attributes. When you hotwire your [Processor](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Processor "Processor") (a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")), you gain a +5 circumstance bonus on [Intelligence](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence")- and [Wisdom](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wisdom "Wisdom")-based [Skill Checks](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Checks "Skill Checks"), and a +1 circumstance bonus on [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") rolls. A hotwiring lasts for a number of rounds equal to one-half your Heroic Level (rounded down). When the hotwiring ends, you move -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The penalties imposed by this condition persist until you receive [Repairs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repairs "Repairs").

### Power Surge

You temporarily surge your power systems to enhance your physical abilities. When you initiate a **Power Surge** (a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action")), you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") rolls, +1 die of damage on melee damage rolls, and an increase of 2 square to your [Speed](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed "Speed"). A **Power Surge** lasts for a number of rounds equal to one-half your Heroic Level (rounded down). When the **Power Surge** ends, you move -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step along the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track"). The penalties imposed by this condition persist until you receive [Repairs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repairs "Repairs").

### Skill Conversion

When you [Reprogram](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogram "Reprogram") yourself, you can sacrifice a single [Trained Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Skill "Trained Skill") for a bonus [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") feat. You must meet the prerequisites for the feat (you must be [Trained](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained "Trained") in the [Skill](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill "Skill") you choose to gain [Skill Focus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skill_Focus "Skill Focus") for), and you can do this only once per [Reprogramming](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reprogramming "Reprogramming").

## Additional Talents

### Power Boost

*Reference Book: [Scavenger's Guide to Droids](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scavenger%27s_Guide_to_Droids "Scavenger's Guide to Droids")*

*Prerequisite:* **Power Surge**

You channel your **Power Surge** into a boost for your [Locomotion System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_System "Locomotion System"). When you initiate a **Power Surge**, you can use one of the following bonuses with your installed [Locomotion System](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Locomotion_System "Locomotion System"):

- [Jump](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jump "Jump") +4 squares ([Walking](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Walking "Walking") or [Wheeled](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wheeled "Wheeled") Locomotion), or
- Increase hovering height by 4 squares ([Hovering](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hovering "Hovering") Locomotion).

You can use this Talent for a number of rounds equal to one-half your Character Level (rounded down). At the end of a **Power Boost**, you move -1 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") step on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") . The penalties imposed by this [Persistent Condition](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent_Condition "Persistent Condition") persist until you receive [Repairs](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repairs "Repairs"). You can use both **Power Surge** and **Power Boost** at the same time, but you must move -2 [Persistent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persistent "Persistent") steps on the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track").
