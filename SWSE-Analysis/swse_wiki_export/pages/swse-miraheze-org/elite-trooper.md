# Elite Trooper

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:25Z.

*Reference Books: [Core Rulebook](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Core_Rulebook "Core Rulebook"), [Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_Campaign_Guide "Knights of the Old Republic Campaign Guide"), [Force Unleashed Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Unleashed_Campaign_Guide "Force Unleashed Campaign Guide")*

The Elite Trooper represents an individual who has received advanced combat training. More than a mere [Soldier](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soldier "Soldier"), the Elite Trooper is highly trained and capable of taking on any number of combat-related missions, such as guarding an important location, assaulting an enemy base, or participating in a stealthy insertion into enemy territory. They're a crack shot and, though trained in the use of armor, can easily operate without it if the situation warrants. When the fighting gets particularly brutal, the Elite Trooper can dispatch foes hand-to-hand as easily as they can with a blaster.

Elite Troopers exist in a variety of roles, but unlike Imperial [Stormtroopers](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stormtroopers "Stormtroopers")- the "elite troops" of the Empire, they rely on skill and ingenuity more than sheer force and blind obedience to the Empire's will. Thus, the best missions for them involve penetrating attacks deep behind enemy lines, or guerrilla warfare conducted on enemy-held worlds- situations where versatility and sheer determination are the Elite Trooper's best armaments and armor.

**Examples of Elite Troopers in Star Wars:** General Madine, Major Derlin, Kyle Katarn, Lieutenant Page, Kell Tainer, ARC Trooper Alpha-Ø2.

**Prerequisites:** To qualify to become an Elite Trooper, a character must fulfill the following criteria:

- **[Base Attack Bonus](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Base_Attack_Bonus "Base Attack Bonus"):** +7
- **[Feats](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feats "Feats"):** [Armor Proficiency (Light)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Light) "Armor Proficiency (Light)"), [Armor Proficiency (Medium)](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Proficiency_(Medium) "Armor Proficiency (Medium)"), [Martial Arts I](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Arts_I "Martial Arts I"), and either [Point-Blank Shot](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Point-Blank_Shot "Point-Blank Shot") or [Flurry](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flurry "Flurry")
- **[Talents](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talents "Talents"):** At least one [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from the
  - [Armor Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Armor Specialist Talent Tree"),
  - [Commando Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Commando_Talent_Tree "Commando Talent Tree"),
  - [Mercenary Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mercenary_Talent_Tree "Mercenary Talent Tree"), or
  - [Weapon Specialist Talent Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Specialist_Talent_Tree "Weapon Specialist Talent Tree")

### Class Features

- **Defense Bonuses:** At 1st level, Elite Troopers gain a +2 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense"), and a +4 [Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Class "Class") bonus to their [Fortitude Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude_Defense "Fortitude Defense").
- **Hit Points:** At each level, Elite Troopers gain 1d12 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") + their [Constitution](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Constitution "Constitution") modifier.
- **Force Points:** Elite Troopers gain a number of [Force Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Points "Force Points") equal to 6 + one-half their Character Level, rounded down, each time they gain a new level in this [Prestige Class](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prestige_Class "Prestige Class").

### Delay Damage

Elite Troopers are among the toughest individuals in the galaxy. After being exposed to numerous threats, foes, and combat situations, you've developed the ability to delay effects that would drop lesser creatures. Once per encounter as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), you can choose to delay the effect of a single attack, ability, or effect used against you. The damage or effect does not take hold until the end of your next turn.

### Talents

At every odd-numbered level (1st, 3rd, 5th, and so on), an Elite Trooper gains an additional [Elite Trooper Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper_Talent "Elite Trooper Talent"). An Elite Trooper may choose a [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") from any of the [Elite Trooper Talent Trees](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elite_Trooper_Talent_Trees "Elite Trooper Talent Trees") they wish, but they must meet the prerequisites (if any) of the chosen [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent"). No [Talent](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Talent "Talent") can be selected more than once unless expressly indicated.

### Damage Reduction

At 2nd level, you gain [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") 1 (DR 1), which means that you reduce the damage you take from any attack by 1. At every even-numbered level after 2nd, your [Damage Reduction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Reduction "Damage Reduction") improves by 1 (DR 2 at 4th level, DR 3 at 6th level, and so on).
