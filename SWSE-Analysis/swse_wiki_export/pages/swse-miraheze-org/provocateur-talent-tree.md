# Provocateur Talent Tree

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Provocateur_Talent_Tree](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Provocateur_Talent_Tree) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:16Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

As a provocateur, you specialize in turning your enemies against one another, giving your allies the advantage. This is accomplished through a combination of gaining an enemy's confidence, making careful use of deception, and coordinating your allies' attacks.

## Core Talents

### Cast Suspicion

As a [Swift Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swift_Action "Swift Action"), you can select one enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). That enemy loses all morale and insight bonuses on attack rolls and cannot be aided (using the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action) by its allies until the end of your next turn.

### Distress to Discord

You encourage your allies to sow discord among your enemies by fighting with renewed vigor. Whenever an ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") takes its [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), all enemies within 2 squares of that ally lose their [Dexterity](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dexterity "Dexterity") bonuses to their [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") until the end of your next turn.

### Friend or Foe

*Prerequisite:* **Cast Suspicion**

Whenever an ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") is missed by a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), you can (as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), once per turn) designate one enemy adjacent to that ally. Compare the attack roll of the missed attack to the [Reflex Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflex_Defense "Reflex Defense") of that enemy; if the attack would hit, the attack targets that enemy and is resolved as normal.

### Seize a Moment

*Prerequisite:* **Distress to Discord**

Once per turn as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction"), when an enemy is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") or is moved down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") by any means, you allow one ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") to take their [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind") immediately (as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")). Furthermore, when your ally takes their [Second Wind](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Wind "Second Wind"), they regain a number of additional [Hit Points](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hit_Points "Hit Points") equal to your Class Level.

### Stolen Advantage

*Prerequisite:* **Cast Suspicion**

Whenever an enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") uses the [Aid Another](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid_Another "Aid Another") Action to grant one of its allies a bonus, you can (as a [Reaction](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaction "Reaction")) designate one ally within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight"). The enemy automatically fails to aid its ally, and the ally you designate gains a +2 bonus on its next attack roll made before the end of your next turn.

### True Betrayal

*Prerequisites:* **Cast Suspicion**, **Friend or Foe**

As a [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action"), make a [Persuasion](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Persuasion "Persuasion") check against the [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") of one enemy within your [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight") that can hear and understand you. If your check result equals or exceeds the target's [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense"), that target immediately makes an attack (as a [Free Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Free_Action "Free Action")) against another target of your choice. This can be a [Melee Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Melee_Attack "Melee Attack") against an adjacent target or a [Ranged Attack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") against a target within the attacker's Point-Blank [Range](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Range "Range"). The target gains a +5 bonus to its [Will Defense](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Will_Defense "Will Defense") if it is of a higher level than you. This is a Mind-Affecting effect.
