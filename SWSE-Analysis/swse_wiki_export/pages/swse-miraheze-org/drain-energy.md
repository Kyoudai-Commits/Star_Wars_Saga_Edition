# Drain Energy

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drain_Energy](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drain_Energy) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:37:22Z.

*Reference Book: [Jedi Academy Training Manual](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jedi_Academy_Training_Manual "Jedi Academy Training Manual")*

You can draw the energy out of a powered object, such as a blaster's [Power Pack](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Pack "Power Pack") or a [Power Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator "Power Generator").

**Time:** [Standard Action](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Standard_Action "Standard Action")

**Targets:** One powered weapon or object within 6 squares of you, and within [Line of Sight](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Line_of_Sight "Line of Sight").

**Make a [Use the Force](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Use_the_Force "Use the Force") check.** The result of the check determines the effects, if any:

| DC | EFFECT |
| --- | --- |
| 20 | You drain the energy from the energy cell or cells powering a Small or smaller weapon or powered object. You completely drain the object of its energy, and it cannot be used until its [Energy Cells](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cells "Energy Cells") are replaced. |
| 25 | You drain the energy from the energy cell or cells powering a Medium or smaller weapon or powered object. You completely drain the object of its energy, and it cannot be used until its [Energy Cells](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cells "Energy Cells") are replaced. |
| 30 | You drain the energy from the energy cell or cells powering a Large or smaller weapon or powered object. You completely drain the object of its energy, and it cannot be used until its [Energy Cells](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cells "Energy Cells") are replaced. |
| 35 | You drain the energy from the energy cell or cells powering a Huge or smaller weapon or powered object. You completely drain the object of its energy, and it cannot be used until its [Energy Cells](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Cells "Energy Cells") are replaced. If the object is being powered by a [Power Generator](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Generator "Power Generator"), you drain all of the power from that instead. |

**Special:** You can spend a [Force Point](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Force_Point "Force Point") to increase the size of the object that can be drained by one size category (up to Gargantuan).
