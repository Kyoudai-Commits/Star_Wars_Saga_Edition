# Galactic Alliance Military Training

> Source: [https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galactic_Alliance_Military_Training](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Galactic_Alliance_Military_Training) · Retrieved 2026-10-09T09:36:45Z.

*Reference Book: [Legacy Era Campaign Guide](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Legacy_Era_Campaign_Guide "Legacy Era Campaign Guide")*

Service in [The Galactic Alliance](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Galactic_Alliance "The Galactic Alliance") has honed your toughness.

**Effect:** You do not move down the [Condition Track](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Condition_Track "Condition Track") the first time an attack exceeds your [Damage Threshold](https://swse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damage_Threshold "Damage Threshold") in an encounter.
